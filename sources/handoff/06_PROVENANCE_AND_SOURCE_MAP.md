## 5. Mathematical foundations to borrow explicitly

The packet already contains a broader bibliography in sources/v2_packet/09_REFERENCES_AND_PROVENANCE.md. The most relevant primary routes for the next program are:

- **Chiribella, D'Ariano, Perinotti:** [Theoretical Framework for Quantum Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.4483). Quantum combs, composition, testers and causal trace constraints.
- **Pollock and colleagues:** [Non-Markovian quantum processes: Complete framework and efficient characterization](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00589). Multi-time intervention semantics and memory.
- **Pollock and colleagues:** [Operational Markov Condition for Quantum Processes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.09811). Distinguishing process memory from a single-time state description.
- **Hartle:** [Decoherent Histories Quantum Mechanics Starting with Records of What Happens](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.04145). Records and legitimate history probabilities.
- **Everett:** [“Relative State” Formulation of Quantum Mechanics](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.29.454). Universal quantum description and relative states.
- **Ashby:** [An Introduction to Cybernetics](https://ashby.info/Ashby-Introduction-to-Cybernetics.pdf). Regulatory variety relative to disturbances and responses.
- **Aubin:** [Viability Theory](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-0-8176-4910-4). Continuation under specified constraints; linked 2009 reissue.
- **Arrow and Fisher:** [Environmental Preservation, Uncertainty, and Irreversibility](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1883074). Option-preservation intuition, not a universal irreversibility veto.
- **Ollivier, Arnold, Auger, Hansen:** [Information-Geometric Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.3708). Invariant optimization on parameterized distributions; not a universal optimizer or a lushness definition.

These are existing reference routes, not a new literature review conducted for the migration.

## 6. Cosmological and phenomenal neighbors

- **Don Page:** [Sensible Quantum Mechanics](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9506010) and [Mindless Sensationalism](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0108039). Positive-operator experiential measures are a close precedent. Borrowing that apparatus does not adopt all of Page's metaphysics.
- **Wolfram:** [Observer Theory](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2023/12/observer-theory/) and [the physics project's one-year update](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2021/04/the-wolfram-physics-project-a-one-year-update/). Relational/multiway and observer-centered neighbors.
- **Levin:** [Technological Approach to Mind Everywhere](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnsys.2022.768201). Cross-scale cognition and agency, not a solved valuer criterion.
- **Rovelli:** [Relational Quantum Mechanics](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9609002). Relational indexing without a consciousness-collapse claim.
- **Chalmers:** [Facing Up to the Problem of Consciousness](https://consc.net/papers/facing.pdf). Location of the explanatory gap.
- **Mead:** [Possible Connection Between Gravitation and Fundamental Length](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.135.B849), and **Hossenfelder:** [Minimal Length Scale Scenarios for Quantum Gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.6191). Physical motivation and limitations of terminal-resolution conjectures.

## 7. Attribution practice for new work

For each result, name what is borrowed, what is a routine specialization, what is newly implemented, and what interpretation remains conjectural. A novel synthesis need not claim novel component theorems. A close intellectual neighbor need not become the framework's identity.

Before publishing ported code, check the source license and preserve notices. This seed does not assign a new blanket license to imported materials or transcripts.
