**Status date:** 2026-09-15. Read with the conversation guide. This is the current-intent overlay on unchanged historical sources.

## 1. Two kinds of authority

For intended meaning, the originator's latest explicit clarification takes precedence over an older AI-written draft. For established results, inspect the theorem, model assumptions, implementation, and retained evidence. A user endorsement cannot broaden a theorem's scope; a theorem about a surrogate cannot settle what the originator intended.

The newest correction does not automatically erase all earlier context. In particular, global-object primacy is compatible with action-conditioned or branch-specific comparison. It rejects replacing the object by an observer's estimate, not examining a conditional part of its structure.

## 2. Settled research orientation

- Investigate the consequences of the premise that value requires valuers.
- Keep valuerhood broad and incompletely formalized.
- The global futures object is the proposed substrate; lushness is a proposed property/signature of it.
- Establish the object and meaningful structural distinctions before choosing an ordering.
- Treat persistence as a filter that reveals a corridor, not a cosmic preference or guaranteed champion.
- Investigate whether generativity emerges from relevant structure; do not insert it into a score.
- Retain the physical limits on situated control inside a complete description.
- Treat rights and virtues as hypotheses about vulnerabilities in particular interagent regimes.
- Distinguish generative integration from excisive control; terminal solitude chiefly concerns the latter.
- Keep phenomenality, soul identity, universal habitation, and terminal physical resolution open.
- Keep theology apophatic and suggestive.
- A positive capability benefit is optional, not a requirement.
- The current process-geometry proposal is approved as a lead to investigate, not a definition to defend.

## 5. Open decisions, not missing permissions

The local researcher can choose a small fixture, implement reversible analyses, and compare candidate descriptors without asking the originator to resolve the entire philosophy. Report when the choice affects interpretation:

- Which full physical state and component structure are being modeled?
- Which operations are physically implemented, and who can perform them?
- Which histories are actual records rather than arbitrary coherent paths?
- What is the common interface for process comparison?
- Is a summary exact, a lower bound, an upper bound, or a sampled estimate?
- Which property is being described without yet being ranked?
- What part of the valuer correspondence remains an input or unresolved?

If no comparison is warranted, preserve the structural result and say the ordering is unresolved. Do not hide that fact behind a default utility.
