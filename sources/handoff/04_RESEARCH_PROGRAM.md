# Research program: from continuation structure toward lushness

**Status:** proposed local program, not a claim that the experiments have run. The originator accepted the process-geometry direction as tenable. Specific fixtures, metrics, and budgets remain revisable research choices.

## 1. The question to keep fixed

Does the weighted, compositional organization of physically possible continuation contain a natural structural signature of available value substrate?

The target is the global object under a declared Logos. A bounded estimate is a later representation of that target. A finite physical model can be an exact instance for investigation, but finite computability does not redefine the target.

Avoid three shortcuts:

- Supply a favored generativity/persistence score and prove that its maximizer wins.
- Replace the field by marginal outcomes or a bounded interface, then treat everything discarded as irrelevant.
- Demand a commercial capability benefit or a universal moral command as the first deliverable.

## 2. Workstream A: establish the local starting point

Read the guide and current-position overlay before the historical packet. Run the included integrity check and exact baseline once. Read the four selected legacy reports before claiming a new result on horizons, joint compatibility, recovery, abstraction, or boundary transport.

Deliver a short intake note that states:

1. What the originator means by lushness.
2. Which statements are established, conjectural, or superseded.
3. Which part of the proposed experiment is a choice of representation.
4. Which old result is being reused.
5. One concrete way the preferred candidate could fail.

Then inspect the existing verifier. Reuse exact arithmetic or extract it into a new module without editing the source snapshot. Do not rebuild a quantum simulator or introduce a proof assistant before a concrete need arises.

**Exit condition:** the baseline reproduces and the next model's assumptions are explicit. If a mismatch occurs, resolve it before expanding the experiment.

## 3. Workstream B: specify one complete finite world

The source–memory–actuator fixture is the suggested seed. Extend it only as needed for at least two genuine interaction opportunities and a residual continuation comparison. Another fixture is acceptable if it more faithfully isolates the question.

Declare:

- The full Hilbert space or exact classical special case, components and initial state.
- The physical control implementation and which subsystem holds each record.
- The fixed law, resources and legal operations at each stage.
- The comparison actions and their physical consequences.
- The finite temporal scope and any stationary extension being claimed.
- Which records have ordinary probabilities and where coherence remains.
- The common interface/type for comparing residual processes.
- The complete object retained before any bounded observation is introduced.

State changes to future availability through physical modes, resources or controller memory. Do not arbitrarily attach a label saying “generative,” “dead,” or “agent,” then use the label as evidence.

If modeling irreversible loss, include the relevant environment or specify that irreversibility is relative to actual future access. Closed-unitary information preservation and situated unrecoverability are different statements.

**Deliverable:** MODEL_SPEC.md, a machine-readable fixture, and a diagram only if it clarifies interactions. This is model specification, not yet lushness measurement.

## 4. Workstream C: retain the full action-conditioned structure

For each comparison action, compute exact reachable subnormalized records and their continuation processes. Preserve:

1. Outcome weights under each actual instrument or policy.
2. Coherent relations still relevant to future interactions.
3. Physical state constraints and joint correlations.
4. Legal sequential composition and stage/type information.
5. Repeated occurrences and histories separately from equivalence classes of suffix responses.

Do not average over control policies unless a physical randomizer or an explicitly chosen prior is part of the model. If the object contains a family of policy-indexed outcome laws, keep it as that family.

Compare full objects first. Then compute descriptors as maps from those objects. This makes each descriptor's loss of information inspectable.

**Deliverable:** exact serialized objects, reconstruction instructions, and minimal witnesses of any difference.

## 5. Workstream D: diagnose the proposed descriptors

Start with small exact diagnostics; their role is to expose information loss, not prove the desired ethics.

| Probe | Held fixed | Varied | Meaning of a useful result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D1: presentation | Entire physical process and allowed interactions | Consistent coordinate or component renaming | Descriptor transports invariantly under the verified correspondence |
| D2: duplicate labels | Physical instrument/process | Alternative textual labels for the same description | No artificial increase from representation alone |
| D3: rare versus common | Reachable normalized residual family | Physically implemented record probabilities | Support/packing can agree while the full weighted object differs |
| D4: continuation response | Immediate observed distribution | Record-sensitive future interaction | A descriptor that sees only present performance is insufficient |
| D5: hidden joint structure | Restricted interface behavior | Coherence/correlation exposed by a later lawful joint interaction | Reuse the existing witness to test scope honesty |
| D6: occurrence versus diversity | A residual suffix response | Number, locations, or weights of actual occurrences | Retain multiplicity consequences without confusing them with new response types |
| D7: composition | A chosen diversity summary, exactly if possible | Typed incidence or legal sequential structure | Show what the summary loses, with an explicit witness |

For D2 distinguish changing a label from adding a physical randomizer or record. The latter changes the world and may be consequential.

For D6 actual copies consume resources and can change robustness, reach, or joint interactions. Do not require actual duplication to be universally neutral.

For D7 be explicit about whether the matched summary is cardinality, the full distance matrix, or a packing profile. A complete process can already encode future composition in its response semantics. Different complete semantics cannot be declared identical just because one summary agrees.

**Exit condition:** report which descriptors preserve which distinctions, not a single winner chosen by intuitive appeal.

## 6. Workstream E: implement a bounded first geometry

Begin with a finite family of exactly represented residual processes. If using a finite tester list, compute the corresponding exact pseudometric and explicitly call it a restricted-test distance. A sampled maximum is a lower bound on the global allowed supremum.

For a small finite family, exact packing numbers can be computed through the separation graph: connect a pair when its distance is greater than epsilon; a maximum clique is a separated set. Exhaustive enumeration is acceptable for small n. Report the distance matrix and optimal subsets as witnesses.

This graph is a computational representation of a chosen metric, not a replacement for coherent quantum histories. Do not turn its edges into a new physical branch ontology.

Use exact rational arithmetic where possible. For nonlinear optimization or more general quantum channels, add numerical machinery only when needed. Retain feasible witnesses/lower bounds and certified upper bounds where available; distinguish a solver's numerical optimum from a proof.

Since a finite distance matrix has finitely many distinct distances, the exact profile changes at identifiable thresholds. Record the strict “greater than epsilon” convention. Do not name epsilon a Planck scale.

Do not force a finite list to approximate the entire continuum until that approximation is the stated research question.

## 7. Workstream F: test the stronger intuition

Only after structural adequacy is understood, examine controlled contrasts involving:

- Repetition that returns to an existing repertoire.
- Noise with many distinguishable outputs.
- Feedback that changes subsequent causal possibilities.
- A controller maintaining dominance by suppressing alternatives.
- Integration that preserves or expands internal variety.

These descriptions identify questions, not supplied moral labels. Choose independent structural observations and state what they show. If a candidate treats uncontrolled noise as richly differentiated, that may be a limitation of the candidate, a genuine physical fact, or evidence that physical variety and value substrate require a further relation. Do not automatically repair the score.

If “generativity” is used in reporting, specify the observable structural property being described and distinguish it from a contribution to a lushness objective. A finite world cannot literally demonstrate unlimited novelty; investigate a finite counterpart and state that limit.

The target is to find out whether the geometry explains any of the intended distinctions. A negative result can reject the packing profile while leaving the compositional object useful; or it can reveal a deeper problem in the intuition. Preserve either conclusion.

## 8. Deferred workstreams

After the object and candidate descriptors are clearer:

- **Bounded access:** map a limited observer's representation to the complete finite object and identify Goodhart opportunities. A proxy must not become the global target.
- **Rights/fences:** identify a specific vulnerability, the heuristic that protects it, its costs, substitutes, and regime of validity.
- **Rare-event decisions:** separate probability/model errors from structural aggregation and normative treatment of tails.
- **Valuer correspondence:** investigate whether a structural feature connects to valuation without assigning agent labels by fiat.
- **Broader cosmology:** examine phenomenal coupling, terminal resolution, or multiway models only when they address a concrete unresolved question.

These are not prerequisites for the first local comparison. A capability benchmark, theological synthesis, or universal optimizer is not on the critical path.

## 9. Evidence and result reporting

Use research/PROBE_TEMPLATE.md. Every retained result should have a model/assumption description, claim status, exact command, code revision or source hash, output artifact, and interpretation.

Use three separate result labels:

- **Structural result:** what the calculation or proof establishes.
- **Interpretation:** why it may bear on lushness or GE.
- **Unresolved bridge:** what would have to be added to justify that interpretation more strongly.

Do not write tests merely to restate a definition. Write tests for concrete risks: probability normalization, composition, hidden memory, false equivalence, type mismatches, or a claimed invariant. Reuse the baseline once rather than repeatedly rerunning it as ceremony.

Keep failures and counterexamples. A candidate revision should name the failure it addresses and the new freedom it introduces.

## 10. Resource discipline and stopping

Start with a small exact finite case. Before scaling enumeration, estimate the state/protocol growth and state the question that extra compute answers. Use explicit finite budgets and resumable outputs for long searches.

Stop expanding a probe when it has answered the stated structural question, when a counterexample rejects its central claim, or when a representational ambiguity prevents interpretation. Do not broaden tests merely to accumulate green checks.

No milestone requires the user to approve routine reversible coding. Ask a focused conceptual question only when alternative answers would materially change the object being modeled. Do not ask the originator to resolve all of valuerhood to unblock a finite structural comparison.

## 11. First milestone and acceptable outcomes

**Milestone:** one exact pair of action-conditioned continuation structures, under a declared common finite model, with a description of their consequential differences that survives a verified change of presentation.

Acceptable outcomes include:

1. A candidate aspect detects a genuine difference missed by a simpler summary.
2. An attractive candidate fails because it discards weights, composition, or scope.
3. No new distinction is found beyond a known legacy result.
4. The intended comparison cannot be posed without an additional modeling decision.

Only a further justified bridge would make any of these a result about value. The project should advance by making that bridge explicit, not by changing the label on a quantum calculation.
