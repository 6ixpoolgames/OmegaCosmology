# Gradient Ethics v2 — foundation draft

## Agency, continuation, and the conditions of interagent life

**Status:** first reconstruction of the core argument, 15 September 2026. This is a working foundation for a new paper, not a completed empirical study or a validated alignment method.  
**Originator:** S. Poole. AI-assisted reconstruction following the originator's current clarifications.

## 1. The question

Gradient Ethics investigates how agency alters the conditions of subsequent agency and valuation. Actions change environments, relationships, capabilities, and the kinds of participation that remain possible. The subject includes how action changes the future space of action.

The framework begins with the philosophical commitment that value requires valuers. Valuerhood remains deliberately broad and incompletely formalized. This premise does not itself establish a duty to maximize populations, preserve every individual, or satisfy every preference. It identifies a dependence that motivates investigation of value-capable continuation.

The futures field is the structured substrate of such continuation. Lushness is a proposed property through which that structure might eventually be compared. Its mathematical identity remains open. Generativity and persistence are not supplied as its scoring criteria, and no lushness ordering is adopted in this draft.

The immediate task is to describe the dynamics and examine how familiar moral intuitions fare within them. Rights, justice, honesty, restraint, and cooperation are candidates for practices that constrain interaction productively under particular conditions. Their scope and limits must be investigated rather than inferred from their names.

## 2. The persistence filter reveals conditions, not a preferred winner

A persistence filter has no preferences. Organizations that continue have remained compatible with the conditions of their continuation. This can produce differential persistence without an explicitly represented survival objective.

The descriptive question is which trajectories, organizations, and policies remain viable under particular gradients, interactions, and disturbances. This does not entail that the survivors are morally superior, that the most generative organization wins, or that persistent systems converge on familiar ethics.

The conjecture that generative organization can support richer possibilities over suitable horizons remains a live, human-adjacent valuation and empirical hypothesis. It is not a premise required for the Darwinian engine to operate.

There are two levels to keep distinct. The dynamics determine whether an identified organization can continue. A finite corridor calculation represents that question using declared continuation requirements, allowed policies, and environmental assumptions. The calculation does not independently derive the correct definition of valuerhood or its necessary conditions.

The corridor may depend on the organization followed, the surrounding ecology, available repair, and the relevant horizon. It need not select a unique strategy or consist of one simple connected region. Actions can change its practical shape by altering the conditions under which subsequent action occurs.

## 3. Consequence is a change in a continuation process

A list of reachable endpoints can omit consequential structure. Two processes may have similar endpoint statistics while differing in memory, coordination, or responses to later interventions. Likewise, separate descriptions of participants may omit dependencies present in their joint organization.

GE therefore treats the relevant object as a continuation process: lawful transformations, records, correlations, and capacities for further interaction. In a specified model, a physical choice changes a conditional state or plugs an operation into a process, leaving a residual continuation at the remaining opportunities for interaction.

This description does not require a completed-block ontology. Under that interpretation, however, foreclosure concerns the continuations available relative to a situated process. An action does not subtract already existing histories from the totality of being. Its consequences remain part of the internal relations of the completed structure.

Successful compression of a description should preserve the consequences for which it is used. A model need not answer every possible question about the universe. It must support the particular claims being made through it, including the effects of interventions that become possible later.

## 4. What the finite quantum construction contributes

The new formal note supplies a finite realization of this process view, including a method for propagating allowed observations backward through quantum dynamics and instruments. It constructs a quotient that preserves the probabilities of the declared continuation protocols.

Let $q$ retain an effect space $\mathcal E$ before a physical operation $\Phi$, and $q'$ retain $\mathcal E'$ afterward. An exact quotient update exists precisely when

$$
\Phi^\dagger(\mathcal E')\subseteq\mathcal E.
\tag{1}
$$

The condition says that every later distinction represented by the model must have its relevant antecedent distinction retained. It is a condition for full predictive transport at the declared scope. It is not a necessary condition for every individually safe decision, and failure does not show that the operation is morally wrong.

The worked quantum model contains physical record acquisition and a memory-controlled interaction. Two preparations give identical process operators through one interface but become distinguishable when the joint system is reopened to a later interaction. The example demonstrates that adequacy depends on the available interactions, not merely the present observation.

Much of this lesson has classical counterparts already represented in the repository. The specifically quantum content includes coherence, interference, incompatible measurement contexts, and the composition constraints on physical processes. These determine which descriptions and interventions are physically legitimate. They do not supply moral standing or an ethical ordering.

The construction does not derive the Planck/Logos limit. Its finite dimensions, interaction opportunities, and access scopes are supplied. It therefore cannot yet anchor GE in a universal physical resolution of consequential difference.

## 5. Preservation, replacement, and repair

Claims of preservation should identify what is preserved and across which future interactions. Similar current performance is insufficient to establish equivalence of later capacities. Preserving each participant's separate output statistics may also fail to preserve their joint organization.

This matters for proposals to replace a process, consolidate independent participants, delete records, or remove a route of correction. An adequate argument must retain the relevant future responses and dependencies, or bound the consequences of what is omitted.

The requirement is epistemic before it is ethical: a preservation claim must mean what its evidence establishes. Ethical importance enters through the stakes being protected. A detected difference is not automatically a loss, harm, or reason to prohibit an action.

Irreversibility must likewise be specified relative to the claim. In the closed unitary part of the quantum model, information can remain in global correlations while becoming inaccessible to a particular participant. Its continued global existence does not give that participant the operations, resources, or records needed to recover its former capacities.

GE should therefore distinguish physical record retention, practical access, possible repair, and continued agency. Those notions may be related, but none substitutes for the others without an argument. A hypothetical global reversal does not establish practically available recovery.

Conversely, a local loss can participate in preserving a joint organization. The repository's sacrifice/cancer witness already establishes a finite version of this distinction relative to declared local and joint requirements. It does not by itself assign moral authority to the joint requirement.

## 6. A conditional bridge to the existing corridor machinery

The following is an elementary bridge from predictive equivalence to a declared safety requirement. It is not a new derivation of rights.

Fix a finite physical model, an admissible policy family $\Pi$, and a requirement expressed as a modeled violation record. For each $\pi\in\Pi$, let $E_{\mathrm{bad}}^\pi$ be its violation effect at the input. Assume these effects belong to the retained effect space $\mathcal E$. The same policy family and record semantics must be available in both descriptions.

For a declared risk limit $\epsilon$, define

$$
\operatorname{License}_{\epsilon}(\rho)
=\left\{\pi\in\Pi:
\operatorname{Tr}(\rho E_{\mathrm{bad}}^\pi)\leq\epsilon
\right\}.
\tag{2}
$$

If $q(\rho)=q(\rho')$, then

$$
\operatorname{License}_{\epsilon}(\rho)
=\operatorname{License}_{\epsilon}(\rho').
\tag{3}
$$

**Proof.** Equality under $q$ gives equal expectation for every element of $\mathcal E$, including every $E_{\mathrm{bad}}^\pi$. Each policy therefore satisfies the same inequality in (2). $\square$

The requirement, risk limit, and legitimacy of its protection are declared inputs. A latent classical violation flag cannot simply be assigned to incompatible quantum alternatives; the record/effect must be justified within the process semantics. A robust version over a supplied family of models requires the corresponding preservation statement for every model in that family, with common admissibility conditions.

Exact quantum completeness is not required for all practical applications. If a justified approximation gives a uniform bound

$$
\left|p_{\mathrm{bad}}^{\mathrm{true}}(\pi)
-p_{\mathrm{bad}}^{\mathrm{model}}(\pi)\right|\leq\delta,
$$

then a model prediction at most $\epsilon-\delta$ certifies a true probability at most $\epsilon$. This follows by addition of the error bound. It does not establish that such a bound is available, that the true system lies in the model family, or that arbitrary small numerical errors are harmless.

Conservative abstractions can therefore support sound decisions without preserving every distinction. What GE needs is a defensible relation between the representation, the proposed operation, and the protected requirement.

## 7. Rights-like practices as hypotheses about regimes

Rights-like practices can be investigated as comparatively stable rules for interaction when full downstream calculation is unavailable or too costly. They may preserve conditions that individual decision-makers have incentives or limited models to overlook.

The proposed mechanisms include:

- **Protected participation and contestation:** retaining routes through which affected participants can expose missing information, challenge a model, and influence correction.
- **Restraint in destructive interference:** limiting irreversible alteration of organizations whose continuation dependencies are poorly understood.
- **Procedural regularity:** making intervention conditions intelligible and predictable enough to support coordination.
- **Integrity of communication and evidence:** reducing failures caused by corrupted models or fabricated recovery claims.
- **Proportionate defensive capability:** making some protective constraints enforceable when cooperation or reciprocity alone cannot sustain them.

These are research hypotheses and candidate reconstructions of familiar practices. They are not consequences of quantum mechanics and are not already demonstrated across realistic agent ecologies.

Relevant regime variables include uncertainty, causal interdependence, the distribution of effective capability, enforcement costs, repair resources, time horizons, and the speed at which intervention opportunities change. No numerical phase diagram or sharp transition threshold has been established.

The inquiry should retain cases where a familiar heuristic protects joint continuation, cases where its support weakens, and cases where rigid application blocks necessary repair or adaptation. Failure of enforcement, absence of instrumental incentive, and failure of moral justification are separate matters. A powerful actor's ability to violate a constraint does not alone decide whether the constraint is justified under the participants' accepted commitments.

The persistence of an agent also does not imply preservation of an ecology, and the persistence of an ecology does not by itself establish its moral quality. The human-facing application must state the continuations and capacities it seeks to protect.

## 8. Power, integration, and excision

GE should analyze how capability and dependence are distributed rather than infer outcomes from the number of named agents. A plurality may combine resources and independent correction, but coordination failures and asymmetry can prevent those advantages from becoming effective power.

A generative integrated controller may embody substantial internal variety. Excisive control concerns what it closes off in the coupled field, including outside itself. No theorem currently makes every singleton fragile or every plurality robust.

At manageable asymmetry, reciprocal dependence, distributed information, and enforcement may support constraints on destructive interference. At greater asymmetry, some of those mechanisms may fail. This changes the feasible policy space; it does not reveal an ethical champion selected by dynamics.

Defensive containment can be examined from the commitments of threatened participants without proving that the threatening agent shares them. Its possibility, efficacy, proportionality, and justification remain separate questions.

The old draft's claim that preserving distributed agency is instrumentally necessary for every sufficiently powerful agent is withdrawn from this reconstruction.

## 9. The positive alignment proposal

The practical research hypothesis is that some constraints preserve capabilities needed for later learning, repair, coordination, and revision. An agent that destroys such conditions may perform well under its current model while making subsequent adaptation impossible.

The repository already contains a finite case in which safe probing and sound learning enlarge the feasible adaptive corridor relative to frozen uncertainty. It also contains a case in which unjustified elimination of the true model produces apparent safety. These establish bounded mechanisms, not a general competitive advantage in deployed AI.

An alignment application can declare human and successor agency, correction, and legitimate control as design commitments. Their inclusion is a human choice; it is not deduced from the mere persistence of the AI. The positive offer to developers is a hypothesis about sustained useful capability under novelty and self-modification.

Evaluation should examine whether the proposed constraints retain task-relevant adaptation and recovery at acceptable cost, and identify the regimes where specialization or commitment is beneficial. It should not call all restraint safe or presume that a corridor-preserving orientation wins every race.

The quantum construction helps specify what preservation and transfer of justification would require. A practical deployment can use a classical model where its adequacy or error bounds are justified. No existing result requires a lab to simulate a complete quantum universe before acting.

## 10. Changes to the earlier argument

| Earlier formulation | Position in this reconstruction |
| --- | --- |
| Value is probability-weighted future reachability. | The continuation process is a substrate for possible valuation; lushness remains unidentified. |
| Persistence and coherence determine admission to the value measure. | Persistence and generativity are investigated in the dynamics, without being supplied as lushness criteria. |
| Small probabilities of irreversible loss dominate reversible benefits. | Irreversibility makes some errors unrepairable; tradeoff claims require stakes, probabilities, horizons, and alternatives. |
| Uncertainty makes distributed agency necessary for every dominant agent. | Integration, plurality, and excision have regime-dependent consequences; no universal necessity is established. |
| Familiar rights follow as unavoidable constraints of persistent agency. | Rights-like practices are hypotheses about productive constraints in particular forms of interagent life. |
| Present predictive similarity supports preservation. | Preservation claims must cover relevant future interventions, dependencies, and correction capacities. |
| More detailed representations are always required. | Use the information needed for the declared claim, with exact preservation or justified conservative/error bounds. |
| Quantum or block structure supplies additional moral authority. | It constrains the process semantics; ethical commitments and valuer correspondence remain explicit. |

## 11. Cosmology and apophatic theology

The cosmology is a compatible interpretation and a source of research questions. GE's finite conditional arguments can stand under multiple ontologies.

Theology should remain a modest apophatic commentary. Logos may redescribe lawful relational organization; divine perspective may name completeness relative to a manifestation; terminal solitude may suggest a reading of excisive closure. These correspondences are provisional. This draft does not pursue a summa, derive classical divine attributes, or claim that a physical formalism establishes universal habitation.

## 12. What is ready and what remains

The reconstructed program has a coherent descriptive question, an explicit dependence premise, and finite tools for continuation, recovery, learning, and preservation of predictive claims. Its quantum development adds a concrete process realization and precise scope limitations.

It does not yet have a general definition of valuerhood, a derived value-space, a lushness quantity, an empirical map of rights-supporting regimes, or a demonstrated alignment advantage in capable deployed systems.

The next research deliverable should connect one well-specified interagent mechanism to a candidate practical constraint, retaining both favorable cases and cases where its supporting conditions fail. The choice of protected stakes must be explicit. Discovering those conditions can improve the account even when a familiar moral intuition requires revision.
