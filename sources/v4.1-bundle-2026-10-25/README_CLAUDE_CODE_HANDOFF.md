## 2. The theory in one page

**Philosophical foundation.** Value requires valuers. A valuer is a physical participant in consequential continuation—not necessarily conscious, purposive, biological, self-identical, long-lived or agentic. Consequence is dependence of what follows on what is here now. This holds under determinism as well as indeterminism. Prospective value concerns what can be gained, lost, preserved or created in accessible continuations. The past is realized and common to current alternatives; its remaining effects still matter through the future.

**Scope.** Valuers overlap across scales. Adding separate scores double-counts joint consequences, while selecting privileged nonoverlapping boundaries requires a moral exclusion not granted merely by finding a physical boundary. Omega's ethical comparison is of the **whole field together**, counting joint outcomes once. Local measures remain physically informative, but local and whole-field rankings can disagree. No one gains standing through greater agency or complexity.

**Physical object and measure.** Omega is the physically specified continuation structure from admissible presents under the native law. A *frame* specifies an admissible present section; it is not a system, observer or valuer; a global universe has frames but is not itself a frame. If a given complete present has a stochastic continuation law at a declared physically meaningful grain and horizon, the effective breadth (lushness) is `L = 2^H`, with `ell = log_2 L = H` in bits over the physically weighted complete accessible history. Log lushness composes additively; lushness compounds multiplicatively. Objective probability, time, records and resolution matter. Noise is counted; it cannot be dismissed because it is morally inconvenient.

**Comparison discipline.** An ethical gradient compares physically implemented, admissible options from the **same complete present under the same native law**, not deterministic histories hypothetically selected after the fact. A changed mechanism law or a different preparation can be a valid **scientific contrast**, not a same-present moral choice. Under complete determinism only one continuation follows and lushness is one; no comparison among two deterministically arising futures from that identical complete present is possible. Do not mistake epistemic uncertainty for objective branching.

**Central thesis.** Expansion of effective whole-field accessible continuations is expansion of accessible prospective value, and contraction is contraction. This is an ambitious **identification/conjecture**, tested by physical structure and ethical correspondence, not a theorem proved by Shannon's chain rule and not a mere proxy for what people already call good. Context and access change what is valuable or disvaluable; there need not be a fixed intrinsic ranking of isolated fragments. Nonisomorphic universes may tie in lushness. Full-field value can permit omniscient tradeoffs, but this does **not** license a bounded maximizer to cause harm.

**Gradient and situated ethics.** The gradient compares the relevant whole-field lushness/persistence profiles. It can have ties, incomparabilities at current resolution, no attainable maximum or horizon-sensitive rankings. The score's information-theoretic composition supplies specific conditional planning terms; generic Bellman recursion and value-of-information reasoning are not exclusive inventions of Shannon entropy. Gradient Ethics—**Book Two**—will address uncertainty, protection margins, deference, corrigibility and practical authority. This book diagnoses but does not solve the maximizer problem. Rights-like protection here means consequence-grounded safeguards under uncertainty, not extra standing or absolute deontic constraints.

**Alpha is optional.** Parts I–VII require a physically oriented continuation law. Chapters 27–28 offer an explanatory route toward its existence and a quantum realization, neither of which is a premise needed for the ethical argument. Phenomenal awareness is not derived from formal structure; the hard problem remains open.

## 4. Voice and construction (mandatory editorial requirements)

The user wants good philosophical prose that happens to be technical: elegant, intelligent, warm enough to read, not dry exposition or pseudo-technical mystique. Chapter 3 r4 is the reference voice. Strong but **not code-compliant** ASD-STE100 influence: clear syntax, stable terms, explicit logical bridges, one substantial claim per sentence, rhythm and personality preserved. The author likes an objection conceded at its strongest before the reply.

**Every chapter's TL;DR is a stand-alone miniature of its argument. Assume most readers read only the TL;DR and glance at the body to clarify.** It begins with one or two sentences, then several concise bold result statements with supporting plain-language explanation. A reader who reads **only the bolded sentences** must grasp the result and its sequence. Don't use a bullet merely to announce an agenda, call a chapter important, or promise a later proof. The body earns and qualifies the claims made up front. No toy-model numerics in TL;DRs.

Use the status tags as the current manuscript actually uses them (foundation F, norm N, physical premise P, definition D, argued result, theorem T, model M, central conjecture C, open), and cite Appendix A propositions precisely. Put background primers where outsiders need them, not everywhere. No undefined notation or bare cross-reference standing in for reasoning. Short, strong section headings should tell a story in sequence. Terms are chosen by the reader's likely understanding, not by the author's comfort with jargon.

**Avoid these recurrent errors:**

- Writing “not still open” or convoluted negatives when a clean direct statement exists.
- Treating phenomenological perception as a prerequisite for valuerhood.
- Describing a frame as a persistent physical system or calling the universe the frame.
- Comparing two deterministic outcomes from the identical complete present.
- Conflating `L` and `log L` or treating `E[log L]`, `log E[L]` and `log L(E[T])` as interchangeable.
- Calling increased correlation or reduced conditional entropy *harm* without showing relevant futures narrowed.
- Making moral judgments from separate local scores, or confusing universal whole-field evaluation with authorization to maximize it.
- Treating noise as an error in the measure, requiring organization to win every test, or equating autocatalytic lineage with indefinitely sustained life.
- Replacing the central conjecture with the weaker claim that lushness merely resembles familiar moral intuitions.

## 5. Current open issue: consequence flux and chapter 25

The candidate chapter-25 quantity is

`Phi_n(X) = I(X_0^set ; R_(1:n) | R_0)`, using physically implemented changes in the state of a part X to see how the remaining field's history responds. The chapter proposes **internally sourced, record-conditioned and selective** flux as a graded marker of agency, not moral standing. This is **not yet a validated formal definition** and is not in Appendix A.

Important methodological critique to keep open for author discussion:

1. This conditional mutual information measures **distinguishability of downstream histories**, not how much total future lushness or physical consequence has changed. A binary switch has `Phi <= 1 bit` regardless of how vast its downstream effect becomes. A gradient/leverage profile in `Delta ell_n` is a separate quantity.
2. The scalar does not itself establish the three agency qualifiers. Conditioning on the **rest's initial state** does not prove dependence of the action on the **controller's internal record**.
3. Physically setting X while all of R is fixed may be impossible under conservation/coupling; define admissible interventions and their actuators. Across different initial states one is studying causal structure, **not** the same-present ethical gradient.
4. A thermostat or microbe can run on externally supplied gradients. “Internally sourced” may be better characterized as **endogenously mediated record→action feedback** than as self-supplied energy. “Selective” must not mean simply lowering whole-field entropy.
5. The intervention distribution over X's states is unspecified; mutual information depends on it. Natural-policy weighting and channel-capacity maximization ask different questions.

The robust conceptual point is: **agency is physical feedback control, a special organization of consequence; agency, causal influence, whole-field lushness leverage and moral standing are distinct.** Do not replace the candidate with a confident new theorem in chapter 26 without discussing this with the author. The author may choose to retain the provisional definition and leave precise agency measurement for later research.

## 6. Experimental arm: what is and is not established

Codex is independently upgrading the experimental platform. The supplied `ARCHITECTURE_VISION_SPEC_r2.md` describes a compositional, physically costed, reversible continuous-time Markov mechanism with explicit source/off baths, shared rule semantics, exact and sampled backends, resolution/conditioning contracts and source-frozen reporting. The engineering handoff proposes M0–M2 first; **a specification is not evidence that the new code has already passed its tests.**

Latest supplied E1 (`RESULTS(2).md`, interpretation companion): 65 law/cadence cases, 650 physical history profiles, a fixed small chemical CTMC with mechanisms from catalytic ablation through local cycles to two-site cross-assistance/reciprocity. The reported 1,935 contrasts share profiles and are not independent experimental repetitions. At physical horizon 4096, local catalytic cycles exceeded ablation in **18/18** whole-field contrasts, and cross-assistance was positive in the tested whole-field comparisons. In one anchor, catalysis added about **2,719 bits** of whole-history log lushness relative to ablation, but also hundreds of additional events and some absorbed work.

**Interpret honestly:** This is a valuable finite-model existence result for physically costed catalysis expanding measured history breadth against its comparator. It is **not** proof that organized generativity beats every physically matched thermal competitor. Large source-off gains can arise from thermally reversible kinetic traffic. Near-additive reciprocal effects in **log** lushness need not show a special reciprocal synergy (though log-additivity implies lushness multiplication). Supplied assembly success does not imply indefinite survival of the catalyzed lineages. Projection intervals sometimes leave local ranking signs unresolved. Same-law different preparations eventually share asymptotic rate while retaining finite offsets. Cadence and source matching remain consequential. No controller experiment has yet established chapter 25's agency proposal.

If evidence for a chapter depends on the new upgraded arm, **leave an honest evidence stub** with exact conditions for promotion. A single well-matched, nonproductive physical comparator can be more decisive than a giant sweep; do not dictate that actual absorbed work, events and all realized outcomes be identical, which would erase the mechanism's causal effects. Never force a positive result. A real measure should be allowed to prefer noise under some conditions and organization under others.
