# Distinct-slot module bindings

27 October 2026. Executable finite-classical adapter contract, not a new
definition of Omega or a physical-faithfulness theorem.

A Module declares owned coordinates, external coordinate ports, native rule
pairs, energy terms, forbidden configurations and executable material inventory.
A Binding gives the module a unique namespace and maps every port explicitly to
one owned coordinate in the assembled world. A module's local names, rule IDs,
parent/child marks and material exchange references are mapped together.

Port roles and finite domains must agree exactly. An additive energy port can
also declare its rational energy per tick; the composed Hamiltonian must supply
that tick without conditional interactions on it. No implicit bank/carrier is
chosen, and local aliases are rejected. All declared guards, updates and energy
terms must reference owned coordinates or bound ports. LocalModel then audits
native events, inventory changes and source-off balance as usual.

Bindings are static descriptions. They add no state coordinates, choice prior,
entropy contribution or free control event. Namespacing alone preserves the
physical generator up to the corresponding state/coordinate identification.
The small reference case verifies that numerical statement; broader symmetry
and refinement claims retain their own obligations.

The current implementation covers distinct finite slots and additive bank
energy. Resource-conformation energy is supplied by its owning fragment, not
inferred from the word resource. This contract alone does not prove material
adequacy, a fair construction bill, causal necessity or exterior cancellation.
Those must be inspected in each actual model and executable comparison.

Future interaction-energy ports, quotient-equivariant lineage, transport,
population changes and dynamic graph rewrites need explicit extensions. They
must not silently reinterpret the current role/domain/tick contract.
