# R2 interpretation r1

24 October 2026. Interpretation of the unchanged
[source-frozen numerical results](results-2026-10-24/RESULTS.md).

**Paid further capacity is used, contributes to actual reconstruction and
increases lushness in this finite world.** The result separates useful capacity
from the activity of constructing a component. It also separates total use
from the success of any particular construction episode.

## The physical loop exists

The original assembled interface assists paid construction of a store. The
store exposes bank states that were previously inaccessible. In the driven
assembly-seed arm by T1024, there are 7.908 expanded-only captures, opposed by
5.421 reversals. The bank occupies its new range with probability .543366 at
that deadline, and its stationary probability is .546879.

There are 6.382647 paid activations starting with energy above the old capacity.
They spend 5.032940 energy units from that excess range. These events restore
physical active conformations; the upkeep transition's heat dissipation alone
is not credited as repair. The bank is fungible, so this does not identify the
ancestry of each energy packet or show that every repair needs the new store.

The conservative path witness completes with probability .043289: first new
store construction → expanded-only capture → high-bank paid activation restoring
both constituents → re-binding of the functional interface. The monitor aborts
if the episode breaks and never silently restarts. Thus it instantiates an
ordered return through the new capacity, rather than just counting constructions.
Four ordered steps are not four enabling generations.

## Lushness records a positive consequence

At T1024 and cadence .25, assisted construction exceeds basal construction by
50.784027 bits. Its limiting rate increment is .027108202 bits/time. Against
the equally costed construction sham, useful capacity adds 27.936800 bits and
.010155583 bits/time. Both differences have the same positive sign at all
three cadences. Across all preparations/horizons/source settings, all 72
assistance contrasts and all 72 capacity-versus-sham contrasts are positive.

Compared with the sham, the extra capacity increases stationary bound
probability by .009789 and mean active units by .023193. The finite T1024
increases are .008645 and .020793. This accompanies a concrete functional
change rather than an isolated register contributing its own switching.

In the declared coordinate order, the T1024 capacity advantage includes
positive active-coordinate and carrier contributions, while the store
coordinate itself contributes -.424034 bits and the bank coordinate -24.056641.
The total gain consequently does not require an entropy bonus from the new
store's own fluctuations. Coordinate attribution is nevertheless order-dependent;
the full history score is unchanged by this explanatory split.

## What the qualified findings say

The first-episode witness is less likely with assisted than basal construction
from the assembly seed: .043289 versus .095202 by T1024. Meanwhile assisted
construction produces more total expanded capture, more high-bank repair, and
more function. These are different questions. A faster first construction does
not guarantee that its particular episode completes every subsequent step.
Catalysis accelerates both construction and its reverse; this is a plausible
contributor to the first-episode difference, not a separately established causal
decomposition. No missed guessed probability is scored as a theory failure.

The useful-capacity arm also has about 4.956 fewer accumulated units of bound
time than the sham by T1024, despite its higher endpoint and stationary function.
More capacity need not maximize every protection or uptime readout.

Prebuilding the store at the same total energy costs one bank tick in the
initial preparation. In the driven assisted law that preparation loses
14.508251 bits to starting with the assembled first interface and unbuilt store
by T1024. This finite preparation comparison is retained. A productive mechanism
can matter without every larger initial stock being the lusher preparation.
All same-law preparation rates tie. Across the entire preparation map,
99 comparisons are positive and 45 negative, with no temporal sign flips.

## Matching and scope

Relative to the sham, the capable driven arm accepts 2.945972 more gross work
units and uses 2.293764 more net external work by T1024. It also has 3.843371
more expected jumps. It therefore demonstrates an advantage under the same
source prescription, not equal-work or equal-activity superiority. Source-off
controls and all native variation remain in the published map; none is subtracted
from lushness. Every energy account closes with its declared reverse refunds.

These are 180-state worlds, with 150 states in the capacity sham, fixed material,
one contact/carrier and unresolved bath/source histories. The store cannot
dissolve through its coupled refund path when doing so would overflow the bank;
an independent spill path is absent. No disruptive-shock robustness, microscopic
gas comparison, controller policy search or ethical correspondence is established.
