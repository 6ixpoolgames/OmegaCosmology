Six laws × three cadences × three preparations × four horizons = 216 profiles.
Every root contains the same two apparatus constituents, latent store
constituent and carrier, with energy 4.5. The basal arm removes only the paired
assisted-store construction path. The sham retains the costed construction
kinetics but exposes no extra bank capacity. Source-off removes only the
declared source pair. Matched conductances do not imply equal realized work
or event traffic.

The 25 October audit narrows the causal claim: the monitor certifies ordering,
not enabling ancestry; upkeep is not maintenance necessity; and the loaded
store lacks a spill/breakdown route. Cadence stability does not imply horizon
stability. Three preparation contrasts reverse with horizon at every cadence.

The new shared `finite_study` assay consumes protocol-defined physical models,
observables, event readouts and passive monitors. The same assay reproduces
the retained R1 profile. `monitors` defines ordered path queries with explicit
absorbing aborts. Its labels never enter the physical entropy calculation.
Marked-path queries on nontrivial symmetry quotients await an equivariance
audit; invariant whole-history/count observables remain supported.
