## What the results establish

The stipulated costed storage capacity is used. Expanded-only captures and
paid activations occur, and useful capacity has positive whole-modeled-field
history differences against the stipulated sham. Construction assistance also
has a positive comparison across the retained grid. Every original score and
unfavorable observation remains evidence for those specific laws.

The grid has six physical laws, three preparations, three cadences and four
horizons. It is not a sweep of construction cost, hazard, source strength or
loss/repair ratios. The 72 positive capacity comparisons are repeated queries
of this limited physical family, not 72 independent physical regimes.

## Corrections to causal interpretation

The ordered witness accepts basal store construction and resource capture
through native routes. It establishes that an event sequence occurs; it does
not establish that the original assembly made the captured energy accessible,
that this energy caused its own reconstruction, or that connected descendants
enabled subsequent generations. The phrase "physical loop exists" in r1 was
too strong if read as that stronger causal assertion.

Capture is possible without active apparatus. The assembly accelerates resource
unlocking and construction rather than making them strictly necessary. These
substitutes are legitimate physics, but their contribution must be distinguished
from an ancestry-based self-maintenance claim.

The rule named upkeep only changes bank energy. It is reversible relaxation,
not an expenditure whose payment preserves function. Bound constituents cannot
deactivate directly and must first unbind. Empty-bank operation is allowed.
Paid activation is represented, but maintenance necessity is not established.

Store breakdown is restricted to a reverse-construction refund route. A loaded
store cannot dissolve at bank ticks 3–6 because the refund does not fit in the
post-dissolution capacity. No spill/dissipation alternative exists. This is a
physical-model restriction that can support persistence, not a numerical bug
or a validated model of storage destruction. Its quantitative contribution
requires a changed-law comparison; the structural audit does not estimate it.

## Preparation and time-profile corrections

The prebuilt-store root reallocates .5 energy from bank to store, maintaining
total initial energy 4.5. Actual construction spends 1, incorporates .5 and
dissipates .5, leaving total modeled energy 4 from the assembly-seed root.
Thus the equal-energy prebuilt root is not that actual construction outcome.
Both are valid preparations, but they do not compare building now versus
waiting from a common present with the same paid construction history.

The source-off sham's prebuilt-root advantage also exists despite the store
having no useful extra capacity. Storage usefulness alone is therefore not an
adequate causal explanation of the source-off preparation pattern.

"Zero temporal sign flips" in the original report meant no flips across the
three cadences at fixed horizons. Three preparation contrasts change sign with
horizon at every cadence: nine comparison/cadence groups. The driven basal
store-seed contrast changes sign twice over the sampled windows. The new
report gives brackets and interval accumulation differences; exact crossover
locations and all between-grid behavior remain uncomputed.

Equal same-law limiting rates do not erase accumulated preparation differences.
The transient history differences remain as offsets when the asymptotic
conditions hold. Source-off has native bath fluctuations; it is not the
non-generative comparator of a different driven law.

## How to use R2 now

R2 is retained as a bounded capacity-use result and a valuable structural-audit
fixture. It does not establish autocatalytic closure, connected three-generation
compounding, maintenance necessity, spatial disorder, or an ethical verdict.

The new functional queries distinguish uninterrupted function, deadline function,
loss followed by return, and repeated entries/exits. None is renamed reconstruction
without its own physical evidence. All represented random activity stays in the
lushness total. Parameter exploration remains welcome; no guessed threshold or
missed prediction is converted into a theory failure.

Next work follows the [ecology-analysis specification](../../platform/specs/ECOLOGY_ANALYSIS_SPEC_r1.md):
repair the fixture's storage/maintenance assumptions, establish connected
enabling ancestry, then study interacting organizations with resolved local and
whole-field consequences under explicit shared-resource and niche physics.
