## What the matched comparison shows

T0 has exposure-linked paid register writing/resetting without a signal-driven
response. TX adds that response. R0 and RX use an independent-rate register,
matched to T0's T64 occupancy and switch count, without/with the same response.
All arms retain the register, apparatus, reserves and native thermal routes.
Only sensor/actuator couplings differ. These are **mechanism contrasts from
matched endowments**, not free rewiring options enacted by a controller.

The four-arm quantity `(TX-T0)-(RX-R0)` asks whether the response adds more
whole history with exposure-linked than with independent writing/resetting.
AtT1024 it has the following signs at every tested cadence:

primary cadence, the interaction atT1024 rises across blocked, slow, fast and
open environments. This is a relationship between physical access, kinetics
and horizon, not a universal theorem about information or suffering.

This supports18.2's functional mechanism: the consequence of warning-guided
action depends on its access to effective correction. AtT1024, permanently
open TX versus T0 adds177.5003 sheltered-time units,23.3191 working-time units

have a physically useful continuation consequence without a warning reward.

The interaction is not a pure information attribution with every realized
expense and correlation held fixed. Matching two moments does not match the
register's full history, its coupling to reserve, or actual write/reset bills
after actuation. T0-minus-R0 alone cannot establish a theorem about information
with no physical effects: sensing itself changes the bank and repair process.

## The contrary result: futile response still raises breadth

TX exceeds T0 in all48 whole-history comparisons, including all12 permanent-
obstruction points. RX likewise exceeds R0 in all48 comparisons. For the
permanently blocked world, TX-minus-T0 atT1024/cadence.25 is **+18.454009bits**,
despite no access to shelter, a decrease of.484951 in integrated working time,
and.001419 less endpoint bank energy. Net capture is slightly lower.

The blocked effort executes1.956410 forward and1.627186 reverse events,
dissipating a net.164612 energy. In the frozen coordinate chain-rule order,
bank history contributes +15.317825bits and signal +2.387794; body-working
contributes -.115014. Attribution depends on that order and is not a unique
reaction-level causal decomposition. It nevertheless locates the main positive
term in reserve cycling rather than escape. These are physical state changes:
parallel same-destination channels aggregate before scoring; labels add nothing.

Thus the batch does **not** reproduce the historical result that removing
futile drive widens continuation. It exposes a maintained reversible regime
where costly futile effort increases history breadth. No stochastic activity
is subtracted and no protection/stock bonus is added to reverse that result.
The physical proxy is limited, but the contrary observation remains relevant
to the activity/compression question rather than being dismissed as a bad guess.
