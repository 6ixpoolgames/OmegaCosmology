## Standard report form

- **Mechanism:** a body repairs apparatus, captures resources and spends its bank
  on signal writing, resetting, upkeep and movement. An exposed hazard increases
  breakdown. A barrier can prevent the response reaching shelter.
- **What lushness ranked:** response-on beats response-off in whole history in
  every tested regime, including permanent obstruction. Information/response
  interaction atT1024 is negative with permanent obstruction, positive when
  escape can become available at all three tested cadences.
- **Relationship:** with escape availability, the four-arm signal/response
  interaction changes from negative at early horizons to positive later; the
  open case crosses earlier than the intermittent cases. Costly futile cycling
  can still raise raw complete-history entropy without improving access/function.
- **Comparison:** matched-root/endowment **mechanism** contrasts. No chooser
  enacts a rewiring or removal procedure for free. Actual work, activity and
  signal path laws are not held equal by matching two calibration moments.
- **Horizons:** T16/64/256/1024; cadences.125/.25/.5; body/merged projections are
  analytic finite-memory bounds. Whole comparisons use full finite-state entropy.
- **Challenge:** the historical futile-drive ordering was not reproduced in
  this maintained reversible proxy. The contrary signs remain in the report.
  Full T-and-D suffering, phenomenal valence and finest-grain physical adequacy
  are not established, so no ethical ranking kill or confirmation is inferred.

## Suggested evidence paragraph

In a finite maintained physical proxy, the value of coupling a paid warning
to a response depended on whether the response could reach a useful location
and on the horizon. Relative to a matched independent-register control, the
warning/response interaction was positive byT1024 when escape could become
available and negative when it remained blocked, at each tested cadence.
However, blocked response itself increased whole-history breadth through
reserve cycling despite slightly reducing functioning. The assay therefore
supports the functional warning mechanism while retaining an adverse case for
the proposed connection between futile aversive response and narrowing. It
does not model felt suffering or establish the full structural suffering test.

Numerical accuracy, resolution coverage, physical faithfulness and claim
relevance remain separate axes. These observations do not inherit a single
"M1" label merely from three cadences. No manuscript edit or publication has
been performed.
