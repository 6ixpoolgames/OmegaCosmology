# R1 interpretation r1

24 October 2026. Interpretation of the completed
[numerical report](results-2026-10-24/RESULTS.md). This is separate from the
unchanged source-frozen case results and does not revise the objective.

**The new substrate reproduces a positive costed-access mechanism and a
preparation advantage.** Both are visible without an optimizer, organizational
reward or removal of thermal activity. They answer different questions.

## 1. Organization changes the same-law finite continuation

Under the driven intact law, the built root beats the equal-energy unassembled
root and the nine-root same-energy reference at every tested horizon and
cadence: 24/24 comparisons. At cadence .25, its advantages over the unassembled
root are +5.778, +15.410, +27.590 and +33.258 bits at T16,64,256,1024.

The limiting preparation offset is +33.336 bits at that cadence. The two
roots have identical limiting rates because they inhabit the same finite
irreducible law. This is an enduring preparation offset, not a growing rate
advantage and not a defect in the test. There is no resolved claim here about
a mobile microscopic gas. Count-state R1 has no translational degree of
freedom and does not overturn the earlier spatial comparison.

## 2. The access mechanism makes a sustained difference

Retaining the assisted reversible unlocking pair beats ablating it in all
72 comparisons, across source-on/off, preparations, horizons and cadences.
For the driven built root at T1024, gains are +171.865,+140.926,+108.924 bits
at cadences .125,.25,.5. The corresponding rate increments are
+.165752,+.136222,+.105676 bits/time. Their signs agree throughout this tested
temporal corridor; physical faithfulness of finer/state refinements is open.

At T1024, the access-capable driven arm has .025563 greater bound probability
and .052473 more active units than the ablation. Thus the gain accompanies a
functional difference. It is not solely a numerical fluctuation of a register
that drives nothing. Most of the positive coordinate contribution is in the
carrier, with additional bond/bank contributions and negative activation-coordinate
contributions in the declared order. Those shares are explanatory, not an
order-independent attribution of value.

Catalytic speed also raises history entropy without the drive: the source-off
rate increment at cadence .25 is .017637 bits/time, although both source-off
laws have the same Gibbs equilibrium weights. The source-by-mechanism rate
interaction is consequently +.118585 bits/time. That supports the interaction
of access with a gradient, while keeping the thermal kinetic effect visible.
No contribution has been subtracted from either arm's lushness.

## 3. Costs and reconstruction are actually paid

The built intact driven root accumulates, by T1024, 4.129528 net units captured
into the bank and 3.775623 net units spent internally on activation/upkeep.
The remaining .353905 raises stored bank energy. All reverse refunds are kept
explicit. The bank residual is below 2.6e-14 across the complete batch.
This account includes capture from the stipulated carrier/bath/source network;
it does not identify the ancestry of each captured energy packet or prove
equal-work conversion superiority.

Both paid activation and re-binding occur repeatedly. The unconditional chance
of at least one dissolution followed by re-binding by T1024 is .999866 for
the built root and .995079 for the unassembled root. This witness neither
restarts nor contributes entropy. A re-binding need not require paid activation,
so it is evidence of reconstructability, not a generation-depth certificate.

At the same deadline, the intact driven law has about 1.699 active units and
.4645 bound probability. Repeated rebuilding and intermittent function coexist;
having ever rebuilt is not continuous persistence. All states still have native
paths back to function, so this world does not test irreversible closure or
robustness to destructive shocks.

## 4. Matching and the observations that limit the reading

The capable arm accepts 9.620749 fewer gross external work units than its
ablation at T1024, but also returns 12.029907 fewer. Its net external work is
therefore 2.409158 higher. The result cannot be described as equal net accepted
work, equal activity, or simply more incoming work. There are 28.006762 more
expected physical state jumps. All are retained in the score, and Nbar/F plus
the coordinate shares disclose their role. A matched-work/activity comparison
would be a separate question.

Five of the 96 preparation contrasts favor the unassembled root. All five
occur at T16 with the access pair ablated. The driven ablated contrast flips
sign between cadences .25 and .5; the source-off ablated contrast is negative
at all three. They remain in the result map. The intact access comparison
does not inherit that early control reversal.

Source-off versus driven are different boundary laws, not choices from a single
common present. Bath/source histories are unresolved, so statements apply to
the whole modeled field. This is a physical mechanism experiment, with no
agent-policy search or interpersonal ethical correspondence tested.

## 5. The next physical question

Proceed to R2's already-expressed paid storage construction: can the first
access interface improve construction and sustained use of a second interface,
with the latent material present in every arm? Keep both interfaces' basal
paths, all native variation, paid/reverse construction and matched endowments.
Measure new usable capacity and its effects on the full continuation rather
than equating another unit or more turnover with another enabling generation.

That is a bounded further-enabling test. Three-link recursive compounding,
robustness under disruption, and population/cross-scale ecology remain later
explicit physical additions rather than interpretations of this R1 witness.
