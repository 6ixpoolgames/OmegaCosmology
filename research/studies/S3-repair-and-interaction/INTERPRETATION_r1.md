## 2. Reconstruction feedback changes the matched-fault ordering

Repair-fault and access-fault roots each lack one of R/Y and.5 formation energy.
All other material, stored bank energy and boundary settings match. Under
feedback, Y can assist rebuilding R; removing only that paid conversion pair
retains its binding contact and native/unassisted reconstruction.

At T4096/.25, repair-fault minus access-fault is:

| Source conductance | Feedback present | Feedback conversion absent |
|---:|---:|---:|
| .02 | +.459174 bits | -2.326080 bits |
| .12 | +.351315 bits | -3.717296 bits |

The same directions hold at every registered horizon for these laws. The two
feedback-present laws also retain their positive fault-comparison sign at.125
and.5. The ablated laws were only evaluated at.25; do not extend the audit to them.

Both fault types have the same limiting rate within each law. Their long-run
differences are offsets; the largest rate-offset residual at T4096 is2.2e-9 bits.
This is the expected finite irreducible-law behavior, not a missing sustained
rate advantage.

Repair loss gives slower unconditional first restoration of the complete R/Y
motif even when its whole-history score is higher. For source.02 with feedback,
both working by some time before T16 has probability.4640 after repair loss
versus.4677 after access loss; without feedback it is.3408 versus.4675. At T64
the corresponding probabilities are.8844/.8963 and.7464/.8954. By T4096 both
are nearly one, but integrated function and first-recovery profiles still differ.
Restoration ever reached is not function maintained or present at the deadline.

Thus the downstream repair structure changes the weighted continuation and
the ranking of matched immediate losses. It does not establish that damage to
repair capacity must always be worse: access loss also affects the resource
path needed to fund reconstruction. Nor does a higher history score establish
better recovery, greater stock or an ethical benefit.

## 3. Both fault preparations outrank the intact diagnostic

All80 registered fault-minus-intact preparation comparisons are positive,
including every tested horizon/cadence. At T4096/.25 their differences range
from+16.993 to+26.489 bits. This observation is retained prominently, not removed
because it is unfavorable to a simple protection intuition.

The intact reference starts with5 energy, versus4.5 after either fault. Those
are different-energy preparations, whereas the primary fault-versus-fault
comparison is matched. No damage operation, its implementation costs, or bath
record of the lost energy has been modeled from a shared earlier present.
Therefore this is not yet a verdict in favor of enacting damage.

The initially intact/full-bank configuration has no precursor available for
immediate repair, no open binding contact and no room for a capture deposit.
The fault configurations have immediate paid reconstruction/binding routes
that can spend bank energy and change subsequent access. Their transient
branching can exceed the intact root's. That is a plausible mechanism, not a
complete causal attribution proven by this comparison. A paid fault procedure
and work/activity controls would test the stronger interpretation. Do not add
a protection bonus or erase reconstruction randomness to change this result.

## 4. Interaction relationships and kinetic limits

At T1024/.25, compared with the appropriate baseline:

- Local productive conversion exceeds nonconverting contacts by149.136 bits.
- One-way help exceeds local conversions by29.822; reciprocal exceeds local
  by59.905 and exceeds one-way by30.083.
- Higher offered source conductance raises history; lower source lowers it.
- Faster binding/release kinetics raise history; slower kinetics lower it.
- Faster paired local conversion raises history; slower conversion lowers it.

All these signs hold at the other registered horizons. There are no sign flips
on the sampled temporal/horizon grids; absence of flips is not proof of none
between samples. Only local/one-way have the second interaction cadence.

Binding conductance changes arrival and release together, not residence alone.
It preserves the binding equilibrium ratio. The raw gain from faster binding
is large (+926.866 bits at the fast-contact point), emphasizing that kinetic
activity remains consequential. Productive conversion gains are real full-law
differences but not isolated against a work-and-event-matched noise process.
Such a comparator still needs a feasible physical construction.

All interaction arms have the same slots, contacts, energies and initial root;
the control does not simply delete the signal/contact coordinate. Occupancy,
correlations and realized work can nevertheless differ. Higher rates here are
differences between induced physical laws, not open-ended population growth.

declared whole/R/Y sampled observation. Candidate transition support is full,
so each finite future word remains possible. The query excludes the conditioned
initial symbol; it does not mistake a different root label for a lost future.

Nonzero foreclosure, contraction without foreclosure, projection memory,
suppression LP optima and irreversible graph closure are independently checked
on small reference cases. Exponential underflow cannot manufacture a structural
zero. The suppression diagnostic is implemented only for finite word laws;
long-history suppression curves and a universal physical definition remain open.
