Completed:20 cases,168 profiles,254 comparisons. Early one-way assistance
increases whole history while lowering the donor projection at both audited
cadences; the donor's later bounds remain unresolved. Reconstruction feedback
changes the ordering of matched repair/access faults. Both fault preparations
also outrank the different-energy intact diagnostic, which remains reported.
Foreclosure and graph closure stay zero in these reversible laws despite
different recovery and history profiles. Physical faithfulness remains open.

The repair map is a two-constituent R/Y loop with an occupied repair contact,
resource access through Y and optional Y-assisted reconstruction of R. Matched
fault roots each lose one constituent and.5 formation energy. The intact root
has.5 more energy and is an explicitly different-energy diagnostic. All damage
is reversible; permanent repair-hardware destruction is not implemented.

The interaction map keeps four component slots, two banks, one shared carrier
and all six contacts in every arm. It varies paid contact conversions, supply,
binding kinetics and competing local conversions. Native alternatives, release,
all paired reverses and physical random activity remain. Component groups are
not identified with human agents or suffering states.

Projected histories retain hidden-state memory. Exact foreclosure is a
structural-support query, not entropy contraction or a cutoff on small
probabilities. Graph closure and unconditional recovery are reported separately.
The finite-word suppression LP is validated as a diagnostic; long-history
suppression curves are not claimed for this study.
