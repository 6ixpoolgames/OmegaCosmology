# E1 precision refinement: interpretation

25 October 2026. [Numerical report](evidence/REPORT.md).
The E1 physical laws, roots, represented state grain, horizon and total scores
are unchanged. This adds an instrument for small local-history effects; it does
not replace the original map or claim a new physical experiment.

## What became clearer

The exact observation-memory ladder is expensive and converges slowly in this
example. Memory3 costs276.5 seconds for two laws and still leaves the assembled
donor difference atT4096 in [-75.58,+78.18] bits. More local observation memory
is not the same as a different physical resolution: it tightens the readout of
the same hidden-history quantity.

A separate hidden-state filtering estimator can retain the full observation
past without enumerating every word. Exact short references validate its
expectation identity. An exact-mean control variate reduces sampling variation
while preserving the quantity. AtT4096 the controlled standard errors are .283
and .296 bits, versus3.029 and3.329 without the control, with512 paired paths.
That is roughly a tenfold precision improvement in these two comparisons,
not a universal performance guarantee.

For AtoB versus local cycles at low supply, low bill and slow native turnover:

From the unassembled root, however, the donor difference estimates atT16,T64
andT256 are negative, with approximate intervals wholly below zero. Whole-model
differences at those same horizons are +.989,+8.070 and+34.475 bits. This is
statistical evidence for a transient part/whole opposition that the analytic
memory1-3 intervals could not resolve. AtT1024 the donor interval straddles zero;
byT4096 it is positive. The assembled root follows a different local profile.

The complete result is conditional on the present and the horizon. An aggregate
gain need not mean every projected history gains at every horizon. The profile
matters; a single late estimate cannot substitute for it.

## What is still unresolved

These are approximate paired Student t intervals across independent histories,
not rigorous finite-sample or rounding certificates. The independently retained
analytic intervals still span zero. Distribution-free Hoeffding intervals also
span zero by a wide margin. Short exact calibration supports implementation
correctness; it does not establish95% coverage at every long horizon or after
selecting interesting comparisons. All ten donor estimates remain reported.

No cadence robustness is established for these small donor effects: the
refinement uses .25 only. No local limiting rate was computed by this method.
The main E1 whole-model results and their original cadence/horizon limits stand.

The helper still has no separate fuel bill or occupied catalytic complex. The
recipient pays construction, and both interact through one shared carrier.
The early donor loss is consistent with resource competition, but this study
does not isolate a unique cause, instantiate coercive control or identify these
cycles with human valuers. It supports neither an ethical correspondence nor a
theory failure on its own.

## Consequence for the next physical module

The instrument can now examine small projected-history effects without treating
coordinate entropy shares as private lushness or silently making projections
Markov. Use analytic bounds alongside separately labelled estimates. Where a
certified sign is essential, this estimator alone does not provide one.

The next useful physics is a costed, saturating interaction: catalytic occupation
or sequestration with finite ports, native release/reverse paths, and actual
effects on the helper's competing uses. This makes a donor cost physical rather
than attaching a harm label. Resource transfer, material loss and distinct but
interacting niches can then extend that same interaction grammar. No general
population or ethical policy optimizer is added by this analysis step.
