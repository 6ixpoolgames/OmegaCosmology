## Scientific object

Two two-member component cycles share one finite carrier under the same bath.
What do costed local and cross-organization catalytic relations change about
construction ancestry, function, resource use and complete continuation breadth?
How do the relationships depend on offered supply, paid construction cost and
native turnover? Does an initially organized preparation change the finite
profile relative to the same unassembled material and energy?

This is an exploratory map of one finite chemistry. It need not favor assistance,
organization or either party. Missed expectations are not theory failures.

## Physical model and matching

Use the generic `autocatalytic_ecology` grammar. Groups A and B each have x/y
slots and a bank; the shared carrier is spent/locked/usable. Four fixed material
slots and one carrier are present in every arm. Every rule has its reverse.
Formation energy .5, bank quantum .5, bank capacity two ticks (=1 energy),
one capture adds one tick (=.5 energy). These remain fixed across all costs.
No new material, niche transport or structural capacity is generated.

The paid bill is one or two ticks: .5 or 1 energy, with .5 incorporated and
0 or .5 heat exported. Cost changes do not silently change bank capacity,
capture yield, formation energy, inventory or initial endowment.

Native precursor/construction conductance .001 or .012 changes BOTH thermal
construction and breakdown. This is **turnover**, not isolated damage hazard.
Paid unassisted construction .02, assisted .06, basal capture .004, assisted
capture .05. Work-source conductance .02 or .12; work per source event stays3.
The two-level 2x2x2 grid has eight physical regimes. Beta stays1. A source-off
anchor at the low bill/low turnover adds a ninth boundary regime.

Five mechanism arms in each regime:

1. **Catalytic ablation:** remove paired assisted capture and assisted construction
   channels; retain the same material, energy states, basal capture, paid and
   thermal construction/breakdown. Components have no catalytic effect here.
2. **Local cycles:** A.x assists A.y and vice versa; similarly B; designated x
   helps capture into its own bank.
3. **A to B:** local cycles plus A.x assisting B.y using B's bank.
4. **B to A:** the opposite one-way edge, computed independently.
5. **Reciprocal:** both cross edges.

Every added catalyst accelerates its reverse as well. Its usefulness is not
assumed. These are changed mechanism laws, not physically chosen policies.
The full four-arm cross-edge factorial allows an interaction contrast without
assuming additivity or inferring an unrun mirror arm by symmetry.

Roots have total energy2:

- assembled: all four constructed, both banks empty, carrier spent;
- unassembled: all precursor, both banks full, carrier spent.

Each is a known full present. The unassembled root can self-assemble under
the same law. It is not held disordered and is not described as microscopic gas.
Equal-energy preparation is not an enacted build-versus-wait process. The
carrier starts spent to avoid crediting an initial charged carrier to uptake
from the source. Sources are reversible work ports; finite net work may still
be negative. Match offered opportunity, not imposed equal absorbed work.

- Reciprocal minus local cycles and each one-way arm.
