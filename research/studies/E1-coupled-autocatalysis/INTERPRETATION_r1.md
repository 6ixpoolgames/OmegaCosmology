## What this map adds

We now compare coupled, costed autocatalytic organizations under a common
physical prescription within each regime. The machinery separates function,
construction ancestry, local projected history and whole-modeled-field history.
It can expose disagreements without inserting a protection or generativity reward.

At T4096 and cadence .25:

- Local catalytic cycles exceed catalytic ablation on whole history in all18
  comparisons (nine regimes, two preparations). Their local-history increases
  are also resolved for both organizations in all36 subject comparisons.
- The five cross-assistance contrasts increase whole history in all90 comparisons.
  Of180 local-subject comparisons,108 resolve positive and72 remain unresolved.
  None resolves opposite to the whole. This is not evidence that private and
  encompassing interests always align: donor effects often lie inside wide bounds.
- Assembled minus unassembled is positive in30 and negative in15 same-law
  comparisons. The finite gaps range from -10.51 to +258.20 bits.
- The cross-edge factorial interaction is positive in6 and negative in12 cases.
  Reciprocal assistance therefore need not add up linearly, even when each
  individual channel and their combination increase total history.

These are scoped physical comparisons, not choices made by an optimizer.

## Parameter relationships

Holding the other factors fixed, higher offered supply increases total history
in all40 T4096 comparisons, a higher bill decreases it in all40, and faster native
turnover increases it in all40. The bill changes from .5 to1 while bank capacity,
capture yield, formation energy and initial energy stay fixed. This avoids
mistaking an enlarged energy store for a change in construction cost.

Turnover accelerates both native construction and breakdown, so its positive
effect is not evidence that damage is good or that precaution failed. Much more
physical activity becomes possible under that faster native law. The same
turnover can reduce the advantage of an initially organized preparation.

The15 negative preparation comparisons belong to three regimes, across all five
mechanisms: low-cost/fast-turnover at each supplied strength, and the source-off
anchor. A costed catalytic mechanism can raise history breadth without every
organized initial preparation dominating unassembled material. Both preparations
can develop through the same native reactions; none is held disordered.

Only two levels per physical factor are tested. They demonstrate relationships
over these intervals, not a continuous frontier, a cost break-even or an exhaustive
set of favorable regimes. Fixed inventory also limits the feasible paid bill.

The recipient pays its construction bill. The donor has no separate per-catalysis
fuel bill or represented sequestration intermediate. Its functional loss arises
through the changed coupled ecology, including shared-resource competition;
this experiment has not isolated a unique causal decomposition of that loss.

This is a resolved physical tradeoff alongside a resolved whole-field ranking,
but not a resolved self/whole conflict, appropriation result or ethical verdict.
Small losses of stock/function do not by themselves constitute channel closure.

## Construction ancestry and persistence

In supplied local-cycle laws from the assembled root, the probability of having
reached connected depth>=3 by T4096 ranges from .84548 to .99997. A newly built
component must actually assist a later construction. Initial objects have depth
zero; basal/unlisted replacement resets depth; destruction ends the instance.
These are paid native construction channels, not a count of unrelated births.

But live depth>=3 descendants at the deadline range from about .0200 to .3520
in those same local-cycle cases. Ever reaching a chain is different from retaining
its descendants. Function, uninterrupted function, turnover and return are
separate profiles. The current report does not claim a joint proof of indefinitely
persistent functional depth-three compounding or an expanding population.

Within-group genealogy treats cross-group births conservatively as unlisted
births. It does not measure complete community ancestry, resource-packet origins,
or the loss of all substitutes. All laws remain irreducible and permit rebuilding.

## Where the positive breadth comes from

Both accepted work and event traffic differ across mechanism arms. At the same
low-supply example, local cycles versus catalytic ablation add2719.29 bits,
433.52 expected events and8.03 net work units by T4096. The no-catalyst arm keeps
all native capture and thermal dynamics. No such activity is subtracted afterward.

The source-off anchor also has positive catalytic history differences. Its
native equilibrium distribution is fixed by the same energy landscape across
mechanisms, while catalytic kinetics differ. Faster equilibration and continued
thermal event diversity can raise history breadth without a sustained net
productive current or a changed equilibrium function. That is counted physics,
not evidence that a single bath supplies net work indefinitely.

Therefore the mechanism result does not isolate productive recursive enabling
from all possible non-enabling activity at matched event counts or absorbed work.
It establishes what these catalytic pathways do to the actual weighted histories.
The same-law preparation comparisons supply the complementary organizational
question. A microscopic mobile gas comparator and non-enabling activity matching
remain different, still-open contrasts. We have not compared against the strongest
possible disordered arrangement or established ethical correspondence.

## Horizons and resolution

Same-law preparation limiting rates tie exactly. Their earlier accumulation
differences remain as offsets under the finite irreducible model; an equal
limiting rate does not erase their historical difference.

T4096 is long enough to reveal multiple finite-window relationships but not
uniformly to settle every transient. Maximum total variation to stationarity
is .002841 and maximum absolute rate/offset profile residual is2.314 bits.
The finite values are the reported quantities; slow regimes are not silently
replaced by stationary predictions. All45 primary preparation signs agree with
the computed limiting offset signs, but that does not certify other horizons.

There are44 comparison/cadence groups with horizon sign changes. In the two
audited physical corners there is one cadence-sensitive sign: the tiny factorial
cross-edge interaction at T256 in the high-supply/high-cost/fast-turnover regime
from the assembled root is +.000474 at .125, -.000109 at .25 and -.000816 at .5.
That interaction is resolution-dependent there. Other physical regimes were
not given the full cadence audit, and physical grain faithfulness remains open.

## What to carry forward

The reusable achievement is a verified instrument for costed coupled cycles and
their different scopes. Its first map shows conditional organization advantages,
paid construction ancestry, non-additive cross-effects and physical/local/whole
distinctions. It does not show that every helper helps, that more stock is always
better, or that more history entropy establishes value by definition.

The main remaining empirical question is sharper now: which interactions alter
each organization's continuation at the expense of the encompassing field, and
when? Resolving small donor effects needs tighter projected-history analysis.
Additional niches, saturation/sequestration, material loss and actual transfer
should be added for the questions that require them, with this cycle available
as a reusable reference rather than repeatedly rebuilding autocatalysis.
