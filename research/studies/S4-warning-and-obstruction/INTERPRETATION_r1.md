## Observed relationship

Adding the response raises whole modeled history in all48 TX-minus-T0
comparisons, including permanent obstruction. Independent response RX-minus-R0
also raises it at all48 points. No whole-history sign flips across the three
tested cadences. T0-minus-R0 and TX-minus-RX are positive atT16, negative at
T64/256/1024 in all environments/cadences:24 horizon-flip groups. Overlapping
horizons and control comparisons are not independent replications.

AtT1024/cadence.25, TX-minus-T0 gives +9.492281bits when permanently blocked,
+9.862240 for slow openings, +9.701432 for fast openings and +10.163679 when
permanently open. The blocked contrast lowers integrated working time by.6114
and endpoint bank energy by.0017; it cannot reach shelter. Its bank coordinate
contributes +9.221607bits in the frozen chain-rule order, versus a total
+9.492281. Blocked effort executes1.093965 forward and.837739 reverse events,
net dissipating.128113 energy. Parallel bank decrements aggregate before the
history calculation: this is physical reserve cycling, not action-label entropy.
Conditional coordinate attribution is explanatory, not unique causal isolation.

In the open environment the same response increases integrated sheltered time
by84.4411 and working time by16.2937 atT1024. Response therefore has a functional
access benefit there, but its entropy gain is not restricted to useful access.
Actual work and activity differ; no matched-activity attribution is claimed.

Body history bounds resolve an early TX-minus-T0 gain atT16 across all four
environments/cadences. Later body intervals overlap zero. Whole gains must not
be translated into resolved late private gains. Nothing is subtracted because
the extra activity is futile, and improved stock is not inserted as an objective.

## Sensor audit and subsequent version

The signal is paid and its write rates depend15-fold on exposed hazard. This
alone does not create a strong sustained warning. T0's stationary signal/
exposure MI is only about1.1e-5..2.2e-5bits. In the open/intermittent settings,
the nominal independent control has more stationary correlation through its
coupling to body reserve/location. Initial T64 integrated occupancy and switch
count matching remains valid; matching those moments is not matching temporal
correlations or useful information. Independent *write rates* do not ensure
independent occupancy.

This is a model limitation relevant irrespective of the entropy sign. The
sensor mostly changes relaxation kinetics and lacks a paid error-correcting
reset route. [S4b](../S4b-paid-warning/SPECIFICATION_r1.md) adds that missing
physical process and recalibrates the independent control before its new
rankings. These original laws, data, receipts and snapshots remain unchanged.
The response/no-response comparison is valid for the original law; it is not
deleted merely because it did not reproduce the historical futile-drive ordering.

## Chapter18 relevance

The model demonstrates one way that costly obstructed activity can increase
complete-history breadth while lowering reserve/function. That is a useful
adversarial mechanism and a limit on identifying functional proxies with
suffering. It does not support a universal claim that futile drive always
narrows, nor establish intrinsic valence or its absence. Warning value when
escape pays, genuine future-breadth tracking, and the meaning of suffering
need separate evidence. No ethical conclusion is hidden in the sensor audit.
