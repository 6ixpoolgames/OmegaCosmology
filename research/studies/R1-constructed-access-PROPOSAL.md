# R1 proposal: constructed access and costed reconstruction

Status: implemented and executed after the M0–M2 calibration, 24 October 2026.
The proposal text below records the original question. See the
[executed study](R1-constructed-access/README.md) for exact inputs, results and interpretation.

**Question.** In this finite stipulated world, what does a constructed access
interface change in the continuation, its resource flow and its own rebuilding?
The experiment permits either sign. It does not require the seed to defeat
every disordered root or mistake a long-run rate tie for a failure.

Use the existing [R1 model](../platform/models/R1.json), without adding a new
engine primitive. No optimizer is needed for this first physical test. Any
later controller is represented in the physical state before comparing choices.

## Comparison and endowment

Same-law preparation pair, coordinate order `(a.active,b.active,bond,carrier,bank)`:

- Built interface: `(1,1,1,1,2)`.
- Unassembled: `(0,0,0,1,3)`.

Both retain two constituents, one carrier, a four-tick store, and energy 4.5.
They differ in organization and allocation of that energy. The same source and
bath law applies; accepted work can differ and is reported. Optionally include
an average over the same-energy full-state reference set, weighted by intrinsic
degeneracy, with the root draw conditioned out. Call it a coarse modeled
reference, not microscopic gas. List all roots and weights before evaluation.

Baseline families in this slice:

1. Same driven law, matched unassembled preparation: transient profile/offset.
2. Source off: same preparations in their declared conserved sector.
3. Mechanism contrast: remove only the paired assisted-unlock channel; retain
   basal unlocking, native resource variation and paid reconstruction paths.

The third is a between-law comparison, not a choice from one common present.
No sensor is involved, so blind-register matching is inapplicable. Best tested
alternative means only a specifically declared feasible family, not a global
claim. No best-alternative search is proposed at this stage.

## Queries and causal account

Full conditional history at T=16,64,256,1024; cadences .125,.25,.5. These are
toy-resolution probes; physical faithfulness of this corridor remains open.
Report Delta ell for every baseline, with no minimum favorable margin. Finite
profiles are primary; stationary rate/offset are diagnostic where valid. Under
the same irreducible law the two preparation rates must coincide.

Alongside the profile report bound/active probabilities, cumulative functional
uptime, paid and basal activation in both directions, carrier conversion,
capture, bank occupancy, accepted/returned work, heat, energy residual and
free-energy availability. Separate repair flux from enduring function. An
increase in a scalar is not itself evidence of recursive reconstruction.
If a first-return or chain monitor is added, it observes paths without adding
its labels to the scored physical history.

Explain which channels changed, what they depend on, whether they reconstruct
the interface, and what remains without it. Keep negative contrasts and cadence
flips. No Rule A filter, survival reward or generativity bonus is introduced.

## Budget and decision after the slice

Use the frozen-snapshot supervisor with a 120-second/2-GiB cap per case and
32-MiB output budget. Exact worlds are small; store compact profiles/ledgers,
not dense kernels. Confirm the full requested set fits before proceeding.

The standard report states mechanism, lushness ranking, relationship, comparison
kind, horizon and what was challenged. The next module follows the diagnosed
physical bottleneck, potentially capacity, damage/recovery or another usable
interface. Storage expansion alone is not proof of three-step compounding.
