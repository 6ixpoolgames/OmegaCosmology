# Research roadmap — v4.1 starting reference

The next leg builds a reusable instrument around the reviewed
[architecture](platform/specs/ARCHITECTURE_VISION_SPEC_r3.md).

## Immediate engineering

[Task 002: M0–M2](tasks/002-next-generation-platform.md): six semantic records,
a small relational/count rule grammar, shared exact/sampled native events,
physical/symmetry checks and formal reference cases. M0 must express both R1
constructed access and R2 further enabling without bespoke engine primitives.

The instrument is not yet implemented. User authorization to prepare the repo
does not launch the engineering sprint or a scientific survey.

## Scientific progression

1. R0 physical/numerical calibration.
2. R1 constructed access, paid self-maintenance and matched baseline families.
3. R2 costed composition, further enabling, finite compounding and persistence.
4. R3 interdependence, appropriation and resolved local/whole rankings.
5. R4 contextual coupling/compression: structural equality, consequential
   response, generative-regime comparisons and ethical correspondence.
6. Boundary expansion: additional observed scopes versus explicitly changed
   environment physics, with correlations and endowment mappings retained.

Population, spatial transport, cross-scale composition, recovery/substitution
and bounded/ML control fit the architecture but are added when a physical
question requires them. They do not become scoring bonuses.

The available-work/access audit is an analysis capability and guide to the next
enabling module. It does not define lushness or guarantee that more layers win.

instruments. Their claim scopes are preserved in claims.json and their reports.

active starting plan; they are not silently rerun or discarded as evidence.
