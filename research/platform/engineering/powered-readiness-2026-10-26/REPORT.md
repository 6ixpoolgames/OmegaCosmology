## Controls identify limits and a real physical relation

remaining transients. Source removal is a mechanism contrast, not a matched
resource option. Powered tracking depends on a maintained gradient; no free

**Held open/closed:** full and empty banks and initially right/wrong cues are
explicit preparations, each with its actual energy. All carriers start spent.
From a wrong cue in a held-open world, by T4 the probability of ever becoming
correct is .424–.428, while current correctness is .389–.393. In a held-closed
world the corresponding values are .476–.479 and .455–.458. Thus the four-unit

First correction does not imply persistent accuracy. At T64 first correction
is near1, but current correctness is only about .68 open and .77 closed.
Each held sector has only one true class, so balanced accuracy/information
there do not independently establish dynamic tracking.

**Memory control:** with response/attempt pathways absent but clearing hardware

register therefore tends to retain suppression behind a closed barrier and

acquire .01818/.01298 expected marks. These are preparation/mechanism diagnostics
with paid clearing and different physical environments, not evidence of
neurological learning or a claim of perfect failure memory.
