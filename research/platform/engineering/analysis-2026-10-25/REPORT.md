## Remaining physical and analysis scope

The loaded-store dissolution restriction and non-maintaining upkeep remain in
the original fixture; this step diagnoses them rather than silently changing
its physics. A versioned repair is needed before using it to support stronger
self-maintenance claims. Native substitutions remain legitimate physics.

Ancestry is exact for declared native mechanism marks in the supported labelled
slot representation. It is not resource-packet provenance, necessity of a
catalyst, a general genealogy of exchangeable populations, or validation of
the physical meaning of construction. The engineering reference cycles have
no claim to being costed autocatalytic ecological experiments.

Long-horizon projected-history bounds, physical transport/niches, arbitrary
membership, multiple-parent attribution and whole-population identity remain
separate tasks. Functional queries support reducible generators, but the common
study assay's stationary/rate layer still requires irreducibility. No relationship
or ethical label is inferred automatically from a lushness sign.
