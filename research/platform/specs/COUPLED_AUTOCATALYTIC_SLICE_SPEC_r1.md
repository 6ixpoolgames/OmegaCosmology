## Costed autocatalytic blocks

or constructed. Each has formation energy .5 by default; bank quantum is 1,
capacity two quanta. Paid construction spends one quantum, retaining .5 in the
constructed state and dissipating .5. Bills/formation energy are rational
parameters and can be fractional. No new material appears.

Every member assists paid construction of its cyclic successor while remaining
present. Its paired reverse dismantles the child. Basal construction/breakdown
and unassisted paid construction stay present. Basal breakdown is possible at
empty and full banks and with other components constructed. Resource use funds
replacement after damage; there is no separately billed but ineffective upkeep.

Defaults: basal construction conductance .003; paid .02; assisted .06.
A group's designated access member assists capture into its own bank (.05),
with basal capture .004 retained. All groups compete for a single shared carrier:
spent/locked/usable with energies 0/3/2. A capture consumes usable carrier into
spent and puts one energy unit in the recipient's bank. Reverse release remains.
Native conversion and bath routes remain; the maintained reversible source is
the existing .08 conductance, work 3, spent/locked pair. One capture changes
the opportunity for every other group; the shared resource is not duplicated.

Additional declared assistance edges can connect groups using the same chemistry
and the recipient's bank. Parent, child, contact and cost are physical inputs.
Such an edge is not automatically classified as beneficial symbiosis.

This block is generic in number of groups, members and assistance edges. Fixed
