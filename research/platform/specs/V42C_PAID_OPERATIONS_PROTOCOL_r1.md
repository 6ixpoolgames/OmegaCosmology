1 November 2026. Draft for author/scientific review, not authorization to score

## Comparison contract

Use one enlarged spatial/chemical grammar and the same full modeled initial
state for all options. Add the same finite actuator in each option. An external
instruction controls its coupling during a finite preparation interval; all
native chemistry and movement continue. Command generation and controller memory
outside this actuator are unresolved boundary processes. The comparison is
conditional on the prescribed instruction, not on achieving an arrangement.

Its quantities are whole-modeled-field complete history laws conditional on the
common root and instruction. Do not call this a completely closed autonomous
universe, infer exterior cancellation, or add entropy for choosing an instruction.
The encompassing controlled physical law specifies state, instruction and their
response; the prescribed instruction traces differ across options.

## Common hardware and operation

Actuator displacement d=0/1 has energy .25d. A common reversible native thermal
displacement/relaxation rule has conductance .02 in every phase and option. It
does not disappear in the idle arm. Its initial state is d=0. Its constituent
count is conserved and equal across options; displacement is not an event label.

An eligible preparation stroke forms one nearest-neighbor bond between two
currently free constituents, displaces d:0->1 and spends two storage charges.
One charge is the construction reactant, one is an additional handling expense.
With bond energy .5, stroke deltaE=-1.25 and exported heat1.25, zero external work.
Native actuator relaxation releases .25 heat. The complete forward stroke and
relaxation therefore spends2 bank units, stores.5 in the bond and exports1.5 heat.
Its full inverse restores two bank charges and removes the bond/displacement;
keep this reverse and report refunds. The handling expense is not claimed to be
strictly irreversible on each path. Initial bank2 permits at most one initial
forward stroke without replenishment/refund, not one guaranteed successful build.

The stroke must preserve material, occupancy and incident-bond constraints. Use
explicit construction/operation roles and reaction ports. When its resulting
bond breaks natively, only one charge is returned. This produces a dissipative
operation cycle whose actual net cost must be measured; nominal bill2 alone is
not the net expenditure. Report ordinary bond reactions separately from strokes.

Candidate pulse duration16 and per-eligible-pair stroke conductance.1 are physical
engineering settings, not optimized policy values. After the pulse, the actuator
decouples from assembly and retains its common native thermal dynamics. Turning
off the stroke changes a reaction barrier, not stored-state energies; retain a
constant Hamiltonian and the same state alphabet. Barrier command generation
cost remains outside the modeled scope; the explicit handling charge does not
prove every cost of a real external controller has been accounted for.

## Candidate options

carrier, bank2, actuator relaxed. Initial energy2 and all inventory match.
No source, basal assembly, mobility, binding or capture route is deleted.

- Leave available: no actuator assembly coupling. The actuator still fluctuates

- Invest in A: couple the actuator stroke to eligible aa pairs during the pulse.
- Alternative assembly: couple the same stroke physics to eligible ab pairs.
  Mixed bonds compete for the same constituents, can move and dissociate, and
  may later become A/B. They are not forced to remain nonproductive.

alternative arrangement. The two have equal bills per executed stroke, not

about competing arrangements and access, not only geometric separation of
otherwise identical dimers. If that narrower separation claim is retained,

## Scientific limits and handoff

After a finite preparation pulse, a common finite irreducible recovery law gives
equal eventual entropy rates. This assay targets finite profiles and offsets.
It cannot deliver a permanent organization-over-disorder rate contrast by
renaming different preparations as different laws. Activity/work attribution
remains an additional diagnostic; same nominal bills are not a joint match.

This draft does not establish sustained maintenance under irreversible wear.
The physical extension determines what the reversible enabling loop actually
does. If the loop stabilizes through reverse capture without continued net
chemical investment, report that structure, not an unearned self-funding claim.
