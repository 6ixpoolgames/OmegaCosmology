## Quantities and numerical decisions

comparison 1e-8 absolute + 1e-9 relative; generator/state/physical ledger checks
1e-10 absolute + 1e-10 relative; independent propagation/first-law residual
1e-8. These are float64 consistency tolerances, not verified rounding intervals.
Projected histories retain analytic finite-memory bounds; their asymptotic
rate/offset is unsupported unless separately established. Do not fit a quotient
kernel or call unresolved bounds ties. Full modeled field excludes unresolved
external bath/source histories.

Expose full-state kernel rates and Poisson biases, their normalizations and
remainder bounds. Any general finite-kernel projector extension must be checked
on reducible and periodic references; the existing CTMC asymptotic API remains
unchanged. Compute profiles at original endpoints and a log-spaced prefix, and
where affordable through three times the conservative tail threshold. Hard
analysis cap: 65,536 sampled steps per case. A cap-limited tail is reported
unresolved, not guessed. Equal/near-equal rates do not generate a crossover by
division. A line crossing is an estimate; report all observed sign changes.

Report independent geometric endings on {0,1,...} with expected physical
durations 10,100,1000,10000 (converted to steps at each cadence), plus uniform
ending through the evaluated profile window. This intentionally uses common
physical duration rather than r2's cadence-dependent common number of steps.
All are E[log lushness], not expected perplexity or log lushness at E[N].
