## Purpose and comparison

Replace the S3 fault-preparation diagnostic with actual operations from one
intact sufficient present. Keep S3's old laws and results unchanged. Add a
reusable piecewise-native-law calculation, rather than treating a damaged root
as an enacted choice or restarting entropy at the operation boundary.

The base is the occupied R/Y repair motif. Every arm has the same two-state
actuator, bank, carrier, material slots, Hamiltonian and initial state. The
actuator stores 0.25 energy when displaced. It relaxes thermally in both
directions. During a one-unit pulse an externally prescribed barrier permits
one of these paired physical reactions:

- R damage: R(working) + relaxed actuator + bank tick -> R(precursor) +
  displaced actuator; one bank tick (0.5 energy) is consumed.
- Y damage: the same operation on Y.
- Sham: relaxed actuator + bank tick -> displaced actuator, without changing
  either component or its contacts.
- Idle: thermal actuator motion and ordinary ecology only.

A damage event dissolves any incident contact, with its binding energy included
in heat. Material remains in its precursor slot. Reverse operations reconstruct
the component/contact and refund the bank; none are deleted. Reaction rates
follow the unchanged local detailed-balance grammar. The operation can fail to
occur, reverse, recur or be followed by native repair. All outcomes count.

At pulse end, the same recovery law applies to every arm. No state, distribution,
query memory or entropy is reset. The schedule changes barriers, not energies;
there is no Hamiltonian quench. External barrier-setting hardware and its history
are unresolved: this is a controlled-boundary process contrast within the whole
**modeled** field, not a fully autonomous controller or a universal-field result.
There is no random prior over commands and no entropy for a clock label.

coordinates, energies, degeneracy, symmetry, constraints and material inventory.
Reject energy-changing switches: they need an explicit quench ledger. Sampled
phase boundaries must align with cadence. Compose the native kernels in order;
accumulate H(X_next | X_now, declared schedule), including the complete prefix.
Do not score entropy of the composite endpoint kernel as history entropy.

Projected histories retain hidden-state word operators spanning phase boundaries.
Report analytic lower/upper inequalities evaluated in float64, without assuming
the observed process is Markov. Do not sum independently restarted projection
bounds. Only a known full root is supported in this slice. No stationary rate is
assigned to a finite scheduled process. The common recovery law's rate can be
reported separately in later analysis.

Continuous occupation propagates through the same phases for native event
counts, gross accepted and returned work, heat, energy changes, bank bills and
function. Parallel event mechanisms are aggregated for represented state-jump
activity; their labels are not extra history branches. A passive event monitor
reports any forward operation and subsequent return to both components working.
It is never included in the physical state or score.

## Matching contract

The sham is a real paid actuator cycle, not an entropy counter. Two pulse-only
knobs are its paid-stroke and thermal-relaxation conductances. The recovery law
and source prescription remain common. Thermal motion stays in every arm.

Before history ranking, calibrate at T16 using only continuous expected native
state-changing jumps and gross accepted external work. Probe a fixed positive
conductance box [0.0001, 2] per knob, then use bounded numerical least squares
from the best moment-only probe, at most 24 evaluations per target. No outside
extrapolation, altered supply, altered endowment or score-dependent tuning.
Keep the targets, all probes, residuals, and replay of the returned parameters.
A match requires relative residual <=0.001 in each moment (scale max(1, target)).
Failure to find a match is “not matched within this bounded search”, not proof
that no physical match exists. A T16 match makes no assertion at other horizons,
about net work, flux correlations, timing or which physical events occur.
