# Version C: scientific core of the minimal spatial batch

1 November 2026. Prepared for S. Poole and Sol.

Status: scientific design for review, not an executable protocol. The author
authorized this first design step. No implementation, pilot, scientific run,
manuscript edit, commit or push is performed by this document. Numerical grids,
budgets and tolerances remain for the implementation specification after
feasibility assessment.

## 1. Intention and claim

Build one small physical world in which arrangements change the conditions for
further development. Organization and disorder must be developments of the same
constituents under the same physical rules. The world, its relationships and its
weighted continuations come first; a controller, if needed, is part of that world.

The target existence claim is:

> A costed arrangement can enable the construction and consequential use of a
> further structure. Within a physically specified opportunity window, this
> relationship can produce a positive complete-history lushness difference
> against a physically available alternative use of the same endowment. Its
> benefit depends on the opportunity and persistence of the enabling relationship;
> costs can outweigh that benefit in other specified conditions.

This is a claim to investigate, not a guaranteed outcome. It does not require
organization to win universally, at every horizon, or on asymptotic rate. It
does require physical enabling to be demonstrated separately from a favourable
entropy score. Mere coexistence of two structures is insufficient.

A successful batch is an existence demonstration of specified consequential
organization and its measured effects. It is not a proof that lushness captures
every difference relevant to prospective value, nor a proof of the central
ethical thesis. Independent ethical correspondence remains a separate question.

## 2. The measured object

For an admissible implemented option a from the common present x, retain the
complete resolved history law P_a, including execution of the option. At cadence
dt and horizon T = n dt:

    ell_a(T; dt) = H_a(X_1, ..., X_n | X_0 = x)
    Delta_ab(T; dt) = ell_a(T; dt) - ell_b(T; dt)
    L_a(T; dt) = 2 ** ell_a(T; dt)

X includes all represented physical coordinates, not just apparatus or useful
events. No entropy bonus for organization, penalty for disorder, subtraction of
thermal activity, or division by energy or activity enters the score. Parallel
reaction labels with indistinguishable physical outcomes are not new histories.

The profile is primary. Native event counts, physical flows, construction and
functional persistence explain it. They do not replace it. Changes in profile
increment are not automatically changes in stationary rate.

The scope is the whole modeled field. If reservoirs have accounted exchanges but
unresolved histories, their omitted contribution is a stated limitation. Equal
boundary prescriptions do not establish cancellation of changed correlations.

## 3. Common-present option contract: settle before engineering the assay

Every core comparison must identify:

1. The identical full initial physical state, including free material, stored
   energy, controller and preparation apparatus, and boundaries.
2. The physical operation available in each option and the machinery carrying
   it out. Declining construction is also a physical process, not a freeze.
3. What selects or implements each operation, what resources it uses, and
   whether its effects persist after the preparation phase.
4. The probability law being evaluated and the exact comparison semantics.
5. A common time origin before execution, without discarding unsuccessful
   construction paths or charging only the successful ones.

Reachability of both resulting arrangements is necessary but does not by itself
give two option laws from an identical complete present. An autonomous fully
specified stochastic law supplies one unconditional history distribution.
Conditioning that distribution on successful assembly or a later selector
outcome is not interchangeable with carrying out an option.

Sol must therefore return a concrete operation contract showing how the
controlled-law comparison is physically interpreted. A researcher switching
barrier schedules externally is not silently an embodied choice. If external
actuation remains necessary, account for its prescribed work and label the
comparison as controlled/interventional, with its unresolved physical scope.
If only different initial selector states are implemented, label that result
as a matched-preparation comparison. Neither receives the stronger core
common-present claim merely by using the same model identifier.

This is a scientific decision gate. If the present framework cannot support
the required semantics cleanly, return the gap for review instead of encoding
an arbitrary interpretation in software. No new prior over policy labels is
introduced to create extra breadth.

## 4. Minimum physical content

The feasibility design should seek the smallest world containing:

- Positions, empty space, native motion, encounters and reversible association.
  Binding may affect mobility through declared physics. The dispersed reference
  is allowed to encounter, assemble, capture and subsequently grow.
- A paid first structure A whose arrangement changes a physical pathway for
  constructing or activating a distinct structure B.
- A consequential use of B, such as accessing a further resource or maintaining
  an enabling component. Merely adding another stochastic coordinate is not
  evidence of the intended enabling chain.
- Competing uses of the same constituents, sites and resources. Their
  opportunity costs must arise within the dynamics.
- Explicit maintenance, degradation and reconstruction where claimed, with
  no free restoration of energy, configuration or control memory.
- Native stochastic dynamics in every arm, including after loss of the
  constructed structures.

All configurations are subject to the same motif rules wherever they occur.
No permanent organization tag grants a capability to one arm. A constructed
interface earns its function through its physical configuration and couplings.

This can establish bounded further enabling. It does not by itself establish
unbounded growth, three genealogical generations, a self-sustaining ecology,
dilute-gas physics, life or agency. A small spatial lattice is a spatial
disordered reference at its stated density, not automatically a thermal gas.

## 5. Three core comparisons

### C1. Invest versus leave available

From the common present, compare an operation investing in A with an alternative
that leaves those resources available for native motion, encounters and other
uses. Begin before construction; spontaneous assembly is permitted in both.

Question: does the enabling chain actually function, and where does its complete
profile exceed or trail the alternative? Record preparation expense, subsequent
use of A and B, degradation, recovery, and the reference's own assembly.

Without C1 there is no direct comparison with native disordered development or
the full cost of obtaining the arrangement.

### C2. Productive connection versus alternative arrangement

Use the same constituent grammar to compare operations producing different
physical arrangements or connections. Aim for comparable construction
commitments, but report actual differences rather than asserting equality.

The alternative must have its native physical consequences. Do not forbid it
from becoming useful or delete unrelated reactions to make it nonproductive.
If it rearranges into the productive configuration, that development counts.
An ablated law can supplement the analysis, explicitly as a mechanism contrast,
but cannot replace the same-law arrangement comparison.

Question: how do the specific enabling connections change subsequent access,
use, work, activity and lushness? Demonstrate the A-to-B dependence from native
transition rules and physical observations; a history ancestry witness alone
does not establish the entire causal claim.

Without C2, a favourable C1 result might be attributed only to preparation,
extra components or the operation apparatus. C2 narrows those alternatives;
it does not eliminate all confounding by declaration.

### C3. The investment under reduced opportunity

Repeat the investment comparison in a physically described regime with less
usable opportunity: for example a shorter-lived source or reduced accessible
supply. Choose the dimension for a physical reason, before inspecting scores.

Both options share their present and boundary prescription within each case.
Cases across this small map need not share the same present. Parameter changes
are a family of laws unless the varied quantity is an actual state of one law.

Question: where do construction and upkeep cease to be repaid functionally,
and what does the lushness profile then do? Functional repayment and lushness
repayment are separate readouts; neither mathematically entails the other.
Retain cases where an unproductive arrangement still adds history breadth.

Without C3, the batch shows one advantage but not its dependence on opportunity.
A negative comparison would demonstrate a boundary, not prove an ethical harm.
Failure to find a boundary in the explored range leaves this part open; it does
not justify repeated tuning until a negative score appears.

Together C1-C3 can support the bounded existence claim in section 1. They are
not sufficient for a claim of universal organization-over-disorder dominance,
activity-independent advantage, or ethical sufficiency.

## 6. Rate, persistence and opportunity

For a finite autonomous law where both developments reach the same unique
recurrent class, their asymptotic history rates agree. Initial organization can
nevertheless produce a long sustained transient advantage and a lasting offset.
Adding a finite physical controller does not automatically evade this result.

Distinguish three reports:

- Complete finite profiles and their increments across the stated window.
- A sustained finite-window excess, with its duration and physical mechanism.
- A genuinely asymptotic rate difference, with the recurrent-class or maintained
  control conditions that allow it and the physical cost of those conditions.

Do not introduce artificial absorbing partitions to secure a rate difference.
Metastable persistence is useful evidence but is not infinite persistence.
No assertion of all-horizon or eventual dominance follows solely from a dense
finite grid. Boundaries after organizational failure remain in the whole-field
continuation; organizational survival time is not automatically its cutoff.

Independent ending laws can summarize the profile when physically appropriate.
State-dependent disruption must be modeled jointly; do not substitute entropy
at mean survival time or assume independence because it is computationally easy.
Opportunity-window tests must distinguish an observation cutoff from physical
source termination followed by continued evolution.

## 7. Activity and work: attribution without changing the objective

Same starting endowment is not equal realized work. Equal stationary event
rates are not equal finite event counts, waiting-time laws or branching
destinations. Equal mean work and activity still leave many structural
differences. Reports must identify precisely which quantities match.

For C1-C3 retain actual accepted work, returned work, net work, heat and event
activity alongside the profiles. Keep boundary flux and internal expenditures
distinct to avoid counting recycled energy as fresh input. A simple ratio of
capture to upkeep is not by itself evidence of self-funding or net production.

A dedicated paid activity/work comparator is a conditional extension, not a
prerequisite for scoring physically valid C1-C3. Its qualification requirements
apply to the extra attribution claim. If it cannot match, ordinary comparisons
remain reportable with that limitation. Do not select paths retrospectively,
rescale time to erase costs, or subtract switching entropy.

Physical-only calibration is permissible and must be visible. Prospective
calibration is not intrinsically invalid; lack of matching by construction is
not a reason to conceal the mismatch. J and J2 establish only that their bounded
searches found no qualifying joint control, not that matching is impossible.

More activity may be a genuine consequence of enabling access. The question is
what arrangement produces it and what else changes, not whether organization
can be credited only after all its consequences have been held fixed.

## 8. Evidence and decision discipline

Before scientific scoring, record the operations, physical law, model versions,
conditioning, resolutions, horizons, physical qualification checks, numerical
tolerances and the small physically motivated case set. Keep mechanism
requirements separate from guesses about winners or crossover locations.

Physical-only qualification can establish conservation, payments and functioning
links. A physically valid negative comparator remains eligible for scoring even
when it lacks productive function. Inability to instantiate the intended chain
is reported before treating its score as evidence about that chain.

Report all evaluated cases and all observed crossings. A positive existence
result requires a numerically resolved advantage in a stated regime together
with a functioning physical mechanism; it need not be positive at every horizon.
Record the entire explored set so a favourable point is not presented as typical.
Any exploratory score-informed redesign becomes a new, explicitly exploratory
design; it cannot retain the status of an untouched advance prediction.

Numerical precision, resolution sensitivity, physical adequacy and relevance
to the claim remain separate evidence axes. Audit temporal sampling and one
meaningful state-resolution issue where feasible. Labelled versus symmetry-
reduced calculations are useful invariance checks, not by themselves two
independent physical grains. Do not award faithfulness for trying more cadences.

No missed threshold is automatically a failure of the principle. Equally, do
not retrospectively rename a functioning but unfavourable system nongenerative.
The outcome can be a demonstration, a limited or mixed profile, an unresolved
comparison, or a physical design limitation. Each receives its actual evidence.

## 9. Handoff to Sol: bounded feasibility questions

The next engineering assessment should return, without selecting on lushness:

1. One preferred minimal spatial grammar and, only if necessary, a smaller
   fallback. Show precisely what scientific content the fallback loses.
2. A concrete common-present operation proposal under section 3, identifying
   any external intervention or unmodeled selection machinery.
3. State-count, memory and time estimates or bounded enumeration measurements;
   symmetry assumptions and a small labelled-reference validation route.
4. The native paths establishing A-to-B enabling and B's consequential use,
   plus available alternative uses of the same material.
5. Conservation and energy-accounting obligations, source/bath scope, and the
   physical measurements available without computing history entropy.
6. Which existing compiler, validator, profile and evidence components can be
   reused; the smallest necessary adapter extensions and unresolved contracts.
7. A recommendation: feasible as stated, feasible with explicit scope reduction,
   or not yet feasible. Sampling trajectories alone is not evidence that
   complete-history entropy can be estimated to useful accuracy.

The feasibility return precedes an executable protocol with resource caps and
scientific scoring permissions. No exact state count, operating point, numerical
tolerance or runtime is invented in this scientific core.

## 10. Scope, carry-over and source basis

Retain the existing audited event accounting, conservation checks, comparison
validation, exact profile mathematics and reproducibility machinery where their
contracts fit. Fresh physical comparisons do not require rewriting entropy.
Prior studies remain evidence within their stated scope and can serve as
numerical reference cases. This batch does not retroactively withdraw them or
turn their mechanism contrasts into choices from a common present.

Defer social populations, suffering, justice, a broad plurality campaign and
exact equal-value compression pairs. Part-whole experiments can follow if the
physical platform supports them, not because every chapter needs a new assay.
