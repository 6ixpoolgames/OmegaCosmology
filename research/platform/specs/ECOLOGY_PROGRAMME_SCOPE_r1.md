# Interacting autocatalytic ecologies: programme scope and implementation handoff

25 October 2026. Astra scoping pass requested by the author following Claude Code's
Evidence requests r1. This is a proposed delivery sequence, not an instruction
to execute all studies. It preserves the current architecture and completed
evidence. No scientific runs, manuscript edits or publication accompany it.

## 1. Intention and scientific object

Build one extensible physical ecology in which organizations obtain resources,
construct and repair themselves, occupy one another's productive machinery,
transfer material, and exploit different interacting niches. Competition,
parasitism, symbiosis and plurality should emerge as descriptions of measured
relations. They must not be reaction types with benefits or harms assigned by
name. Later controllers must be constructed and maintained within this ecology.

The central question is how these relations deform the weighted continuation
from a sufficient present: for the whole modeled field, and for specified parts
under the same conditioning. The platform computes these quantities and their
physical causes. Correspondence with ethical judgments is assessed separately.

Do not build a separate simulator for each manuscript chapter. Each new physical
module should answer a missing causal question and compose with earlier modules.
Keep the finite exact examples as reference instruments as population and spatial
models become too large for exhaustive calculation.

This scope extends the [architecture](ARCHITECTURE_VISION_SPEC_r3.md),
[analysis contract](ECOLOGY_ANALYSIS_SPEC_r1.md) and
[coupled physical slice](COUPLED_AUTOCATALYTIC_SLICE_SPEC_r1.md).
The imported formal reference remains Appendix A r19. Claude's request uses
outline r31 and later editorial conventions; their numbering is supplied
metadata, not a claim that those newer source documents were checked here.

## 3. Clarifications to the manuscript request

1. **Reachability versus weighted access.** In a finite irreducible CTMC, both
   preparations may reach the same states with positive probability within any
   positive window. A preparation advantage concerns probabilities, passage
   times and weighted histories, not necessarily exclusive support. Measure
   strict support differences only when the dynamics actually provide them.
2. **Mechanism contrasts remain legitimate.** Removing an enabling coupling
   changes the law. Retain unaffected states, routes and boundary prescriptions,
   specify the changed rates, and call it a mechanism contrast. It does not
   become an implemented choice from one present without a physical procedure.
3. **Evidence quality has several axes.** Numerical confidence, resolution
   audit, physical adequacy and claim relevance are separate. Two cadences do
   not establish physical faithfulness. Emit the manuscript's M1–M4 mapping
   only with an explicit mapping version and its qualifications.
4. **Reproducibility does not require guessing a winner.** Fix the quantity,
   physical comparison and execution plan; record predictions when useful.
   Distinguish prospective predictions, exploratory findings and later
   interpretation. A missed repayment guess is not a failed principle.
5. **Counterexamples require stated claims.** 'As often as' requires a measure
   over cases. Failure to find an existence regime in a finite search does not
   disprove its existence. Conversely, do not dismiss an adequately modeled
   contrary result merely because additional mechanisms could change it.
6. **Persistence and repayment need distributions.** Mean lifetime exceeding
   an asymptotic crossover is not a general payoff theorem. Evaluate the actual
   continuation under the specified disruption and recovery process.
7. **Historical evidence has a fourth status:** retained historical evidence,
   not yet replicated on this platform. Exact reproduction, conceptual
   replication, corrected interpretation and withdrawal are distinct records.
   Migration alone is not grounds for withdrawal.

An interior optimum between stasis and rapid variation is a scientific
hypothesis, not an engine acceptance test. Changing a kinetic parameter is a
family of laws unless that parameter has a physical implementation within one
law. Fixed inventory cannot demonstrate indefinite repertoire expansion.

## 6. S1: reusable analysis, in priority order

### S1a. Confidence for projected histories

Keep the analytic memory bounds and full-history filtering estimator as distinct
methods for the same quantity. Keep coordinate chain-rule shares as a third,
explanatory quantity. None may silently substitute for another.

First investigate the unassembled E1 donor contrasts at T16, T64 and T256,
cadence .25. Benchmark promising methods at the hardest requested horizon
before scaling. Candidate methods are variance-sensitive finite-sample bounds
for bounded independent path scores, and selective observation-tree expansion
with a rigorous bound on the unexpanded probability mass. Preserve exact
filtering; clipping, pruning or posterior compression needs an explicit error
bound. Repeated looks require a confidence sequence or allocated error budget.

Any certificate must separately identify statistical coverage, numerical error
and simultaneous coverage across requested comparisons. A concentration theorem
around float64 path scores does not alone certify the exact physical entropy.
No guarantee of a cheap certificate is made. Report feasibility before adding
samples; the early T16 effect is especially small. Add a second cadence after
method validation, reporting it as an audit, not proof of a finest grain.

### S1b. Common structural and continuation report

Add first-passage to declared functional sets, usable stock and gradients,
accepted work, dissipated heat, event counts, and occupied/free resource time
to the existing persistence/recovery/ancestry outputs. Availability of a route,
flux through it and its causal contribution remain different quantities.

Foreclosure is a history-support query under a declared observation and common
alphabet. A missing endpoint or lower stock alone does not establish it. For
finite sampled processes, build a support-language comparison/reference case;
for hidden projections, retain an automaton or label the check unsupported.
Specify whether initial symbols are included when comparing different roots.
Positive-probability native reverse paths are not dropped by a probability
threshold. Very unlikely recovery is reported separately from impossibility.

### S1c. Horizons and source windows

At cadence delta, whole history from root distribution mu obeys
`L(n) = sum[k=0..n-1] mu P^k h(P)`, with initial-root entropy excluded when the
query is an average over preparations. Validate finite profiles independently
of stationary calculations. Locate sign changes on this physical-time grid;
an off-grid endpoint requires a declared last sampling interval. Do not claim
a unique crossing from the asymptotic estimate `-Delta offset / Delta rate`.
Multiple crossings and no crossing are legitimate.

For an externally specified independent deadline distribution, distinguish
expected conditional profile `E[L(N)]` from entropy of the stopped history
including the random stopping length. If termination depends on the state,
model that coupling: independent averaging is invalid. Turning a source off
changes subsequent dynamics; it does not erase the subsequent thermal history.
Run a piecewise law schedule or a physical source state with its own ledger.

### S1d. Activity and throughput controls

Report expected state-changing jump count and fine-cadence coefficients beside
history profiles. Event marks that lead to the same represented state are
physical-accounting diagnostics, not automatically distinct history outcomes.
Expected jump-chain surprisal is not by itself the entropy of the random-length
jump sequence. Keep event count, sequence and timing quantities separately typed.

Match accepted work or expected activity through a physically declared control
parameter and interpolation within its feasible range. Report residual mismatch
and interpolation error; no extrapolated 'match'. Matching work and activity
usually needs two degrees of freedom and may be physically impossible. A match
at one horizon/root need not match the entire profile or its stationary limit.
Show unmapped raw contrasts as well. Neither matched statistic proves all
background correlations cancel.

## 7. S2: finite catalytic occupancy, the next physical slice

The current E1 helper accelerates construction while remaining available for
every other use. Add an explicit reversible complex so assistance can occupy
scarce machinery. Use the existing two-group, two-member cycles first.

An illustrative module is `H + P <-> HP` followed by a reversible paid conversion
`HP + fuel <-> H + C + waste`. Here H is the helper, P/C are the recipient's
precursor/product states, and HP is the bound complex. These are semantic roles
mapped onto conserved material slots and bank/carrier coordinates, not extra
material introduced by labels. If C later becomes a helper, its construction
and maintenance remain the same physical events.

Required contracts:

- A helper has a finite port capacity. The occupied port cannot concurrently
  service another substrate or perform a competing capture/local-construction
  task requiring that port. Native unbinding always exists.
- Binding energy, degeneracy, source work, bills and heat close for every
  directed reaction. Source-off detailed balance is checked over the entire
  world. A catalyst changes paired kinetics without silently changing affinity.
- Maintain independent basal construction, breakdown and capture where they
  belong in the model. No hidden 'organization required' restriction.
- Specify who pays each bill. Recipient-paid conversion and helper occupancy
  suffice for the first slice. Donor-paid assistance is a later parameterized
  physical channel, not an assumed altruistic expense.
- Bound assemblies can be damaged or dismantled when the stipulated material
  chemistry permits it; the products and stored loads have explicit destinations.
  No immunity because the bank lacks room for a refund.
- Bank quantum, bank capacity, construction bill, binding energy and catalytic
  conductance vary independently where the finite representation permits.
  Accept rational fractions; changing the quantum is a resolution/model change.
- The same motif/rule fires wherever the physical prerequisites occur. No
  group-name exceptions, off-ledger grants or invisible exclusive ownership.

Use a constrained discrete complex coordinate plus derived occupancy if that
is sufficient; do not add independent occupancy flags that admit inconsistent
states. The full state must remain sufficient for the law. Chemical binding
retains physical identity only to the extent resolved by the adapter.

Minimum engineering cases: a single reversible occupied port; two competitors
for that port; two cycles with local occupied catalysis; one cross-assistance
edge; reciprocal edges. Include a source-off reference. Confirm competition
by checking the native event set/rates while occupied, not by requiring a
particular lushness sign.

Ancestry remains query-side. Attribute assisted construction to the helper
actually represented in the native complex. Define turnover/death of that
instance explicitly. Ambiguous multiple parents require a new query contract;
do not assign every child to the most favorable ancestor. Occupancy is physical
and counts; genealogy labels do not create extra entropy.

Completion: conservation/detailed balance, saturation and release, competing
use, repair/damage accessibility, physical-marginal preservation of monitors,
and unchanged results for untouched old adapters. Pass a capped smoke and
report state count, operator memory and marginal-query cost before S3.

## 8. S3: a small physical interaction map

Use the same S2 substrate for a small number of informative contrasts:

1. Local assistance versus cross assistance with actual competing helper use.
2. One-way versus reciprocal assistance under the same resource endowment.
3. Productive conversion versus a reversible binding/release control whose
   coupling does not complete the conversion; retain the baseline thermal routes.
4. Maintained source versus a specified finite source window, retaining the
   post-shutdown continuation.

Map source supply, occupied residence time and the relative demand for the
helper's local versus cross use. Fix a small anchor map from physical feasibility
and smoke cost, then explore neighborhoods or boundaries transparently. A large
Cartesian grid is not required. Don't remove unfavorable functional regimes.

Report effects on each part's history, function, reconstruction, resource access
and work, as well as whole history. Classify a relation per metric and horizon:
one-way dependence can be recipient-positive/donor-negative in one readout and
not another. A cross edge is not automatically cooperation. Shared-source
competition without appropriation is not domination.

The non-enabling activity control belongs here. Test work and activity matching
as separate contrasts first. If a jointly matched comparator exists, add it;
otherwise report the physical feasibility limitation instead of inventing a
random-event channel to hit the target.

A stasis/variation survey can reuse these mechanisms once its knob is physically
specified. 'Fully random' needs a defined endpoint. A finite saturated ecology
may favor continued variation; this does not test indefinite expansion.

## 9. S4–S5: expansion seams that must remain open

### Niches, population and cross-scale composition

Niches require local inventories and resource conditions, finite transport
interfaces and reversible costed transfer. A graph edge gives a possible route;
moving material along it must be a native event with travel/handling costs and
capacity. Colonization requires transported founders, construction and a local
enabling loop. Merely relabeling a group's niche is not colonization.

Compare duplicate and differentiated organizations at matched total material,
stored work and boundary prescription. Report that division changes interfaces
and transport overhead where physically true. Study substitution, complementary
resource use and recovery after selective loss before claiming plurality pays.

For exchangeable populations, require a two-copy labeled reference and a
validated symmetry quotient. Permutation-only changes add no history entropy.
Counts alone are insufficient when contact pattern or lineage changes rates.
Do not multiply population into lushness as a correction factor.

Cross-scale assemblies expose interfaces through their paid constituent bonds.
Treat an assembly as a coarse effective unit only with an observation/memory
contract and a validated approximation; don't assume its projected law is
Markov. Preserve a microscopic small-instance reference as the size increases.

### Physical controllers and bounded agents

First introduce a small class of actual embodied controller mechanisms: records,
sensor transitions, actuation and upkeep, with specified null behavior. An
external oracle may rank these under full model information; its computation
is an evaluation tool, not free physical control inside the world.

Specify whether different controllers are preparations, different mechanisms,
or enacted installations/commands from one common present. Compare the same
candidate family by whole and private histories. A private history is not a
chain-rule share. Policy selection uncertainty must survive winner selection;
overlapping estimates yield a candidate optimum set, not an invented winner.

Appropriation then means physically funded transfer or occupied access with
alternative productive use. Self-defense and punishment/incentive add native
channels and costs. After this oracle layer, add uncertain observations,
inspection, correction and receding-horizon decisions deliberately. Leave an
adapter seam for learned policies; no ML dependency belongs in S0–S3.

## 10. Implementation boundaries and resource gates for Sol

Retain `semantics.py`'s six public records and stable quantity identifiers.
Extend `physical_modules.py` through composable module builders returning native
coordinates, rules, constraints and ledger terms. Studies assemble these
modules; they must not override entropy routines. Queries consume the compiled
event model and observation maps. Reports consume typed evidence records.

Add optional fields with explicit versions and legacy defaults. Preserve old
law fingerprints and source snapshots; new occupancy physics is a new adapter
version. A generic capability registry should describe supported conditioning,
observations, error guarantees and costs, rather than claim every backend
supports every query. Existing runners and compact evidence retention remain.

Measured anchors: E1's 65 cases took about368 seconds, under200MiB/job. Exact
memory3 refinement took276.5 seconds for two laws and about518MiB peak while
still not resolving the donor. The fixed filtering follow-up took123 seconds,
about160MiB peak. Occupied complexes may increase state space substantially;
these are measurements of old workloads, not forecasts for S2.

Proposed initial gates, adjustable from measured engineering cost:

| Work | Initial budget | Escalation/stop rule |
|---|---|---|
| S0 | Retained files only | No scientific rerun needed |
| S1 method feasibility | 10 min/case, 4GiB/job, 64MiB output; 30 min total | Report achievable interval width and scaling; no sign-driven repeated sampling |
| S2 smoke | 5 min/case, 4GiB/job, 32MiB output; 30 min total | First largest case single-worker; reject caps explicitly |
| S3 science proposal | At most2 hours, 8GiB aggregate worker commitment, 256MiB compact output | Set actual grid only after smoke; separate exact whole and expensive local queries |

Before execution, inspect free RAM and competing jobs. Leave the larger of4GiB
or25% of available physical RAM unused; reduce workers to fit that reserve.
A per-process cap is not an aggregate memory limit. Initially use at most two
workers, increasing only from measured peak commitments and the user's compute
allowance. Check disk space for source snapshots, temporary kernels and results.
No silent reduction in inventory, observation resolution or physical channels
to satisfy a cap. If needed, redesign the algorithm or explicitly propose a
different scientific model. HDD output does not reduce dense operator RAM.

Retain source/configuration hashes, compact tables, exact requested summaries,
query provenance and reconstruction instructions. Dense kernels and paths are
rebuildable caches, evicted only under the existing manifest/pinning policy;
unique evidence and source snapshots are retained. Do not delete legacy records
as part of this scope. Byte-preserving Git and evidence hash checks apply.
