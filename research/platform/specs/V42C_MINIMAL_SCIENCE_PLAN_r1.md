1 November2026. Proposal accompanying the implemented paid-operation assay.
The author approved proceeding after Astra review on 1 November. Final
readiness refers to the accompanying engineering report and its actual caps.

## Claim and comparator

Investigate whether costed local assembly changes subsequent weighted spatial
and chemical histories, whether its particular connections matter, and how the
balance depends on opportunity duration. Seek existence demonstrations within
the specified coarse world, not universal advantage or an ethical proof.

All options start with a on0,3 and b on1,4, no bonds, spent carrier, bank2,
actuator relaxed, initial energy2 and identical inventories. Native chemistry,
motion, spontaneous construction, reverses and shared actuator thermal motion
remain present. Conditional exterior instruction lasts16: idle, aa assembly,
or competing ab assembly. Instructions activate reaction barriers according to
one controlled response model; their generation outside the modeled field is
not represented or assigned random selection entropy. Full actuator execution, refunds and the entire preparation prefix are included.
Histories without construction remain included. Separate costly unsuccessful
attempts are not represented: an eligible stroke forms a bond.

Per stroke: form bond, spend2 charge, displace actuator. Native dissociation
restores1charge; inverse stroke restores2. These are actual counted physical
channels, not nominal charges deducted from scores. Total realized spending,
stroke multiplicities and activity can differ between options.

The same law after the pulse allows spontaneous loss or reconstruction. This
is a controlled comparison from a common modeled present, with an exterior
command boundary, not a fully closed autonomous universe. Baths and source
histories are also unresolved. No universal exterior cancellation is claimed.

This gives six physical schedules, each observed atcadences.125,.25,.5:

aa minus idle (C1) and aa minus ab (C2). Compare how these two contrasts change

boundary prescriptions, not a single autonomous root.

C2 tests productive versus competing arrangements. It is not a controlled
comparison of separated versus contacting copies of the same dimers. The

dissociate and become aa/bb, without forced nonproductivity.

No intermediate source lifetime, larger geometry, catalytic-strength sweep,
irreversible damage or extra policies are core. A source lifetime256 would be
a ranked strengthening case requiring separate approval, not automatic follow-up.

## Observation window and quantity

Compute complete full-state sampled-history entropy in bits from the common
root, for every prefix through physicalT4096, including0 and phase boundary16.
Keep scalar scores at every prefix; retain state distributions only at the
diagnostic horizons. Headline horizons16,64,256,1024,4096 are summaries of those full profiles, never
the only observations. Keep every observed comparison crossing with its cadence
bracket. No crossing on tested prefixes is not an all-horizon certificate.

At a phase boundary retain the propagated distribution and accumulated history
entropy. Do not replace prefix history by endpoint entropy, restart from a
prepared root, remove actuation, or condition on successful assembly. Independent
ending-law summaries are optional later analyses, not core score changes.

The full modeled state includes absolute site occupancy, bonds, carrier, storage
and actuator. No arbitrary individual serial labels, command labels or reaction
names add histories. Marked physical channels are retained for accounts but
aggregate by common physical target in the generator used for entropy.

Cadences audit temporal sensitivity of this fixed finite physical model. They
do not establish its microscopic faithfulness. Tiny labelled/unmarked references
test relabelling invariance, not a second independent physically faithful grain.
No state projection is promoted to a full-field ranking, and no hidden projection
is approximated as Markov. State-resolution adequacy remains a stated open axis.

## Physical readouts

For each instruction/source schedule retain actual forward/reverse strokes,
thermal actuator motion, A/B/mixed-bond assembly and loss, assisted versus basal
B construction, enabled versus basal capture, bank and bond counts, contact
and simultaneous A/B occupancy, source gross/returned/net work, heat and activity.
Report at the headline horizons and preparation boundary, unconditionally.

Use charge accounting C=bank+bonds with deltaC=net capture-net operation strokes.
It is distinct from physical energy, since one bond stores.5 energy. Shared
bank access is rapid mixing, not resolved fuel transport. No independent wear
drain exists: findings cannot establish maintenance under irreversible damage.
Do not equate forward capture to net chemical gain or to pathwise financing.

## Predictions and interpretation

Physical commitments tested before scoring: native aa can assist local bb,
bb can change capture, operations pay and refund according to their channels,
and the alternative uses the same material. These can be audited without an
entropy prediction. A functional but unfavourable result remains functional.

Investigative expectations, not consequences of the mathematical definition:
investment may buy a finite lushness advantage after its expense; its comparison
can differ from ab assembly and change with source opportunity. No exact winning
horizon, sign under short supply, number of crossings or repayment threshold
is assumed. Earlier physical replenishment does not prove any of these signs.

A positive existence demonstration requires a numerically resolved advantage
over a declared horizon interval and physically functioning enabling links.
Report the complete explored set, early losses, later reversals and temporal
sign disagreements. A physically qualified negative result is retained as a
model result, not automatically a central-theory refutation or a design error.
Conversely, do not reinterpret a missed expectation as an advance prediction.

Because recovery is common and finite with one recurrent class, expect equal
eventual entropy rates after finite preparation. The target is a finite profile
and potentially a lasting offset. Do not infer different asymptotic rates from
long transient slopes. No numeric limiting offset or eventual/all-horizon
dominance claim is required for the core; it needs a separate calculation/bound.

## Numerical gates and resource plan

Required: complete common-state enumeration, literal common root/Hamiltonian/
inventory/resolution, per-route material/energy audit, source-off Gibbs checks,
finite first-law and charge residuals1e-8 absolute, kernel row residual1e-10,
and agreement between sampled versus continuous phase endpoints1e-10. Numerical
probability negativity beyond1e-12 fails; structural reachability never uses an
arbitrary probability cutoff. Missing or failed physical gates are reported,
not interpreted as a lushness ranking.

Proposed ranking tolerance for review: epsilon_ell(T)=1e-7+1e-9*(T/dt) bits.
This is a conservative floating-point decision allowance, not a rigorous
rounding certificate. Before scoring, justify it with synthetic exhaustive and
split-schedule identities at comparable step counts; if those residuals exceed
the allowance, repair or enlarge it based on numerical tests before scientific
results. Comparisons inside the allowance are numerically unresolved, not exact
equal-lushness examples. An all-horizon tie requires proof, not this tolerance.

Run one4GiB job at a time with the existing4GiB/25percent host reserve. Proposed
maximum3600seconds per schedule/cadence and64MiB output, at most18jobs. Store no
dense kernels or raw trajectories in the evidence/repository. Report projected
total cost from benchmark timings before approval; do not silently launch the
18hour worst-case cap. Reuse common recovery calculations where validated and
resource-accounted; any batching/phase cache must preserve state order, prefixes
and the same results as independent tiny-reference schedules.

Source-off uses native reactions with only the registered source channel removed.
Pulse and shutoff boundaries align exactly with all three cadences. Changing
barriers without changing Hamiltonian introduces no hidden quench; external
controller-generation costs are still outside the physical scope.

## Attribution and reporting

The core does not match realized work and activity simultaneously. Equal initial
resources or stroke bills do not do so. Report the mismatches and the physical
pathways instead of normalizing or subtracting activity. A dedicated paid
work/activity comparator is a separately qualified strengthening study.

Deliver full input/model hashes, executed-source snapshots, literal schedules,
numerical/physical gate results, full profiles and comparisons, observed
crossings, physical readouts, and one study card per comparison family. Evidence
axes: numerical confidence, resolution coverage, physical adequacy and claim
relevance. No result is promoted to ethical correspondence by its sign alone.
