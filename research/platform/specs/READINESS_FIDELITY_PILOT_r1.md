# Readiness fidelity and long-horizon feasibility pilot

26 October 2026. The user's next Proceed authorizes this bounded follow-up to
the readiness smoke. No native law is changed. This is not the wider scientific
cost/supply/opening map or publication.

Evaluate T64 and T256 at cadence .25 for all four half-energy response circuits
and one fully intermittent quarter-energy re-engage case. The quarter case goes
first, with its full state retained. Different quanta/costs are engineering
cases, not a scientific cost contrast. All physical history remains counted.

Measure cue/barrier joint probabilities at the endpoint, in the stationary
distribution, and averaged over continuous physical occupancy. Report mutual
information, sensitivity, specificity, positive predictive value and base-rate
accuracy. These are association diagnostics: high accuracy alone can come from
always declaring the predominantly closed barrier. Time-pooled information can
also arise from shared transients. No test statistic establishes causal sensing.

Split the actual mark increments into paid failed attempts, reverse cue clearing
and thermal forgetting reverses; split decrements into reversed attempts, paid
cue clearing and forgetting. Verify their net equals the expected physical mark
change. This reports the mechanism of marks, not whether a particular stored
mark descends from a particular historical attempt. Reverses can refund energy;
they must stay in both the law and total history. Conserve is a useful control:
it cannot write attempt marks, yet can acquire thermal marks.

Carry over reserve, readiness, funded/unfunded openings, sheltered function,
source work, effort/damage/reverse flux and private history bounds. No private
ordering is inferred from overlapping bounds, and body-core is not full body.
Finite whole history stays primary; rate/offset and convergence remain diagnostics.

Budgets: one worker; 600 seconds/case, 1800 seconds/batch, 2.75 GiB/job with the
existing host reserve preflight, 16 MiB/case and 64 MiB/batch output. Explicit
caps: 3000 physical states, 12000 lifted functional states, 2 GiB occupation
workspace, 256 MiB kernel and 256 MiB per projection workspace. The supervisor
stops on cap failure. No lower resolution, shorter horizon or smaller memory
is substituted. Source snapshot and all failed attempts are retained.

The initial 4 GiB cap was refused by host-memory preflight before any worker
started. The revised cap changes no law or query. A native full-world memory
failure remains a feasibility result; a repeated refusal is not permission to
reduce the host reserve. The supervisor now also records preflight refusals.

Questions, not required winners: do full-resolution quarter-energy dynamics fit;
does the cue carry usable association over actual opportunities; what fraction
of mark writes are not failed attempts; do physical and entropy diagnostics
approach their limiting values by T256; which numerical/private/fidelity limits
constrain the next map? A favorable policy sign is not an engineering criterion.
