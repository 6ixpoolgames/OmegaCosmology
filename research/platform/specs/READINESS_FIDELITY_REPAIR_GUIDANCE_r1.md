# Physical fidelity before the readiness map

26 October 2026. Design guidance from the bounded fidelity pilot; not an
implemented successor, an instruction to launch another batch, or a revision
of retained readiness-v1 physics/results.

## Diagnosis

The intermittent quarter-energy and half-energy re-engage pilots show the
same limitation: at T256 roughly 73% of physical mark increments do not come
from failed attempts. Most come from the native reverse of paid cue clearing.
Their cue has weak association with the barrier. These observations do not
invalidate the exact history computation or show a theory failure. They limit
what the model instantiates: thermally noisy reserve/register circuits, rather
than an established mechanism of learning failure and recognizing recovery.

There is a structural reason stronger conductances alone are not a remedy.
A multiplier accelerates each native reverse as well as its forward reaction.
Closed-barrier cue reset has a fast reverse that creates a cue and refunds
reserve; reverse memory clearing creates a mark and refunds reserve. Low bank
states prevent the paid forward reset while permitting such reverse events.
The energetics and conditional occupations, not the reaction names, determine
whether these circuits are informative. Do not suppress their entropy or erase
them from the current results.

## A small successor, using the existing grammar

Expose separate energetic bills/affinities for failure writing, cue writing,
cue resetting and memory clearing, with explicit rational representability.
Separate those from paired kinetic conductances and passive memory decay/error
rates. Make cue selectivity and response speed parameters. Preserve every
forward/reverse pair, energy/material bills and source-off equilibrium audit.
No new simulator primitive is needed for such a slice.

If the body's bank cannot sustain discrimination, consider coupling the
write/reset cycle to the existing physically charged carrier, with explicit
stoichiometry and the same source/endowment in every arm. Such a coupling is
new physics and must be versioned. It must not smuggle in an unlimited fuel
store, a free observer or a one-way reset. A slow structural memory variable
can be explored later if an energetic count cannot give useful retention.

## Calibrate the claimed mechanism independently of lushness

Use full and depleted reserve preparations in held-open and held-closed
barrier sectors to measure cue false positives, misses, latency and debit/
refund flux. Then test the unchanged native brief-opening process. Report
accuracy, balanced accuracy, predictive value, base rate and information,
not one headline accuracy. A held sector alone cannot establish tracking.

For memory, measure spontaneous marks with the attempt-writing pathway absent,
retention/forgetting with a stored mark, net writes associated with attempts,
and false writes through reverse clearing. Count origins with the existing
passive event ledger. Any claim about the ancestry of retained marks requires
a passive history monitor, not merely endpoint counter occupancy. Monitoring
does not feed the controller or add a physical state to its entropy.

Use the opening duration (4 units here), waiting scale (50 units), reserve
turnover and intended suppression/restart duration to state the design's
functional demands. Report the measured accuracy/cost/retention tradeoff;
do not tune parameters to make a desired policy win on lushness. Keep native
activity in the total, regardless of the result. Physical calibration and
later ranking are separate questions, with both original and successor data
retained.

## Limits carried into any wider map

Fix a common bank quantum across a cost comparison. Use no automatic grain,
memory-depth, horizon or inventory fallback. Count all warning, cue and memory
coordinates in whole and full-body history. Private score bounds may remain
unresolved; coordinate shares and body core are not substitutes.

T256 includes only about 4.75 openings on average and does not establish full
memory mixing or stationarity. Select any longer horizon for its physical
question and measured compute cost, not to make the finite score approach a
preferred stationary ranking. No permanent-death, neurological learned-
helplessness or phenomenal-suffering interpretation follows from this slice.
