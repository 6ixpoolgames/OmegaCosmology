## Purpose and invariants

Ask when spending reserve on an obstructed response costs a later opening,
and when stopping/re-engaging changes the continuation. Extend the existing
paid warning world using the same LocalModel event grammar, exact backend,
history measure and physical accounting. S4/S4b models and retained outputs
remain byte-identical; the added physics has a separate version/module.

Costs, supply, native thermal reverses, repair and physical response memory
are mechanisms, not rewards or retrospective entropy subtractions. Reversible
costs can matter within a finite horizon. Adding damage does not guarantee a
negative lushness difference. No required winner is an engineering test.

## Modules and physical state

1. **Reserve specification:** rational bank quantum with fixed1.5-energy
   capacity. Existing .5 bills for capture/metabolism/ordinary movement,
   signal reset/upkeep and1-energy write/repair bills are represented exactly
   as integer ticks. New effort and memory bills must fit the bank and be
   integer multiples of the declared quantum. Fractional costs are supported
   by refining this representation, never rounded to the nearest affordable
   bill. The compiler's state cap rejects an expensive refinement explicitly.
2. **Supply and recovery:** expose paired source and paid-repair conductances
   independently. Optional low-reserve damage accelerates both directions of
   a native apparatus breakdown/restoration pair below a declared reserve.
   Optional effort-associated damage is a distinct response outcome, with
   energy/material accounted. Its conductance weight is not assumed to equal
   an outcome probability; report actual flux. Repair remains possible.
3. **Brief opportunities:** specify opening and closing rates. Their paired
   conductance is their geometric mean and the barrier's stored energy is
   `log(closing/opening)/beta`, represented as a finite rational decimal.
   The same thermodynamic rate law then implements the rates up to numerical
   precision. Openings require no free clock. A permanently closed conserved
   sector is the explicit control, not a tiny positive rate thresholded away.
4. **Failure memory:** a finite count register0..k, energy.125 per mark.
   A failed response writes one mark, until saturation, by paying a separate
   bank bill. Slow native mark relaxation and its thermal reverse remain.
   Reverse response reactions may undo a mark and refund reserve; report them.
   The memory is a noisy physical count, not an irreversible analysis counter.
5. **Opening cue:** an energetic bit (.25energy), with bank-paid write and
   reset reactions, faster writing at an open barrier and faster reset at a
   closed one. Passive relaxation, thermal excitation and on-state upkeep
   remain. All policy arms carry the same cue hardware and costs. Neither
   the cue nor failure memory is calculated from lushness.

There is no permanent death, repair-apparatus type distinction, broad ecology
or model-based learning in this slice. Optional failure of apparatus is
recoverable. Later irreversible material export and repair-capacity modules
can compose with these records but require their own accounting and scope.

## Four fixed physical mechanisms

- **Always respond:** read the warning and respond at any failure count.
- **Conserve:** remove signal-driven effort/escape; keep ordinary movement,
  warning, cue and memory hardware and their native dynamics.
- **Stop:** read the warning only below k failure marks; native relaxation/
  reversal can eventually undo suppression. This is not perfect permanent memory.
- **Re-engage:** the same threshold, plus cue-catalysed paid mark clearing.
  A stale/false cue can cause premature restart. Clearing is not an oracle
  instruction that the barrier has truly opened.

Common physical root: working apparatus, full bank, exposed location, active
hazard, closed barrier, spent carrier, warning off, memory0 and cue off.
Root energy2.5. Every policy shares the same domains, energy function, native
routes and boundary prescription. Declared couplings differ. All physical
register histories count in whole history and the full-body projection.
No free policy switching or prior over policy choices. These are mechanism
comparisons; a later external oracle may rank them, but no learner is built in.
