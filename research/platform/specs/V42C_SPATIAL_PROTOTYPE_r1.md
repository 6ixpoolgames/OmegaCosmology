Use the 2x3 ladder proposal and retain all site positions. Two a and two b
constituents occupy four sites; at most one particle/site and one bond/particle.
Nearest-neighbor aa, bb and ab bonds compete for the same constituents. Translate
intact dimers into legal vacancies as well as allowing dissociation/reassembly.
No spatial symmetry quotient or permanent organization labels.

The chemical medium is rapidly mixed: one carrier with spent/locked/usable
energies 0/3/2 and one storage molecule with three conformations carrying zero,
one or two charge units. Bank n is its charge, with energy n, n=0,1,2. Species,
carrier count and storage molecule counts are conserved. Storage charge is an
energetic state, not newly
created material. This is a stipulated finite chemistry, not a microscopic gas
or localized fuel-transport model. Baths/source histories remain unresolved.

All bonds have energy .5. Formation spends one storage charge; reverse
dissociation restores it. Thus formation releases .5 heat under the declared
energy function. It is reversible chemical assembly, not irreversible wear.
Basal formation conductance .003. A=aa locally assists B=bb formation and its
reverse with additional conductance .15 whenever an endpoint of A neighbors
an endpoint of the prospective B pair. B assists capture usable->spent with
bank+1, additional conductance .12; basal capture .004. Capture releases one
heat unit. Unlock locked<->usable has conductance .04. Native spent<->locked
and spent<->usable conductances .002 remain in every state. Source spent->locked
supplies work 3, conductance .08, with native reverse/refund. beta=1.
Monomer hopping .08 and rigid dimer translation .04 preserve bonds and energy.
These are engineering anchors selected before scores, without claims of
calibrated chemistry. Preserve all results if the anchor does not sustain a loop.
