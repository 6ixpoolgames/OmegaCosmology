# S3 additions: interaction, repair capacity and evidence quality

25 October 2026. Draft response to Opus's review of the
[completed sprint](../engineering/occupied-sprint-2026-10-25/README.md).
Extends the [programme scope](ECOLOGY_PROGRAMME_SCOPE_r1.md); it does not launch
a study, change retained results, or authorize publication.

## Accepted direction and qualifications

Retain E1's small donor estimates as statistically informative and uncertified
(M3 under the old editorial mapping). Recommend no further attempt to resolve
them by extending the same sampling method. S1's capped feasibility is complete.
The physical interaction map is more valuable than millions of extra paths.

Occupied assistance introduces a real competing use, but it does not guarantee
a large donor history loss, a part/whole conflict or tight analytic bounds.
Reserve all signs. Find physical relationships rather than a case selected to
produce a manuscript-friendly clash. Resource stocks can fall while history
breadth rises; every such case remains in the account.

The evidence record should use independent fields for numerical confidence,
resolution coverage, physical faithfulness and claim relevance. Existing M1–M4
values remain versioned historical metadata. Do not silently regrade old results
or call them invalid because a new tier scheme is introduced. Historical
unreplicated, exact reproduction, conceptual replication, corrected interpretation
and withdrawal are separate statuses.

## 1. Keep the initial interaction map small

Use local, one-way and reciprocal occupied assistance, plus a nonconverting
binding/release control. Map source availability, helper residence time and
competing local demand. Unbinding and basal construction remain present. Report
whole and projected local histories, contact occupancy, productive flux, work,
function, reconstruction and native state-changing events. Keep cross-contact
scope explicit in the local projection.

Changing conductances changes a family of laws. A selector inside the field
is not introduced merely to describe a parameter sweep as 'one law'. The
external evaluator ranks stipulated processes, not fictitious free choices.

Work/activity matching is a separate causal control with a physical feasibility
check, not a requirement to tune all arms to a desired ranking. If no valid match
exists, report the raw contrast and the remaining confound.

## 2. Add harm to repair capacity as a distinct motif

S2 currently gives native failure and reconstruction of bound constituents.
It does **not** contain irreversible destruction of a dedicated repair system.
Do not relabel ordinary constituent turnover as that stronger experiment.

Introduce the smallest explicit repair motif using the existing grammar:

- R is paid repair apparatus that assists conversion of damaged Y into
  functioning Y through an occupied contact; Y can enable resource access.
- A paid/basal pathway can reconstruct R. If Y assists that reconstruction,
  declare it as a physical feedback edge rather than assuming endless repair.
- Basal alternatives for both R and Y stay present unless a separately declared
  physical loss actually removes their substrates or interfaces.
- Baseline equal formation energies permit matched-energy diagnostic faults:
  R functional->damaged versus Y functional->damaged. Their functions differ
  deliberately. Unequal energies require an explicit damaged product/heat map,
  not a hidden adjustment to a bank.

Begin with this small motif, not a multiplied two-ecology state space. It can
later be assembled as the repair module of either S3 organization. Reuse the
same native-event, ancestry and history machinery.

### Two comparison kinds, both reported honestly

**Post-fault preparations:** compare R damaged versus Y damaged under the same
law, matter inventory, boundary prescription and declared endowment. This
isolates different downstream structures after matched immediate stock loss.
It is a preparation contrast; no enacted choice or fault cost is inferred.

**Procedures from a common present:** a later intervention arm must implement
the actual fault transition or pulse, including damage work, product material,
released stored energy, contact dissolution and chance of failure. If externally
selected pulses change the boundary schedule, label that contrast explicitly.
Do not condition the common law on a favorable fault outcome and call it an
option carried out.

### Repairable fault versus destruction

Keep a reversible-damage primary assay in which R and Y can be rebuilt. This
tests relative recovery structure, not strict irreversibility. Add longer-lived
debris/trap states only with explicit reactions, energies and destinations for
all lost material and bonds. A positive reverse route means recovery is rare,
not impossible. If an irreversible exported-material sink is scientifically
needed, identify it as an open-boundary idealization with a matter/work ledger;
the complete sink world cannot then be advertised as an equilibrium control.

Test the no-substitute and substitute regimes through an explicit additional
repair route or spare physical machinery, paid for in the common inventory.
An ablated backup is a mechanism contrast, not the same-law preparation test.
Replication into a full spatial population is not necessary for this first
repair-capacity question.

### Readouts and possible findings

Measure the difference caused by each fault relative to the common intact
reference at all declared windows. Report repair flux, lost paid routes, first
recovery, deadline function, integrated uptime, remaining work capacity, actual
work accepted, history entropy and appropriate rate/offset readings. Where
possible compute both faults' effects on the other organization separately.

The question is whether losing a reconstruction pathway has consequences that
depend on repair delay, supply, substitutes and opportunity window. Do not require
it to be universally worse than losing Y: losing Y may also remove resource
access, or make R temporarily unproductive. Stronger repair can also increase
turnover and event entropy. Those physical alternatives are part of the test.
State which capability is lost, which survives, what can rebuild it and what
the continuation does after loss. No stock or persistence bonus is introduced.

## 3. Foreclosure is a separate analysis deliverable

Use the [formal proposal](../../../formal/contracts/foreclosure-resolution-proposal-r1.md)
as the review contract. Preserve exact support loss and its reference mass;
do not call small probabilities zero. The existing finite full-state reference
queries can be integrated first. Projected support requires word/subset memory
and an explicit capacity bound.

Add a separate probability-suppression curve for finite short word laws if
needed to describe severe weighted-access loss with no exact support loss.
It does not change the entropy measure, establish practical irreversibility,
or resolve physical-grain faithfulness. Keep recovery/closure queries alongside
both. No claim that the general physical foreclosure problem has been solved.

## 4. Cadence and confidence policy

Pick a primary cadence with a stated physical interpretation. Audit a few
predeclared representative comparisons, including any discovered sign boundary
with its exploratory status, where temporal dependence could change the
interpretation. Do not fill an entire Cartesian grid merely to obtain a tier.
Observed cadence stability is separate from proof of faithful resolution.

Start with analytic local-history bounds. Profile their width and computation
on the actual occupied world before promising a resolved conflict. Use the
filter estimator as separately typed evidence where useful. If a claim requires
certified private signs and they remain unresolved, that claim stays unresolved;
whole and structural findings can still be reported. Numerical certificates
and physical adequacy are different requirements.

## 5. Next implementation scope

Prepare the concrete S3 protocol after feasibility of the repair motif and
projection queries is measured: state/energy table, native rules, comparison
kinds, initial endowment maps, observation scopes, windows, controls and caps.
Use the programme's resource gates and retain source snapshots. Independent
conformance examples precede a scientific map. No favorable signs, automatic
manuscript edits or additional E1 sampling are completion criteria.
