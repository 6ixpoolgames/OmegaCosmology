## Why this successor

R2-v1 already demonstrates useful paid storage. Its corrected interpretation
does not establish connected enabling ancestry or maintenance necessity. R2-v2
adds spill and bound damage; that engineering smoke also already exists. This
slice supplies the missing integration rather than reproducing those studies.

The fixture tests two distinct functions: constructed A accelerates primary
resource unlocking and paid construction of B or C. B accelerates access to a
second resource route. C instead accelerates capture of the first resource.
Only one precursor is available for B/C, so those products exclude each other
through a conserved constituent, not an optimizer restriction. A and products
can break down and be rebuilt through paid or thermal paths. Maintenance means
measured repeated reconstruction; paid maintenance is not logically necessary.

The native resource process remains stochastic in every arm. Two metastable
carriers each have empty/locked/usable conformations, a maintained reversible
source, bath channels and basal unlocking. All construction, capture, unlocking
and rotor reactions retain their native reverses. Source-off detailed balance
and event-by-event inventories are required.

## Reusable composition

`modules.py` composes existing grammar fragments through named coordinate ports.
Every external connection specifies its target coordinate; role/domain mismatches,
unknown/aliased ports and energy-tick mismatches are rejected. Namespaces own
coordinates, rule IDs and inventory IDs. Parent/child metadata is mapped along
with guards, changes and energy. Bindings contribute no branches or work.

This is a bounded distinct-slot additive-energy adapter. It is not a general
graph rewrite, population, transport or interacting-Hamiltonian adapter. These
future extensions must expose their own contracts rather than bypassing ports.
