## Engineering choices

The new fixture uses explicit owned coordinates and external ports. It does not
rewrite old builders or change retained R2 laws. The energy bank is pre-existing
infrastructure in every arm; A/B/C are constructed conformations of declared
material. In particular, B/C draw on one shared precursor, whose inventory is
checked on every native transition. A and the rotor retain fixed material slots.

Both resource carriers remain present and stochastic after ablation. Removing
assistance removes only specified catalyst channels, not the carrier's native
motion or bath law. Catalysts accelerate forward and reverse equally through
the existing local detailed-balance formula. Bound structure can disappear through
bank-independent thermal reversal; paid construction provides another recovery
route. No immortal internal controller, survival bonus or activity subtraction
is added.

The rotor control is non-enabling with respect to a new resource interface. It
can still affect reserve occupancy, thermal returns and the other productive
channels indirectly. Therefore the contrast is a declared control-family
comparison, not a universal isolation of "organization minus noise". Matching
two expected moments does not match trajectories or event timing.

No spatial diffusion, population reproduction, sensing, uncertain controller,
recursive third generation or ethical correspondence is assessed by this slice.
These are future directions for the module and analysis contracts. This smoke
must report its signs and mismatches before any further regime search.

## Interpretation correction after the first integration smoke

The first version gave B another unlocking reaction on a separate, otherwise
similar carrier. It demonstrates a chain across two routes, but this is a weak
distinct-function comparison. Its30 profiles, rotor calibration and original
interpretation remain retained under r2-integrated-2026-10-27/. The later v2
law makes B a transducer: locked carrier energy3 becomes usable energy2 plus.5
bank energy and.5 heat. A's unlock exports1 heat with no bank return. This is a
physical stoichiometric change, with a bank-space guard, and a fresh smoke under
specificationr2. The correction was made to instantiate the intended object;
it is not a post-hoc claim that the original ranking violated the principle.

In the first smoke the two-moment control did not match accepted work, and its
fitted activity was almost entirely thermal rather than paid rotor cycling.
Those are limits of the comparator. A higher score there is not a clean ranking
of productive organization against a predominantly energy-consuming cycle.
