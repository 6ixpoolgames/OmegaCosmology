## Assessment

The direction is good: distinguish a signal's report from its behavioural
prompt, keep felt suffering distinct from the physical proxy, and test the
consequences of continuing, conserving and re-engaging. The work should map
the relationship among cost, supply, controllability and horizon. It should
not search until a desired ranking appears. User instructions about snapshots
and missed guesses continue to govern over the request's stronger prediction-
filing language. Preserve definitions/comparators and disclose exploration;
no guessed sign is an engine acceptance condition.

The highest-leverage next deliverable combines18-1 and18-2 in the same world.
It extends the paid [S4b sensor/response](../../studies/S4b-paid-warning/README.md)
with consequential reserve depletion, a finite opening opportunity, and paid
physical response memory. The later anaesthesia/capacity comparisons should
reuse those mechanisms. Fidelity/valence18-7 remains a later physical-model-
register experiment; it must not be silently folded into an oracle mechanism
comparison or defined by the score it is supposed to predict.

## Correct the diagnosis of the current cost before expanding it

The blocked S4b TX root has a maximum bank of1.5 energy. Each forward blocked
effort consumes.5, the same increment that one successful capture supplies:
one-third of maximum reserve. It is not an infinitesimal charge per attempt.
AtT1024, cadence.25, the retained native law gives:

|---|---:|
| Forward blocked efforts |1.956410|
| Reverse blocked-effort reactions |1.627186|
| Gross bank debit from effort |.978205|
| Reverse bank credit |.813593|
| Net effort energy |.164612|
| Fraction of gross debit returned by reverse reactions |83.1721%|

| Net effort / net bank capture |13.7143%|
| Probability of empty bank atT1024 |62.7295%|
| Fraction ofT0..1024 spent at empty bank |61.7044%|
| Probability bank cannot pay the two-tick repair bill atT1024 |87.2487%|

These come from retained event fluxes and sparse occupation propagation of
the frozen64-state law, not a new parameter run. The corresponding T0 bank
empty probability is62.5860% and insufficient-repair-reserve probability
87.1619%. Effort's incremental functional effect is small because attempts
are infrequent, much reverse cycling is available, and native recovery remains.
The source's accepted work61.800720 is not the body's bank income: the source
charges a carrier and returns much work through its own reverse. Do not use
that gross external number as if it were available body reserve.

Thus "reserves always refilled" is too strong, as is "nothing was at stake".
The correct limitation is: no irreversible loss, no lasting response memory,
strong native recovery and a weak sustained warning. Recoverability does not
imply zero finite-horizon narrowing. Likewise, permanent damage or death does
not guarantee an entropy ranking; surviving environmental activity still counts.
The original positive futile-effort result remains a valid physical observation.

Source profiles: S4b results-2026-10-26/dynamic/blocked_{TX,T0}__dt0p25/RESULT.json,
protocol native model records and known root. Existing
[interpretation](../../studies/S4b-paid-warning/INTERPRETATION_r1.md) supplies
the complete-history and projected-history limitations. A future cost sweep
must vary reverse thermodynamic affinity as well as conductance: multiplying
both rates changes kinetics, not net energy cost or equilibrium bias.

## Revisions needed before implementation

1. **Report truth, action calibration and ranking separately.** A signal can
   accurately report a hazard while prompting an unhelpful response. A
   high-fidelity model does not by itself specify what response its bearer
   selects. Define the full-picture reference mechanism, physical objective
   or homeostatic criterion independently of lushness. Do not name the lushness
   winner the "true" signal and then test whether true signals win.
2. **Self and whole are separate predictions.** Request18-3 says the true
   signal should beat false signals for both the bearer and the whole. The
   chapter itself rightly permits a self-serving accurate signal to cost
   others. Make the self prediction primary and the whole comparison a
   separate readout, or state the additional conditions excluding that conflict.
3. **Fidelity is not valence calibration.** Section18.5 calls net valence the
   bearer's estimate of its future breadth, which risks assuming Prediction18.1.
   The request's independent homeostatic/error-signal construction avoids that
   circularity and should govern the implementation. Accurate prediction can
   coexist with a signal tracking some other physical property. Whether they
   converge is the experiment, not a logical consequence of fidelity.
4. **Avoid a net-effect tautology.** "Narrows when its narrowing exceeds its
   widening" identifies the target but cannot independently predict a boundary.
   Specify costs, opening probabilities, damage and reconstruction mechanisms
   without using the resulting lushness to classify them. Map the sign, then
   explain it with physical flux/access diagnostics. No universal sign follows
   just from adding lasting damage.
5. **Anaesthesia matching concerns the insult.** Match the immediate injury
   mechanism and its resource bill. Allow subsequent damage, movement, repair
   and recovery to differ as consequences of the signal. Requiring the full
   injury consequences to be the same would erase the mechanism being tested.
   Suppression itself needs an explicit costed physical process. This is a
   signal-suppression analogue, not a model of every clinical anaesthetic effect.
6. **Policy memory belongs to the physics.** A counter of failed attempts that
   changes later actions is a physical register with writing/reset/upkeep and
   possible damage, included in the history. It cannot be an analysis-only
   monitor. A control-return cue also needs sensing. Fixed physical response
   circuits can precede the full S5 model-based agent module, but they are not
   free controllers.
7. **Regime change needs an explicit clock convention.** A return at a fixed
   external deadline is a declared boundary schedule, not a stationary CTMC
   with a free internal timer. Compose kernels across that schedule and report
   its boundary, or use a costed native stochastic timer/cue. Do not add an
   arbitrary countdown to the objective's state solely to keep score.
8. **Activity matching is a distinct physical control.** Matching one expected
   event count at one horizon does not match histories, costs or times. Use a
   nonaversive paid process only where it can physically achieve the match.
   Report residual differences and unmatchable points. The raw total remains
   primary; never subtract its activity term after seeing the outcome.

## Recommended first slice

- Keep S4b-v1 unchanged as the maintained reversible reference.
- Add finite reserve/source delivery and costed slow recovery. Then add a
  declared damaged/irrecoverable-material channel as a separate mechanism,
  with a true sink explicitly identified as an open-boundary idealization.
  Irreversibility is not required merely to make finite costs consequential.
- Add brief openings that require a funded, working actuator; use the same
  opening process across response mechanisms. A low bank can then cost a real
  opportunity without any added survival reward.
- Compare always responding, no response, a small paid failure-memory circuit
  and a cue-responsive circuit that re-engages. Reuse memory hardware across
  controls so extra counter flicker is visible. Separate an oracle comparison
  of these circuits from a claim that a body learns the optimal circuit.
- First map effort bill/affinity against supply and opening hazard in a small
  exact world. Add damage and response-memory persistence as named factors,
  rather than crossing every requested mechanism in the first Cartesian grid.
- Report whole and body profiles, native event counts, bank-empty/repair-
  disabled probabilities, work/heat, opening opportunities missed, damage and
  reconstruction, plus scope and unresolved intervals. Strong private-history
  claims wait for resolving bounds; whole gains cannot fill their place.

This tests the proposed conservation/readiness mechanism. It does not by itself
show that the empirical syndrome of learned helplessness is an optimal energy
calculation. Maier and Seligman's2016 account instead emphasizes default
passivity after prolonged aversive stimulation and learned control overriding
it. Conserving reserve can be an adaptive interpretation to test separately
from that proximate mechanism; persistent passivity after control returns is
the overshoot case. Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4920136/ .

## Requests already partly delivered or requiring other corrections

- **Definition/version mapping:** r6's operational valence signal is not the
  historical T-and-D definition. S4b already supplies a paid register that
  changes response disposition, bearing on the new operational proxy. Keep
  that mapping explicit rather than requiring the old T test as an unannounced
  gate on every new question. Phenomenal suffering remains unestablished under
  either definition.
- **The shared warning module already exists** in `testbed/signals.py`, with
  paired bank-paid write/reset and escape/blocked-effort chemistry. Extend it
  and expose the currently fixed cost/supply settings; do not build another
  simulator or overwrite the retained world. Gain/reset fidelity and costly
  threshold/memory mechanisms need further physical work.
- **S3 early part/whole opposition is already resolved** by analytic bounds
  evaluated in float64, atT16 and cadences.25/.5. A loses while B and the whole
  gain; later A bounds remain unresolved. This is not a rigorous rounding or
  finest-faithful-grain certificate. The request's conditional "if S3 later
  resolves" can now be updated from the local
  [S3 report](../../studies/S3-repair-and-interaction/INTERPRETATION_r1.md).
- **Repair capacity:** the S3 repair/fault map is complete. Later shock,
  persistent repair loss and substitutes are new comparisons, not reruns of
  something still absent. Same finite irreducible-law fault roots cannot give
  distinct limiting rates. A removed route does not automatically give a rate
  harm; check recurrent classes and reachable recovery routes.
- **Foreclosure:** structural projected support is implemented; long-history
  probability-suppression curves are not. The existing suppression LP covers
  finite enumerated words only. Zero foreclosure does not mean zero harm.
- **Cooperation:** joint entropy cannot exceed the sum of its own marginals.
  Define a synergy claim through explicit same-endowment mechanism contrasts,
  e.g. combined effect minus separate effects from a common baseline. Do not
  interpret "beyond the sum" as violating the entropy chain rule.
- **Training/countersignals:** label an independently defined effort/error
  signal aversive, then measure continuation separately. Do not make its sign
  a lushness calculation embedded inside the world. A physical expectation
  register is additional mechanism, not a decorative observation.
- **Ecology, plurality and model-based agents:** these remain dependent future
  modules. A confined population is not delivered by relabeling chemical slots,
  and no toy whole modeled field includes unrepresented displaced production.
