## Corrections to the common protocol

1. **Freeze inputs, not desired winners.** Record configurations, quantities,
   assumptions, tolerances, budgets and interpretation rules before fresh
   scoring. Separate theorem checks, physical qualification, existence probes
   and speculative predictions. A missed guess is not a principle failure.
   Use a content-addressed local snapshot; the document's request to commit
   first does not authorize a Git commit or push.

2. **Report applicability instead of manufacturing certificates.** Run the
   current paired validator where its premises hold. An old unmatched-boundary
   contrast may reproduce numerically while remaining inapplicable to that
   certificate. A port with changed rules, inventory or boundaries is a new
   versioned experiment, not a literal replay. Do not weaken the validator,
   omit boundary metadata or silently replace the historical point.

3. **Separate evidence axes.** Numerical reproduction, paired-contract checks,
   resolution coverage, physical adequacy and claim relevance are different.
   One replay cannot certify an entire parameter map or establish faithful
   physical resolution. The drafting assertion that no study can carry any
   tier before P0 is a proposed manuscript classification, not a scientific
   consequence of the existing validator.

4. **Exact finite model is not exact arithmetic.** A float64 kernel/Poisson
   solve is a numerical evaluation of a finite mathematical model. Report
   residuals and conditioning; use verified error enclosures before calling
   a sign rigorously certified. Record unsupported asymptotics explicitly.

5. **Ablate a relation consistently.** Preserve physical components, native
   background channels, endowment and the applicable cost law. Removing an
   interaction necessarily changes some rates; demanding identical dynamics
   would remove the contrast. Unused hardware need not incur identical realized
   expenditure. Report that difference rather than inventing compensating cost.

6. **Whole modeled field, not automatically the whole universe.** External
   baths, sources and intervention-setting apparatus remain unresolved where
   they are not modeled. Matched boundary prescriptions alone do not establish
   cancellation of exterior histories or correlations.

## Profiles: the required analytical repair

For a finite irreducible sampled kernel P, let g be its row-entropy vector,
pi its stationary distribution, r = pi g its bits-per-step rate, and b the
zero-stationary-mean solution of (I-P)b = g-r. For initial distribution mu:

    ell_n = n r + mu b - mu P^n b.

This supplies a directly checkable finite-profile identity and a transient
bound, not just a fitted line. The current `history.py` already computes the
irreducible numerical rate and bias internally; exposing the bias norm,
normalization, residuals and remainder is a small extension. Its deliberate
rejection of reducible asymptotics must remain until class/absorption handling
is implemented and checked. Scheduled experiments need their operation prefix
carried into any common recovery-tail analysis.

For two arms, define Delta r and Delta b at the declared roots. The line
crossing -Delta b/Delta r is an asymptotic estimate, not necessarily an actual
crossing, a unique crossing or a positive physical horizon. Opposing transient
contributions can cause several crossings, including when the line suggests
none. The retained R2 card already records a twice-changing preparation sign.

Let M be the sum of the two bias sup norms. Then

    |Delta ell_n - n Delta r - Delta b| <= M.

For Delta r > 0, n Delta r + Delta b > M guarantees positive difference at
every such later n. Check the earlier finite prefix explicitly before claiming
all-horizon dominance. A sharper validated remainder can improve this bound.
With zero rate difference, report a limiting offset only where convergence is
established; a tail enclosure may remain inconclusive. Periodic general kernels
require separate treatment. With a tiny unresolved rate difference, do not
divide by it to manufacture an enormous crossover.

Use verdicts such as observed crossing(s), certified eventual dominance,
certified all-horizon dominance, no crossing observed on the tested window,
and unresolved. The requested binary crossing/no-crossing rule is insufficient.
State both steps and physical duration: T = n times cadence. A geometric law
of 1,000 steps changes its physical lifetime when cadence changes; either hold
physical persistence fixed or label the step-based comparison explicitly.

Projected histories are generally hidden-Markov processes. Their rate and
especially their offset do not follow by applying the full-state Poisson
equation to a quotient kernel. Keep finite-history bounds and only report
asymptotic bounds that have actually been established. This is a separate
analysis capability, not a prerequisite for replaying a whole-model result.

## Corrected T verification

An equal endpoint does not establish earlier-profile dominance. Construct the
pair so ell_early(n) >= ell_late(n) for every n in the ending law's support.
Then E[ell_early(N)] >= E[ell_late(N)], strictly only when the ending law gives
positive probability to a horizon with a strict gap. Merely having probability
before T is insufficient, since both profiles can agree there, including n=0.
An unbounded geometric ending law also needs the post-T comparison specified.

Verify independent geometric and uniform endings by direct finite sums or
kernel resolvents, including the geometric support convention. Compare
E[ell_N], not E[2^ell_N], ell at E[N], or the entropy of a stopped record with
its stopping-time contribution silently added. For a state-dependent ending,
execute the joint stopping law; an independent-duration formula is inapplicable.
If stopping becomes a represented physical event, account for it explicitly.

## What may count against what

The packet labels several hypotheses as principles that do not follow merely
from complete-history entropy: every retained chain beating every link cut,
all useful information repaying its physical costs, and every access block
lowering whole-field breadth. Retain these as explicit conditional physical
hypotheses or existence questions. A single clean positive construction can
demonstrate capability; an unsuccessful bounded family does not establish that
the capability is impossible.

Conversely, a demonstrably negative long-run rate difference failing to dominate
after a valid global transient bound is a contradiction of the numerical/model
assumptions or the theorem implementation. It is not an ordinary empirical
counterexample to that algebraic result. Repair-capacity versus repaired-part
damage has no general entropy ordering without additional physical assumptions.
