## Agreed stopping rule

Treat the present spatial batch as the final planned experimental package for
Book One's version C. Finish it, verify and interpret its results, and align the
manuscript with exactly what it establishes. Another ecology, agency, suffering
or social-model campaign is not a publication gate.

The manuscript needs finite demonstrations of consequential relationships, not
universal rankings of organization over disorder. A bounded world can represent
paid structure changing the pathways for constructing and using another structure
without demonstrating indefinite acceleration, artificial life or ethical sufficiency.

This stopping rule does not promise favourable comparisons. If valid results are
unfavourable, narrow the empirical wording and explain the mechanism. Numerical
or accounting defects warrant repair. An inconvenient valid result does not
automatically warrant another fixture to secure a win.

## Redraft guidance

Separate three claims throughout the evidence discussion:

1. Representation: the specified model contains the claimed enabling relationship,
   with its physical costs and alternatives.
2. Ranking: complete history lushness favours or disfavors a specified option over
   a stated horizon and resolution. The sign is an observation, not a consequence
   of calling a structure productive.
3. Interpretation: correspondence with ethical judgments remains independently
   assessable. Neither function nor a positive score proves that bridge.

Lushness remains complete conditional sampled-history perplexity. Its logarithm
is the history entropy reported in bits. Access, organization and generativity
act through the native continuation law. No activity subtraction, semantic reward,
work denominator or extra access multiplier is introduced.

Suggested statement of the empirical aim:

> Under specified physical laws, resources and boundary prescriptions, constructed
> arrangements can change the weighted continuations available from a present.
> These models investigate those enabling relationships and measure their complete
> history consequences, including their physical expense and alternatives.

Keep the central thesis stated as the thesis. Limiting the experimental claim
does not require replacing the philosophical thesis with an unspecified claim
that lushness merely helps. Distinguish the argument from what the models establish.

Revise passages requiring organization to win universally, forever or on rate.
Preserve thermal variation in every arm. A valid contrary result need not be a
bug or refute the entire programme: identify the specific claim it bears on.
A missed guessed threshold is not a theory failure; an unexpected result is not
an advance prediction. Open questions can remain research directions.

## What this batch contains

Six resolved lattice sites hold two unmarked constituents of each of two species,
mobile monomers/dimers, competing bonds, a resource carrier, chemical bank and
physical actuator. Native motion, thermal dynamics, spontaneous assembly, reverses
and recovery remain in every option. Carrier and bank chemistry are rapidly mixed;
chemical transport is unresolved.

An AA dimer assists local BB construction; BB enables resource capture. These
configuration-dependent reversible pathways operate wherever the motif forms.
No permanent organizational label gives one arm free capability.

Every option starts with the same literal modeled state, energy and inventory.
An exterior instruction couples the shared actuator to AA construction, competing
AB construction or neither during a pulse of duration16. Strokes spend two bank
charges; their reverses refund them. Native bond loss also returns charge. Keep
the entire preparation history and paths without construction. A separate costly
unsuccessful attempt is not represented.

- C1: AA-directed investment versus leaving material available under native dynamics.
- C2: AA-directed versus competing AB construction. This tests competing uses and
  connections, not isolated placement of identical dimers or matched activity.
- C3: how both contrasts change between a maintained source and one shut off
  after16. Evolution continues afterwards. Thermal routes and stored resources
  remain; these are two boundary prescriptions.

Three instructions, two source prescriptions and cadences0.125/0.25/0.5 give
18 full profiles. Scalar scores are retained at every prefix throughT4096;
headline horizons are16/64/256/1024/4096. No parameter tuning or policy search.

## Limits that must accompany the findings

- Scope is the whole modeled field. Exterior instruction generation and reservoir
  histories are unresolved. This is a controlled comparison from a common modeled
  present, not a closed autonomous universe. Shared boundaries do not establish
  cancellation of omitted correlations.
- Equal nominal stroke bills do not match actual stroke counts, refunds, work or
  activity. Ordinary comparisons remain meaningful; attribution beyond activity
  remains open. Do not claim that the activity objection has been settled.
- This is a dense tiny lattice, not dilute gas or microscopic thermal physics.
- Reversible enabling and reconstruction are represented. Independent wear,
  maintenance under irreversible damage, pathwise capture-funded descendants,
  three genealogical generations and robust compounding are not established.
- Finite preparation is followed by common finite irreducible recovery within
  each source case. Eventual entropy rates agree. Finite profiles and possible
  offsets are the target. Transient slopes do not prove permanent rate differences;
  T4096 is not an infinite-horizon certificate.
- Three cadences audit temporal sensitivity, not physical faithfulness or all
  state-resolution questions. Floating-point checks are not rigorous rounding
  certificates. An unresolved difference is not an exact tie; all-horizon equality
  requires proof.

These limits are not demands for another pre-publication campaign.
