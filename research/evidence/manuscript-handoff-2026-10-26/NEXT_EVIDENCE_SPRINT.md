## A. Enacted damage plus physical activity controls

Start with the S3 R/Y motif, one common intact present and a shared apparatus/
energy inventory. Compare an actual R-fault procedure, Y-fault procedure,
non-damaging paid operation and idle. Every operation keeps its failure/no-op
outcomes. Include the complete operation prefix in history, with energy
released to heat or retained products, actuation bills and any bound-contact
dissolution. Do not prepare the damaged state and call that damage enacted.

Two legitimate implementations are a physical finite actuator and a declared
external pulse schedule using native paid reactions. A schedule is an explicit
boundary prescription; it is not an internal free clock or conditioning on a
successful fault. Compose prefix and continuation kernels with their entropy
rewards and retain memory across the boundary. The post-pulse recovery law
can then be common. No artificial prior over chosen commands is counted.

This requires a reusable phased/controlled-operation analysis path; the current
single-generator stationary helper cannot silently stand in for it. Verify
prefix composition on tiny exact laws, then use capped feasibility on the
small repair motif. Finite profiles are primary. A permanent exported-material
variant, if added, is an explicit open-boundary extension, not an equilibrium
control advertised as reversible.

Use a genuine non-enabling paid physical cycle as the activity control. Give
its hardware/state variables to all arms. A paired cycle may dissipate work
and fluctuate but cannot secretly perform repair/capture or add entropy-only
labels. Calibrate expected *represented state-changing* jumps, gross/net work
and any bank costs independently of the lushness winner. Two knobs may be
needed to match work and activity. Match inside the feasible family, report
residuals/interpolation and unmatchable regions; never extrapolate a match.
A one-horizon moment match is not an all-horizon, timing or correlation match.

Report R/Y recovery/uptime, usable interfaces, operation success, material/
energy fate, work and native event traffic beside whole/private history and
foreclosure. Outcomes in which reconstruction activity dominates remain
valid. The experiment tests a physical relation, not the assumption that a
common-present procedure must restore an intuitive intact-wins ordering.

## E. Equal-lushness benevolent-controller pair

Construct two physical controller/processes with a declared equality target
for whole history, but different ability to respond, repair or preserve a
neighbor's channel. Match endowment, hardware and boundaries. Equality at
one horizon, a profile, a rate and a magnitude under a chosen projection are
different claims. **For the intended all-horizon pair, equality must be proved
from the declared laws, presents and observation—not certified by a finite
numeric grid or an interval that merely contains zero.** A proof of a stronger
history-law correspondence can suffice, but all-horizon entropy equality need
not imply structural identity. Numerical checks validate the construction;
they do not supply the missing proof.

If exact equality is unavailable, a calibrated finite-horizon near-tie is a
separately labelled exploratory compression test, not the requested proved
pair. Preserve whether equality was constructed rather than discovered. Then apply the
same independently specified disruption/opportunity probes, paying for them,
and compare complete continuations. Do not label a controller benevolent by
the lushness score it was tuned to tie. Determine that label's physical role
before the measurement. No lexical moral hierarchy is added.
