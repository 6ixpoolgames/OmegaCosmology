Functional capacity minus sham raises history breadth in72/72 comparisons;
construction assistance minus basal does so in72/72. These overlap across
horizon/cadence/root and are not144 independent regimes. Capacity is actually
used; total activations and first-episode completion are distinct. All396
comparison rows, activity/work differences and216 full profiles accompany
this card. No new calculation is needed to retain these findings.

The original cadence audit found no sign flips, but the later horizon audit
found nine groups changing sign: three preparation contrasts at each of three
cadences. Driven/basal changes sign twice. Cadence robustness is not horizon
robustness. Exact crossing times have not been bracketed.

## Corrections that travel with the result

The original witness accepts basal/native events and certifies ordering, not
connected enabling ancestry. Capture is possible without active apparatus;
catalysis accelerates it. 'Upkeep' in this model is bank relaxation that does
not preserve working apparatus. Bound constituents cannot deactivate before
unbinding. A loaded store lacks independent spill/breakdown at all loads:
the refund pathway can be blocked by the reduced bank capacity.

Therefore this is a bounded capacity-use result. It is not a demonstration of
necessary paid maintenance, robust recursive generativity, or three generations.
Those omissions motivated a new versioned physical module; they did not alter
the original stored numbers. S2 occupancy is another explicit new law version.
