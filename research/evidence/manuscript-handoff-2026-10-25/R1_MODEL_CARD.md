# R1: constructed access

Draft Appendix C entry. Study [R1](../../studies/R1-constructed-access/README.md);
authoritative [interpretation](../../studies/R1-constructed-access/INTERPRETATION_r1.md).

Two apparatus constituents, their bond, one carrier and a bounded bank form a
finite reversible CTMC. The constructed assembly assists resource access.
Unassisted thermal/native routes persist. Work and heat are explicit boundaries;
the bath/source histories are unresolved. This is a fixed count-world, not gas.

All preparations have energy4.5 and the same constituent endowment within each
law. Built, unassembled and nine-root equal-energy reference preparations are
compared under each law. The reference averages histories conditioned on known
roots; it does not add entropy for randomly choosing a root.

Four laws cross source on/off with assisted-path intact/ablated; three cadences
.125/.25/.5 and horizons16/64/256/1024 give144 profiles. A mechanism contrast
changes paired channels; a boundary contrast changes the work source. Neither
is an enacted option of a controller. Same-law preparation contrasts have a
common limiting rate and differing offsets.

## Result and relationship

For driven/intact, built minus unassembled at cadence.25 is +5.778,+15.410,
+27.590,+33.258 bits at the four horizons; limiting offset difference +33.336.
The built preparation beats both same-law references throughout the tested
driven/intact corridor. Across all laws, five preparation comparisons favor
unassembled, all T16 in the ablation. One preparation comparison changes sign
across cadences. Preserve the full map rather than replacing the baseline name
with 'disorder'.

Intact minus assisted-path ablation is positive in72/72 comparisons, across
preparations, horizons and cadences. At driven/built/T1024, the gains are
+171.865,+140.926,+108.924 bits at the three cadences, with rate increments
+.165752,+.136222,+.105676 bits/time. At.25 this also adds about2.409 net work
and28.007 state-changing events. It is not a work- or activity-matched contrast.
Source-off catalytic gains at unchanged equilibrium weights are kinetic gains;
they are counted and prevent attributing every bit to productive organization.

## Five-item report and scope

Profiles/rates/offsets are in R1_PROFILES.json and all240 contrasts in
R1_COMPARISONS.json, keyed by law, preparation, cadence and horizon. Whole
modeled history is calculated; projected private histories and history-support
foreclosure were not calculated. Numerical residuals are retained in each row.
Physical-grain faithfulness remains open; three temporal settings were audited,
not established as universal temporal cutoffs. Exact crossovers were not found.

The rebinding witness measures ordered reconstruction events, not connected
ancestry or necessary paid maintenance. Native recovery remains possible.
There is no population, physical policy selector, suffering or domination.
This bears on finite physical access; it establishes no ethical correspondence.

To challenge the bounded physical interpretation, inspect whether the access
gain disappears against a meaningful matched activity/work control or changes
under a physically adequate refinement. This is a pending causal question,
not a retrospective statement that the observed positive map failed.
