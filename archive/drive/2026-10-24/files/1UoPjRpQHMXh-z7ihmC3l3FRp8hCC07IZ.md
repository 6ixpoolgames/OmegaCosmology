# 14 — Provenance

This chapter says where the program came from and how that origin shapes the
account the manuscript gives. It is not a history of drafts. The earlier
editions remain in the program's research record for anyone who wants them.
What matters here is the motivation, the sequence of ideas it set in motion,
and why the manuscript tells the story in the order it does.

---

## 14.1 The motivating question

The program began with an ethical question rather than a physical one: is there
a standard for good and bad that is drawn from how the world works, rather than
from anyone's preferences? The early intuitions were about spaces of meaning,
about intelligence as something with geometry, and above all about persistence
and continuation: what keeps going, what it depends on, and what it makes
possible for whatever comes next.

Those intuitions became **Gradient Ethics**. The name records its starting
picture. Everything that happens draws on differences that permit change, and
everything that lasts has passed through conditions that select what remains.
The program called these the **gradient** and the **filter**, and for a long
time they were its two primitives. The gradient was any oriented difference that
can shape what follows: free energy, chemical potential, information, an
opportunity. The filter was whatever decides which processes are still present
later: stability, competition, cooperation, selection in Darwin's sense
generalized across scales.

The guiding aim was economy. The program wanted to expose the least machinery
that would make Gradient Ethics work, so that nothing in the ethics could hide
in an unexamined assumption.

---

## 14.2 How the ideas unfolded

The ideas developed in a sequence, each step answering a difficulty the
previous one exposed. The originator's recollection of that sequence, checked
against the program's working records, runs as follows.

**From gradient and filter to Alpha.** Asking what gradient and filter
themselves require led to three more basic ingredients. A gradient needs
differences, so there must be **distinction**. Differences must bear on one
another, so there must be **relation**. And there must be a direction in which
one thing gives rise to the next, so there must be **asymmetry**. The program
named these three together **Alpha**. Gradient and filter turned out to be one
expression of them: in the causal-set form of chapter 10, growth plays the part
of the gradient and the constraint on what may be added plays the part of the
filter (Bombelli et al. 1987; Rideout and Sorkin 2000). What had been sought as
the minimum for an ethics proved to be the minimum for structured dynamics of
any kind (Cook 2004; Spencer-Brown 1969; Bateson 1972).

**From Alpha to the adapter.** Alpha alone does not make a world. A world needs
one further stipulation: the bare ruleset by which its distinctions and
relations actually evolve. The program calls this the **adapter**, and a
world's adapter its **Logos**. Everything else in that world, including every
organism and institution, emerges from the adapter rather than being added to
it.

**From the adapter to the totality of rules.** If one adapter makes one world,
the natural next question is the totality of all of them. The program took up
Wolfram's ruliad, the entangled limit of all possible computations, as the
model of that totality (Wolfram 2021). Early in the program's life this
Wolframian picture was also part of its motivation.

**From the totality to orientation.** The ruliad does not come with a time
direction. Nothing in a totality of rules says which way is forward. The
asymmetry in Alpha therefore could not be assumed to be time. It had to enter as
an **orientation**: a direction read across the structure, of which time is one
case. This is why time is an orientation axis in the program rather than
something fundamental, and why the **perspective** of an observer became
central. Each perspective is situated within an orientation, and only some
orientations support records, gradients and observers at all. The whole that
every orientation describes is **Omega**.

**From perspective to physics.** The step the originator remembers least
clearly is the one the program's records document best. Once perspective was
central, the work turned to making the account answerable to physics. Its
working notes recast the perspective as a **present frame**: the physical state
on a spacelike slice, together with the laws that carry it forward. They took
the field-first view that every consequential process belongs to the object,
noise and failure included. They required that organization enter only through
the ordinary law, with no bonus for structure. They fixed lushness as the
effective weighted breadth of complete histories, the exponentiated entropy of
chapter 4. And they began the finite probes and exact examples that make the
program able to fail. The present manuscript is the result of that turn.

## 14.3 How the origin shapes the story

The program's ideas arrived in roughly the reverse of the order in which the
manuscript presents them. The motivation ran from an ethical question down to
the least machinery that could support it, and from there out to a picture of
reality as a whole. The manuscript runs the other way: from the physical
possibility structure, through the measure, to the ethics and the alignment
framework, with Alpha, Logos and Omega placed last as an interpretation. The
reversal is deliberate. A reader outside the program can check each step of
the physical and ethical argument without first accepting the interpretation
that motivated it, and the ethics depends on none of chapter 10.

The origin still shows throughout.

- **The gradient survives in the name.** Gradient Ethics asks how the
  universe's free-energy gradient should be spent (chapter 5), and generativity
  is the conversion of existing gradients into durable conditions for further
  ones (chapter 3).
- **The filter survives as selection.** The persistence filter of chapter 3, the
  Price equation's gap between share and whole, and the metavalue of persistence
  in chapter 5 are the filter made precise.
- **Economy survives as the no-bonus rule.** Organization receives no special
  credit anywhere in the account. Every advantage it has must come through the
  native law, which is the original wish to expose the least machinery stated as
  a rule of method.
- **Orientation survives as the present frame.** All assessment is made from a
  present (chapter 2), the arrow is a property of oriented developments, and the
  ultimate frame is Omega used as a reference rather than an observer.
- **Perspective survives as the limit on every agent.** No one holds the whole
  (chapter 8), and the persistent reason to seek correction (chapter 9) is the
  ethical consequence of being situated.

The theological connections in chapter 10 have the same origin. The program did
not set out to reconstruct a theology. As its picture of reality took shape, it
recognized structures that older traditions had long described, and it maps its
account onto them where the connection is real, leaving the weight with those
traditions.

## 14.4 How the manuscript was made

The originator supplies the program: its motivating question, its intended
definitions and its corrections when a formulation drifts from what was meant.
AI systems have served as drafting partners, literature guides, calculators and
critics throughout. This edition was drafted with Claude and reviewed by the
assistants the research record names Astra and Sol; earlier editions drew on
Codex, Opus and Grok, among others, whose contributions are attributed in that
record.

That way of working has a known weakness, and the record should say so. Across
many sessions with different assistants, some formulations drifted: a
definition was narrowed to something tractable, a conjecture was stated as a
result, or an earlier wording survived because the originator's attention was on
another part of the program and the correction waited. This manuscript corrects
those drifts where they have been checked against the originator's intended
meaning, and it marks the status of every claim so that drift of the opposite
kind, a thin result presented as a large one, is visible.

Two rules govern authority. A definition's authority comes from the program the
originator intends. A mathematical or empirical claim answers to its argument
and its evidence, whoever proposed it. Agreement among assistants is not
independent evidence, and a calculation that runs is evidence only for the
calculation it checks. The worked examples are reproducible from the scripts
listed in chapter 11, and the program's repository keeps the earlier editions,
probes and reports as records rather than as current statements.

The north star has not changed since the first intuition: characterize physical
possibility, and learn how its organization supports further possibility and
value.
