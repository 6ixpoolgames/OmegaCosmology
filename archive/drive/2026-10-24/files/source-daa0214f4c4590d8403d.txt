LUSHNESS: CURRENT RESEARCH TARGET
v2.5 checkpoint
Current through 21 September 2026

CURRENT STATUS

Lushness is the program's name for the richness of physically differentiated, nonredundant consequential possibility.

It is intended to characterize a consequence space after the relevant physical Perspective and continuation structure have been identified.

The current research target is not a single universal scalar formula. The more general object is a structured profile of consequence geometry: how broad it is, how many independent directions it contains, how those directions are weighted, how persistent they are, how continuation changes across horizon and scale, and whether existing possibilities generate access to further structured possibility.

A compact description is:

    Lushness concerns the effective extent and organization of consequential possibility.


1. WHY RAW POSSIBILITY IS NOT ENOUGH

A physical system can contain enormous numbers of microstates without supporting correspondingly rich consequential continuation.

Examples include:

- high-temperature molecular motion;
- chaotic mixing;
- independent random noise;
- duplicated copies of the same pattern;
- microscopic differences that are rapidly erased.

These systems may have high entropy, large state counts, or strong sensitivity to initial conditions.

Those properties are real, but they do not by themselves establish that the system occupies many independent consequential directions.

Lushness therefore distinguishes:

    abundant variation

from

    differentiated continuation.


2. THE CENTRAL INTUITION

Consider two systems with comparable physical resources.

System A produces vast numbers of microscopic differences, but most quickly mix away or remain redundant.

System B supports a smaller number of distinctions that become stable records, tools, memories, policies, new capabilities, or persistent structures that open different future regimes.

The Lushness intuition is that System B can possess a richer consequential possibility structure even if System A has more raw microstates.

This is not because organization is assigned value by definition.

It is because organization can change the geometry of continuation.


3. NONREDUNDANCY

Redundancy is central.

Suppose a consequence space contains one direction v repeated a million times.

Compare it with a space containing many mutually independent directions of comparable physical weight.

A useful Lushness measure should not treat these as equivalent.

In a covariance approximation,

    Sigma_narrow
        ~ M |v><v|

while a broad structure may have

    Sigma_broad
        ~ sum_k (M/r) |u_k><u_k|.

A log-determinant statistic,

    log det(I + beta Sigma),

is larger when comparable mass is distributed across genuinely independent directions.

This captures one important aspect of Lushness:

    breadth of nonredundant structure matters beyond sheer multiplicity.


4. EFFECTIVE POSSIBILITY VOLUME

The phrase "effective possibility volume" is a useful shorthand.

It does not require ordinary Euclidean volume.

Depending on the geometry, effective volume might be characterized through:

- covering numbers;
- metric entropy;
- covariance determinant;
- information volume;
- intrinsic dimension;
- process distinguishability;
- topological complexity;
- weighted support;
- reachable-region measure.

The essential requirement is that the quantity reflect the physically grounded consequence geometry rather than arbitrary coordinate scale.


5. LUSHNESS AS A PROFILE

A single number may eventually be useful for comparisons in restricted settings.

The more general object is likely a profile.

Possible axes include:

    L(P, H, epsilon, ...)

where:

- P identifies the situated Perspective;
- H represents continuation depth;
- epsilon represents physical or analytical resolution;
- additional variables may encode resource or scale conditions.

A profile can reveal structure that one scalar hides.

For example, two systems may have similar short-horizon Lushness but radically different long-horizon generativity.


6. SUPPORT LUSHNESS

One channel asks:

    How much consequential possibility exists at all?

This is support-sensitive Lushness.

It emphasizes the geometric extent of physically available differentiated continuation regardless of how quantum weight is distributed within that support.

Support Lushness is useful for questions about reachability, possibility breadth, rare alternatives, and structural capacity.


7. BORN-WEIGHTED LUSHNESS

A second channel asks:

    How is physical quantum weight distributed across consequential possibility?

Once a set of alternatives is sufficiently decohered to support additive probabilities, Born weights can contribute a natural physical measure.

A weighted Lushness construction can then distinguish:

- broad possibility concentrated on negligible weight;
- broad possibility carrying substantial weight;
- narrow high-weight continuation;
- mixed structures.

Keeping support and weight conceptually distinct allows the mathematics to determine later whether they should be combined and how.


8. LUSHNESS AND ENTROPY

Entropy measures important physical structure but is not identical to Lushness.

Thermodynamic entropy can be high in a gas whose microscopic variation has little long-range consequential differentiation.

Shannon entropy can be high in IID noise that contains no predictive memory.

Von Neumann entropy can be high in a mixed quantum state without implying rich agent-relative continuation.

Entropy may correlate with Lushness in some systems, especially when it reflects genuinely occupied independent consequence directions.

It is not the definition.


9. LUSHNESS AND COMPLEXITY

Algorithmic or computational complexity is also distinct.

A random string can have high Kolmogorov complexity while supporting little reusable structure.

A simple generative rule can produce a rich hierarchy of persistent structures.

A very complicated microstate can rapidly thermalize.

Therefore description length and consequence richness must be treated separately.

Complexity measures may become useful predictors or components in specific regimes, but no generic complexity measure is assumed to equal Lushness.


10. LUSHNESS AND CHAOS

Sensitive dependence can amplify tiny differences.

This makes chaos relevant to consequence.

But chaos has at least two regimes.

In one, initial differences rapidly become macroscopic but then mix into a common statistical regime. Much of the microdetail becomes unrecoverable and structurally redundant.

In another, amplified differences are captured by stable records, control structures, ecological niches, or other persistent organizations and continue to shape distinct futures.

Lushness should distinguish these cases.

Chaos contributes when it produces nonredundant consequence, not merely exponential separation in coordinates.


11. PERSISTENCE

A consequential direction that disappears immediately and one that survives for centuries are structurally different.

Lushness may therefore depend on persistence across continuation horizon.

A horizon profile can track:

- emergence of new consequence dimensions;
- survival of distinctions;
- recombination;
- erasure;
- long-lived memory;
- stability of generated structure.

Persistence need not be an independent scalar term. It can be represented by how the consequence geometry changes with H.


12. GENERATIVITY

Generativity is a particularly important candidate feature.

A generative structure does more than occupy existing possibilities.

It reaches states from which new consequential distinctions become accessible.

Examples include:

- tool construction;
- learning;
- biological reproduction;
- programmable computation;
- scientific discovery;
- institution formation;
- ecological niche construction.

In a completed physical description, these possibilities are not created from nothing.

Rather, a generative transition makes a region of continuation reachable from the situated condition.

The relevant structure is therefore nested:

    present state
        -> generative transition
        -> new state
        -> enlarged or reorganized conditional continuation.

Lushness may need to register this recursively organized accessibility.


13. CONSEQUENCE CAPACITY VERSUS OCCUPIED LUSHNESS

A system may have large capacity to generate differentiated futures while currently occupying a narrow state.

A programmable computer provides a simple example.

Its present register state is one point, but its architecture supports many possible computations and transformations.

This suggests distinguishing:

    consequence capacity

from

    realized or occupied Lushness.

Capacity concerns the dimensionality and richness of continuation that can become active.

Occupied Lushness concerns the structure actually instantiated or weighted in the present possibility field.


14. RESOURCE CONSTRAINTS

Lushness is physically constrained.

Relevant resources include:

- free energy;
- matter;
- spatial extent;
- time;
- memory;
- control bandwidth;
- computation;
- communication;
- low-entropy reservoirs;
- stable environmental structure.

Resources do not automatically count as Lushness.

They matter because they determine which consequential structures can be maintained, transformed, and reached.

A reservoir of free energy is therefore better viewed as enabling possibility than as itself constituting rich possibility.


15. SELF-MAINTAINING STRUCTURES

Persistent organized systems can concentrate and redirect consequence.

An organism, institution, machine, or ecosystem can:

- preserve internal variables;
- repair damage;
- exploit environmental gradients;
- accumulate memory;
- condition future behavior;
- create tools;
- coordinate subsystems;
- alter its environment.

Such systems may therefore reshape consequence geometry strongly.

This is one reason Lushness later connects naturally to agency and valuers.

The physical definition remains prior to those normative categories.


16. DUPLICATION

Copying the same consequential structure many times should not automatically multiply Lushness linearly.

The correct behavior depends on whether the copies create independent continuation.

A million identical isolated copies may add physical support and perhaps weight, but much of their structure is redundant.

If the copies interact, diversify, learn independently, or occupy different environments, they may generate new consequence directions.

Thus duplication is a useful test of whether a candidate measure distinguishes multiplicity from differentiation.


17. RANDOM PERMANENT MEMORY

A difficult adversarial example is a system that permanently stores random bits.

It has:

- persistence;
- many distinguishable states;
- potentially high effective dimension.

But it may lack organized generativity or control.

A Lushness theory should not prejudge the answer.

If those random distinctions genuinely remain physically consequential, they should count in the geometry.

The comparison with structured memory then tests whether additional relational, compositional, or generative organization matters beyond persistent distinguishability alone.


18. GAS AND TURBULENCE

Gas and turbulence are important because they prevent the theory from equating organization with consequence by intuition.

A gas can contain:

- enormous state-space volume;
- chaotic dynamics;
- long-range hydrodynamic modes;
- fluctuations;
- transport;
- phase transitions;
- rare amplification events.

Some of this structure may be genuinely consequential.

A valid formalism must count whatever survives consequence-preserving compression.

The question is empirical and mathematical:

    how much nonredundant continuation remains after redundant microvariation is removed?


19. QUANTUM LUSHNESS

At quantum scale, Lushness cannot be branch count.

Coherent alternatives can interfere.

The physically relevant structure may include amplitude geometry before decoherence and probability geometry afterward.

A quantum Lushness construction must therefore handle:

- superposition;
- phase;
- entanglement;
- interference;
- decoherence;
- records;
- process memory;
- Born weighting.

The classical picture of a tree of exclusive futures is at most an emergent approximation.


20. MULTISCALE LUSHNESS

A system can look lush at one scale and simple at another.

At microscopic scale, a biological cell contains huge molecular variation.

At cellular scale, it may occupy a structured regulatory state space.

At organism scale, it supports perception, action, memory, and learning.

At ecosystem scale, it participates in larger networks.

The program therefore expects Lushness to be scale-sensitive while remaining grounded in the same physical structure.

A multiscale description may be more informative than a single absolute value.


21. LUSHNESS AND VALUE

Lushness is not itself moral value.

It describes structured consequential possibility.

The later valuer layer asks how systems with endogenous stakes relate to that possibility.

A world can be physically lush while containing severe conflict or suffering.

A low-Lushness region can contain something of great importance to a particular valuer.

Normative conclusions therefore require additional structure beyond the physical geometry.


22. CURRENT MATHEMATICAL CANDIDATES

Several mathematical summaries remain useful.

Metric entropy:

    log N(epsilon)

for covering the consequence space at resolution epsilon.

Log determinant:

    log det(I + beta Sigma)

in finite approximately linear regimes.

Effective dimension:

    rank-like, spectral, manifold, or information-theoretic measures.

Persistent profiles:

    change of consequence geometry across horizon.

Weighted geometry:

    integration against Born or other physically supplied measures.

Generative structure:

    growth or reorganization of accessible consequence under conditioning and action.

These may turn out to be complementary views of one richer object rather than competitors for one universal scalar.


23. WHAT SUCCESS WOULD LOOK LIKE

A mature Lushness construction would do several things simultaneously.

It would:

- arise from physical consequence structure;
- discount redundant microvariation;
- register genuine independent continuation;
- handle quantum coherence correctly;
- behave sensibly under physical coarse-graining;
- support multiscale analysis;
- remain invariant under irrelevant re-description;
- distinguish support from physical weighting where needed;
- represent persistence and generativity;
- survive adversarial examples.

The next document specifies the stress-test program used to evaluate candidate constructions.
