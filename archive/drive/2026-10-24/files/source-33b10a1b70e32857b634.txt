Omega Cosmology — start here

A standalone, multipart primer for the research program.

The central question

How do physical situations support differentiated continuation, and how do agents alter the conditions under which further valuation becomes possible?

The packet develops the answer from quantum processes and physical perspectives through consequence, lushness, identity, recovery, agency, Gradient Ethics, and Omega Decision Theory. Awareness and the Alpha–Omega metaphysical horizon receive dedicated treatments.

What is included

- 18 numbered parts, approximately 22 thousand words in total.
- Matching Markdown and UTF-8 plain-text editions, with the same substantive content.
- 10 worked examples and a formal core with explicit assumptions and derivations.
- A glossary, research program, and 36 primary references with relevance notes.
- Complete single-file editions for continuous reading or model ingestion.
- Two runnable research archives covering covariance, calibration, rare alternatives, and a reversible continuation dependency test.
- An unrestricted lushness research-branch note and a research index, in Markdown and plain text.

Reading paths

For the whole program, read the numbered parts in order. For the physical core, read 00–05, then 13–14. For ethics and alignment, read 00, 05, and 07–10, using the physical parts as foundations. For awareness and metaphysics, read 00–03 and 11–12.

For a model or local research assistant, ingest the complete Markdown edition (OMEGA_COSMOLOGY_COMPLETE.md) or the complete plain-text edition (OMEGA_COSMOLOGY_COMPLETE.txt). Part 16 specifies research dependencies and working discipline. Preserve the difference between definitions, hypotheses, explicit results, and normative commitments when extending the work.

For the finite evidence and next experiments, read the research index (research/Omega_Research_Index.txt). The covariance summary loses a dependency that a permitted continuation makes usable; the full continuation model retains it. Richer response profiles detect the change, while their interpretation as global lushness remains the research question.
