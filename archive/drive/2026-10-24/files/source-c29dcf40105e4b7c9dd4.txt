# Volume IV — Theological Reconstruction

## Purpose and claim discipline

This volume gives the current state of the theological branch of the project. Its main provenance is *Perspective and Divine Completion: Logos, Creation, and the Limits of Formal Reality*, Parts I–III. It also incorporates the Omega repository's earlier formal laboratory work, the post-repository *Alpha–Omega Framework v6* synthesis, and the subsequent conversation about consciousness, valuers, irreversibility, terminal solitude, and control asymmetry.

No source archive is required to understand this volume. Every symbol, inherited doctrine, project-specific reinterpretation, and material limitation needed below is stated here. References to “the paper,” “the repository,” or “v6” indicate chronology and provenance only; they are not invitations to recover missing premises elsewhere. In particular, v6 was written after the repository work as a synthesis and audit of the larger program. It is not itself an implemented repository specification, and its theoretical summaries must not be mistaken for verified code-level results.

The branch is a reconstruction, not a demonstrated theology. It asks how classical theological predicates look when they are interpreted from inside a completed, perspectivally encountered reality. It does not prove that manifest reality is a conscious personal God, derive Christian doctrine, solve the hard problem of consciousness, or close the is–ought gap.

Three kinds of statement must remain separate throughout:

1. **Architectural claims** define the framework and its distinctions.
2. **Reconstructive claims** show how inherited theological language can be mapped into that architecture.
3. **Speculative extensions** test possible bridges to consciousness, agency, normativity, and doctrinal theology.

Calling a proposal “reconstructive” or “speculative” is part of its content. It must not be promoted into a result merely because the same vocabulary is used repeatedly.

## 1. The original cosmological intuition

The theological branch was conceived against an atemporal block-Everett picture from the beginning. The originating image was a cross between Einstein and Everett: a completed universal wavefunction, or block multiverse, in which the entire branching structure is present as one completed formal actuality.

This is not a later reinterpretation imposed on a theology first developed in another setting. The familiar moves of the branch—completed actuality, perspectival process, branch-relative lived trajectory, non-successive divine knowledge, and the distinction between formal completion and phenomenal actuality—were generated inside that picture.

The basic intuition is:

\[
\text{the whole branching structure is complete,}
\]

while:

\[
\text{a bounded creature encounters an oriented trajectory from within.}
\]

Completion therefore does not erase process. It relocates process. From within a creaturely perspective, states are remembered, present, and anticipated; choices and consequences occur in succession. From the standpoint of the completed block, the oriented relations among those states are already part of the whole.

The architecture deliberately preserves some ontological neutrality that the founding intuition does not. It allows both:

\[
A_S^O=E_S,
\]

where all admissible branches for a creature are ontologically instantiated, and:

\[
A_S^O\subset E_S,
\]

where only some formally admissible trajectories are instantiated. The first fits the block-Everett source intuition most directly. The second remains available so that the architecture does not depend on proving Everettian ontology.

“Completed universal wavefunction” should accordingly be treated as a generative model, not as a settled claim about quantum mechanics. It is closest to the completed formal projection of reality. It is not automatically identical with total reality, because the framework leaves open whether phenomenality is exhausted by formal description.

## 2. The full architecture

The framework's compact architecture is:

\[
\boxed{
G^{*}
\;\xrightarrow{\mathcal L_{\alpha}}\;
D_{\alpha}
\;\xrightarrow{P_S}\;
(C_S,I_S)
\;\xleftrightarrow{\kappa_S}\;
\Phi_S
\;\xrightarrow{\sigma_S}\;
\gamma_S^{\Phi}
}
\]

The terms are typed as follows:

- \(G^*\) is God-as-such, named apophatically rather than positively described as a formal object.
- \(\mathcal L_\alpha\) is logotic articulation under grammar \(\alpha\).
- \(D_\alpha\) is divinity as manifest under that grammar.
- \(P_S\) is a creaturely perspective operating within manifest actuality.
- \(C_S\) is the finite embodied creation constituted through that perspective.
- \(I_S\) is the strong identity or soul invariant of that creation.
- \(\kappa_S\) is the unknown relation between formal perspectival organization and phenomenality.
- \(\Phi_S\) is phenomenal awareness or phenomenal presence associated with the subject.
- \(\sigma_S\) is the speculative steering relation by which phenomenal directedness may help determine the lived continuation.
- \(\gamma_S^\Phi\) is the trajectory lived as actual from that subject's perspective.

Two further maps are essential:

\[
F_{\mathrm{DRO}}
=
\mathfrak F(D_{\mathrm{DRO}}),
\]

the maximal formal description or projection of manifest actuality, and:

\[
M_S
=
\operatorname{Apprehend}(D_\alpha;P_S),
\]

manifest divinity as positively available to a finite creature.

The architecture is therefore not simply “God equals the universe.” It distinguishes:

1. the apophatically indicated source;
2. source as manifest under a determinate grammar;
3. the formal projection of that manifestation;
4. a finite creature within it; and
5. the creature's limited positive apprehension of divinity.

Those distinctions carry most of the branch's conceptual load.

## 3. Awareness, formal reality, and total reality

The epistemic starting point is deliberately minimal:

\[
\Phi_0=\text{awareness occurs}.
\]

This does not initially establish a substantial self, will, memory, or external world. It establishes that phenomenal occurrence is actual. The subject must be reconstructed, if possible, from the organization through which awareness becomes determinate.

The framework distinguishes the epistemic order:

\[
\Phi_0\Rightarrow B\Rightarrow
\text{investigation of the conditions of determinate awareness},
\]

from the proposed ontological order:

\[
B\rightarrow D\rightarrow R\rightarrow O\rightarrow P^F\dashrightarrow\Phi.
\]

Here \(B\) is manifest actuality, \(D\) distinctions, \(R\) relations, \(O\) orientation, and \(P^F\) a formally constituted perspective. The final arrow is dashed because formal perspective has not been shown to entail awareness.

The metaphysical bounds are:

\[
\Omega_{\min},
\]

the minimum conditions an adequate ontology must satisfy to contain awareness-bearing perspectives;

\[
\Omega_{\max}^{F},
\]

a maximally expressive formal domain; and:

\[
\Omega^{R},
\]

total reality, whatever its final constitution.

The framework leaves open:

\[
\Omega_{\max}^{F}=\Omega^R
\]

under formal closure, or a formal-plus account in which phenomenal reality or psychophysical law is not exhausted by formal description.

This neutrality matters for the theology. A completed formal “God-object” is not yet demonstrably a conscious God. Conversely, admitting an explanatory gap does not by itself establish substance dualism. Identity theory, emergence, dual-aspect theory, neutral monism, panpsychism, idealism, and fundamental psychophysical law remain open at this level.

The symbols \(\Omega_{\min}\), \(\Omega_{\max}^{F}\), and \(\Omega^R\) predate or sit apart from the later use of **Omega** for valuer compatibility geometry. They must not be read as the same object.

## 4. God-as-such and logotic articulation

### 4.1 God-as-such: \(G^*\)

\(G^*\) is not a positive model of a hidden entity behind the universe. It is the name assigned to the divine source while withholding the presumption that the categories of manifest reality exhaust it.

At the point where \(G^*\) is introduced, the framework does not claim that it:

- is beyond being;
- is an undifferentiated substance;
- is conscious or personal;
- is one rather than many in an ordinary numerical sense;
- temporally precedes manifestation;
- occupies another realm; or
- is already known to be the Thomistic *ipsum esse subsistens*.

The minimal claim is epistemic:

\[
\text{no finite manifestation can certify that its positive grammar exhausts }G^*.
\]

The framework remains open between a more Dionysian reading, on which the source exceeds the being/non-being contrast as manifest to us, and a more Thomistic reading, on which the source is unlimited subsistent actuality. It does not settle that dispute by notation.

### 4.2 Logos and the DRO grammar

The technical term **logotic articulation** names the non-temporal relation under which the source becomes manifest through a determinate grammar:

\[
D_\alpha
=
\operatorname{Manifest}_\alpha
(G^*;\mathcal L_\alpha).
\]

For our manifest reality, the grammar is provisionally represented as:

\[
\alpha=\mathrm{DRO},
\]

where DRO means distinction, relation, and orientation.

Distinction permits determinate articulation. Relation permits structure. Orientation gives the structure a directed grain relative to a locus: accessible and inaccessible, toward and away, predecessor and successor, pastward and futureward, or some more general asymmetry.

The Logos is called a **world-generating perspective**, but “perspective” here is not psychological. \(\mathcal L_\alpha\) is the determinative boundary at which formal description first becomes applicable. It does not describe God first existing without a world, choosing rules, and later entering a changed state. Time, alternatives, transition, and deliberation are themselves downstream categories.

Here **Logos** is deliberately technical and non-Trinitarian at base:

\[
\mathcal L_\alpha\neq\Lambda,
\]

where \(\Lambda\) denotes the personal Logos or Son in classical Christian theology. A later relation between them is an optional theological extension, not an established identity.

### 4.3 Manifest divinity: \(D_\alpha\)

\(D_\alpha\) is divine actuality as manifest under grammar \(\alpha\). For the working grammar:

\[
G^*
\xRightarrow{\mathcal L_{\mathrm{DRO}}}
D_{\mathrm{DRO}}
\xrightarrow{\mathfrak F}
F_{\mathrm{DRO}}.
\]

The first relation is manifestation; the second is formal description. Their separation prevents the formal projection from being silently equated with all manifested reality before consciousness has been explained.

Nothing in the core architecture establishes that \(D_\alpha\) is:

- one phenomenal subject;
- a personal agent;
- intentionally chosen by \(G^*\);
- morally perfect;
- contingent rather than necessary; or
- exhaustive of the source.

It is therefore possible, within these terms, for \(D_\alpha\) to function as structurally manifest divinity without being a conscious God.

### 4.4 Perfect and non-exhaustive manifestation

Two possibilities remain open:

\[
D_\alpha\equiv_{\mathrm{manifest}}G^*,
\]

where the boundary manifests the source perfectly, and:

\[
D_\alpha\neq_{\mathrm{exhaustive}}G^*,
\]

where it manifests the source truly but incompletely.

Perspectival imagery makes non-exhaustion intuitive, because a perspective normally reveals through limitation. That intuition is not a proof. A boundary may fit what it bounds perfectly, and ordinary part–whole relations may not apply to \(G^*\).

### 4.5 The two apophatic veils

The first veil lies between \(G^*\) and \(D_\alpha\). Whether manifestation is exhaustive remains metaphysically unresolved.

The second lies between \(D_\alpha\) and \(M_S\). Here limitation follows from creaturely finitude: a bounded perspective cannot apprehend the completed whole in the same manner in which the whole is complete.

Positive theology primarily describes \(M_S\), while trying to speak truthfully about \(D_\alpha\) and only more tentatively about \(G^*\). The project therefore treats apophatic restraint as an internal typing rule, not merely as a devotional qualification.

The later consciousness discussion also used “the apophatic veil” for a possible formal/phenomenal or formal/Being boundary. That conversational use has not yet been reconciled with the two veils above. In particular, **Being-as-such** has not been identified formally with \(G^*\), \(D_\alpha\), or \(\Omega^R\).

## 5. Perspective as creation

### 5.1 Creaturely perspective

Within manifest actuality, a creaturely perspective operates as:

\[
D_\alpha\xrightarrow{P_S}C_S.
\]

A formal perspective is represented schematically by:

\[
P_S^F=(\sim_S,O_S,I_S),
\]

where \(\sim_S\) identifies distinctions that are equivalent, continuous, or internal for that perspective, \(O_S\) supplies orientation, and \(I_S\) supplies an identity-conferring locus or invariant.

Perspective is more than a camera aimed at a pre-existing object. The relevant boundary helps determine which relations count as internal to the creature, which organization counts as its body, and which states belong to its trajectory.

### 5.2 Boundary and non-excisive creation

The creature is written:

\[
C_S=\operatorname{Bound}(D_\alpha;P_S).
\]

The boundary establishes an internal/external distinction relative to \(S\), but it does not sever the creature from the world. Environmental relations and dependencies remain partly constitutive of it.

Creation through this boundary is **non-excisive**:

\[
\operatorname{Bound}(D_\alpha;P_S)
\not\Rightarrow
D_\alpha-C_S.
\]

Nothing is cut out of a divine material. The creature is a real bounded organization within manifest actuality, not a detached fragment of God-as-such and not an aggregate assembled from independently complete parts.

This is not yet creation *ex nihilo* in the Thomistic sense. It is a reconstruction of finite individuation through perspectival determination. The two should not be made equivalent merely because both are called creation.

Not every arbitrary boundary creates a creature. A constitutive boundary must correspond to a sufficiently stable, integrated, world-supported organization. Relational closure, persistence, regulated exchange, and internal integration are candidate features, not a final criterion.

### 5.3 Weak identity, strong identity, and soul

**Weak identity** is identity under a description or concept. It can pick out a ship, organism, nation, or person without settling whether one strongly unified bearer persists.

**Strong identity** is the world-supported relation under which distinct states belong to one creature:

\[
x_n\approx_S x_m.
\]

The corresponding completed trajectory is:

\[
\Gamma_S
=
\operatorname{Comp}_{\approx_S}(x_0).
\]

The formalism specifies what strong identity must accomplish without deciding whether its metaphysical ground is substance, biological continuity, causal organization, form, process, or some deeper bearer.

Perspective and identity are mutually constraining. The boundary determines which relations count as internal, while the persistence relation distinguishes a genuine creaturely perspective from an arbitrary collection. Their approximate fixed point is the initial structural meaning of **soul**:

\[
\boxed{
\text{soul}
=
\text{the inward identity of an embodied perspectival creation}
}
\]

The soul invariant is deliberately thinner than an immaterial substance. It does not by definition imply consciousness, agency, immortality, postmortem survival, or independence from embodiment. A formally perfect zombie organism could possess strong structural identity without phenomenal subjecthood.

The body is the outward organization of the creature in manifest actuality; the soul is the inward continuity by which changing embodied states belong to one creature. This is adjacent to hylomorphism but does not derive the subsistence of an intellectual soul.

In the notation used later, \(B_S(n)\) is the embodied organization of \(S\) at section \(n\). A creaturely section may be represented as \(C_S(n)=\langle B_S(n),I_S,O_S\rangle\): embodied organization, identity invariant, and orientation taken together.

### 5.4 Completed essence and modes of actuality

The **completed essence** of \(S\) is the full space of coherent trajectories belonging to that identity:

\[
E_S
=
\{\gamma\mid \gamma
\text{ is a coherent trajectory of }S
\text{ within }D_\alpha\}.
\]

This is a project-specific reconstruction of essence. It is not simply a verbal definition or the standard Thomistic account of whatness.

The framework then separates:

\[
A_S^O
=
\operatorname{Inst}(E_S,D_\alpha),
\]

the portion of the essence ontologically instantiated in manifest actuality, from:

\[
A_S^\Phi
:=
\gamma_S^\Phi,
\qquad
\gamma_S^\Phi\in A_S^O,
\]

the trajectory lived as actual by a conscious subject. Since \(A_S^O\) is a space of instantiated trajectories, the lived trajectory is typed as an element of that space, not as another trajectory-set.

In the Everettian source model, this is an indexical distinction. The whole branch structure may be actual while a subject experiences one oriented continuation as “mine” or “now.” Phenomenal actuality does not make unexperienced branches nonexistent, and phenomenal steering does not automatically collapse or delete them.

The word **existence** is therefore reconstructed in this part of the project. “Phenomenal existence” means first-person actuality, not the Thomistic act of *esse*.

### 5.5 Imago Dei

The nested structure:

\[
G^*\xrightarrow{\mathcal L_\alpha}D_\alpha
\xrightarrow{P_S}C_S
\]

supports a structural reading of *imago Dei*. The similarity is not that a creature possesses miniature copies of all divine faculties. It is that unity becomes manifest through determinative limitation at both levels:

\[
\text{boundary}\rightarrow\text{manifestation}\rightarrow\text{identity}.
\]

This is an analogy, not an identity. It neither proves that \(G^*\) has a creaturely mind nor makes the creature a fragment of divinity.

## 6. Completion and the reconstruction of divine attributes

### 6.1 Completion is not universal actualization

Manifest divinity is represented as completed actuality under its grammar:

\[
D_\alpha
=
\operatorname{Complete}(\mathrm{DRO}_\alpha).
\]

An internal sequence can be real without requiring the completed whole to acquire its stages one by one:

\[
\text{change within }D_\alpha
\not\Rightarrow
\text{change of }D_\alpha\text{ as completed whole}.
\]

Completion is not the actualization of every logically describable possibility. A contradiction is not an unrealized creation, and a larger branch count does not by itself make a world more complete in the theological sense.

### 6.2 Pure act

The project reconstructs pure actuality narrowly as:

\[
\boxed{
\text{completion-pure-act}
=
\text{the absence of an unrealized structural remainder in manifest actuality}
}
\]

This captures the claim that manifest divinity is not progressing toward fulfillment. It does **not** derive the full Thomistic doctrine of *actus purus*, which denies passive potency in a stronger metaphysical sense. Modal plenitude, even an Everettian plenitude of branches, is not identical with pure actuality.

At the manifest level:

\[
\operatorname{Ess}(D_\alpha)
=
\operatorname{Act}(D_\alpha),
\]

means that manifest divinity does not possess a completed form awaiting some external act of realization. It does not prove that \(G^*\) is subsistent being itself.

### 6.3 Perspectival decomposition and simplicity

Finite creatures distinguish being, knowing, willing, acting, and achieving because these are separated across incomplete access to their own trajectories. The project asks whether divine attributes are the finite decompositions of one completed actuality:

\[
K=\pi_K(D_\alpha),\qquad
W=\pi_W(D_\alpha),\qquad
P=\pi_P(D_\alpha),\qquad
A=\pi_A(D_\alpha).
\]

The projections differ because they answer different creaturely questions. Their source is one manifest actuality.

This yields **articulation without assembly**. Internal distinctions can be articulations of a whole rather than independently prior components combined to make it. The result is a limited reconstruction of divine simplicity:

- manifest divinity is not assembled from prior complete parts;
- its positive attributes share one completed source;
- it does not develop by adding faculties or actualities.

This is weaker than strict Thomistic simplicity. Non-assembly alone does not establish the absence of every real distinction or every form of metaphysical composition.

### 6.4 Divine awareness and knowledge

The occurrence of local awareness shows only that manifest or total reality supports phenomenality. It does not establish that every part of \(D_\alpha\) is conscious, that \(D_\alpha\) is one subject, or that awareness can be predicated of \(G^*\).

A world-level conscious divinity would require a further relation:

\[
\kappa_D:D_\alpha\leftrightarrow\Phi_D.
\]

If such awareness obtains, it cannot be an acquired transition without contradicting completion. Divine awareness is therefore reconstructed conditionally as **non-successive self-presence**, not infinitely fast reasoning or endless recursive self-modeling.

Omniscience is reconstructed as:

\[
\boxed{
\text{omniscience}
=
\text{completed manifest actuality interpreted under the creaturely category of knowledge}
}
\]

No fact internal to the completed manifestation remains to be discovered, inferred, remembered, or forecast. This establishes neither phenomenally conscious knowing nor exhaustive knowledge of \(G^*\) if the logotic manifestation is non-exhaustive.

### 6.5 Power, action, and omnipotence

Finite power bridges a difference between present actuality and possible result. At the completed level, the framework reconstructs:

\[
\boxed{
\text{manifest divine power}
=
\text{the complete efficacy of manifest actuality}
}
\]

and:

\[
\boxed{
\text{manifest divine action}
=
\text{that efficacy interpreted through the finite separation of agency from result}
}
\]

This is the disciplined meaning of “completed action.” It is not perpetual activity and does not mean that every abstract possibility is actual.

Incoherent pseudo-possibilities do not fall within power:

\[
\neg\operatorname{Coherent}(x)
\Rightarrow
x\notin\operatorname{Poss}_\alpha.
\]

The reconstruction recovers complete efficacy within manifest actuality. It does not establish the full modal reach of divine omnipotence beyond the actual manifestation.

### 6.6 Providence

Providence is not modeled as a divine observer forecasting events and later intervening. For a creature with completed trajectory \(\Gamma_S\):

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Prov}(S)
=
\operatorname{Order}(\Gamma_S,D_\alpha)
}
\]

From within the creaturely perspective, consequences arrive successively. In the completed whole, the relation between acts, other causes, and consequences belongs to the total order.

This need not eliminate creaturely causation. If phenomenal steering is efficacious, that efficacy is one of the causal relations contained in the completed whole. Completion does not replace secondary causality; it includes whatever secondary causality actually obtains.

The reconstruction does not show that the total order is benevolently intended, morally proportionate, or consciously supervised.

### 6.7 Structural divine will

The framework defines a candidate:

\[
W_D=\operatorname{Ord}(D_\alpha),
\]

the non-successive ordering of manifest actuality.

For this to count as a candidate for divine will rather than a relabeling of structure, the order would need to be:

- intrinsic rather than externally imposed;
- efficacious;
- consequence-sensitive to creaturely causation; and
- compatible with worlds or branches lacking particular creatures.

This motivates the conjectural reconstruction:

\[
\boxed{
\text{structural divine will}
=
\text{the intrinsic, efficacious, consequence-sensitive ordering of manifest actuality}
}
\]

Three gaps remain decisive:

1. efficacy does not establish intentionality;
2. structural variation does not establish that total manifestation is freely contingent; and
3. consequence does not establish moral fittingness.

Structural divine will is therefore an explicit conjecture, not a completed derivation.

### 6.8 Apophatic aseity and necessity

The Logos move treats \(G^*\) as source rather than as one caused object inside the causal grammar of \(D_\alpha\). This limited result is called **apophatic aseity**.

It does not prove:

\[
\neg\Diamond(\neg G^*).
\]

Modal necessary existence remains unestablished. The narrower claim is that ordinary causal regress cannot simply be projected beyond the boundary at which causal and modal grammar first becomes applicable.

## 7. Phenomenal coupling and steering

The hard problem enters the architecture at a precise point. A structural perspective, identity invariant, body, memory, self-model, and behavioral repertoire can all be formally described without transparently entailing that anything is lived.

The missing relation is marked as:

\[
\kappa_S:
(P_S^F,I_S,B_S)
\leftrightarrow
\Phi_S.
\]

The notation does not assume two separately complete substances. It leaves open identity, emergence, dual aspect, fundamental psychophysical law, panpsychist extension, and other metaphysical interpretations.

The architecture distinguishes three layers of identity:

1. **structural strong identity**, which does not require phenomenality;
2. **phenomenal subject identity**, in which states are given to one field or locus of experience; and
3. **agentive identity**, in which the phenomenal subject participates in directed selection under the steering model.

Let \(W_S(n)\) denote the will or directedness of the presently aware subject, and let \(\operatorname{Succ}_{E_S}(x_n)\) denote the admissible successors of section \(x_n\) within completed essence \(E_S\). Then:

\[
\sigma_S(W_S(n),x_n)
=
x_{n+1}^{\Phi}
\in
\operatorname{Succ}_{E_S}(x_n).
\]

The claim is not that will creates a formally unavailable branch. Under its strong reading, it says that phenomenal directedness helps determine which admissible continuation becomes lived actuality. Under weaker readings, \(\sigma_S\) may be a compatibilist description or a correspondence between phenomenal and formal aspects. Only the strong interactionist reading requires psychophysical influence not exhausted by the prior formal state.

No reading is established. Branching physics by itself supplies neither awareness nor agency.

The symbol \(\kappa\) has a serious cross-project collision: in the Omega work, \(\kappa_F(\mathcal E)\) is the covering number measuring how many policies are needed to secure continuation family \(F\) across a declared context family \(\mathcal E\). That \(\kappa\) is unrelated to phenomenal coupling.

## 8. The vortical consciousness extension

The later conversation explored a vortical image of identity and consciousness. Its defensible structural core is narrower than its metaphysical imagery.

A vortical process is a cross-boundary absorb–transform–emit loop whose throughput helps regenerate the organization that channels it. Identity persists through recirculation and reconstruction rather than through the numerical persistence of every constituent. This is naturally compared with the soul invariant: both describe unity maintained across changing embodiment.

The project's formal repository adopts the following explicit guardrail, restated here in full:

- loop closure is not agency;
- loop closure is not selfhood;
- loop closure is not consciousness;
- loop closure is not valuerhood;
- loop closure is not moral standing; and
- loop closure is not value.

“Vortical” is therefore an alias for a family of closed-loop deformer, cross-boundary circulation, reflexive-maintenance, and recovery profiles. It is not a result about consciousness.

The recent consciousness vocabulary further distinguished:

- the **wake**, meaning the formal/internal feedback dynamics produced as signals pass through the vortical subject;
- **awareness**, tentatively imagined as the transformation or bridge by which wake becomes phenomenal; and
- **qualia**, the phenomenal character once that transformation occurs.

An earlier phrase treating the wake itself as qualia, including “qualia without awareness,” was explicitly corrected. It should not be retained as the current view.

The black-hole/white-hole/wormhole imagery remains quarantined speculation:

- the black-hole aspect is identity viewed externally through uptake, compression, and futures-field deformation;
- the white-hole aspect is identity from within, phenomenal manifestation, or emission;
- an event horizon figures the privacy or projection boundary of first-person access;
- a bidirectional wormhole was imagined as a trans-formal coupling between formal possibility and Being-as-such.

The image does not hold literally under known general relativity. A true event horizon is one-way; the white-hole region of the eternal Schwarzschild solution is not the output side of an ordinary collapsed black hole; standard Einstein–Rosen bridges are non-traversable; and no established physics connects a wormhole to a domain beyond formal possibility. If no information crosses the proposed relation, “bidirectional” can mean at most gluing, common constraint, or co-determination rather than two-way causal signaling.

The hypothesis therefore relocates the hard problem behind an apophatic boundary; it does not solve it. The relation of the conversational **Being-as-such** to \(G^*\) and \(\Omega^R\) is also unresolved.

Panpsychism remains a live hypothesis. The possibility that unified subject-level consciousness emerges only in systems already exhibiting valuerhood does not by itself refute microphenomenal or panpsychist accounts, but neither is that emergence thesis established. The project cannot use the absence of a settled consciousness criterion to declare rocks definitely nonphenomenal.

The most promising current physical hook is irreversibility. Negentropic consumption is a marker of irreversible process, especially where a bounded process recruits throughput to preserve or reconstruct its continuation. It is not sufficient for valuerhood or awareness: flames, hurricanes, stars, passive dissipaters, and many control systems also consume gradients. No consciousness theorem follows from dissipation.

## 9. Formal completion is not goodness

The normative branch begins with a negative result:

\[
\text{formal completion}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{goodness}.
\]

A completed history of domination is as structurally complete as a completed history of communion. A completed hell is no less formally determinate than a completed heaven. Neither the block universe nor total consequence by itself supplies moral sign.

The theological reconstruction initially says normativity enters with plural agency. The post-repository v6 synthesis sharpens the relevant substrate: futures matter as value only relative to **valuers**. A valuer is not yet cleanly demarcated, and it is not definitionally a conscious subject, full agent, or moral patient. The current constraint is:

\[
\text{no valuers}\Rightarrow\text{no value}.
\]

Accordingly, raw deformation of a futures field is not automatically morally relevant. The relevant question is how transformations affect valuer-bearing continuation, especially through nonrecoverable loss. Raw entropy, branch count, novelty, or complexity does not constitute lushness.

The v6 synthesis states the later definition as:

> **Lushness is open-ended compatible differentiation in the valuer-bearing continuation structure.**

It is intended to exclude duplicate branch inflation, random noise, capture disguised as plurality, and raw state-count maximization. The associated normative claim—that preserving or increasing such a substrate is better—remains a declared commitment rather than a proved theorem.

This post-repository valuer-centered account should not be silently merged with the theological text's language of plural conscious agents. **Valuer**, **agent**, **conscious subject**, and **moral patient** remain distinct types.

Consciousness may turn out to be a mode in which a valuer's stakes become phenomenally manifest. That would distinguish conscious valuers without yet proving that consciousness confers categorical priority over every nonconscious valuer or value-supporting structure.

Valence does not supply the missing bridge. Suffering is not always bad and pleasure is not always good. Their moral significance depends on their place in the structure of identity, action, relation, and the valuer-indexed futures field. No hedonistic axiom is part of the current framework.

## 10. Communion, virtue, love, and justice

The theological reconstruction introduces **compositional compatibility**:

\[
\Gamma_i\bowtie\Gamma_j,
\]

meaning that trajectories can participate in a larger coherent relation without one identity being erased or reduced to an instrument of the other.

The intended constraints include agency preservation, reciprocity, non-domination, truthful coordination, repair, and shared participation without absorption. The relation is not presented as one scalar to maximize.

On that basis, it offers provisional structural analogues:

\[
\text{virtue}
=
\text{dispositions tending toward sustainable communion among distinct agents},
\]

\[
\text{love}
=
\text{communication of being and good that preserves and enlarges distinct agency},
\]

and:

\[
\text{justice}
=
\text{proportionate ordering of perspectives toward mutually sustainable participation}.
\]

These are interpretations of structural role, not reductive definitions of moral reality. They remain conditional on treating continued agency, shared reality, and communion as goods.

The gap is explicit:

\[
\operatorname{Value}(\text{communion})
\Rightarrow
\operatorname{Reason}(\text{virtue}),
\]

but the framework has not shown that every rational or powerful valuer categorically ought to value communion. A valuer can reject it. What that valuer cannot do is reject communion while retaining all of communion's relational goods without contradiction.

The repository work and its post-repository v6 synthesis make the gap sharper rather than removing it. Compatibility, recoverability, lushness, and terminal solitude can expose what a policy destroys. They do not make a genuinely orthogonal controller care. Control adequacy does not generate moral authority.

## 11. Karma, judgment, heaven, and hell

### 11.1 Karma

Under the phenomenal-steering conjecture, an act changes the creature's relation to other perspectives. Write its propagated consequences as:

\[
\mathcal K_S(x_n,\sigma_S).
\]

**Karma** is then used analogically for:

\[
\boxed{
\text{the structural propagation of the consequences of phenomenal steering}
}
\]

This is not an identification with any specific Buddhist, Hindu, or Jain doctrine. It also does not mean that consequences are fair, proportionate, educative, or inescapably corrective.

Power asymmetry exposes the limitation. A dominant controller may redirect or externalize consequences, eliminate those who could answer it, or nonrecoverably deform the shared field. Structural consequence propagation still occurs, but it no longer functions as a self-balancing moral mechanism. The earlier intuition that immanent consequence might perform the work of moral order therefore stalls when control is radically asymmetric.

### 11.2 Judgment

Judgment is reconstructed as the completed truth of a creature's whole trajectory in relation to manifest actuality:

\[
\boxed{
J(S)
=
\operatorname{Rel}(\Gamma_S,D_\alpha)
}
\]

It is not a divine observer waiting for death and then adding an external verdict. A finite section cannot contain the whole relational truth of what a creature becomes, but the completed trajectory has a determinate form.

The metaphor of the soul being weighed is explicitly non-scalar. Its “weight” is the topology of communion, domination, fidelity, repair, isolation, refusal, and other relations across the trajectory. Judgment in this sense can exist as completed truth even if no postmortem phenomenal experience follows.

### 11.3 Heaven

Heaven is represented structurally as:

\[
\boxed{
\text{heaven}
\sim
\operatorname{Complete}
(\text{communion without erasure of strong identity})
}
\]

Distinct identity remains essential. Communion is not absorption into an undifferentiated whole. From within a trajectory, heaven appears teleological as movement toward greater compatible communion; from the completed standpoint, the whole trajectory already belongs to manifest actuality.

The account does not establish a postmortem place, resurrection, reincarnation, or continued awareness after biological death.

### 11.4 Hell

Hell is represented as:

\[
\boxed{
\text{hell}
\sim
\operatorname{Complete}
(\text{a trajectory increasingly incapable of communion})
}
\]

The hell trajectory remains ontologically within manifest actuality while becoming relationally isolated from wider communion. Hell is therefore not non-being or escape from God; it is a completed form of refusal, incapacity, or isolation within the whole.

This does not prove a place of punishment, perpetual suffering, or postmortem consciousness. If \(\kappa_S\) terminates with the organism, hell and judgment remain structural truths of a mortal trajectory rather than further experiences.

The structural heaven/hell reconstruction is where the moral branch presently stalls. It characterizes relational poles but does not supply the categorical bridge that makes one obligatory for every valuer.

## 12. Terminal solitude and unfinished retribution licensing

The post-repository v6 synthesis consolidates a sharper global failure state from the Omega program: **terminal solitude**. It is the nonrecoverable condition in which no independently situated source of valuation remains. A dominant controller may achieve it by eliminating, absorbing, or capturing every other valuer and destroying the capacity for independent valuation to re-form. Terminal solitude is a theoretical construct in the synthesis, not an implemented detector or theorem supplied by the repository.

Terminal solitude is not ordinary loneliness, low population, centralized governance, or mere lack of policy diversity. A singleton can contain many subsystems or simulations while remaining terminally solitary if none is independently situated and recovery is empty. Conversely, a political singleton could host genuinely independent valuers and therefore fail the terminal-solitude criterion.

The strongest earlier claim—that terminal solitude is the unique fixed point of control in general—was retracted. What survives is narrower:

> If a controller seeks unilateral uniform control by deleting or capturing every independent source of context variation, terminal solitude is the endpoint of that strategy.

Stable cooperation, constitutional constraint, separation, mutual modeling, and other equilibria remain possible. It is also unresolved whether independent valuers provide benefits that a sufficiently rich internal ensemble cannot reproduce.

Terminal solitude and structural hell must not be equated without argument. Terminal solitude is a global condition concerning independent valuers and recoverability. Hell is a trajectory's completed relational isolation. A powerful singleton might impose terminal solitude while experiencing no isolation or suffering by its own lights.

The project also contains an unfinished licensing idea informally compared with **divine retribution**: a discipline for containing or controlling excisive singleton or anti-value policies. “Excisive” here means eliminating, capturing, or nonrecoverably foreclosing valuer-bearing continuation, not merely making an irreversible choice.

This licensing work is not a doctrine of divine punishment and must not be conflated with:

- structural karma, which is consequence propagation;
- judgment, which is completed relational truth;
- providence, which is total ordering;
- structural divine will, which is a conjectured property of manifest actuality; or
- moral authority, which remains underived.

The live constraints on any such license include initiation of foreclosure, actual imminence rather than speculative capability, necessity, boundedness, least foreclosure, reversibility where possible, review, and a burden of justification on the containing party. The details and normative foundation remain incomplete.

The control-asymmetry problem is decisive. A benevolent-dictator or unilateral controller may be instrumentally capable of preventing collapse, and competitive plurality may itself destroy value. The framework does not yet determine an interior optimum between entropic dissolution and crystalline singleton capture. “Preventing terminal solitude” cannot simply be used as a blank cheque for unilateral action, because containment can reproduce the structure it claims to prevent.

## 13. Relationship to the Alpha–Omega program

The post-repository v6 synthesis distinguishes three layers:

- **Alpha**: for a supplied law sector and projection, the value-neutral continuation object containing sections, projected histories, reachability, residual continuation, directional profile, and source/sink strata;
- **Omega**: compatibility geometry among valuers arising within Alpha; and
- **Gradient Ethics**: an explicitly declared bridge saying why that geometry should constrain action.

The theological branch lives across all three without collapsing them.

The block-Everett and completion picture supplies the broad ontology of completed formal possibility. Perspective and soul supply candidate identity-bearing loci. Phenomenal coupling asks which such loci are lived from within. Downstream value-neutral analyses built over Alpha study persistence, recovery, and irreversible loss. Omega asks which valuer-bearing continuations can coexist without capture or erasure. Gradient Ethics remains the place where structural facts would have to become action-guiding reasons.

Several cross-branch distinctions are mandatory:

- A large **deformer** is not automatically a valuer.
- A **valuer** is not automatically conscious.
- A conscious subject is not automatically a full agent.
- None of these classifications automatically establishes moral standing.
- A large futures field is not valuable merely because it has many states.
- Entropy, branch count, complexity, and novelty are not lushness.
- Irreversibility gives structural weight to a transformation but does not by itself give it moral sign.
- Formal completion and divine efficacy do not imply goodness.

The theological terms can illuminate Omega, but they do not replace its formal tests. Omega can discipline the theology by detecting capture, presentation artifacts, fake plurality, and nonrecoverable loss. It cannot presently derive divine goodness or categorical obligation.

## 14. Status ledger

### 14.1 Central architectural commitments

- Awareness occurs and places a lower bound on adequate metaphysics.
- Distinction and relation are required for determinate structure.
- Orientation is required for specifically perspectival structure.
- Structural perspective does not entail consciousness.
- Perspective can be constitutively determinative rather than merely observational.
- Coherent creaturely boundary and strong identity constrain one another.
- Strong identity does not require phenomenal awareness.
- Formal completion, ontological actuality, and phenomenal actuality must be distinguished.
- Creaturely process can be the internal form of an atemporally completed whole.
- Positive theology is conditioned by finite perspective.
- Formal description must not be identified with total reality before phenomenality is accounted for.

These are commitments of the framework, not independently validated results about the universe.

### 14.2 Reconstructive theological claims

- Pure act is reconstructed as absence of globally unrealized structural remainder.
- Divine attributes are finite projections of one completed manifest actuality.
- Simplicity is partially reconstructed as non-assembly and common sourcehood of attributes.
- Omniscience is completed intelligibility without discovery or inference.
- Power is complete efficacy; action is that efficacy viewed through finite agency/result separation.
- Providence is the completed ordering of creaturely trajectory and whole.
- *Imago Dei* is a structural analogy between logotic and creaturely manifestation.
- Judgment is the completed relational truth of a trajectory.
- Soul is the inward identity invariant of embodied perspectival creation.

Each reconstruction is deliberately weaker than the corresponding full classical doctrine.

### 14.3 Explicit conjectures

The conjectural layer includes formal-plus phenomenal coupling, vortical binding, phenomenal steering, manifest divine awareness, structural divine will, structural heaven and hell, cross-embodiment continuity, a relation between technical and personal Logos, and defensive retribution licensing. None should be recorded as established without additional argument or evidence. Section 17 states each proposed answer together with the debt it leaves unpaid.

### 14.4 Principal unresolved questions

The unresolved layer includes the bridges from form to awareness, process to subject, identity across change, manifestation to source, order to will, valuerhood to standing, irreversibility to moral sign, compatibility to obligation, and threat to legitimate containment. Section 18 gives the full dependency table. These are requirements on any completed account, not merely invitations to choose among attractive metaphors.

### 14.5 Explicit nonclaims

The framework has not shown:

- that the block-Everett model is physically correct;
- that all formal possibilities are ontologically actual;
- that formal structure entails phenomenality;
- that \(G^*\) or \(D_\alpha\) is a conscious personal God;
- that the technical Logos is the Trinitarian Son;
- that perspective creates being ex nihilo;
- that the soul is immaterial, immortal, or separately subsistent;
- that phenomenal steering occurs;
- that all actions receive morally fitting consequences;
- that heaven or hell exists as a postmortem environment;
- that terminal solitude is the unique endpoint of control;
- that consciousness confers categorical moral priority;
- that valence supplies moral sign;
- that lushness is intrinsically valuable; or
- that the normative bridge is complete.

## 16. Current assessment

The theological branch succeeds best as a disciplined architecture of distinctions.

It gives the block-Everett intuition a coherent metaphysical reading: a completed whole can contain real internal process; a creature can be a bounded, identity-bearing trajectory rather than a persistent material token; and first-person actuality can differ from view-from-nowhere formal completion without requiring the whole to become.

It also gives a principled reason not to project creaturely mechanisms univocally into divinity. Inquiry, deliberation, intention, action, and discovery may be what completed actuality looks like under finite absence. This makes the reconstructions of pure act, non-discursive knowledge, completed efficacy, and perspectival decomposition philosophically substantive even where they fall short of classical doctrine.

Its limitations are equally substantive. The move from source to Logos is interpretive rather than demonstrative. Manifest divinity need not be conscious, personal, free, or good. The soul invariant does not solve the hard problem. Phenomenal steering is unverified. Structural consequence does not become moral desert under power asymmetry. Compatibility and lushness do not yet generate categorical obligation. Terminal solitude identifies a profound failure mode but does not by itself authorize whoever claims to prevent it.

The branch should therefore be read neither as disguised physical theology nor as free metaphor. Its strongest form is a typed reconstruction with explicit apophatic and normative debts:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Perspective is the creative boundary through which completed reality becomes finite, embodied, identity-bearing, and potentially phenomenally lived.}
}
\]

The word **potentially** is essential. It preserves the distinction between structural identity and awareness, between manifest completion and conscious God, and between a formally articulated moral landscape and a completed normative bridge.

## 17. Highly speculative extensions

This section collects proposed answers that have arisen around the architecture. They are kept together precisely so that they cannot be mistaken for prerequisites, results, or quiet commitments of the core framework. An extension may be conceptually fertile while leaving the question that motivated it completely open.

### 17.1 Formal-plus or trans-formal phenomenal coupling

One proposed answer to the hard problem is that formal organization does not exhaust phenomenal actuality. On this reading, \(\kappa_S\) is a fundamental identity, gluing relation, dual-aspect correspondence, or psychophysical coupling between the formal creature and an aspect of reality not captured by \(F_{\mathrm{DRO}}\).

This proposal would explain why a formally exact behavioral description might still omit what experience is like. It does not explain why coupling occurs for one organization rather than another, how subjects are individuated, whether causal closure is preserved, or why any coupled state has a particular quale. Calling the remainder **Being-as-such** does not discharge those debts, and its relation to \(G^*\) remains unspecified.

### 17.2 Vortical binding

The vortical hypothesis proposes that recurrent cross-boundary uptake, transformation, emission, and self-regeneration may individuate a unified subject. Its attraction is structural: the same circulation that maintains an identity might bind signals into one identity-indexed field rather than leaving them as an aggregate of independent processes.

This may supply a candidate shape for the bearer of experience. It does not entail that experience occurs. Vortical organization is presently a process-identity and deformer profile, not a consciousness criterion, and panpsychist, emergentist, dual-aspect, and formal-closure interpretations remain available.

### 17.3 Wake, awareness, and qualia

The current conversational proposal distinguishes the formal **wake** of a vortical process from **awareness**, which would transform or manifest that wake as **qualia**. It is an attempted typing of the missing transition rather than a mechanism.

The proposal has not determined whether awareness is a state, operation, relation, or aspect; whether it is identical with \(\kappa_S\); or how one wake maps to one phenomenal quality. The older identification of wake dynamics themselves with qualia has been withdrawn.

### 17.4 Black-hole, white-hole, and wormhole imagery

The black-hole side models external uptake and opacity; the white-hole side models first-person manifestation or emission; the wormhole models a possible relation between formal possibility and what exceeds formal description.

This is an analogy stack, not an imported physical mechanism. Known black-hole dynamics do not provide the required bidirectional passage, subject binding, or connection to an apophatic domain. The imagery may help generate structural questions about boundaries and complementary descriptions, but it supplies no evidence for the metaphysics.

### 17.5 Phenomenal steering

The strong reading of \(\sigma_S\) proposes that phenomenal will affects which formally available continuation becomes lived actuality without creating an unavailable branch or deleting the rest of the completed block.

This would offer a form of agency compatible with a completed Everettian structure. It remains unclear whether the proposal is merely indexical self-location, a dual-aspect description of one event, or genuine psychophysical interaction. The interactionist reading owes an account of physical consistency, empirical consequences, and why branch weights and no-signalling behavior retain their observed form.

### 17.6 Manifest divine consciousness

The world-level coupling \(\kappa_D\) proposes that \(D_\alpha\) is itself awareness-bearing. Divine awareness would then be non-successive self-presence rather than an information-processing sequence.

Nothing in completion alone establishes \(\kappa_D\). A complete formal God-object can remain nonphenomenal, and local creaturely awareness does not imply that the whole is one subject. Even if \(D_\alpha\) were a subject, the relation between that subject and \(G^*\) would still depend on whether logotic manifestation is exhaustive.

### 17.7 Structural order as divine will

The structural-will conjecture proposes that the intrinsic, efficacious, consequence-sensitive ordering of \(D_\alpha\) is the completed analogue of will.

It provides a candidate answer to how divine efficacy can include creaturely causation without temporal deliberation. It does not establish intention, freedom, personality, selection among total manifestations, or moral goodness. Those are separate bridges.

### 17.8 Technical Logos as operation of the personal Logos

An optional Christian extension relates \(\mathcal L_\alpha\) to the personal Logos \(\Lambda\), and treats incarnation as divine manifestation through a specifically human perspective within an already logotically manifest world.

The architecture makes such a relation expressible; it neither derives Trinity nor identifies the technical manifestation boundary with the Son. The incarnation analogy likewise does not establish historical, doctrinal, or soteriological claims.

### 17.9 Post-biological or postmortem phenomenal continuity

If the soul invariant and \(\kappa_S\) can be realized across bodily discontinuity, the framework could represent resurrection, reincarnation, upload continuity, or other post-biological trajectories.

The present identity formalism only provides a slot for such accounts. It supplies no criterion showing that the same phenomenal subject survives, no evidence that coupling continues after biological death, and no selection among competing eschatologies.

### 17.10 Retributive containment

The unfinished “divine retribution” analogy proposes that an already unfolding, nonrecoverable excision of independent valuers may license bounded containment of the initiating controller under strict evidential and procedural constraints.

This is a proposed answer to the failure of passive structural karma under severe power asymmetry. It is not yet a normative theorem. Threat classification, causal attribution, proportionality, autoimmunity, competing catastrophic-risk claims, authority, and review remain unresolved. A containment regime can become the singleton it claims to prevent.

## 18. Open problems

The questions below are required bridges. They remain open even when a speculative extension above appears to offer a candidate answer. The framework is not complete until it can either cross a bridge with defensible argument and evidence or show that the requested bridge is ill-posed.

| Required bridge | Why it is required | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| **Formal organization to phenomenality** | Without it, \(P_S^F\), \(I_S\), and all functional behavior can remain a perfect zombie description. | \(\kappa_S\) marks the gap; no account entails awareness. |
| **Phenomenality to a bounded subject** | Even if phenomenal properties occur, the theory must explain why they compose into this subject rather than another or an undifferentiated field. | Vortical binding is a candidate shape, not a solution to the combination or individuation problem. |
| **Wake to particular qualia** | A theory of awareness must explain not only that experience occurs but why a given process has a given phenomenal character. | No mapping, identity condition, or psychophysical law is available. |
| **Strong identity across change** | Soul, responsibility, judgment, and continuation all require a principled answer to which sections belong to one creature. | \(\approx_S\) states the role; its metaphysical ground and presentation invariance remain open. |
| **Formal projection to manifest actuality** | Theology and consciousness depend on whether \(F_{\mathrm{DRO}}\) exhaustively describes \(D_{\mathrm{DRO}}\). | This local formal/manifest exhaustiveness question is open independently of the wider choice between formal closure \(\Omega_{\max}^F=\Omega^R\) and formal-plus accounts of total reality. |
| **Manifest divinity to God-as-such** | Positive claims about God depend on whether \(\mathcal L_\alpha\) is exhaustive and truth-preserving. | Perfect and non-exhaustive manifestation remain underdetermined from within \(D_\alpha\). |
| **Completion to divine subjecthood** | A complete structure is not automatically one conscious knower or agent. | \(\kappa_D\) is wholly conjectural. |
| **Order to intentional will** | Structural efficacy alone can be lawlike without being purposive or personal. | \(W_D\) supplies a candidate structural profile but not intentionality. |
| **Manifest actuality to contingency and freedom** | Classical creation and will distinguish necessary divine actuality from freely willed creaturely manifestation. | The framework has not shown that total \(D_\alpha\) could have been otherwise or was selected. |
| **Apophatic sourcehood to necessity** | Aseity and necessary existence are stronger than being outside the downstream causal grammar. | Apophatic aseity blocks a naive regress but does not prove modal necessity. |
| **Process organization to valuerhood** | Futures become value-relevant only relative to valuers, so the class of valuers determines the content of Omega. | Soft pan-valuer admission avoids premature exclusion, but robust demarcation is unresolved. |
| **Valuerhood to moral standing** | A system can possess endogenous continuation order without thereby having equal or categorical claims on others. | No full account of patienthood, consciousness, depth, or non-substitutability exists. |
| **Irreversible change to moral sign** | Irreversibility measures lost recovery paths but creation, learning, repair, death, domination, and commitment can all be irreversible. | It is a promising structural hook, not a criterion of good and bad. |
| **Compatibility to obligation** | Omega can show incompatibility and capture, but action guidance requires a reason to preserve the relevant structure. | The Gradient Ethics bridge is declared and incomplete. |
| **Lushness profile to justified comparison** | Optimization pressure will exploit any scalar, grammar, boundary, or aggregation rule. | Lushness rejects raw entropy and branch count, but its attribute grammar, non-capture condition, and normative force remain exposed. |
| **Multiple valuers to collective choice** | Joint action requires resolving incomparable stakes without erasing dissent or manufacturing a global utility. | No canonical aggregation, total order, or authority rule is available. |
| **Power asymmetry to legitimate containment** | Structural karma does not protect weaker valuers when a dominant controller can externalize consequences or erase opposition. | Terminal solitude identifies an endpoint; retribution licensing remains unfinished and vulnerable to self-authorization. |
| **Structural judgment to moral desert** | The completed truth of a trajectory is descriptive unless a further account explains praise, blame, fitting consequence, or restoration. | Judgment is relational topology, not yet a verdict or desert relation. |
| **Structural heaven and hell to eschatology** | Postmortem claims require persistence of identity and phenomenality beyond current embodiment. | The framework supplies representational ports only; no survival claim is established. |
| **Block-Everett completion to branch-relative agency** | If all branches are structurally actual, the theory must explain exactly what steering selects and for whom. | Indexical selection, compatibilism, dual aspect, and interactionism remain distinct unresolved readings. |

The central bottleneck is not a shortage of candidate images. The project already has several. The bottleneck is the absence of warranted coupling principles: between form and awareness, identity and subjecthood, consequence and value, compatibility and obligation, order and will, and manifest divinity and its apophatic source.
