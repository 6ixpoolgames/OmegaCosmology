# Volume VI — Glossary, Notation, and Claim Status

## Purpose

This appendix is the fast lookup layer for the cosmology. It records the meaning a term has **inside this project**, especially where that meaning differs from ordinary philosophy, theology, physics, or mathematics.

The source order below is included only to settle historical conflicts in terminology. This glossary is fully self-contained; access to none of these sources is assumed:

- *Alpha–Omega Framework v6* is the latest supplied Alpha–Omega synthesis and postdates the current Omega repository.
- The repository supplies the proof, experiment, and negative-result provenance behind v6.
- *Perspective and Divine Completion* governs the theological notation.
- The vortical-consciousness vocabulary from the current conversation remains provisional unless explicitly linked to a stable older definition.

## 1. Claim-status labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Proved** | Machine-checked relative to definitions, or exhaustively enumerated over a fully declared finite class |
| **Measured** | Reproduced experimental result on declared fixtures |
| **Defined** | A stipulated object whose usefulness does not establish that it maps reality correctly |
| **Working architecture** | A coherent system of distinctions with one or more bridge relations still functionally specified |
| **Reconstructive** | A traditional term given a narrower, analogical, or structurally translated meaning |
| **Conjecture** | A live substantive claim without current formal or empirical establishment |
| **Highly speculative extension** | A proposed mechanism or metaphysical answer with little direct support and which the core architecture does not require |
| **Metaphor** | A structure-generating analogy carrying no imported theorem |
| **Open problem** | A load-bearing missing bridge explicitly acknowledged by the project |
| **Rejected/demoted** | A stronger past claim withdrawn, falsified, or retained only as an instrument |

Proof status is always conditional on the definitions. A verified theorem does not verify that “valuer,” “compatibility,” “recovery,” or any other supplied predicate captures what matters in reality.

### Cross-volume status crosswalk

Some volumes preserve locally useful labels inherited from their subject matter. Read them through this canonical crosswalk:

| Local label | Canonical reading |
|---|---|
| **Established** in Volume I | A definition or constraint adopted by the framework; normally **Defined** or **Working architecture**, not empirical establishment |
| **Formal result** | **Proved** relative to the stated formal definitions and hypotheses |
| **Model-bounded result** | A result limited to a declared finite model; **Proved** when exhaustively enumerated, **Measured** when obtained experimentally |
| **Architectural claim** | **Working architecture** |
| **Reconstructive claim** | A typed analogy or reinterpretation; its adequacy remains philosophical unless separately supported |
| **Speculative extension** | **Conjecture** or **Highly speculative extension**, depending on evidential distance |
| **Survived / narrowed / demoted / quarantined** in Volume V | Provenance status describing conceptual evolution, not proof status |

Where labels appear to conflict, the more specific status and declared scope control. An exhaustive finite counterexample may be proved as a possibility result while its extrapolation to real systems remains open.

## 2. Alpha–Omega glossary

### Foundational structure

**Distinction** — determinate difference. In Alpha–Omega, one of the primitive-facing structural ingredients; in the theological paper, the first formal articulation of manifest being.

**Relation** — connection among distinctions. Relations may be genuinely polyadic rather than reducible to pairs.

**Asymmetry** — non-equivalence under a declared reversal, exchange, or comparison. The project distinguishes primitive, structural, statistical, historical, agentive, and normative asymmetries rather than treating direction as one thing.

**Continuation** — a generalized future: transition, path, derivation, completion, perturbation response, or downstream dependence. It does not require an objectively open future.

**Law sector \(\mathcal R\)** — a declared family of rewrite or transition rules within which continuation is evaluated.

**Projection \(p\)** — a declared query mapping configurations or histories into an ordered or observational index. Every projected conclusion is indexed by the projection used.

**Anchor** — a declared frame or vantage relative to which projection and orientation are evaluated. It is an input, not something the universal structure automatically derives.

**Directional grain** — an observed difference between the two directions of a projection. It may be null.

**Cycle affinity** — under positive forward and reverse edge weights, a reference-independent directional invariant: the logarithm of the product of forward/reverse transition ratios around a closed path. In a controlled system the stated finite comparison is policy-relative and assumes a fixed stationary deterministic policy.

**Grain** — indistinguishability relative to a selected family of contexts or observations.

**Gauge** — a stronger equivalence: a difference that no admissible context can detect or amplify.

**Alpha** — for a supplied law sector and projection, the value-neutral continuation object containing sections, projected histories, reachability, residual continuation, directional profile, and source/sink strata. Capital Alpha is the indexed family across sectors and projections. It contains no standing or moral objective. **Defined/working architecture.**

**Layer A** — the repo-era continuation-map integrity layer: whether presentations, abstractions, summaries, and policies preserve declared consequence-bearing continuation. It is part of Alpha–Omega's provenance, not a synonym for Alpha as defined in v6.

**Consequence-bearing distinction** — a difference whose erasure changes what can follow under the evaluated context.

**Proto-teleology** — consequence-bearing direction without purpose, preference, agency, or value.

**Presentation** — a way of describing or decomposing a process. Presentation-sensitive properties require an admissibility or reflection argument before they can be treated as properties of the process itself.

**Action-wise strong lumpability** — exact aggregation condition under which same-block states send identical probability mass to every target block under every action. It supports exact finite-horizon transport under further policy and target assumptions. **Standard formal result implemented and audited.**

### Processes, valuers, and agency

**Pattern** — a projection-relative regularity that may be reidentified through a history. Patternhood alone gives no identity or standing.

**Vorticality** — identity regenerated through compositional turnover: constituents change, causal organization reconstructs the pattern, and records/effects remain attributable along the trajectory. **Working architecture, not consciousness.**

**P-invariant** — a Petri-net conserved quantity. Standard technical term; too strong to model most living identity by itself.

**Trap** — a Petri-net set that, once marked, remains marked. Persistence without necessarily implying regeneration.

**Siphon** — a set that, once empty, remains empty. Used structurally as an extinction pattern.

**T-invariant** — a firing cycle returning a Petri-net marking to its initial state: consumption plus regeneration. It is not by itself a living process or valuer.

**Candidate** — a trackable projection-relative pattern admitted into the valuer envelope with no automatic standing.

**Soft pan-valuerhood** — refusal to impose a sharp valuer threshold before testing. Candidates are admitted broadly and graded by endogenous consequence, maintenance, policy sensitivity, reconstruction, and causal reach. It is not panpsychism or equal standing for all things.

**Valuer** — an unresolved family of related formulations. The thin entry condition admits bounded self-maintaining processes into a graded candidate envelope; the substantive formulation requires distinctions among reachable continuations to affect the process's own organization, policy, or reconstruction, thereby inducing a continuation ordering \(C_1>_v C_2\). Stronger formal targets add robust agency, generativity, and compatibility. The relation among these formulations is unsettled, and none automatically confers standing. **Central open demarcation.**

**Future field** — a valuer-indexed structured continuation surface relative to a state, actions, boundary, facts, presentation, and horizon. Alpha can describe neutral continuation without a valuer; a future field becomes value-relevant only through the valuer index. It is not raw branch count or a literal scalar field.

**Future Field Atlas (FFA)** — a finite instrument for reachable-frontier morphology, including topology, transport, and product-versus-coupled support. It is not the future field itself and does not detect value, valuers, agency, standing, or full semantic compatibility.

**Value contact** — a process's capacity to encounter, register, create, compare, or be affected by what matters relative to its own organization. It is a proposed valuer-profile coordinate, not a proof of standing or consciousness.

**Agent** — a stronger effective organization involving endogenous selection, feedback, counterfactual control, and constitutive maintenance. Agent, valuer, conscious subject, and moral patient are distinct types.

**Deformer** — a process whose state, policy, or existence changes another system's reachable continuation. Deformation does not imply agency, value, or standing.

**Branch-point capacity** — reliable ability to create, prevent, or control interventions that disproportionately and nonrecoverably narrow continuation, including through persistent technological, informational, or institutional structures. It marks causal reach, not moral authority.

**Maintenance gap** — the counterfactual difference between an actively maintained organization and a matched passive, ablated, randomized, or unsupported reference.

**Pre-proto-valuer / proto-valuer** — recent working ladder: maintenance of recoverable distinctions above a null, followed by self-conditioning toward preserving that capacity. This is not yet the v6 valuer definition or a consciousness detector.

**Negentropic consumption** — use of free-energy/exergy gradients with entropy export. A possible marker of organized irreversible activity, not value or consciousness.

### Omega and compatibility

**Realization fiber \(\mathrm{Real}(F)\)** — the complete set of histories or configurations jointly realizing candidate family \(F\).

**May compatibility** — some coherent joint realization exists:

\[
\mathrm{May}(F)\iff \mathrm{Real}(F)\neq\varnothing.
\]

**Robust compatibility** — one policy secures a joint realization across every declared environment:

\[
\mathrm{Robust}_{\mathcal E}(F)
\iff
\exists\pi\;\forall e\in\mathcal E,
\operatorname{outcome}(\pi,e)\in\mathrm{Real}(F).
\]

May means possible coexistence; Robust means guaranteeability under the declared uncertainty scope.

**Witness-retaining** — preserving how a claim is realized, including configurations, policies, runs, or counterexamples, instead of returning only true/false.

**Capital Omega** — the full decorated compatibility structure: compatible faces, realization fibers, May and Robust relations, obstructions, incomparable maximal faces, and transports across presentations. It is not one selected maximum or global controller. **Defined/working architecture.**

**Non-flag / hollow triangle** — a compatibility family in which every pair is compatible but the triple is not. This proves that pairwise conflict data can be insufficient. **Proved on finite structures.**

**Polyadic** — involving an arbitrary finite family as one irreducible relational fact, rather than only pairs.

**Obstruction** — the correct output when local realization, security, or licensing does not extend globally. It is not a software failure and not automatically a moral prohibition.

**Policy atlas / atlas** — a family of local securing policies covering the context space when no global policy exists.

**Covering number \(\kappa\)** — the extended-natural infimum of the number of policies required to cover all declared contingencies, using \(\inf\varnothing:=\infty\). \(\kappa=1\) is ordinary robust securability; finite \(\kappa>1\) means functional policy plurality is required; \(\kappa=\infty\) means no cover exists in the declared policy class.

**Attenuation** — reducing or eliminating environmental variation so that fewer policies are required. Structurally this can improve robust securability while destroying independent variation; this is a recognized live defect.

**Independently situated valuer** — a source of valuation not merely a copy, absorbed subsystem, simulation controlled by one source, or another presentation of the same will. The exact independence test remains open.

**Capture / non-capture** — absorption of putatively distinct valuers under one controlling source, or preservation of their independent situation. Non-capture is load-bearing and may currently be partly stipulated.

**Terminal solitude** — a state in which no independently situated source of valuation currently remains and no such source can be restored or generated within the declared recovery scope. Robust incompatibility with a controller is not sufficient: independent valuers may exist while being jointly indefensible. Terminal solitude is not identical to headcount-one, Bostromian singleton governance, structural hell, or low internal diversity.

**Lushness** — the aspirational umbrella of open-ended compatible differentiation in valuer-bearing continuation, intended to distinguish genuine emergence, correction paths, nonduplicate attributes, and joint capabilities from duplication and capture. An older finite proxy compared declared lushness profiles by inclusion rather than a canonical scalar; that proxy does not define or validate the full target. Lushness's exact content and normative force remain conjectural.

**Entropic dissolution** — the metaphorical failure wall where unconstrained plurality does not persist long enough to bear value.

**Crystalline death** — the opposing metaphorical wall of perfect order with no independent source of valuation remaining.

**Corridor** — in v6's substantive image, regenerative continuation between entropic dissolution and crystalline death. In repo decision machinery, “corridor” can instead mean a controlled invariant satisfying declared requirements. The two meanings must not be conflated.

**Anti-value** — destruction of the substrate admitting value-bearing continuation, rather than merely low value. **Conjectural normative term.**

### Recovery, licensing, and Gradient Ethics

**Recovery frame** — declared states, facts, repair actions, horizon, boundary, and fact ordering relative to which recovery is evaluated.

**Recoverable within/up to \(h\)** — a registered fact can be restored in exactly/at most \(h\) allowed repair steps. This does not mean exact microstate reversal.

**Recovery profile** — graded, source-indexed record of which declared facts can be recovered and under what conditions.

**Operational irreversibility / nonrecoverable contraction** — a registered fact held before an action, fails afterward, and cannot be recovered within the declared frame. This is not automatically thermodynamic irreversibility, harm, or wrongness.

**Correction register** — facts and capacities needed for future comparison, revision, probing, or error correction.

**Correction-register collapse** — preservation of coarse task success while nonrecoverably destroying correction capacity. The historical rhetorical name was “self-lobotomy.”

**Phantom property** — recoverability, safety, reachability, viability, license, or compensation present in an abstraction/belief frame but absent from the concrete/true frame.

**Reflection** — a one-way guarantee that accepted abstract or believed-frame facts transport soundly to a concrete or true frame.

**Loss profile / expansion profile** — registered lost or gained facts, compared under a supplied fact order. Neither is moral harm or benefit by definition.

**Compensation claim** — a declared relation proposing that expansion facts cover lost facts. Current formal surfaces do not establish cross-valuer fungibility or moral compensation.

**NOLP** — No Omniscient License Presumption: refuse same-frame nonrecoverable contraction unless a complete certified compensation cover is registered. Defeating this refusal is not final moral permission.

**License** — a certificate that declared gates pass. It is not a moral right, legitimate authority, recommendation, or optimum.

**Gradient Ethics** — a conditional licensing discipline governing presentation integrity, irreversible contraction, recovery, compensation, compatibility, and defensive action relative to valuers. It is not a complete moral theory.

**Omega Decision Theory (ODT)** — working name for the staged decision-support stack operating over declared Omega/recovery structure. It is not a completed decision theory or source of moral authority.

**ODT0** — licenses or refuses action routes by checking declared facts, presentation/reflection conditions, and robust corridor preservation. It does not rank all licensed actions.

**ODT1** — partially compares already licensed outcome surfaces using a declared preorder and can return incomparability or failure certificates. The preorder is supplied rather than value-free.

**ODT2** — applies registered arbitration data, such as a violation score, to an unresolved finite frontier. It validates selection relative to the registered score, not the legitimacy of the score or the authority applying it.

**Residual/signed premise** — the explicit normative bridge that affected valuers' endogenous continuation gradients count as prima facie reasons and cannot be erased by flattering presentation. It is supplied, not derived.

**Authority hole** — the unresolved source of legitimate standing, cross-valuer comparison, tie-breaking, enforcement, and authority to define or amend the register.

**Excisive policy/controller** — one that captures, removes, or nonrecoverably forecloses independently situated valuers or value-bearing continuation.

**Defensive licensing** — principles for resisting ongoing foreclosure or excision, with imminence, necessity, proportionality, alternatives, and standing thresholds still open.

**Divine retribution** — recent metaphor for a possible licensing principle governing containment of robust excisive controllers. It is not a current theorem, completed doctrine, or synonym for karma or judgment.

## 3. Theological glossary

### Reality and manifestation

**Completed universal wavefunction** — the original intuition of the entire Everettian branching history as one atemporal completed object. Not a final-time collapse state.

**Block Everett / atemporal block multiverse** — all branch-structured history is globally present while succession remains internal to oriented creaturely trajectories.

**Completion** — absence of globally pending structure relative to the specified whole. Completion does not mean goodness, maximal possibility, terminal time, optimization convergence, or absence of internal process.

**Formal completion** — complete formal specification. Normatively neutral: a completed hell is as formally complete as a completed heaven.

**\(\Omega_{\min}\)** — minimum ontological conditions required to accommodate awareness-bearing perspectives.

**\(\Omega_{\max}^F\)** — a maximally expressive formal domain containing all relevant formally representable structure.

**\(\Omega^R\)** — total reality, whatever its ultimate constitution.

**Formal closure** — the option that maximal formal reality exhausts total reality.

**Formal-plus** — any option on which formal description does not exhaust total reality; not automatically substance dualism.

**DRO** — the provisional manifest grammar of distinction, relation, and orientation.

**\(G^*\), God-as-such** — apophatically indicated source, not a hidden formal object or a deity with every classical predicate assumed in advance.

**Logotic articulation \(\mathcal L_\alpha\)** — the non-temporal world-generating articulation through which a determinate manifestation \(D_\alpha\) is articulated under grammar \(\alpha\). The apophatic source \(G^*\) is not thereby claimed to become determinate or exhaustively formalized.

**Logos** — technical shorthand for \(\mathcal L_\alpha\). It is not automatically the personal Trinitarian Son.

**Manifest divinity \(D_\alpha\)** — complete actuality manifested under grammar \(\alpha\). It is not thereby conscious, personal, morally good, or exhaustive of \(G^*\).

**Formal projection \(F_{\mathrm{DRO}}\)** — maximal formal description of manifest actuality under DRO, not automatically the whole of manifest actuality.

**Finite apprehension \(M_S\)** — manifest divinity insofar as positively apprehensible from creaturely perspective \(P_S\). It is the creature's finite God-object, not God-as-such or an exhaustive representation of \(D_\alpha\).

**Two apophatic veils** — uncertainty about whether manifestation exhausts \(G^*\), and necessary finite limitation in creaturely apprehension of manifestation.

**Being-as-such** — recent phrase for actuality beyond formal possibility. Its relation to \(G^*\), \(D_\alpha\), and \(\Omega^R\) is not yet typed.

### Perspective, creation, and identity

**Perspective** — determinative limitation organizing accessible distinctions relative to a locus or invariant. It need not be conscious.

**Structural perspective \(P^F\)** — coarse-graining/equivalence, orientation, and locus/invariant considered formally.

**Logotic perspective** — world-generating articulation establishing a manifest grammar.

**Creaturely perspective \(P_S\)** — constitutive boundary individuating a creature within manifest actuality.

**Non-excisive creation** — finite individuation within the whole without removing a piece from the whole. Not yet creation ex nihilo.

**Imago Dei** — structural analogy between logotic and creaturely manifestation: unity becomes manifest through determinative limitation at both scales. It does not by itself establish moral likeness, Trinity, or a miniature copy of divine faculties.

**Weak identity \(I^W\)** — descriptive, referential, or classificatory identity.

**Strong identity \(\approx_S\)** — world-supported relation under which changing sections belong to one creature.

**Soul invariant \(I_S\)** — inward identity of an embodied perspectival creation. It does not by definition entail immateriality, consciousness, immortality, or separability.

**Soul trajectory \(\Gamma_S\)** — the invariant realized through an oriented sequence of embodied states, not a ghost moving through bodies.

**Completed essence \(E_S\)** — the full identity-compatible coherent trajectory-space. This is not standard Thomistic essence.

**Ontological actuality \(A_S^O\)** — the portion of completed essence instantiated in manifest reality; potentially all of it under full Everett.

**Phenomenal actuality \(A_S^\Phi:=\gamma_S^\Phi\), with \(\gamma_S^\Phi\in A_S^O\)** — the trajectory lived as actual from the subject's first-person position. It does not erase other ontologically actual branches.

**Process as internal completion** — succession and becoming are real within an oriented trajectory although the global structure does not become.

### Phenomenality and divine predicates

**Phenomenality \(\Phi\)** — lived presence: the fact that there is something it is like. It is not identified by stipulation with information, recursion, complexity, or formal perspective.

**Phenomenal coupling \(\kappa_S\)** — unknown relation between formal identity/body/perspective and phenomenality. A placeholder, not an explanation.

**Phenomenal steering \(\sigma_S\)** — speculative relation between will/directedness and the branch-relative lived successor inside completed essence. It creates no unavailable branch and deletes none.

**Completion-pure-act** — absence of globally unrealized structural remainder in manifest actuality. Narrower than Thomistic *actus purus*.

**Perspectival decomposition** — knowledge, will, power, and action understood as finite categorical projections of one completed actuality.

**Completion-simplicity** — non-development, non-assembly, and common sourcehood of differentiated divine attributes; weaker than strict Thomistic simplicity.

**Completed intelligibility / omniscience** — no fact internal to manifest completion remains to be acquired through discovery. It does not prove conscious knowing or exhaustive knowledge of \(G^*\).

**Manifest divine power/action** — complete efficacy of manifest actuality, not a theorem establishing every traditional modal claim about omnipotence.

**Providence** — completed ordering of a creature's whole trajectory relative to manifest actuality, not prediction followed by intervention.

**Structural divine will** — intrinsic, efficacious, consequence-sensitive ordering. Efficacy is not yet intentionality or moral fittingness.

**Communion** — sustainable compatible participation among distinct identities without fusion or erasure.

**Structural love** — communication of being/good preserving distinct participation. It does not prove affection or universal benevolence.

**Structural justice** — proportionate ordering toward mutually sustainable participation. It does not close the categorical normative gap.

**Karma** — immanent propagation of the consequences of steering. Not a reconstruction of any one historical karmic doctrine.

**Judgment** — completed relational truth of what a trajectory becomes, not a later tribunal or scalar score.

**Structural heaven** — completed compatible communion preserving identity. No scalar maximum or uniquely inclusion-maximal state has been defined.

**Structural hell** — completed isolation or refusal of communion while remaining within manifest actuality. It need not mean punishment, suffering, or non-being.

**Apophatic aseity** — \(G^*\) is not another caused object inside the downstream causal grammar. This does not prove modal necessity.

## 4. Vortical-consciousness working vocabulary

**Wake** — formally describable signal and dynamics generated as the world is processed through a vortical controller. It is not itself qualia.

**Awareness** — currently ambiguous between phenomenal presence and the hypothesized transformation/binding by which wake becomes phenomenal. This is an unresolved typing problem.

**Quale / qualia** — phenomenal character once awareness is present. The earlier equation of qualia with wake dynamics has been withdrawn.

**Phenomenal binding** — the missing relation by which distributed formal dynamics become one subject-indexed phenomenal field.

**Spark of awareness** — informal placeholder for the still-unlocated manifestation of experience; it names rather than solves the hard problem.

**Worldline recirculation** — repeated processing and regeneration of identity through world-interaction. Not a closed timelike curve.

**Black-hole aspect** — external identity inferred from uptake, compression, and futures-field deformation. **Metaphor.**

**White-hole aspect** — internal or expressive identity associated with phenomenal manifestation/emission. **Metaphor.**

**Identity/event horizon** — proposed privacy boundary separating third-person formal description from first-person presentation. Not a literal permanent GR horizon.

**Vortical singularity** — metaphorical opaque identity core, not a spacetime singularity.

**Bidirectional wormhole** — conjectured trans-formal binder between formal possibility and Being-as-such. If no information traverses, bidirectionality can mean only ontological co-determination or participation. **Highly speculative.**

**Panpsychism** — the live family of views on which phenomenality is fundamental or ubiquitous. It need not imply that a rock is one unified subject.

**Pan-valuerhood** — broad or graded distribution of valuer-like structure. It is logically distinct from panpsychism.

## 7. Highly speculative extensions

The following are live or historically important candidate answers, not results:

- phenomenal awareness as a transformation that converts formal wake into qualia;
- a bidirectional trans-formal wormhole between formal possibility and Being-as-such;
- black-hole/white-hole complementarity as an actual phenomenal mechanism;
- a strong interactionist reading in which phenomenal steering causally privileges a lived Everettian continuation rather than merely indexing it;
- consciousness occurring only in already-valuer-like organizations;
- exact identity between the soul invariant and a vortical/circulation invariant;
- substrate structure itself deriving an intrinsically optimal lushness corridor;
- a universal quasi-scalar adequate for cross-valuer optimization;
- terminal solitude as the possible attractor of a controller specifically committed to unilateral uniform control; the universal inevitability claim is retracted;
- a categorical licensing principle for “divine retribution” against excisive controllers;
- completed manifest actuality being a conscious, intentional, omnibenevolent deity.

## 8. Open problems

The core unresolved questions are:

1. How are admissible projections and effective boundaries selected without circularity?
2. What demarcates a valuer, and what makes two valuers independently situated?
3. Which valuers or subjects possess moral standing, and why?
4. How should overlapping, nested, branching, or incompatible valuers be compared?
5. How can cross-valuer aggregation avoid fungibility, double counting, and sovereign scalarization?
6. What constrains an enforcer when control asymmetry defeats reciprocal correction?
7. What is phenomenal coupling, and which formal organizations participate in it?
8. How do subject identity, Born measure, and lived probability behave in full Everett?
9. Is structural divine ordering conscious, intentional, free, or good?
10. How can divine goodness be reconciled with the actuality of horrific Everett branches?

## 9. Explicit noncanonical scaffolds

The following arose during analysis but have not been adopted as project terms or mechanisms:

- “manifest lift” and a projection \(\pi:M\to F\);
- a formal/phenomenal “common-event fiber”;
- black and white “mouths” as technical components;
- a private quantum-instrument model of qualia;
- a formal phenomenal-information horizon distinct from the user's metaphor;
- any specific awareness operator or psychophysical equation proposed only in conversation.

They may be useful future comparison models. They are not part of the current cosmology.

## 10. Misreadings to avoid

1. **Formal verification verifies the worldview.** It verifies consequences of definitions and finite constructions.
2. **All future expansion is good.** The project explicitly rejects raw expansion, branch count, and unqualified richer-is-better.
3. **Every deformer is a valuer.** Deformation is descriptive; value requires valuer-indexed consequence.
4. **Every valuer is conscious or has equal standing.** Neither follows.
5. **Suffering is intrinsically bad and pleasure intrinsically good in the framework.** Valence has no fixed moral sign independent of identity, relation, action, and continuation structure.
6. **Omega is one optimum.** Capital Omega retains incompatible faces, fibers, policies, and obstructions.
7. **The completed multiverse is God by definition.** The theological interpretation is conditional and distinguishes formal description, manifest divinity, and God-as-such.
8. **Completion means perfection.** Formal completion is normatively neutral.
9. **The soul invariant proves immortality.** It supplies a minimal identity architecture only.
10. **The wormhole is a physics claim.** It is currently an untethered metaphor for a missing phenomenal binder.
