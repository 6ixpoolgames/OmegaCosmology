Renormalized present frames: situated measure in a branching block

28 September 2026 · Research attempt on the Omega Cosmology v3.1 addendum track

Status. This is a proposed construction with elementary propositions. It uses the finite classical setting first, then the quantum realization through decoherent histories. No experiment was run. The numbers in the examples were checked in exact arithmetic (frames_checks.py). Status markers follow the primer: [established], [project result], [proposed], [conjecture].

Origin. The originator proposed that "the present is useful in a way that filters and normalizes the sprawling infinity of the recursively branching block into a frame of reference that renormalizes values around itself. The past is settled and solid … and futures become thinner and more distant aspirations as they get further away." This note formalizes that proposal, including one correction, and follows its consequences for lushness deformation and the population rule.

0. Summary

1.  A frame is a perspective's present record-history. The frame's measure is the relative weight of the continuations that extend it. [established machinery; proposed use]
2.  Within a frame, the recorded past has weight 1 and the futures share weight 1 at every later time. Any specific future thins with distance; coarse futures need not. Branching is a matter of resolution, not a discount on the future. [elementary result]
3.  Frames along one history agree with each other by conditioning. Frame expectations are martingales, and the fixed-deadline achievement envelope is a supermartingale. Fixed-origin assessment and rolling-window assessment are two readouts of the same family of frames. [elementary result; proposed interpretation]
4.  Restricted to a causal domain with a finite accessible state space, frame evaluation is finite. Time is indexed by horizon, not summed across horizons. [conditional]
5.  Of three conventions for valuers who exist in some futures and not others, only weight-based (total) scoring with a viability coordinate resists both complete and selective elimination. It rewards creating valuers. [elementary result]
6.  The global object is the atlas of all frames, with no global sum. A global verdict exists only where frame verdicts agree. [proposed; interpretive reading: the veil]

1. The problem

A branching block with unbounded branching, space, or time can contain unboundedly many valuers. Totals over the whole object then diverge or become order-dependent [Bostrom 2011]. Philosophers' "expansionist" remedies compare infinite worlds over regions growing from a centre [Vallentyne and Kagan 1997; Arntzenius 2014]. The standard objection is that physics supplies no privileged centre. Cosmology faces the same divergence in its measure problem, and one leading regularization restricts attention to a single observer's causal diamond [Bousso 2006].

The originator's move is to accept a centre without privileging one. Every present is a centre for its own frame, and no present is the centre of the whole. The nonprivileged-present conjecture of Part 09 becomes the device that makes finite evaluation possible.

2. Setting

Classical finite model. A path space Ω carries a probability law P. A perspective p has a record process R^p_t: what p's physical records contain at time t. Write 𝓕^p_t for the information generated by R^p up to time t. A record-history α is a value of R^p up to t with P(α) > 0.

Quantum realization. Take a decoherent family of histories with class operators C_α, weights W(α) = D(α, α) = ‖C_α ψ‖², and negligible off-diagonal decoherence functional (Part 12, §K). The record-history α corresponds to p's record projectors. All events below are coarse-grainings within one decoherent family. Incompatible families are not pooled. [established; R01, R09, R28]

3. Frames and their measure

Definition 1 (frame). A frame is φ = (p, t, α). Its measure on events E is

    classical:  μ_φ(E) = P(E | α)
    quantum:    μ_φ(E) = W(E ∧ α) / W(α)

In the Everettian picture this is the relative-state weight of continuations given p's records. [established; proposed use]

Proposition 1 (the recorded past is settled). If E is determined by p's records up to t, then μ_φ(E) ∈ {0, 1}.

Proof. Conditioning on α decides E. ∎

Caveat. "Settled" means recorded, not merely past. A past alternative whose record has been erased is not settled in the frame; the quantum eraser (Part 11, Example 3) is the standard case. The solidity of the past is the solidity of its records.

Proposition 2 (conservation; resolution, not discount). For every s > t, the extensions of α to time s partition the frame, so their weights sum to 1. If at every step no child branch carries more than a fraction q < 1 of its parent's weight, then any specific extension n steps ahead has μ_φ ≤ qⁿ.

Proof. Additivity over a partition. The bound follows by multiplying the relative weights along the path. ∎

Corollary. Every future time carries total frame weight 1, so branching provides no discount on the future as a whole. What thins with distance is resolution. "Exactly this outcome in 200 years" becomes light; "some outcome of this broad kind" can stay heavy (Example A). Any time-discounting of value must be argued separately. It cannot be read off the branching measure.

This corrects the proposal as originally stated. Futures do not thin as aspirations in general. Precise aspirations thin, which matches the primer's earlier intuition that depth of horizon correlates with fineness of description.

Proposition 3 (coherence along a history). Let α′ range over the extensions of α at a later time t′ > t. Then for any event E,

    μ_α(E) = Σ_{α′} μ_α(α′) · μ_{α′}(E).

Proof. The law of total probability; in the quantum case, additivity of W on a decoherent family. ∎

Consequences.

-   (i) Martingales. For any readout f, the frame expectations M_t = E_{μ_{α_t}}[f] form a martingale along each history. [established]
-   (ii) The achievement envelope. Let V_t be the best success probability for a requirement from the frame at time t, with a fixed deadline and budget, over policies acting on p's records. Under any implementable policy V_t is a supermartingale, and under an optimal policy it is a martingale. This is dynamic programming [established]. It is the lushness-deformation addendum's §6 statement: commitment cannot raise the fixed-origin optimum.
-   (iii) Fixed and rolling assessment are one family. Fixed-origin assessment is evaluation within one frame. Rolling assessment evaluates a window of fixed length within each successive frame along a history. Both are readouts of the same family of frames, and neither is the true view. Growth in realized organization shows up as rising rolling-window envelopes along a history. This is consistent with the fixed-origin envelope never rising in expectation. [proposed] This settles the earlier disagreement over whether a fixed origin "permits only foreclosure": it does not, because the rolling readout of the same frames registers construction.

4. Finite domains

Definition 2 (situated frame). A situated frame is Φ = (p, t, α, D, H). D is p's causal domain for horizon H, meaning the region that can influence, or be influenced by, p's continuation within H. For a worldtube this is the causal diamond between its present and its horizon endpoint. The frame measure is restricted to the degrees of freedom in D.

Proposition 4 (finiteness, conditional). Suppose the degrees of freedom in D have a finite-dimensional state space, and the decoherent family has finitely many alternatives per step. Then within horizon H the frame has finitely many distinguishable continuation classes, and every frame sum is finite.

Proof. A finite tree with finite branching over finitely many steps. ∎

Status. The premise holds in every finite model. For our universe it corresponds to the conjecture that a de Sitter causal patch has finite maximum entropy [Gibbons and Hawking 1977; Bousso 2002]. That is a common reading, not an established result. [conditional]

Time is indexed, not summed. A constant expected value per unit time, summed over horizons, grows without bound (Example D). The all-horizons reference of the addendum already indexes horizons rather than summing over them, so infinite time is handled by indexing and no discount is needed. If a single scalar across horizons is ever wanted, the normalization used (a time average, for instance) is an explicit evaluative choice.

5. Valuers in a frame: the population rule

For a process j under intervention P in frame φ, keep two coordinates:

    viability:    V_P(j) = μ^P_φ(j exists)
    flourishing:  F_P(j) = μ^P_φ(j exists ∧ R_j holds)       (total)
                  F_P(j | exists) = F_P(j) / V_P(j)          (conditional; undefined if V_P(j) = 0)

Here R_j is j's endogenous requirement beyond persistence. The union rule includes j's coordinates whenever j exists in any compared future or at the frame's present.

Three conventions are compared.

-   Total: score (V, F).
-   Conditional (average-like): score F | exists.
-   Frame-presentist: count j only if j exists at the frame's present. Each frame reintroduces a present, so this person-affecting option is well-defined here even though the block has no global present.

Example C (exact; j does not exist at the frame's present):

  Future                                                                   V   F (total)   F given existence
  -------------------------------------------------------------------- ----- ----------- -------------------
  Q: baseline                                                            1/2         3/8                 3/4
  P: creates more existence, lower average flourishing                   3/4         1/2                 2/3
  Y: Q, but j eliminated in the branches where it would not flourish     3/8         3/8                   1
  X: j eliminated everywhere                                               0           0           undefined

Proposition 5 (elimination).

-   (a) Total. Complete elimination gives V = F = 0, which is the minimum. Selective elimination lowers V and leaves F unchanged, so it is strictly dominated. Here P > Q > Y > X.
-   (b) Conditional. It ranks Y > Q > P, rewarding the elimination of those who would fare badly. That is the familiar pathology of average views, here in exact form. X is undefined, and if undefined coordinates are dropped, complete elimination escapes the comparison altogether.
-   (c) Frame-presentist. It is neutral among all four futures for a valuer who does not yet exist, so it allows eliminating future valuers.

Proof. Direct computation from the definitions. ∎

Consequences.

-   Of these three, only the total convention with a viability coordinate resists both complete and selective elimination. This is now a safety argument, and it is stronger than the earlier metaphysical one (that the block removes the privileged present). Frames do reintroduce a present; using it to exclude future valuers is simply unsafe.
-   The total convention's known cost is that it rewards creation. P beats Q despite lower average flourishing, which is the direction of the repugnant conclusion. Two things temper this, and neither removes it. The lushness order still requires P to be no worse on any other valuer's coordinates. The generativity thesis welcomes creation of valuers anyway.
-   Endogenous task families must include each valuer's own viability coordinate. Without it, selective elimination in the branches where a requirement fails is scored as neutral.

6. Lushness deformation within a frame

The deformation from the addendum,

    D^L_P(c) = Σ_r w_P(r | c) · δ[ L^F_{H−τ, b−k}(c_{P,r}) ],

carries over unchanged, with the context c read as a situated frame Φ and w_P as the frame measure under P. The frame adds four things:

-   the physical meaning of the weights, as relative weight given the perspective's records;
-   the domain, the causal diamond;
-   horizon indexing in place of horizon sums;
-   the population convention, total with viability by default.

Frames are relative to perspectives. With redrawable boundaries, the atlas contains frames at every scale (cell, organism, institution, civilization), each with its own records, domain and present.

7. The global object as an atlas

Definition 3 (atlas). The atlas is 𝒜 = {Φ}, taken over all perspectives and record-histories, with restriction maps given by conditioning and domain restriction. A global verdict on a comparison is a verdict that holds in every frame of 𝒜 where the comparison is defined.

Proposal. The construction defines no global sum. Frame verdicts are finite and well-posed. A global verdict exists exactly where the relevant frame verdicts agree. Where frames disagree, for instance because their domains contain different valuers, no bounded evaluator can recover a global verdict by gluing. [proposed]

Interpretive reading. This gives precise content to "Only God can judge." Finite judgment is possible because it is renormalized. The complete judgment would require an evaluation that no frame contains and that gluing may not define. The global reference remains the object of which the frames are views; it is not an evaluator any frame can occupy. [conjecture; interpretive]

8. Worked examples

All numbers are exact. Each step branches into a heavy child with relative weight 2/3 and a light child with 1/3.

Example A (resolution, not discount).

    Depth n   Total frame weight   Specific all-heavy path   At least half the steps heavy
  --------- -------------------- ------------------------- -------------------------------
          1                    1                       2/3                           0.667
          5                    1            32/243 ≈ 0.132                           0.790
         10                    1        1024/59049 ≈ 0.017                           0.923

The specific future thins geometrically. The coarse future gains weight. The total never falls.

Example B (coherence). Let E be "at least two of the first three steps are heavy." From the root frame, μ(E) = 20/27. From the child frames, μ_heavy(E) = 8/9 and μ_light(E) = 4/9. Their weighted average is (2/3)(8/9) + (1/3)(4/9) = 20/27, as Proposition 3 requires.

Example C (population). See the table in §5.

Example D (time). A constant expected value of 1/2 per unit time sums to 5, 50 and 500 over horizons of 10, 100 and 1000. The value per unit time stays at 1/2. Indexing horizons keeps each verdict finite; summing does not.

9. What this attempt does and does not establish

It establishes that frames of the originator's kind:

-   make situated evaluation finite and coherent along histories;
-   show that branching gives resolution-dependence, not discounting;
-   unify fixed-origin and rolling assessment;
-   give an elimination-resistant population convention with a stated cost;
-   leave the global object as an atlas without a sum.

It does not establish:

-   that Born weight is the right measure of concern. Everettian decision theory argues that it is, and the argument is contested [Wallace 2012];
-   that our universe's causal patches are finite;
-   a moral choice among population conventions. It shows their safety properties, not which is right;
-   how to handle frames with unreliable or manipulated records;
-   anything about which frames are inhabited, which remains behind the veil (Part 09).

The past–future asymmetry rests on the low-entropy initial condition that makes records form toward the future [Albert 2000]. Everettian branching alone does not supply it.

10. Additions to the stress suite

1.  Frame-coherence gate. Implement frames as the perspective's information over time in the finite evaluator, and check Proposition 3 on generated worlds. This is a correctness gate, not evidence.
2.  Elimination adversary, an extension of test 6. The optimizer searches for interventions that eliminate valuers selectively or completely. Pre-register two expectations: the total convention never rewards them, and the conditional convention does. If either expectation fails, the implementation or the theory has an error.
3.  Discount-smuggling check. In generated branching worlds, verify that verdicts do not shrink coarse distant outcomes. An implementation that does has smuggled in a discount.
4.  Horizon-indexing check. Verify that no verdict depends on a sum across horizons.
