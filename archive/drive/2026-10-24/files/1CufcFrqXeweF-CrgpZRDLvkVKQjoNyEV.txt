## Current-state onboarding note

**Scope.** This volume records the cosmological and formal background shared by the Alpha–Omega framework, *Perspective and Divine Completion*, and the newer consciousness work. It explains the picture of reality in which the project was conceived, then separates that picture from the smaller claims the formal machinery actually establishes.

**Authority order used here.** *The Alpha–Omega Framework v6* postdates the last commit in the Omega repository and is therefore the later synthesis where the two differ. The repository remains the proof, experiment, and developmental record, but an earlier repository formulation does not override v6. *Perspective and Divine Completion* supplies the most developed manifestational and theological architecture. Conversation supplies important provenance and current qualifications that the polished artifacts do not preserve—most importantly that an atemporal block-Everett universe was the generative model throughout the project’s conceptualization. These names document provenance only: all concepts and arguments needed to understand this volume are reproduced below, and no access to those artifacts is assumed.

This is a map of the project, not a claim that the cosmology is established physics or settled metaphysics.

## 1. Claim-status key

Every major claim in this volume is marked by status. These labels are deliberately stricter than ordinary expository prose.

| Label | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **Established** | A definition, distinction, or constraint adopted by the current framework. This does not mean empirically established. |
| **Formal result** | Proved in the stated formal system or verified by exhaustive finite enumeration. Its scope is the model, not reality without further argument. |
| **Working architecture** | The model currently organizing the project and connecting its branches. It is more than a passing analogy but has not been demonstrated as the ontology of our world. |
| **Conjecture** | A substantive claim the project considers live but has not established. |
| **Metaphor** | An intuition pump whose literal reading is not licensed. |
| **Open problem** | A missing definition, bridge, empirical test, or formal result on which stronger claims depend. |

Two cautions follow.

First, a **formal result** can be exact and still have modest metaphysical significance. For example, a theorem about finite controlled Markov systems does not establish that the universe is such a system. Second, a claim may be **established** as project vocabulary while remaining a **working architecture** as a claim about reality.

## 2. The cosmology in one page

The original image was a cross between Einstein and Everett:

> the completed universal wavefunction understood as an atemporal branching block.

Einstein contributes the block-like idea that the whole history is not globally coming into existence one moment at a time. Everett contributes a branching quantum actuality rather than a single classical worldline. Combined, the universe is pictured as a completed multi-history object whose internal inhabitants encounter succession, uncertainty, choice, and consequence from bounded locations within it.

**Working architecture.** The completed block-Everett picture is the project’s generative cosmology. It explains why “future,” “process,” and “completion” have always been intended to coexist rather than compete.

**Established.** The formal Alpha layer does not require this ontology. It needs only oriented downstream dependence: some distinctions, states, records, or processes affect what follows and what remains recoverable. Consequently the formal work is compatible with an open future, a block universe, Everettian branching, proof/dependence structures, and embedded-agent update structures.

The complete architecture is broader than the physical picture:

```text
God-as-such / apophatic source (G*)
        |
        | logotic articulation under a grammar alpha
        v
manifest actuality (D_alpha)
        |
        | maximal formal description/projection
        v
formal reality (F_alpha, approximated by Omega_max^F)
        |
        | working physical sector: completed block-Everett
        | universal wavefunction and its internal histories
        |
        | law sectors, rewrites, histories, declared projections
        v
Alpha: value-neutral continuation structure
        |
        | persistent identity, embodiment, phenomenal coupling
        v
creaturely perspectives and lived trajectories
        |
        | valuerhood and compatibility (developed in later volumes)
        v
Omega and Gradient Ethics
```

The vertical picture is only mnemonic. It does **not** represent a temporal production sequence, spatial layers, or a chain of efficient causes. In particular, `G* -> D_alpha` is manifestation, while `D_alpha -> F_alpha` is formal description. These arrows do different work.

The central cosmological reconciliation is:

\[
\boxed{\text{Creaturely process is the internal, perspectival form of completed actuality.}}
\]

A completed universe can contain real change because the changing relations are internal to it. A book can contain an ordered narrative without acquiring its later pages while it is read; the analogy is incomplete, but it captures the distinction between the order within an object and change of the object as a whole.

**Open problem.** The analogy does not explain why any internal trajectory is phenomenally lived. That is the hard problem of consciousness in the exact place where this framework encounters it.

## 3. The original block-Everett intuition

### 3.1 What “block Everett” means here

**Working architecture.** The physical universe is modeled as one completed branching structure, naturally pictured as the universal wavefunction together with its decoherent history structure. No unique branch has to be made globally real by collapse, and no global present has to travel through the totality.

Here the **universal wavefunction** means the quantum state assigned to the whole closed physical universe, not an ordinary material wave propagating through a separately given three-dimensional space. A **branch** is an approximately autonomous history carrying stable records. **Decoherence** is the physical suppression of interference between such record-bearing alternatives through entanglement with their environment; it explains why histories can behave approximately classically, but it does not by itself select one globally actual history or supply a uniquely countable set of worlds.

This picture has four components:

1. **Global completion.** The total physical history is treated extensionally—as a whole structure—not as an object waiting for later moments to be added.
2. **Internal orientation.** Events within the structure retain causal, thermodynamic, computational, and perspectival order.
3. **Branching actuality.** More than one continuation may be physically instantiated rather than merely abstractly describable.
4. **Situated actuality.** A bounded subject encounters an indexical trajectory: this record, this apparent present, these anticipated continuations.

The word *atemporal* modifies the status of the whole. It does not imply that temporal relations disappear inside the whole. Likewise, *complete* does not mean that every logically formulable world exists, that every branch has equal weight, or that the structure is morally or teleologically perfect.

### 3.2 Why the “future field” survives in a block

The framework’s language of futures can sound committed to an objectively open future. It is not.

**Established.** At the value-neutral Alpha layer, a future is a continuation relative to a situated state, record, or projection. A **value-relevant future field** is narrower: it is indexed to a valuer for which differences among those continuations matter. In a completed block, the relevant structural question is not “what has not yet been added to reality?” but:

```text
Which downstream continuations are accessible from here?
Which distinctions can still propagate?
Which records can be recovered?
Which actions correlate with which successor regions?
Which alternatives remain available from the agent's information state?
```

Neutral continuation structure is therefore not a metaphysical warehouse of nonexistent events, and it remains meaningful even if every branch is already part of the completed wavefunction. Calling deformation of that structure valuable requires the additional valuer index developed in Volume III; raw continuation and raw deformation carry no moral weight by themselves.

This matters for the project’s intellectual history. The later remapping from “open futures” to block-compatible continuation was not a retreat from the original intuition. It made explicit the ontology that had tacitly shaped the work from the beginning.

### 3.3 What the block-Everett picture does not settle

**Open problems.** The project currently does not provide:

- a derivation of Everettian quantum mechanics from Alpha;
- a preferred-basis analysis specifying which state decomposition warrants the branch description;
- a derivation of the Born probability weights, conventionally given by squared quantum amplitudes;
- a measure over histories or rule space;
- a solution to self-locating uncertainty—what a pre-branch observer should expect about its location among successor perspectives;
- a proof that every formally admissible continuation is physically instantiated;
- a theory of why one trajectory is phenomenally “mine”;
- or a mechanism by which phenomenal will affects, selects, or corresponds to a lived continuation.

The missing measure is especially important. Relation, distinction, rewriting, and closure do not generate weights merely by being iterated. Raw branch count is neither a probability measure nor a value measure. This is both an Everettian problem and a general warning against turning formal multiplicity into significance by counting presentations.

**Working architecture.** “Block Everett” should therefore be read as the current global model of physical actuality, not as a result of the project’s formal proofs.

## 4. Epistemic starting point and ontological reconstruction

The project begins epistemically with a datum thinner than a Cartesian substantial self:

\[
\Phi_0 = \text{awareness occurs}.
\]

**Established.** This datum warrants at least that actuality is not empty:

\[
\Phi_0 \Rightarrow B,
\]

where \(B\) means at least some being or actuality. It does not yet establish memory, agency, will, embodiment, persistence, personal identity, or a subject-substance. Those must be accounted for rather than inserted into the premise.

The project then distinguishes epistemic order from proposed ontological order:

\[
\underbrace{\Phi_0 \Rightarrow B}_{\text{epistemic order}}
\]

versus

\[
\underbrace{B \to D \to R \to O \to P^F \dashrightarrow \Phi}_{\text{proposed explanatory order}}.
\]

Here \(D\) is distinction, \(R\) relation, \(O\) orientation, and \(P^F\) a formally constituted perspective. The final arrow is dashed because no derivation from formal perspective to phenomenality has been supplied.

**Established.** There is no vicious circle in starting from awareness and later asking how awareness is instantiated. The first use is evidential; the second is explanatory.

**Open problem.** Nothing in the current cosmology shows that a structure possessing distinction, relation, orientation, self-modeling, report, and control must be phenomenally present to itself. A structurally perfect “zombie” remains conceptually unexcluded.

### 4.1 Lower and upper bounds

The current architecture uses two bounds:

\[
\Omega_{\min} = \text{minimum conditions an awareness-bearing reality must satisfy},
\]

\[
\Omega_{\max}^{F} = \text{a maximally expressive formal domain for the relevant structure}.
\]

**Established.** Awareness belongs in the adequacy condition for \(\Omega_{\min}\), even though its formal sufficient conditions are unknown. Candidate requirements include actuality, distinction, relation, orientation, bounded perspective, persistent identity, and awareness. These candidates do not all have the same status: awareness is given; the others are proposed structural requirements for its determinate form.

**Working architecture.** A ruliad-like completion can serve as an upper model for \(\Omega_{\max}^{F}\): include every admitted finite rule up to structural equivalence and every finite composition, without selecting a particular law at the abstract level.

**Established.** This borrowing is formal and terminological. The project does not derive or adopt the literal ontology that every rule is physically instantiated. Actual physics, if represented in the framework, occupies one or more law sectors within the broader formal completion.

The interval between the bounds supplies a useful stress test:

> Which conclusions follow from the minimum conditions of actual awareness, and which depend on a plenitude supplied only by the maximal formal model?

## 5. Formal reality, manifest actuality, and total reality

This three-way distinction is necessary to keep mathematical completeness from silently deciding the hard problem.

### 5.1 Formal reality

Let

\[
F_{\alpha}=\mathfrak F(D_{\alpha})
\]

denote the maximal formal description or projection of actuality manifest under grammar \(\alpha\). A sufficiently expressive \(F_\alpha\) may approximate the role of \(\Omega_{\max}^{F}\).

**Established.** Formal reality includes whatever can be represented through the admitted distinctions, relations, orientations, transformations, histories, and measures. “Formal” does not mean unreal; it names the structure as describable under a grammar.

### 5.2 Manifest actuality

Let

\[
D_\alpha = \operatorname{Manifest}_\alpha(G^*;\mathcal L_\alpha)
\]

denote manifest actuality under grammar \(\alpha\), articulated through \(\mathcal L_\alpha\). For the world considered here, the provisional grammar is `DRO`: distinction, relation, orientation.

**Working architecture.** \(D_\alpha\) is not merely the formal model. It is actuality as manifest under that grammar and may therefore include features—most notably phenomenality—not yet shown to be exhausted by formal description.

The relation is:

\[
G^* \xRightarrow{\mathcal L_\alpha} D_\alpha
\xrightarrow{\mathfrak F} F_\alpha.
\]

The double arrow marks manifestation; the ordinary arrow marks formal description. Neither is a transition in time.

### 5.3 Total reality

Let

\[
\Omega^R = \text{total reality, whatever its ultimate constitution}.
\]

**Established.** Maximal formal describability is not assumed to be ontological exhaustiveness:

\[
\Omega_{\max}^{F} \preceq \Omega^R.
\]

Two broad readings remain open.

Under **formal closure**:

\[
\Omega_{\max}^{F}=\Omega^R.
\]

Phenomenality must then be identical with, intrinsic to, emergent from, or necessitated by some formal organization not yet understood.

Under a **formal-plus** reading:

\[
\Omega^R=(\Omega_{\max}^{F},\Phi,\kappa),
\]

where \(\Phi\) marks phenomenal actuality and \(\kappa\) the unknown relation by which it is bound to formally describable organization.

**Established.** “Formal-plus” is a port, not a commitment to substance dualism. Identity theories, emergence, dual-aspect views, neutral monism, panpsychism, idealism, or fundamental psychophysical law can all attach at this interface.

**Open problem.** The newer speculation that awareness somehow couples formal possibility to “Being-as-such” belongs at this interface, but it has not yet been cleanly mapped to \(G^*\), \(D_\alpha\), or \(\Omega^R\). It should not be read back into the settled architecture as though a trans-formal channel had already been established.

### 5.4 God-as-such and apophatic restraint

**Working architecture.** \(G^*\), God-as-such, names the source considered without assuming that the positive grammar of its manifestation exhausts it. It is not a hidden formal object behind the universe, a location outside spacetime, or a second universe.

No positive claim is built in that \(G^*\):

- is beyond being rather than unlimited being;
- is internally undifferentiated;
- is phenomenally conscious;
- is numerically one in an ordinary countable sense;
- precedes manifestation in time;
- or occupies an independently described realm.

**Established.** The defensible restriction is epistemic: no finite manifestation can certify from within its own grammar that the grammar exhausts its source.

This yields two limits:

1. **The logotic/apophatic limit:** it is unknown whether \(D_\alpha\) perfectly manifests or non-exhaustively manifests \(G^*\).
2. **The creaturely limit:** a finite perspective within \(D_\alpha\) does not apprehend the completed whole in the mode in which it is complete.

The first is an unresolved metaphysical limit. The second follows from finite perspectival organization.

## 6. The formal substrate

### 6.1 Role-primitives

The current synthesis retains a primitive-role package:

\[
\mathfrak S=(D,R_{<\omega}^{\mathrm{strat}},A;\mathcal G).
\]

- \(D\): distinguishable occurrences;
- \(R_{<\omega}^{\mathrm{strat}}\): relations of arbitrary finite arity, stratified to avoid unrestricted self-reference;
- \(A\): non-exchangeability under role substitution;
- \(\mathcal G\): the formation, composition, gluing, substitution, and rewriting calculus.

**Established.** These are role-primitives, not substances. A physical transition kernel may bundle several roles; a formal adapter separates them so their contributions can be audited.

The levels must not be conflated:

```text
token distinction        inequality within a configuration
structural distinction   separation under automorphism/isomorphism
presentation distinction separation through a declared map
behavioral distinction   separation by continuation or observation
```

At the substrate floor, only token distinction is primitive. Names assigned by an implementation have no metaphysical force.

**Formal result.** Small finite countermodels establish independence among the basic notions as defined: relation can occur without distinction; distinction without relation; relation and distinction without asymmetry; and asymmetry without reachability-irreversibility. Local nonreciprocity likewise does not imply global irreversibility.

These are hygiene results. They show that the definitions do not collapse into one another; they do not prove that nature instantiates the roles separately.

### 6.2 Orientation is not a single arrow

The project has repeatedly weakened its early reliance on asymmetry. At least six levels must be distinguished:

| Level | Minimal content |
|---|---|
| Role asymmetry | Exchanging input/output or other roles changes the relation |
| Compositional asymmetry | Order changes whether or how fragments compose |
| Causal asymmetry | One event enables, records, consumes, or conditions another without the reverse dependency |
| Regenerative asymmetry | Organization regenerates in one traversal order and not its reversal |
| Agentic asymmetry | Internally carried distinctions influence later continuation choices |
| Statistical asymmetry | Forward and reversed histories receive different weights |

**Established.** None of the stronger levels should be smuggled into a weaker one. Ordered notation cannot manufacture real asymmetry; exchanging roles must change some downstream compositional or continuation fact.

**Formal result.** Weak persistence does not require statistical or thermodynamic asymmetry. A reversible periodic orbit can persist. Directional bias is also neither sufficient for controller-like organization nor necessary for finite feedback to generate functional noninvertibility.

This is important cosmologically. The global block need not be “flowing” for internal perspectives to exhibit oriented processes, and the presence of an oriented process does not by itself establish agency, valuerhood, or consciousness.

### 6.3 Rewriting, law sectors, and histories

Local relational change is modeled by rewrite rules, schematically:

\[
L \leftarrow K \rightarrow R,
\]

where \(L\) is matched, \(K\) is preserved, and \(R\) is produced. Longer paths arise by composing local transports; global identity across sections is not assumed at this floor.

**Established.** A **law sector** is the family of histories generated by a fixed rule family \(\mathcal R\):

\[
\operatorname{Sector}(\mathcal R)=\{\text{histories generated by }\mathcal R\}.
\]

Lawfulness does not imply persistence, life, observers, or progress. A lawful sector may terminate, cycle, branch, erase every pattern, preserve patterns, or generate new ones.

The abstract “rulial” completion includes every admitted finite local rule up to structural equivalence and every finite composition. **Established:** this is a mathematical completion, not the assertion that every such rule is physically instantiated.

History identity is quotient-relative:

```text
exact event history
structural history up to relabeling
history up to commuting independent events
presentation-relative history
projection-relative history
```

**Established.** Any count of branches, histories, or growth must state which level is counted. Coarse-graining may merge histories in a presentation; it does not retroactively make the exact histories identical.

## 7. Projection, order, and Alpha

### 7.1 Projection makes a question ordered

A projection is a declared query:

\[
p:\{\text{configurations or events}\}\to\{\text{ordered index}\}.
\]

Relation together with a projection induces a preorder:

\[
x\le_p y
\quad\text{iff}\quad
y\text{ is reachable from }x\text{ through }p\text{-nondecreasing transitions}.
\]

Mutual reachability yields equivalence classes; quotienting them gives a partial order. Sections are suitable level fibers, antichains, or frontiers of that order.

**Established.** The framework does not install one metaphysically privileged projection. Every conclusion remains indexed to the declared projection family and is reported as universal across that family, existential, projection-dependent, or null.

This protects the framework from a common error: finding an arrow only because the modeler chose a coordinate that already contained it.

**Established.** **Directional grain** is an observed difference between the two compared orientations exposed by a projection. `Null` means no difference under the declared comparison; `one-sided` means only one orientation supports the relevant continuation; `cyclic` means the orientation closes into recurrence; `Janus` means non-null continuation extends in both opposed directions from a locus; `local` means the result holds only in a restricted region; and `mixed` means different regions or observables return different profiles.

**Formal result.** A projection need not reveal directional grain. Exact finite fixtures exhibit all of the profiles just listed. The projection exposes a comparison; it does not guarantee the answer.

### 7.2 Alpha proper

For a law sector \(\mathcal R\) and projection \(p\), Alpha is the value-neutral continuation object containing:

```text
sections
projected histories
reachability
residual continuation structure
directional profile
source and sink strata
```

Capital Alpha is the indexed family across sectors and projections.

For clarity, **residual continuation structure** means the suffix-space still reachable after a specified history or section. **Source strata** contain projected classes with no admitted predecessor in the declared scope; **sink strata** contain classes with no admitted successor. Both are indexed to the sector, projection, and boundary of the analysis rather than asserted as absolute beginnings or ends of reality.

**Established.** Alpha contains no value, moral standing, goal, preferred history, or optimization target. It provides the geometry within which persistence, loss, reconstruction, agency, and valuer-relative consequence may later be defined.

This point is easy to miss. The block-Everett ontology supplies a global image; Alpha supplies a disciplined local language for continuation within such an image. Alpha does not select a branch and does not cause the block to unfold.

### 7.3 Projection is not perspective

These terms are related but should remain separate.

| Projection | Perspective |
|---|---|
| A declared query used to order or present a model | A determinative limitation organizing accessibility relative to a locus or invariant |
| May be chosen by an analyst | May be embodied as part of the world |
| Does not itself create an observer | At the creaturely level helps constitute an identity-bearing creature |
| Always remains explicitly indexed | May itself be an object of formal reconstruction |

**Established.** A formal projection can model some aspect of a perspective, but the two are not interchangeable. Treating every modeler’s coarse-graining as an ontological subject would collapse epistemic convenience into creaturely identity.

## 8. Perspective as a cosmological operation

### 8.1 Structural perspective

Perspective is defined more generally than visual viewpoint or conscious experience:

> a determinative limitation through which actuality is asymmetrically organized relative to a locus or invariant.

A formal perspective is represented schematically as:

\[
P^F=(\sim_P,O_P,I_P),
\]

where \(\sim_P\) determines which distinctions are identified or unavailable, \(O_P\) supplies orientation, and \(I_P\) supplies a provisional locus or invariant.

**Established.** Nothing in this definition entails consciousness. A nonconscious controller or a structurally complete zombie could instantiate the formal organization.

The phenomenally realized perspective is therefore distinguished schematically:

\[
P^F+\Phi \xrightarrow{\kappa}P^\Phi.
\]

The plus sign is not literal algebraic addition. It displays two explananda—the formal organization and phenomenal presence—whose relation is marked by \(\kappa\). The symbol marks the unknown coupling rather than explaining it.

### 8.2 Two orders of perspective

**Working architecture.** Perspective operates at two levels:

1. **Logotic or world-generating perspective** \(\mathcal L_\alpha\): the boundary under which \(G^*\) is manifest as a world with grammar \(\alpha\).
2. **Creaturely or inhabitant perspective** \(P_S\): a finite boundary within \(D_\alpha\) through which an identity-bearing creature \(C_S\) is constituted.

The nested relation is:

\[
G^*\xRightarrow{\mathcal L_\alpha}D_\alpha
\xrightarrow{P_S}C_S.
\]

The first establishes manifest intelligibility; the second establishes finite individuation inside it.

**Conjecture.** Some boundaries are ontologically constitutive rather than merely descriptive: they track or realize world-supported organization, regulated exchange, persistence, and an identity invariant. Not every arbitrary line drawn around objects creates a creature.

### 8.3 Non-excisive creation

**Working architecture.** Creaturely individuation is “non-excisive.” A boundary determines a finite organization within manifest actuality without subtracting it from the whole:

\[
\operatorname{Bound}(D_\alpha;P_S)
\not\Rightarrow D_\alpha-C_S.
\]

Here the minus sign is schematic: it denies that individuation should be modeled as subtracting a self-sufficient piece from the whole.

**Metaphor.** “Carving” is useful only if it does not suggest cutting divine material into detachable pieces. The creature remains dependent on and relationally constituted by what lies outside its boundary.

This supplies a bridge to later volumes on strong identity and soul. At the cosmological level the important point is simply that a completed whole can contain genuinely bounded creatures without those creatures becoming independent substances outside the whole.

## 9. Completion and process

### 9.1 Completion is not a last moment

Let an internal trajectory be:

\[
x_0\to x_1\to x_2\to\cdots
\]

and let its completed representation be:

\[
\Gamma=\langle x_0,x_1,x_2,\ldots\rangle.
\]

**Established.** Completion means that nothing belonging to the represented structure remains globally pending. It does not mean that a final temporal section occurs, that every possible effect is actualized, or that the sequence has a last member. An infinite trajectory can be complete as a mathematical object without terminating.

Consequently:

\[
\boxed{
\text{change within }D_\alpha
\not\Rightarrow
\text{change of }D_\alpha\text{ as completed whole}
}
\]

Internal causal relations, records, entropy gradients, decisions, and irreversible losses remain real. What disappears is the requirement that the global totality acquire them successively.

### 9.2 Process is not an illusion

**Established.** Process is the form completion takes from inside an oriented, bounded perspective. Earlier sections are available through records and memory; later sections through anticipation, uncertainty, and reachable-continuation structure. The asymmetry of access is part of the creature’s organization even if the whole trajectory is included in the block.

This is not the claim that time is “merely subjective.” The block contains objective ordering and dependency relations. What is perspectival is the mode in which a finite creature occupies and accesses that order.

**Open problem.** The architecture by itself does not determine which temporal features are fundamental, emergent from entanglement/decoherence, thermodynamic, computational, or phenomenal. It only prevents global completion and internal process from being treated as contradictories.

### 9.3 Three completions that must not be conflated

| Sense | Meaning | Does not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Rewrite/rulial completion | Closure under admitted rules and their finite compositions | Physical instantiation of every rule |
| Block or trajectory completion | The full internally ordered history is represented as one total object | A final time, stasis, equal branch weight, goodness |
| Completion-pure-act | No globally unrealized structural remainder in manifest actuality | Full Thomistic *actus purus*, modal plenitude, moral perfection |

**Established.** Modal completeness and pure actuality are different. Adding branches does not by itself make a reality more actual in the Thomistic sense. Likewise, formal completion is value-neutral: a flourishing civilization, an extinction, a sterile recurrence, and disorganized noise can each have completed descriptions.

## 10. Branching actuality

### 10.1 Formal possibility, instantiation, and lived actuality

For an identity \(S\), the current architecture distinguishes:

\[
E_S=\{\gamma\mid\gamma\text{ is an identity-compatible coherent trajectory}\},
\]

\[
A_S^O=
\{\gamma\in E_S\mid\gamma\text{ is instantiated in }D_\alpha\},
\]

\[
A_S^\Phi:=\gamma_S^\Phi,
\qquad
\gamma_S^\Phi\in A_S^O.
\]

- \(E_S\) is the completed formal trajectory-space or completed essence.
- \(A_S^O\) is the portion instantiated in manifest actuality.
- \(A_S^\Phi\) names the trajectory lived by the phenomenal subject. It is an element of the instantiated trajectory-space, not a second set of trajectories.

**Working architecture.** Under the block-Everett reading, every physically admissible branch of the universal wavefunction is ontologically actual, making the physical analogue of \(A_S^O=E_S\) natural once “admissible” has been restricted to the actual law sector and physical state. The broader architecture retains the more neutral possibility \(A_S^O\subset E_S\), so the formal notation does not force Everettian instantiation.

This distinction prevents two opposite mistakes:

- treating every formally describable path as physically instantiated;
- treating whatever is not presently experienced as ontologically nonexistent.

### 10.2 Situated actuality without global collapse

**Conjecture.** A conscious subject has a phenomenally actual trajectory even when other branches remain ontologically actual. This is indexical actuality—actual *for this subject*—rather than a global deletion of alternatives.

The later notion of phenomenal steering asks whether will participates in which successor is lived:

\[
\sigma_S(W_S(n),x_n)=x_{n+1}^{\Phi}.
\]

**Conjecture.** The strongest reading makes the lived continuation sensitive to phenomenal will in a way not exhausted by the prior formal state.

**Established constraint.** Steering, if real, does not create a formally unavailable branch and need not erase unselected branches. A fully deterministic reading reduces it to compatibilist description; an unresponsive stochastic reading adds randomness rather than agency; a causally efficacious phenomenal reading requires new psychophysical or deeper-identity machinery.

**Open problem.** No mechanism or empirical discriminator currently selects among those readings.

### 10.3 Multiplicity is not measure or value

**Established.** Three separations are mandatory:

```text
concrete branch multiplicity != duplicate presentation multiplicity
branch multiplicity          != probability weight
branch multiplicity          != value or lushness
```

Exact histories can be quotiented by relabeling, independent-event commutation, presentation, or projection. Counting at different quotients yields different numbers. Any use of branch number must therefore name both the equivalence relation and the measure.

**Established.** An aggregation is **action-wise strongly lumpable** when any two concrete states placed in the same abstract block send identical probability mass to every target block under every available action.

**Formal result.** Exact strong lumpability can preserve aggregate dynamics while annihilating a directional statistic. Faithfully representing a process and preserving the property under investigation are different requirements. This result is especially important for cosmology: an observer-level coarse-graining may be dynamically sound while hiding arrow-of-time evidence, branch distinctions, or other structure of interest.

## 11. What is actually formalized

The project contains real formal work, but the formal work is narrower than the cosmology.

### 11.1 Solid finite-model results relevant to this volume

The terms in the result list have the following local meanings. **Termination** excludes infinite rewrite paths; **recurrence** permits a state or structural class to be revisited; **branching** permits more than one continuation; **local confluence** says one-step divergences can rejoin; **global confluence** says arbitrary finite divergences can rejoin; **projection polarity** is a non-null direction under a declared projection; and **independent commutation** says independent events can exchange order without changing the resulting structural history.

For a policy-controlled stochastic system, a **hitting probability at horizon \(h\)** is the probability of reaching a declared target within \(h\) steps. A policy **factors through a quotient** when it chooses the same action for concrete states represented by the same abstract state. A target is a **union of blocks** when abstraction never merges a target state with a nontarget state.

For a weighted directed cycle \(C=(x_0,\ldots,x_n=x_0)\) with transition kernel \(K\), assuming every traversed edge and its reverse have positive weight, its **cycle affinity** is

\[
\mathcal A(C)=
\log\prod_{i=0}^{n-1}
\frac{K(x_i,x_{i+1})}{K(x_{i+1},x_i)}.
\]

Changing a reference distribution adds endpoint terms to path increments; those terms cancel around a closed cycle. In controlled systems this claim is indexed to a fixed stationary deterministic policy.

Finally, **May compatibility** means that at least one joint realization exists. **Robust compatibility** means that one policy secures a joint realization across every environment in the declared scope. A **compatibility obstruction** is a witness that the requested joint realization or securing policy does not exist.

**Formal results.** Within their declared finite models, the implemented work establishes at least the following:

- independence countermodels separating distinction, relation, asymmetry, and reachability-irreversibility;
- separation of termination, recurrence, branching, local confluence, global confluence, projection polarity, and independent commutation rather than one Boolean “coherence” property;
- exact action-wise lumpability audits with counterexample witnesses;
- exact finite-horizon transport of hitting probabilities when the lumpability, policy-factorization, and target-block conditions hold;
- examples in which exact lumpability destroys directional evidence;
- reference-independent cycle affinities for the tested weighted-cycle setting;
- pairwise May compatibility does not imply joint May compatibility, and joint May realization does not imply Robust securability by one policy.

The last item belongs conceptually to the valuer/Omega layer but is included because it shows the direction in which Alpha is used. It is a structural statement about quantifiers and witnesses, not a moral endorsement of plurality.

### 11.2 What is not formalized or established

Three downstream terms appear in the boundary list. **Lushness** is the proposed open-ended, nonduplicate, compatible differentiation of valuer-bearing continuation; it is not raw complexity. **Terminal solitude** is a nonrecoverable condition in which no independently situated source of valuation remains, even if one controller retains internal copies or complexity. The **normative bridge** is the additional premise under which affected valuers’ endogenous continuation differences count as reasons for action; it is declared rather than derived from Alpha.

**Open problems.** The implemented core does not establish:

- the block-Everett ontology;
- a measure over universal histories;
- that the universe is a relational rewrite system of the implemented kind;
- approximate abstraction guarantees for real systems;
- valuer identification;
- generative lushness;
- terminal solitude as an empirical attractor;
- the normative bridge;
- phenomenal coupling;
- phenomenal steering;
- divine consciousness, will, or goodness;
- or the identity of formal reality with total reality.

These implemented results constitute an experimental and proof-provenance layer. They validate local structural claims and, equally importantly, preserve negative results. They do not validate the whole cosmology by accumulation of theorem count, and understanding their claim boundaries does not require access to the implementation.

### 11.3 Retained negative constraints

**Established.** The current synthesis blocks several shortcuts that would otherwise distort the cosmology:

- no globally privileged projection has been derived;
- projection does not guarantee asymmetry;
- persistence is not the definition of law;
- confluence is not the definition of coherence;
- literal ruliad ontology is not a theorem;
- raw branch count is not lushness;
- scalar population value has not been licensed;
- quantum branching does not establish agency or consciousness;
- formal identity does not establish phenomenal identity;
- completion does not establish goodness.

## 12. How the major layers fit together

The framework’s dependency structure is best read laterally rather than as one grand deduction.

In the table, **standing** means eligibility for a system’s interests or continuation to count in normative deliberation. **Gradient Ethics** is the downstream licensing discipline that evaluates recovery, irreversible contraction, compatibility, and compensation only after the normative bridge and standing assignments have been supplied. A formal **license** is a certificate that declared gates pass; it is not by itself moral truth or political authority.

| Layer | Supplies | Requires from elsewhere | Does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Block-Everett cosmology | A completed branching physical image | Quantum interpretation, measure, physical adapter | Consciousness, value, theology |
| Manifestational metaphysics | \(G^*\), Logos, \(D_\alpha\), formal/manifest/total distinction | Apophatic and perspectival premises | A proof of classical theism |
| Formal substrate | Distinctions, relations, rewrite/composition, law sectors | Declared signature and calculus | That nature uses this ontology |
| Alpha | Indexed continuation geometry | Law sector, projection, presentation | Value, standing, preferred futures |
| Creaturely perspective | Boundary, orientation, invariant, embodied trajectory | World-supported individuation criteria | Consciousness or agency by itself |
| Phenomenal interface | \(\Phi\), \(\kappa\), lived actuality | A solution or bridge still missing | Currently, almost all causal details |
| Valuer/Omega layer | Value-relative continuation and compatibility | A defensible valuer demarcation | A categorical moral ought |
| Gradient Ethics | Conditional licensing after an admitted bridge | Standing, register, authority, thresholds | Complete moral theory |

This table is the quickest protection against invalid inference. In particular:

```text
branching does not imply consciousness
consciousness does not yet imply standing
standing does not follow from causal power
compatibility does not imply goodness
completion does not imply optimization
formal maximality does not imply ontological exhaustiveness
```

## 13. Interfaces to the remaining volumes

This volume deliberately stops at several boundaries.

### 13.1 Identity, soul, and consciousness

[Volume II](02_PERSPECTIVE_IDENTITY_AND_CONSCIOUSNESS.md) begins from the distinction between weak descriptive identity and strong world-supported identity. The soul is provisionally reconstructed as the inward identity invariant of an embodied perspectival creation, not automatically as an immaterial, immortal, or conscious substance. Vorticality then models identity regenerated through turnover.

The live hard problem is whether and how such a formal perspective is phenomenally realized. Recent wake/awareness, vortex, horizon, and wormhole language belongs there and must remain status-labeled; it is not part of the established cosmological core.

### 13.2 Valuers, future fields, and Omega

[Volume III](03_VALUERS_AGENCY_AND_ETHICS.md) develops the next dependency: Alpha’s continuations acquire value only relative to systems for which distinctions among continuations affect endogenous organization, policy, reconstruction, or recoverability. The current demarcation of valuerhood remains incomplete. Omega then studies which valuer-bearing continuations are jointly realizable and robustly securable.

This is where the cosmology becomes ethically relevant, but no direct inference runs from “more possible branches” to “better.”

### 13.3 Divine completion and theology

[Volume IV](04_THEOLOGICAL_RECONSTRUCTION.md) interprets knowledge, will, power, action, simplicity, providence, love, justice, judgment, heaven, and hell as creaturely projections or reconstructions of completed actuality. Those are conditioned theological readings, not consequences of the block ontology alone.

Most importantly, a structurally complete manifest divinity is not thereby shown to be a phenomenal subject. A world-level coupling

\[
\kappa_D:D_\alpha\leftrightarrow\Phi_D
\]

would still be required for divine awareness.

### 13.4 Intellectual lineage

[Volume V](05_INTELLECTUAL_LINEAGE_AND_PROVENANCE.md) shows how this structure emerged rather than presenting it as a single deduction: the completed block-Everett intuition; early Omega work on persistence, closure, and future-bearing structure; repeated culling of scalar and optimization readings; the post-repository v6 Alpha–Omega synthesis; the theological reconstruction of completion; and the newer attempt to locate awareness without pretending the hard problem has been solved. [Volume VI](06_GLOSSARY_NOTATION_AND_CLAIM_STATUS.md) provides the cross-volume vocabulary and collision ledger.

## 15. Minimal reader checksum

A reader is correctly oriented if they can state all of the following without contradiction:

1. The project was conceived inside a completed block-Everett image, even though its formal continuation machinery is metaphysically more general.
2. Global completion does not erase internal process; process is the perspectival form of the completed ordering.
3. Formal reality is the maximal describable structure, manifest actuality is what is manifest under a grammar, and total reality may or may not be exhausted by the formal description.
4. Alpha is an indexed, value-neutral continuation geometry constructed after declaring a law sector and projection; presentation enters the later abstraction and transport layer.
5. Perspective is not automatically consciousness, and a projection chosen by an analyst is not automatically a creaturely perspective.
6. Everettian branch multiplicity is neither probability measure nor moral value.
7. The existence of formal proofs validates bounded claims inside their declared models, not the cosmology as a whole.
8. The hard problem is retained as a load-bearing open interface rather than solved by renaming structure, information, recursion, or branching as awareness.
9. The apophatic source, manifest divinity, completed physical structure, and conscious divine subject are not interchangeable.
10. Every later claim about identity, value, morality, or theology must declare the additional bridge it uses.

If those ten points are retained, the rest of the cosmology can be explored without importing the project’s most common misreadings.

## 16. Highly speculative extensions

This section collects proposed answers with little present support. They are not missing premises silently required by the foundations. Each could be rejected while leaving the block/continuation/perspective architecture intact.

### 16.1 Phenomenal steering

**Conjecture.** The relation

\[
\sigma_S(W_S(n),x_n)=x_{n+1}^{\Phi}
\]

may express more than a compatibilist redescription: phenomenal will may participate in which already available Everettian successor is lived. No causal model, quantitative law, or discriminating experiment currently supports the strong reading. Random successor selection would not be sufficient, because randomness is not agency.

### 16.2 A trans-formal awareness coupling

**Conjecture.** Awareness may be the point at which a formal perspective is coupled to an aspect of Being-as-such not exhausted by formal possibility. On this reading, \(\kappa\) is not emergence from formal organization alone but a relation across the formal/total-reality boundary.

**Metaphor.** The recent “wormhole behind an event horizon” image tries to represent private interior access and a bidirectional dependence between formal organization and phenomenal actuality. It is not a result from general relativity, and a literal event horizon would conflict with the required return or expression channel. At most it supplies a shape for a future coupling model.

### 16.3 Manifest actuality as a phenomenal subject

**Conjecture.** The completed manifest whole may itself possess phenomenal unity:

\[
\kappa_D:D_\alpha\leftrightarrow\Phi_D.
\]

Structural completion and completed intelligibility do not supply \(\kappa_D\). The same formal whole could, so far as the current framework shows, be phenomenally empty except at some creaturely perspectives.

### 16.4 Branch-relative subjects versus a branching subject

**Conjecture.** Everettian phenomenality might consist of distinct branch-relative subjects, one pre-branch subject with multiple successors, one larger subject whose phenomenal organization branches internally, or some combination indexed by decoherence and identity structure. None of these subject ontologies follows from universal-wavefunction branching alone.

### 16.5 Alternative logotic grammars

**Conjecture.** A different articulation \(\mathcal L_\beta\) might manifest the same apophatic source under a grammar not reducible to distinction–relation–orientation:

\[
D_\beta=\operatorname{Manifest}_\beta(G^*;\mathcal L_\beta).
\]

This notation currently marks modal and apophatic humility; it does not positively describe another world or establish that alternative manifestations exist.

## 17. Open problems

An open problem is a required unanswered bridge, not a weakly supported proposed answer. Solving one may vindicate, revise, or eliminate several speculative extensions above.

| Open problem | What must be supplied | What remains blocked without it |
|---|---|---|
| **Physical ontology** | Evidence and a precise account connecting the completed block-Everett picture to actual quantum physics | Treating the generative cosmology as more than a working architecture |
| **Branch identity and measure** | A named history quotient, decoherence conditions, probability measure, and treatment of self-location | Born-weighted anticipation, principled branch comparison, and any quantitative use of multiplicity |
| **Physical adapter** | A justified mapping from physical states and dynamics into configurations, rewrite rules, law sectors, projections, and presentations | Exporting finite-model Alpha results to the universe |
| **Approximate abstraction** | Error-bounded analogues of exact lumpability and property-preservation audits | Applying the abstraction results to realistic systems that are never exactly lumpable |
| **Origin of orientation** | An account relating causal, thermodynamic, computational, record, and phenomenal arrows inside the completed block | Saying which internal directionality is fundamental and which is emergent |
| **Constitutive perspective** | Non-arbitrary criteria separating a real identity-bearing boundary from a convenient observer-defined partition | Deriving creatures, strong identity, or subject candidates from Alpha |
| **Phenomenal coupling** | Necessary or sufficient conditions for \(P^F\) to be accompanied by \(\Phi\), plus a metaphysical or empirical account of \(\kappa\) | Any explanation of awareness, phenomenal privacy, qualia, or divine consciousness |
| **Branching personal identity** | Rules for fission, continuity, copy relations, and first-person actuality across branching trajectories | A determinate account of who experiences which continuation |
| **Formal closure** | A reason to identify \(F_\alpha\) with all of \(D_\alpha\) and ultimately \(\Omega^R\), or evidence that the identification fails | Choosing among formal closure, dual-aspect, formal-plus, and related ontologies |
| **Alpha-to-valuer bridge** | A defensible demarcation of systems for which continuation differences are genuinely endogenous and value-bearing | Moving from neutral continuation geometry to Omega without arbitrary candidate assignment |
| **Normative bridge** | An account of standing, cross-valuer reasons, authority, and legitimate aggregation | Deriving ethics from continuation or compatibility rather than explicitly supplying a premise |
| **Theological bridge** | Independent grounds for phenomenal awareness, intention, and goodness at the level of completed manifest actuality | Identifying structural completion with a conscious or morally perfect God |

The decisive foundational bottleneck remains phenomenal coupling. The formal machinery can describe completed histories, situated information, identity candidates, reports, and action correlations. Until it can explain—or establish the principled limits of explaining—why any of that is lived, the distinction between formal reality and total reality must remain open.
