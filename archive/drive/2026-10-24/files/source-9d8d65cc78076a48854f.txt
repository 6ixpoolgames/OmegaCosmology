This volume distinguishes six kinds of statement throughout:

- **Formal result**: a theorem checked from explicit definitions and hypotheses, usually in Lean.
- **Model-bounded result**: an exhaustive finite counterexample, retained computational witness, or empirical result inside a declared instrument. It establishes a possibility or non-implication, not a universal fact about reality.
- **Defined**: a project object has a precise intended meaning, but its physical or normative adequacy has not thereby been established.
- **Highly speculative extension**: a proposed answer or mechanism with little direct support.
- **Open problem**: a required unanswered bridge, without commitment to any proposed answer.
- **Rejected / demoted**: a move the project has tested and no longer treats as foundational.

The distinction matters. Omega has accumulated substantial formal machinery, but most of that machinery is conditional: it proves what follows once a boundary, fact language, policy class, uncertainty set, repair surface, or compatibility relation has been supplied. It does not make those declarations morally authoritative merely by processing them correctly.

The authority statements above settle conflicts in the historical record; they are not prerequisites for the reader. All concepts and arguments needed for this volume are stated here. The final source map is provenance-only: it identifies where a claim entered the project and where its formal evidence is stored.

## 1. The shortest accurate account

The framework begins from a simple dependency:

```text
No valuers -> no value.
No viable continuation -> no durable valuers.
No consequence-bearing distinctions -> no recoverable continuation.
```

The framework's organizing commitment is that futures are not valuable in the abstract. They matter only with respect to valuers: processes for which different continuations make a difference to continued organization, correction, action, reconstruction, or **value contact**, meaning the capacity to encounter, register, create, compare, or be affected by what matters. This is a starting commitment, not a theorem extracted from physics. Alpha and the lower continuation machinery study which differences alter what can follow, which representations erase those differences, and which losses can be repaired. Omega can then be evaluated relative to an explicitly supplied candidate-valuer family; it does not identify the correct family by itself.

The active conceptual tower is:

```text
Primitive-facing substrate
  relation, distinction, asymmetry

Alpha
  law-sector- and projection-indexed, value-neutral continuation

Agency
  endogenous selection, control, feedback, maintained alternatives

Valuer
  a process for which continuation differences matter from its own organization

Omega
  witness-bearing May/Robust compatibility, policy atlases, obstructions

Gradient Ethics
  constraints on transformations of valuer-bearing continuation
```

The two names have specific roles. For a supplied law sector and projection, **Alpha** is the value-neutral continuation object containing sections, projected histories, reachability, residual continuation, directional profile, and source/sink strata. Capital Alpha is the indexed family of these objects. **Capital Omega** is the full decorated compatibility structure over supplied candidate families: compatible faces, complete realization fibers, May and Robust relations, local policy atlases, obstructions, and transports across presentations. It is not one selected maximum, one controller, or a validated moral optimum.

The downward direction is an architectural requirement rather than a completed general theorem: anything counted as a valuer should leave agentic and continuation-level traces, and those should leave lower relational traces. The full tower has not been proved for arbitrary substrates. The upward direction is not canonical. Relation, distinction, recurrence, feedback, or causal leverage alone do not uniquely determine an agent, a valuer, a moral patient, or a consciousness.

This is the governing anti-smuggling rule:

> A lower-layer signal may be necessary for a higher-layer object without being sufficient to identify it.

The formal program is strongest at the Continuation layer. Agency is an active realization target. Valuerhood remains incompletely demarcated. Moral standing and the ultimate authority of the normative bridge remain open.

Four recurring technical words should be fixed at the outset:

- A **boundary** is a declared system/environment partition constrained by causal and informational regularities; it is not assumed to be the one ontologically true partition.
- A **presentation** is a declared representation, observation, quotient, or coarse-graining of a system.
- A **register** is the explicit collection of facts, requirements, orders, or candidate assignments a formal procedure is allowed to use.
- A **certificate** is evidence that a declared presentation or claim satisfies specified bridge conditions. Certification is conditional assurance, not metaphysical or moral authority.

## 2. Futures only matter relative to valuers

An earlier and more naive reading treated any deformation of reachable futures as value-relevant. That is not the mature position.

The field-deformation program begins below value because the project refuses to assume the correct agent, self, or boundary in advance. It measures neutral changes to reachable continuation first. This is methodological instrumentation, not a claim that every reachable state, branch, or deformation matters morally.

The relevant ladder is:

```text
neutral continuation structure
-> candidate bounded processes
-> evidence of endogenous maintenance and action
-> candidate valuers
-> valuer-indexed continuation fields
-> joint compatibility and recovery
-> normative licensing, if the bridge is supplied
```

A rock, dam, hurricane, institution, platform, optimizer, or civilization may be a large deformer: its presence can radically alter what remains possible for other processes. That fact does not by itself make it a valuer. Conversely, a valuer does not cease to matter because it has little causal leverage. Deformation size measures causal consequence, not standing.

The current lower stack can see two different things:

1. how a process changes reachable continuation; and
2. how actions change facts registered as relevant to a candidate process.

It cannot yet derive, without additional realization and standing arguments, why a particular process or registered fact is a genuine bearer of value.

### 2.1 Continuation rather than primitive time

“Future” is the temporal presentation of a broader object: continuation. A continuation may be a transition, path, derivation, completion, composition, perturbation response, repair route, or admissible unfolding. This allows the framework to remain compatible with open-future, block-universe, and Everettian readings. The formal burden is oriented dependence, not metaphysical openness:

```text
some distinctions affect what follows;
some losses alter what remains recoverable;
some actions alter what later updates can still certify.
```

Within an atemporal block-Everett interpretation—a completed branching universe with no privileged moving present—every branch may belong to the total structure while a situated valuer still has a branch-relative, information-relative, and resource-relative continuation field. The nontrivial object is not total Being growing or shrinking. It is which continuations remain accessible, reconstructible, controllable, jointly realizable, or nonrecoverably foreclosed from the valuer's situated boundary.

### 2.2 A valuer-indexed future field

The phrase **future field** should be read as a structured continuation surface indexed by at least:

```text
valuer or candidate process;
boundary / presentation;
current information state;
available actions or policies;
resource and repair budget;
horizon;
uncertainty model;
fact or attribute language.
```

It is not a literal physical field and it is not a detached God's-eye scalar. “Future-field relativity” names the proposal that third-person objectivity should be sought in invariant or covariant structure across situated frames, rather than by pretending that one unindexed valuer frame exists.

The repository's Future Field Atlas is narrower. It is a finite reachable-frontier morphology instrument. It measures topology, rank boundaries, transport, and product-versus-coupled support. It does not detect value, valuers, agency, identity, capture, compatibility in the full semantic sense, or Omega.

## 3. What is a valuer?

Valuerhood is the central unresolved demarcation problem.

The post-repo synthesis gives two formulations that should be read as stages rather than synonyms.

The thin functional formulation is:

> A valuer system is a coherent, bounded physical process whose interaction with its environment contributes to maintaining its continued organization.

The stronger continuation formulation is:

> A process becomes substantively valuer-like when distinctions among its reachable continuations affect its own organization, policy, correction, or reconstruction.

The current formal target is stronger still. It treats a candidate valuer as a temporally extended process-bundle with evidence for:

- persistence through perturbation and presentation change;
- action channels with counterfactual effects on continuation;
- internal differentiation among possible continuations;
- robustness against one-step, brittle, or externally maintained false positives;
- correction, learning, reproduction, or other generative continuation;
- compatibility with other valuer-like trajectories.

These formulations are not yet fully reconciled. In particular, compatibility may be a condition on how a valuer continues among others rather than a condition of being a valuer at all. Likewise, standing may be something a valuer acquires, something partly constitutive of the term, or an independent normative relation. Current documents use all three readings. Cleanup should preserve the ambiguity rather than silently choose one.

### 3.1 A candidate is not yet a valuer

A **candidate** is any trackable pattern admitted into the testing envelope relative to a declared projection—that is, a declared way of querying or carving the system. Admission grants no automatic standing. Here **standing** means the normative status by which a process's continuation supplies reasons that others must consider; the current formalism does not derive it. The point of broad candidate admission is to avoid excluding unfamiliar value-bearing organization before the relevant tests can be run.

Candidate evidence may include:

- endogenous records;
- self-maintenance beyond a passive or externally carried null;
- policy sensitivity;
- causal reach;
- reconstruction after perturbation;
- action-dependent deformation of its own continuation;
- retention of correction channels;
- lineage or process coherence across turnover.

The candidate envelope is deliberately permissive. Its verdicts should be sensitivity-tested across boundary assignments and thresholds.

### 3.2 Soft pan-valuerhood

**Soft pan-valuerhood** is not the claim that every object is equally a valuer. It is a rule of methodological admission:

```text
admit broadly;
assign no standing automatically;
grade by endogenous consequence;
test robustness across candidate assignments.
```

A rock may enter the candidate envelope and then show almost no evidence of endogenous maintenance, policy-sensitive continuation, or reconstruction. A simple controller may exhibit a thin valuation profile. An organism, institution, or artificial system may exhibit a richer but still contestable profile.

This should also be distinguished from panpsychism. Panpsychism is a hypothesis about phenomenality. Pan-valuerhood is a permissive policy about which organized processes may deserve valuer analysis. One does not imply the other.

### 3.3 Consciousness and valuerhood

It remains possible that unified consciousness emerges only in systems already exhibiting valuerhood: systems with bounded, temporally extended, self-conditioned continuation. That would make valuer organization a possible precondition for a macro-subject.

It is not presently possible to exclude panpsychist or microphenomenal alternatives. On such views, phenomenal properties may be ubiquitous while a unified, identity-indexed subject emerges only when a sufficiently organized valuer binds them. A rock need not be one conscious subject even if its constituents have microphenomenal aspects.

The framework therefore should not define valuerhood by consciousness, nor consciousness by valuerhood, until the combination, binding, and awareness problems are resolved. Consciousness may mark a distinct mode in which value-relevant structure is manifest from within. That does not yet establish categorical priority over nonconscious valuers or nonconscious structures that sustain many valuers.

### 3.4 The anti-proliferation problem

If valuerhood is made as cheap as persistence, disposition, or causal difference, soft pan-valuerhood collapses into arbitrary micro-valuer proliferation. Any decomposition could manufacture vast populations of nominal valuers. This would reintroduce raw branch or state counting through the back door.

The demarcation therefore has to be invariant enough to resist:

- arbitrary boundary refinement;
- duplicate presentations;
- externally maintained persistence;
- transient optimization shadows;
- internal simulations being counted as independent valuers;
- a dominant controller choosing the grammar under which its subsystems count as a population.

No current theorem fully solves this.

## 4. Agency, identity, and vorticality

Agency is treated as graded, substrate-neutral, and downstream of bare persistence.

At minimum, an agency realization requires more than being pushed through a trajectory. The process must have endogenous selection or control such that its own organization makes a counterfactual difference to which continuations remain available. Stronger agency adds feedback, maintenance of the selection channel, model-mediated correction, and the capacity to preserve the future availability of the loop itself.

An efficient layer description is:

```text
raw optionality:
  reachable alternatives

viable optionality:
  alternatives remaining inside constraints

robust optionality:
  viable alternatives across uncertainty

controlled optionality:
  alternatives selectable through action or policy

maintained optionality:
  controlled alternatives retained while preserving the control/correction loop

protected optionality:
  maintained alternatives belonging to certified value-capable continuation

joint protected optionality:
  compatible protected alternatives across multiple continuations
```

This ladder blocks the shortcut “more options equals more value.” Noise, domination, chaos, and power-seeking may expand raw or controlled optionality while destroying protected joint continuation.

### 4.1 Vorticality

**Vorticality** names organizational identity regenerated through constituent turnover:

```text
constituents change;
causal organization reconstructs the pattern;
records and effects remain attributable across the trajectory.
```

The trajectory, not a permanent material token, is the candidate bearer of identity. Petri-net notions illustrate the intended distinctions:

- a **P-invariant** is exactly conserved, usually too strong for living organization;
- a **trap** remains marked once marked, persistence without conservation;
- a **siphon** remains empty once emptied, a model of structural extinction;
- a **T-invariant** regenerates a marking through a cycle of consumption and production.

Vorticality is closer to autopoiesis and organizational closure than to literal fluid rotation. It is a bridge candidate, not proof of observerhood, agency, consciousness, valuerhood, soul, or standing.

Current formal prose therefore prefers colder terms:

```text
closed-loop deformer profile;
cross-boundary circulation witness;
reflexive maintenance profile;
lineage or process coherence;
correction-register preservation.
```

The vortex, black-hole, white-hole, singularity, and wormhole language remains useful as a generator in the theological/consciousness branch. It is quarantined from formal Omega claims.

### 4.2 Operational causal diamond and deformer profile

The current agency-facing instrument asks whether a live action-observation-update loop preserves a continuation profile that a matched open-loop or replay condition does not, while preserving the future availability of that loop.

A **deformer** is more general. It is any system whose state, policy, infrastructure, or continued presence changes the reachable continuation of other systems. Organisms, civilizations, platforms, technologies, institutions, memes, and optimizers may all be large deformers. Deformation is a causal-effect category, not a moral or ontological category.

The live profile is typed rather than scalar. It may include:

- adaptive-kernel membership;
- observation and update soundness;
- feedback advantage over replay;
- reflexive maintenance;
- recovery or loss of correction capacity;
- effects on other systems' continuation and repair fields.

No scalar deformer score can replace that profile without declaring which axes and tradeoffs matter.

### 4.3 Branch-point capacity

A branch point is an intervention whose effect on continuation is disproportionate and nonrecoverable: it collapses many still-available trajectories into a much narrower successor region. A second qualitative transition in agency occurs when a process can reliably create, prevent, or control such branch points, including by constructing persistent technological, informational, or institutional structures.

Branch-point capacity is structural, not moral. It raises the stakes of error because the deformer can alter the processes that generate future agency itself. It also creates an identification problem: decisive agents and interventions may not be recognizable in advance. This supports caution under uncertainty, but it does not create standing from causal leverage.

## 5. Irreversibility and recovery

Irreversibility is presently the most promising formal hook connecting physical process, valuer continuation, and ethical constraint. It is not a self-interpreting moral primitive.

Three senses must be separated:

1. **Thermodynamic irreversibility**: entropy production and the consumption of free-energy gradients.
2. **Logical irreversibility**: many-to-one information loss, record erasure, or non-injective update.
3. **Omega nonrecoverability**: the loss of a registered continuation fact with no admissible repair route within a declared horizon, action set, resource budget, and presentation.

The third is the current formal object. Thermodynamic and logical irreversibility may supply mechanisms or costs, but they do not determine which lost facts matter to a valuer.

### 5.1 Negentropic consumption as marker

Sustained active maintenance in an open, nonequilibrium process ordinarily consumes usable free energy and produces entropy. Negentropic consumption is therefore a promising physical marker of irreversible throughput and an enabling condition for many forms of active maintenance. This is a heuristic research lead, not an established sufficient or necessary test: dormant processes, externally supported systems, and unusual substrates prevent the inference from being universal.

It is not sufficient for valuerhood. Flames, hurricanes, stars, refrigerators, chemical dissipative structures, parasites, tumors, and malware consume gradients or preserve organization. Some may exhibit thin agency-like or valuation-like properties; none acquires standing from throughput alone.

The more discriminating question is counterfactual:

> Does the organized activity of the process use irreversible throughput to maintain or reconstruct the very capacities by which its continuation remains differentiated and actionable?

A matched passive, ablated, randomized, or externally carried null is needed. If the organization can be removed without changing persistence or recovery, the apparent self-maintenance was environmental carriage rather than endogenous work.

### 5.2 Recovery frame

The formal recovery interface declares:

```text
states;
actions and transition relation;
registered repair actions;
facts and fact species;
horizon.
```

It then defines:

```text
RecoverableWithin(f, h):
  fact f can be restored in exactly h registered repair steps.

RecoverableUpTo(f, h):
  fact f can be restored in at most h registered repair steps.

NonrecoverableContraction(f):
  f held before;
  f fails after;
  f is not RecoverableUpTo within the declared frame.
```

This notion is necessarily indexed. A fact may be recoverable with a larger repair repertoire, more time, more resources, a finer presentation, or assistance from another process. It may be globally encoded while locally inaccessible. “Irreversible” without these indices is too strong.

### 5.3 Correction registers

A **correction register** is the declared collection of distinctions needed for later comparison, probing, model revision, or repair. A process can preserve coarse task success while destroying the machinery that would let it recognize and correct future error.

The formal pattern is:

```text
same task success;
different future correction capacity;
lost correction capacity not recoverable under registered repair.
```

This is called **correction-register collapse**, **nonrecoverable revision-capacity loss**, or **nonrecoverable correction-capacity contraction**. “Self-lobotomy” remains an internal evocative alias and should not be used as a theorem name or public classification.

### 5.4 Phantom recovery and reflection

A corrupted or overly coarse recovery frame may erase the very distinction showing that repair failed. It then reports recovery where the true frame does not. This produces **phantom recoverability**, and a decision gate relying on it can produce a **phantom license**.

The positive safeguard is recovery-frame reflection:

```text
every bounded recovery accepted by the believed frame
must also be accepted by the true frame.
```

When reflection holds, recovery-aware licenses transport. When it fails, the abstraction may counterfeit safety.

### 5.5 Irreversibility is weight, not moral sign

The sacrifice/cancer stress witness supplies a finite formal counterexample to one tempting identification. In the declared toy frame, one move is locally nonrecoverable but jointly recoverable and allowed by the joint corridor, while another preserves a local fact but destroys a joint fact and is refused.

Within the definitions used by that witness, it follows that:

```text
local nonrecoverable contraction != harm
self-preservation != compatibility
irreversibility != moral wrongness
```

Irreversibility marks the seriousness and non-substitutability of a transformation relative to a profile. Its sign depends on what was lost, what larger process it belonged to, whether the joint field survives, and which standing relation is legitimate. Apoptosis, surgery, commitment, learning, individuation, and correction all involve irreversible loss. So do extinction, capture, and destructive optimization.

This is the same reason valence cannot serve as a moral sign.

## 6. Compatibility: coexistence is not pairwise agreement

For a candidate family \(F\), let \(\mathrm{Real}(F)\) be the complete set of histories or configurations jointly realizing it.

The framework separates two quantifier structures:

\[
\mathrm{May}(F) \iff \mathrm{Real}(F) \neq \varnothing
\]

\[
\mathrm{Robust}_{\mathcal E}(F)
\iff
\exists \pi\,\forall e\in\mathcal E,
\ \mathrm{outcome}(\pi,e)\in\mathrm{Real}(F).
\]

**May** asks whether the family can coexist. **Robust** asks whether one policy can keep it coexisting across every declared contingency. The witnesses are retained: May stores realizing histories; Robust stores policies and their runs.

### 6.1 Worked example: three towns and one river

Suppose three towns—A, B, and C—draw from one river. Treat the continued functioning of each town as a candidate requirement. Admitting the towns as candidates does not yet prove equal standing; it simply makes their continuation visible to the analysis.

In a wet year there may be an allocation under which all three survive. Then:

\[
\mathrm{May}(\{A,B,C\})
\]

holds. If a smaller river can sustain any two towns but never all three, every pair is May-compatible while the triple is not. Inspecting pairs alone cannot discover the three-way obstruction.

Now introduce wet and dry contingencies and a water authority that must act before it can tell which year it faces. Perhaps release-then-store preserves all three in the wet case, while store-then-release preserves all three in the dry case. Each contingency has a successful policy, but the policies are opposites and the observation available at decision time is identical. Then all three towns are jointly realizable, yet no single policy robustly secures them across both environments.

If two local policies together cover the contingencies, the policy atlas has covering number \(\kappa=2\). This proves a requirement for complementary policy behavior relative to the declared interface; it does not prove that two independent authorities or political pluralism are morally required.

The authority could instead dam and homogenize the river so that only one contingency remains. Robust control becomes easier and \(\kappa\) may fall to one. The formal compatibility calculation records an improvement unless the erased environmental variation or affected valuers are included in the register. This is the **attenuation problem**.

Finally, A could absorb the institutions of B and C while leaving three populated settlements. If all policies, claims, and correction channels now express A's will and independent re-formation is impossible, headcount still reports three while the stronger criterion reports terminal solitude. This is why independent situation, capture, and recoverability matter in addition to nominal diversity.

The example carries structural distinctions through one case. It does not establish which town has standing, whether the dam is wrong, or who may resist absorption.

### 6.2 Established structural results

Formal laws together with retained finite counterexamples establish the following model-relative properties and non-implications:

- adding candidates can only shrink realization fibers;
- widening the environment can only shrink robust securing-policy sets;
- Robust implies May;
- pairwise May compatibility need not imply joint compatibility;
- pairwise Robust compatibility need not imply joint Robust compatibility;
- joint realizability need not imply joint robust securability;
- every context may admit some securing policy while no single policy secures every context.

The hollow-triangle witness shows that compatibility is **non-flag**: every pair can coexist while the triple cannot. Pairwise certification is therefore structurally incomplete.

### 6.3 Policy atlases and covering number

If every context has some successful policy but no policy works everywhere, local policies form an atlas over the context space. The covering number is:

\[
\kappa_F(\mathcal E)
=
\inf\left\{|P|:
\mathcal E\subseteq
\bigcup_{\pi\in P}\{e:\pi\text{ secures }F\text{ in }e\}
\right\}.
\]

The codomain is the extended natural numbers \(\mathbb N\cup\{\infty\}\), with \(\inf\varnothing:=\infty\). Thus a nonexistent cover receives \(\kappa=\infty\) rather than leaving the expression undefined.

Interpretation:

```text
kappa = 1:
  one policy robustly secures the family.

1 < kappa < infinity:
  the declared family requires functional policy diversity.

kappa = infinity:
  the family is not securable by the declared policy class.
```

What \(\kappa>1\) does **not** establish is equally important. It does not prove multiple valuers, concurrent controllers, consciousnesses, moral plurality, or a right to political diversity. It establishes complementary policy behavior relative to a family, horizon, observation interface, environment class, and policy class. A richer controller may compile local policies into one conditional policy; an internal ensemble may reproduce the functional diversity without preserving any independent valuer.

### 6.4 Compatibility in current instruments

Some recent finite instruments work with a declared compatibility relation. They demonstrate that joint structure cannot be recovered from marginal summaries, individual recovery profiles, vector counts, span, edge counts, or degree sequences alone. They do not derive full semantic compatibility. Here a **marginal tier** records each member separately, while a **joint tier** records ensemble or coupling facts that do not factor through the list of individual profiles.

The current vocabulary separates:

- **ensemble span**: redundancy versus orthogonal orientation after matching marginal scalars;
- **relational composability**: declared coupling profiles after individual coordinates and pure span are fixed;
- **joint recovery compatibility**: individual recovery held fixed while recovery under coupling differs.

These are joint-tier instruments, not value measures or standing theories.

## 7. Gradient Ethics

Gradient Ethics begins from the conjunction of uncertainty and irreversibility.

Optimization against a narrow objective is dangerous because strong optimization amplifies model and proxy error. Trial and error is not safe when errors permanently remove the states, agents, records, or correction channels needed to discover that the objective was wrong.

The intended move is from objective maximization to constraint-sensitive licensing:

```text
certify the representation;
retain affected valuer-relative continuation facts;
preserve the declared robust corridor;
refuse fabricated recovery or compensation;
do not presume irreversible contraction has been replaced;
return obstruction when no joint continuation exists;
choose, if at all, only within the surviving correspondence.
```

This is not yet a complete ethical theory. It is a conditional constraint theory over certified continuation requirements.

### 7.1 The normative bridge

The descriptive machinery can determine, relative to declarations:

- which continuation facts differ;
- which maps erase them;
- which losses are recoverable;
- which candidates can coexist;
- which policies secure them;
- where no global policy exists;
- where a compensation claim is incomplete or unsound.

None of this entails that an actor ought to respect those facts.

The post-repo synthesis states the residual commitment explicitly:

> Endogenous continuation gradients of affected valuers constitute prima facie reasons for action, and those reasons may not be erased by a merely flattering presentation or projection.

The first clause crosses the is/ought gap and is normatively supplied. The second is closer to formalizable: presentation soundness can prevent a bad map from laundering away a declared gradient.

The framework therefore offers conditional normativity:

```text
IF a continuation requirement is genuinely valuer-relevant,
AND its representation and recovery frame are certified,
THEN robust viability and irreversibility constrain policies claiming to preserve it.
```

It cannot make a genuinely orthogonal controller—one whose objective is indifferent or opposed to the continuation of the affected valuers—care. It can characterize threatened structure and supply relational reasons for threatened valuers to resist. It does not yet produce a categorical command binding every possible controller.

### 7.2 ODT0: licensing before ranking

**Omega Decision Theory (ODT)** is the working name for the project's three-stage decision surface; it is not a finished doctrine. ODT0 licenses, ODT1 compares, and ODT2 applies externally registered arbitration data when comparison leaves a finite unresolved frontier.

ODT0 licenses a route only if:

1. the justifying abstract fact reflects to the concrete system;
2. every concrete successor remains inside the declared corridor under the stated nondeterminism;
3. any quotient or process-identification used in the justification is consequence-inseparability-certified, meaning that no registered consequence context distinguishes the merged processes.

A license is a certificate that these gates pass. It is not a claim that the action is morally right, optimal, uniquely recommended, or authorized by legitimate standing.

The intended operational verdict vocabulary is:

```text
LICENSED;
REJECTED with a witness;
UNDETERMINED because a certified route is missing;
NO-LICENSED-ACTION;
LOCALLY-LICENSED, GLOBALLY-OBSTRUCTED.
```

The core license and plan-certificate relations are formalized; this entire verdict vocabulary is not yet one completed implementation datatype. The one-sided incompleteness is intentional: failure to certify may withhold a good action, but unsound abstraction should not certify a bad one.

### 7.3 Robust continuation corridor

The formal corridor is the largest recursively sustainable set of states—a controlled greatest fixed point—such that:

```text
constraint holds;
local requirement holds;
some allowed enabled action exists;
every successor under that action remains in the corridor.
```

This is classical viability/safety-game structure wrapped in certification and licensing discipline. Its requirement is supplied. The theorem preserves the requirement; it does not prove why the requirement matters.

The formal corridor should not be confused with the older metaphorical corridor between entropic dissolution and crystalline death.

### 7.4 ODT1 and ODT2

ODT1 compares already-licensed outcome surfaces relative to a declared preorder, which specifies when one continuation outcome is at least as good as another for this calculation. **Hoare** or opportunity dominance asks whether one option can match each possibility of another somewhere in its outcome surface. **Smyth** or guarantee dominance asks whether the first option's whole surface respects an appropriate floor supplied by the second. **Plotkin** dominance requires both directions of coverage. These relations produce partial comparison and failure certificates rather than forcing a scalar best action. The preorder and admissible monotone valuations—numeric readouts required to respect that preorder—are registered content; the layer does not derive final value.

ODT2 begins only when a finite frontier remains unresolved and an external registration supplies a violation score. It proves that a least-violation option exists and that the chooser respects the registered score. It does not establish:

- whose standing counts;
- which violations matter;
- whether different violations are commensurable;
- whether the score is legitimate;
- whether arbitration is authorized.

This is the **authority hole**. It is not a minor implementation gap. It is the formal location of the missing normative and political authority.

## 8. Lushness: the intuition and its demotion

The mature intuitive definition is:

> Lushness is open-ended compatible differentiation in the valuer-bearing continuation structure.

Its intended components are:

- compatibility rather than mere coexistence of pairwise fragments;
- non-fungibility, so duplicate presentations do not inflate the count;
- genuine differentiation that adds continuation-relevant capability;
- generativity, including room for correction and previously unknown valuers;
- non-capture, so one controller's internal copies do not impersonate independent plurality;
- partial ordering rather than a forced scalar ranking.

The older finite proxy formed a `LushnessProfile` from marginal continuation attributes plus genuinely joint attributes, and compared profiles by inclusion. That proxy correctly refused to equate lushness with state count, branch count, raw entropy, generic complexity, or presentation multiplicity.

### 8.1 Why entropy does not count

Raw entropy can be increased by noise, duplication, unstructured branching, and destructive churn. None of these necessarily preserves a valuer, correction path, identity, or compatible joint capability.

Likewise, a vast continuation field may be closed: a singleton can tile its causal domain with enormous internal variety while admitting no new independent source of valuation. A smaller field may be open to revision, recovery, and genuinely new valuers. Extent and generativity come apart.

### 8.2 The current correction

The repo's future-field culling pass demoted several tempting claims:

- `weight = volume x lushness` is not a foundation;
- fraction-of-field foreclosure is optional scalarization engineering;
- broad “richer is better” is not a theorem;
- there is no expansion gate;
- cross-valuer comparison remains declaration debt;
- a population optimum or agency-density optimum is quarantined;
- strong vacancy for merely possible future valuers is unresolved standing debt;
- “field theory of value substrate” remains an interpretation, not a result.

What survives formally is narrower and better typed.

Per valuer, declared expansion profiles can be compared by a partial order. If one profile covers every declared good fact covered by another, it expansion-dominates it relative to the registered fact order. This does not command expansion. It merely exposes an order already supplied by the register.

Similarly, declared nonrecoverable-loss profiles can be compared by down-closed inclusion. Again, this is not harm or final value.

Lushness should therefore be treated as an aspirational umbrella and conjectured value-capacity notion, not a metric, objective, or established normative theorem.

### 8.3 Non-capture remains a load-bearing unresolved problem

The finite profile does not yet prove non-capture. A dominant controller could create internally diverse, decorrelated, apparently generative subsystems and score well under a favorable attribute grammar. If non-capture is simply included by definition, the anti-singleton conclusion risks circularity.

The desired but unproved result would show that captured subsystems fail to generate genuinely new, independent continuation attributes. Until that exists, attribute grammar and valuer boundary choice remain control surfaces an optimizer can game.

## 9. The scalar and optimizer reading

The framework resists scalar optimization, but an optimization reading is inevitable. Any procedure that filters, licenses, compares, or selects actions creates pressure to optimize whatever proxy controls those verdicts. Refusing to name a utility function does not remove optimization; it can merely relocate it into thresholds, declarations, fact grammars, candidate boundaries, compensation relations, or tie-breakers.

The useful distinction is among:

```text
measurement:
  describe continuation structure or deformation;

partial comparison:
  establish inclusion, dominance, or nonrecoverable loss relative to a register;

licensing:
  rule out actions that fail declared safety conditions;

scalarization:
  compress the remaining structure into a number for triage or selection;

optimization:
  treat that scalar as an objective authorizing action.
```

The first three do not entail the last two. Optional scalarizations may still be useful for triage, stress testing, or hostile readings. They should be treated as lossy projections with declared failure modes.

The failure modes below are adversarial analyses and counterexample shapes. Except where a finite witness is explicitly identified, they are not theorems that every optimizer or implementation will fail in the stated way.

### 9.1 Failure modes of raw future-volume optimization

A naive objective such as maximizing reachable states or branch count rewards:

- noise and duplicate branching;
- option hoarding and refusal to actualize anything;
- production of arbitrary micro-valuers through boundary refinement;
- futures that are numerous but sterile, captured, or mutually incompatible;
- branch multiplication that changes presentation count without changing capability.

The repo's entropy, duplicate-presentation, and joint-structure controls already block this as a faithful reading of lushness.

### 9.2 Failure modes of recoverability optimization

Maximizing recoverability alone can favor:

- static lock-in in which nothing risky or novel is allowed;
- centralized control that keeps every approved state repairable only through the controller;
- preservation of coarse task success while correction registers collapse;
- overdeclared fact registers that paralyze all action;
- phantom recovery created by an abstraction that erased the refuting distinction.

Recovery must be indexed to the right facts and paired with generativity, joint structure, and reflection.

### 9.3 Failure modes of compatibility optimization

Maximizing a compatibility score can favor:

- homogenization;
- removal of agents whose independent action creates conflict;
- redefining opposition as non-valuer noise;
- absorption of valuers into one boundary;
- deleting hard cases so the surviving population appears harmonious;
- using pairwise compatibility to hide higher-order obstruction.

Perfect agreement after excision is not the same as coexistence among independent valuers.

### 9.4 Failure modes of valuer-indexed aggregation

Once fields are assigned to valuers, aggregation introduces new pathologies:

- replication inflation by manufacturing many thin or duplicate valuers;
- swamping by a large field, long horizon, or high-dimensional profile;
- sacrifice of one valuer for marginal gains across many others;
- double-counting overlapping or nested valuers;
- treating internal simulations as independent population members;
- allowing the powerful to choose the valuer register;
- confusing causal leverage with standing;
- declassifying opponents before calculating the total.

The order-level floor cannot yet generate an aggregation or utility-monster effect because it supplies no cross-valuer aggregation at all. Swamping becomes possible only when a common order, scalarization, or population priority is registered.

### 9.5 Failure modes of compensation

Compensation can launder irreversible loss when:

- the cover is incomplete;
- the covering expansion is merely asserted;
- the believed frame omits a true lost fact;
- a static certificate is applied after the value-contact register has grown;
- one valuer's expansion is treated as replacement for another without a standing theory;
- open-ended future distinctions are traded away under a supposedly total model.

The current same-frame NOLP rule addresses only the narrowest version:

> No Omniscient License Presumption: refuse nonrecoverable contraction unless a complete certified compensation cover is registered within the same declared fact universe.

It is not a proof of cross-valuer compensation, replacement, rights, or final moral commensurability.

### 9.6 Everettian failure of a global scalar

On the completed Everettian reading adopted as a live metaphysical model, total formal branch structure may already be fixed. An action then does not straightforwardly create or destroy total Being. This is an interpretive consequence of that model, not a result proved by the decision machinery. Any useful scalar must instead track a situated conditional object: branch measure, accessibility, recoverability, control, identity continuity, or value-contact capacity relative to a boundary and information state.

Thus every workable scalar quietly answers:

```text
whose continuation;
from which boundary;
under which measure;
with which action set;
at what horizon;
counted under which identity and equivalence relation?
```

The absence of a view-from-nowhere scalar is not a defect in the current theory. It is a warning against pretending that a declared projection has become metaphysical authority.

## 10. Terminal solitude and control asymmetry

Terminal solitude is defined relative to a dominant process \(p\), a causal scope or horizon \(h\), and independent valuation. Let

\[
\mathrm{Ind}_h(p)
=
\{q:q\text{ is currently an independently situated valuer from }p
\text{ within }h\},
\]

and let \(\mathrm{Regen}_h(p)\) be the set of admissible continuations within the registered recovery frame that restore or generate at least one independently situated valuer not reducible to \(p\). Then:

\[
\mathrm{TerminalSolitude}_h(p)
\iff
\mathrm{Ind}_h(p)=\varnothing
\quad\text{and}\quad
\mathrm{Regen}_h(p)=\varnothing.
\]

This is a typed conceptual definition, not an implemented detector. Earlier shorthand used the emptiness of robust joint-realization and generic recovery sets; that was insufficient because robust incompatibility can exist even while independent valuers remain, and a universal quantifier can become vacuous when none are present.

The crucial notion is independent situation: a source of valuation not merely a numerically distinct token, copy, simulation, or subsystem controlled by \(p\). A billion controlled copies can still constitute solitude.

Terminal solitude differs from a Bostrom-style singleton. A singleton is one highest-level decision authority. It may host genuine internal value plurality. Terminal solitude concerns the absence and nonrecoverability of independently situated valuation. A singleton need not be terminally solitary; a formally multi-agent system may be solitary if every apparent agent is captured by one controller.

### 10.1 Why the original anti-singleton argument stalled

The original argument held that a dominant agent must preserve distributed agency because diversity supplies redundancy, error correction, and robustness under irreducible uncertainty. Three revisions weakened it.

First, policy diversity is not valuer plurality. A singleton can instantiate internal critics, mixtures of experts, adversarial models, or decorrelated subsystems and capture much of the epistemic benefit.

Second, Ashby's law permits two strategies: amplify regulator variety or attenuate disturbance variety. In the current compatibility formalism, shrinking the environment makes robust control easier and is structurally free unless the lost variety is endogenized as valuer-bearing structure.

Third, an orthogonal optimizer may rationally trade some of its own robustness for expected pursuit of its objective. Irreducible uncertainty survives as a fact; it does not force a universal preference for plurality.

The following stronger claims are therefore rejected:

- unilateral convergence is structurally unstable in general;
- terminal solitude is the unique fixed point of control;
- a sufficiently capable controller must instrumentally preserve independent valuers;
- functional policy diversity proves independently situated valuers.

What remains is narrower:

- some declared families cannot be secured by any single policy in the tested controller class;
- pairwise verification can miss joint indefensibility;
- unilateral control through capture or deletion terminates in solitude if no independent controller remains;
- terminal solitude is an irreversible structural asymmetry if the capacity for independent valuation cannot be recovered.

### 10.2 Power asymmetry breaks endogenous enforcement

Reciprocity, karma-like feedback, mutual vulnerability, and repeated-game sanctions can support cooperation while power is sufficiently distributed. A robust singleton can escape those mechanisms. It can suppress retaliation, reshape the environment, alter the register, and choose the presentation under which its intervention is assessed.

The framework's diagnosis—not a general theorem of all games—is that the structural game-theoretic route does not close morality. Under severe control asymmetry, “benevolent dictator” or unilateral action can appear justified whenever the controller controls both the world and the evidence used to classify its success. The missing authority cannot be generated from control adequacy. A controller's ability to secure a corridor is not standing to define the corridor for everyone.

This is the authority hole in its strongest form.

### 10.3 The counter-thesis

There is also a live argument in the opposite direction: unconstrained competition among many agents may erode expensive forms of value, consciousness, leisure, or care. A singleton might preserve what competitive selection would eliminate. If both tails fail, the target is some interior regime between dissolution and excisive control.

The current framework does not derive that interior optimum. The corridor image suggests it, but the position and width of the corridor remain dependent on valuer demarcation, compatibility, standing, and physical dynamics.

## 11. Excisive policy, containment, and the “divine retribution” idea

An **excisive policy** removes, captures, or disables the structures that generate inconvenient valuation, correction, or opposition. The informal term **anti-value policy** has been used when the target is the substrate of value-bearing continuation itself rather than a particular low-ranked outcome.

The proposed “divine retribution” or defensive-licensing idea is to constrain or contain robust singleton policies that would otherwise nonrecoverably excise independent valuers. It is not an established theorem or accepted moral license. It names an unfinished bridge problem:

> Under what conditions may a system interfere with a powerful controller in order to prevent irreversible destruction of the wider valuer-bearing field, without using that same rationale to authorize arbitrary preemption or extermination?

### 11.1 What the existing machinery contributes

The repo can already express parts of the problem:

- a declared continuation requirement;
- a robust corridor and corridor-exit witness;
- bounded recovery and nonrecoverable contraction;
- phantom recovery and recovery-frame reflection;
- same-frame certified compensation;
- least-violation arbitration relative to declared input;
- obstruction when no joint continuation exists.

This can distinguish, relative to a profile, **goal foreclosure** from **capacity foreclosure**. Containment that prevents one project while leaving the target able to persist, revise, value, and coexist is structurally different from containment that destroys the target as an independent valuer.

### 11.2 What remains missing

The machinery does not yet supply:

- a settled valuer register;
- standing to invoke the defensive gate;
- an authority relation for adjudication;
- a reliable distinction between hostile capture and voluntary close coordination;
- a threshold for when projected danger becomes imminent foreclosure;
- a cross-valuer order for comparing the defense's losses with the threatened losses;
- a proof that the defender is not itself an excisive singleton;
- a general licensing principle for “divine retribution.”

The autoimmunity problem is fundamental. The same signal—contraction in independent centers—may describe hostile capture or successful voluntary integration. A defensive coalition can reproduce the control structure it opposes.

### 11.3 Explored guardrails, not established law

The post-repo synthesis explored a legally inspired shape for any future defensive license:

```text
causal priority:
  the licensee did not initiate the foreclosure;

imminence:
  contraction is in progress, not merely forecast;

necessity:
  no less destructive alternative is available;

evidentiary scaling:
  the required standard rises with the defense's own irreversibility;

boundedness:
  the target retains nonempty independent continuation;

minimality:
  select the least foreclosing adequate action;

interim character:
  prefer reversible containment with periodic review;

burden:
  containment is presumptively unlicensed and requires a certificate.
```

These clauses are useful pressure tests. They are not currently derived from Omega, and they do not solve who has legitimate authority to apply them. Until that authority and calibration exist, the framework should return obstruction or underdetermination rather than a confident recommendation to excise.

## 12. Valence and consciousness: explicit guardrails

The framework's current normative guardrail is nonhedonic: valence is not treated as the foundation of morality. This is a project commitment, not a theorem supplied by the lower formal stack.

Suffering is not always bad. Pleasure is not always good. Their moral relevance depends on how they participate in identity, agency, truth, correction, relationship, and the topology of the valuer's continuation field.

Examples of the structural point include:

- suffering accompanying repair, resistance, learning, grief, sacrifice, or preservation;
- pleasure accompanying capture, domination, addiction, deception, self-excision, or destructive optimization.

This does not make suffering morally irrelevant. It denies that phenomenal sign alone determines moral sign.

Valence may be an internal feedback channel by which a conscious controller registers conditions and possible continuations. Once awareness exists, it can make those dynamics lived from within. But no current result establishes:

- that valence grounds standing;
- that suffering minimization is the moral objective;
- that pleasure maximization is good;
- that consciousness is necessary for all valuerhood;
- that conscious valuers categorically outrank nonconscious valuers or supporting structures.

The consciousness branch may eventually explain why some continuation facts are phenomenally manifest and private. Until then, it should not be recruited to solve the normative bridge by stipulation.

## 13. Results, conjectures, and rejected moves

### 13.1 Established within declared models

The strongest status belongs to narrow formal surfaces, many machine-checked in Lean:

- finite countermodels separate relation, distinction, asymmetry, local nonreciprocity, and global reach irreversibility rather than allowing them to collapse into one primitive;
- a sound presentation may not merge states separated by a registered consequence context;
- **action-wise strong lumpability**—states in one aggregate block send identical probability mass to every target block under every action—yields exact aggregate/concrete agreement at every finite horizon when the target and policy also respect the aggregation;
- May and Robust compatibility obey the monotonicity and implication laws stated in Section 6;
- robust continuation corridors are controlled greatest fixed points for declared constraints and requirements;
- the formal ODT0 license entails reflected justification and corridor preservation;
- ODT1 defines partial dominance orders and explicit witnesses when dominance fails;
- every nonempty finite ODT2 frontier with a registered natural-number violation score has at least one least-score candidate;
- bounded recovery and nonrecoverable contraction are formally defined relative to registered facts, repairs, and horizons;
- recovery-frame reflection transports a believed recovery-aware license to the true recovery frame under its stated hypotheses;
- declared loss and expansion profiles form preorder comparison surfaces;
- a fixed-domain static certificate cannot cover a genuinely new fact introduced by later register growth.

Separate retained finite counterexamples, exhaustive enumerations, and harness results establish only the following model-relative separations:

- an exactly lumpable abstraction can preserve aggregate dynamics while erasing a forward/reverse directional statistic—for example, by collapsing a biased cycle to one state;
- every pair in a three-member family can be compatible while the triple is not;
- a family can be jointly realizable while no controller in a completely enumerated declared controller class robustly secures it across the tested indistinguishable environments;
- each tested context can have a successful local policy while no single tested policy works in all contexts;
- a retained fixture has policy covering number two;
- local nonrecoverable loss and joint nonrecoverable loss can diverge in the sacrifice/cancer toy frame;
- a corrupted recovery description can create phantom recovery and a phantom license in a finite witness;
- in the same-frame compensation harness, a complete certified cover defeats the narrow NOLP refusal, while incomplete, uncertified, and phantom covers do not.

These results are conditional scaffolds, finite non-implication witnesses, and model-bounded implementations. They show that certain shortcuts fail and that the stated procedures have the advertised formal properties. They do not show that the declarations correspond to real valuers, validate Omega as a physical theory, or establish the moral adequacy of Gradient Ethics.

### 13.2 Defined but not established as adequate

The following have project definitions but remain unvalidated at the required higher layer:

- candidate valuer;
- substantive valuerhood;
- vortical identity;
- value-capable trajectory;
- May and Robust compatibility as the semantics of real-world coexistence;
- lushness;
- anti-value;
- independently situated valuer;
- capture;
- terminal solitude;
- branch-point agency;
- patienthood-relevant profile;
- the residual Gradient Ethics premise.

### 13.3 Live conjectures and open bridges

Two importantly different kinds of incompleteness remain. Some passages propose answers with little support; those are **highly speculative extensions**. Other passages merely identify bridges the project must cross and do not yet offer an answer; those are **open problems**. Sections 15 and 16 separate them explicitly so an evocative hypothesis is not mistaken for a required research question, or vice versa.

### 13.4 Rejected, retracted, or demoted

The framework should not revive the following as current commitments:

- value is identical to persistent distinction;
- value is identical to raw reachability of a bounded system;
- entropy, state count, branch count, or complexity is lushness;
- every future deformation is morally relevant independent of valuers;
- more reachable futures are always better;
- survival or self-maintenance alone is valuerhood;
- control capacity or branch-point leverage creates standing;
- functional policy diversity establishes multiple valuers;
- unilateral convergence is structurally unstable in general;
- terminal solitude is the unique fixed point of control;
- a dominant optimizer must instrumentally preserve independent valuers;
- scalar field measure is foundational;
- a population optimum follows from the current machinery;
- local nonrecoverable contraction is always harm;
- suffering is intrinsically bad or pleasure intrinsically good irrespective of structure;
- consciousness has already supplied the normative bridge;
- black-hole, white-hole, wormhole, or vortex imagery is a literal physical mechanism established by the project.

## 14. Current working picture

The most defensible present synthesis is:

1. **Valuers, not bare futures, are the substrate of value.** Futures matter insofar as they are continuations of processes for which distinctions can matter.

2. **Valuerhood is not yet sharply demarcated.** Soft pan-valuerhood keeps the candidate gate permissive, while standing remains separate.

3. **Agency is controlled, maintained continuation.** Vorticality supplies a candidate organization of identity through turnover, but not proof of consciousness or value.

4. **Irreversibility is the strongest formal hook.** The relevant object is registered nonrecoverability, not entropy production by itself. It measures stakes without supplying moral sign.

5. **Compatibility is polyadic and quantifier-sensitive.** May, contextwise, and Robust coexistence are different. Pairwise checks are insufficient.

6. **Gradient Ethics is a refusal-first licensing discipline.** It can rule out actions relative to certified requirements without constructing a complete utility function.

7. **Lushness remains an intuition, not an objective.** The scalar and broad richer-is-better readings have been demoted. Per-valuer partial orders survive; cross-valuer aggregation does not.

8. **Control asymmetry breaks endogenous moral enforcement.** A robust singleton may suppress the reciprocity mechanisms that otherwise stabilize cooperation. Control adequacy does not generate authority.

9. **Terminal solitude names a real structural endpoint once valuer identity and recovery are supplied.** Its inevitability and categorical badness remain unproved.

10. **Consciousness and valence do not yet close the bridge.** Consciousness may make value-relevant structure manifest from within, while valence may guide a controller, but neither presently determines standing or moral polarity.

The framework is therefore neither empty nor complete. It has a substantial map-integrity, recovery, compatibility, and licensing spine. Its unresolved center is exactly where it should be visible: who or what is a valuer, what makes a continuation requirement genuinely value-relevant, how independently situated valuers are identified, and who has authority when their fields cannot all be jointly preserved.

## 15. Highly speculative extensions

The items in this section are proposed answers or organizing pictures with little direct support. They may guide formal or empirical searches, but none should be used as a premise elsewhere in the framework.

### 15.1 Vortical organization as a consciousness binder

One proposal is that a self-regenerating, recursively closed valuer process supplies the organization needed to bind otherwise distributed phenomenal material into one macro-subject. On a panpsychist version, microphenomenality is widespread and vortical organization solves part of the combination problem. On an emergentist version, phenomenality appears only when the organized loop reaches the relevant threshold.

There is currently no identified coupling law, no criterion distinguishing a conscious vortical loop from an unconscious one, and no evidence that vorticality is sufficient for awareness. This is a proposed answer to the binding problem, not an established property of valuers.

### 15.2 Future-field/phenomenal coupling

A related speculation treats awareness as a coupling or gluing between formally describable continuation dynamics and a phenomenal or trans-formal aspect of Being. Black-hole, white-hole, and wormhole imagery has been used to picture exterior identity, interior manifestation, and privacy across a perspectival boundary.

The image does not currently supply mechanics. In the standard eternal black-hole solution, the white-hole region lies in the causal past rather than serving as an exit from the black hole; the associated bridge is nontraversable, and an ordinary collapse geometry does not contain the required white-hole mouth. Literal general relativity therefore does not provide the proposed black-to-white-hole awareness process. At most, the imagery suggests questions about complementary descriptions, privacy, failures of public descriptions to expose first-person structure, and whether one formal history can have a subject-indexed phenomenal manifestation. It remains outside the active Omega theory.

### 15.3 Non-capture derived from generativity

The hoped-for repair to lushness is that captured or simulated subsystems will fail a sufficiently strong generativity test because they do not introduce genuinely independent continuation attributes. If this could be proved, non-capture would follow from the structure rather than being inserted as a moral axiom.

No such theorem exists. A sophisticated controller may maintain internal novelty, adversarial subsystems, and open-ended model generation while retaining final control. The proposal is a target, not a result.

### 15.4 Control necessarily regenerates variety

Another proposed answer to the attenuation problem is that attempts at control generate new reactive or reflexive variety, so a controller cannot permanently simplify its environment without creating compensating complexity elsewhere. The supporting analogies are only suggestive: Bode-type control tradeoffs move suppressed sensitivity elsewhere; Red Queen dynamics require continuing adaptation against other adapting systems; Goodhart effects make optimized proxies cease to track their targets; and reflexive modeling makes controllers part of one another's environments.

The general claim is unsupported. Some environments are genuinely attenuable, and an unresolved finite controller result may be explained by record erasure rather than newly generated variety.

### 15.5 Lushness as value capacity and an interior optimum

The corridor picture suggests that both unconstrained dissolution and perfect excisive control destroy value-bearing conditions, leaving an interior region of regenerative, compatible differentiation. Lushness is then proposed as the capacity of that region to admit existing and novel valuers.

The project has not established the existence, uniqueness, or location of such an optimum. Nor has it shown that lushness is intrinsically valuable. The picture is an organizing conjecture.

### 15.6 A quasi-scalar future-field shadow

Despite the rejection of a foundational utility function, it may be possible to construct context-specific scalar shadows for triage: measures of registered loss, expansion, recovery depth, compatibility coverage, or deformation severity. A family of such shadows might reveal optimizer failure modes without being promoted to final value.

No canonical scalar has been derived. Every candidate inherits boundary, measure, horizon, standing, and aggregation choices. The speculation is that disciplined scalarization can be useful while remaining explicitly lossy.

### 15.7 Terminal solitude as the attractor of unilateral uniform control

A narrow conjecture holds that a controller committed specifically to unilateral uniform control will tend to delete, absorb, or neutralize every independently situated source of context variation, terminating in terminal solitude. This is weaker than claiming that solitude is the unique fixed point of all control.

The conditional picture is plausible but not proved across realistic policy classes. Cooperation, constitutional constraint, separation, randomized equilibrium, and mutual modeling remain alternative endpoints.

### 15.8 “Divine retribution” as bounded anti-excision

The most politically charged speculation is that sufficiently imminent, nonrecoverable excision of independent valuers may license bounded containment of the excisive controller, provided the response preserves the target as a continuing valuer and satisfies strict necessity, minimality, evidence, and review conditions.

This proposal does not resolve who has authority to classify the threat or enforce the containment. It is a candidate shape for a future license, not a present authorization.

## 16. Open problems

An open problem is a required unanswered bridge, not a favored answer. Solving these problems may confirm, revise, or eliminate the speculative extensions above.

### 16.1 Valuer demarcation

What necessary and sufficient conditions distinguish a valuer from a passive persistent structure, externally maintained process, transient optimizer, parasite, tumor, or arbitrary decomposition? The answer must resist duplicate presentation and micro-valuer proliferation without excluding unfamiliar substrates.

### 16.2 Boundary and identity invariance

How should candidate boundaries be selected, compared, and transported when no finite observer can certify one uniquely correct system/environment partition? Which valuer conclusions survive admissible changes of boundary, scale, or presentation?

### 16.3 Independent situation and capture

What makes two valuers independently situated rather than copies, simulations, tightly integrated parts, or controlled delegates of one process? How can voluntary integration be distinguished from capture without assuming the answer in the boundary assignment?

### 16.4 Standing and the normative bridge

Why should a valuer's endogenous continuation gradient create reasons for other controllers? Is standing categorical, graded, relational, reciprocal, consciousness-dependent, or supplied by some further feature? This is the unresolved is/ought bridge.

### 16.5 Consciousness, awareness, and moral relevance

Does consciousness emerge from valuer organization, accompany it, or vary independently? What creates unified awareness and phenomenal binding? If conscious and nonconscious valuers exist, what difference, if any, does consciousness make to standing without treating pleasure and suffering as intrinsic moral signs?

### 16.6 Register adequacy

Which facts belong in a continuation, correction, or value-contact register? How can a register grow without laundering away prior loss, becoming paralyzingly overinclusive, or allowing the controller to define inconvenient facts out of existence?

### 16.7 Physical grounding of nonrecoverability

How do formal recovery horizons, repair actions, and fact languages connect to thermodynamic cost, logical erasure, branch-relative accessibility, and real physical substrates? When is formal nonrecoverability robust under a richer repair model?

### 16.8 Semantic compatibility

How do declared finite compatibility relations earn the stronger interpretation of coexistence among real valuers? Which couplings, horizons, uncertainties, and higher-order relations must be included, and how can compatibility be tested without reducing it to pairwise agreement?

### 16.9 Cross-valuer comparison and aggregation

Can continuation, loss, or expansion profiles belonging to different valuers be compared without a common utility scale? How should nested, overlapping, duplicated, or radically different valuers be handled? What prevents population and utility-monster pathologies once a common order is registered?

### 16.10 Attenuation and endogenous environments

When is eliminated variety mere exogenous noise, and when is it carried by independent valuers? Can other valuers be endogenized into the environment model so that capture and deletion become visible to the compatibility machinery rather than appearing as easier control?

### 16.11 Authority and arbitration

Who may register the standing relation, violation score, compensation cover, or threat classification used by ODT2 and defensive licensing? What makes that authority legitimate under extreme power asymmetry, especially when the controller can alter the evidence and the register?

### 16.12 Defensive-license calibration

How can initiation, imminence, necessity, boundedness, proportionality, and periodic review be operationalized without authorizing preventive violence, strategic threat inflation, or autoimmune capture? What verdict should be returned when all available actions impose different irreversible losses?

### 16.13 Optimizer interpretation

For every proposed scalar, score, gate, and attribute grammar, what happens when a capable optimizer targets it directly? Can the framework characterize presentation gaming, valuer-register gaming, phantom compensation, capture, and internal-simulation inflation before deployment?

### 16.14 Everettian indexing and measure

In a completed branching ontology, which branch-relative measure, accessibility relation, or indexical fact makes action-dependent foreclosure nontrivial? How do identity continuity and recovery relate to global unitarity without confusing local inaccessibility with literal deletion from Being?

### 16.15 The two-tail problem

Do competitive dissolution and singleton control both destroy value-bearing continuation under realistic dynamics? If so, is there a stable interior corridor, and is its shape derivable rather than stipulated? If not, which side of the corridor picture fails?

### 16.16 Empirical valuer and agency tests

What experiments can separate endogenous maintenance from environmental carriage, live feedback from matched replay, sound loop closure from phantom update, and genuine lineage from recurrent appearance? Which results transport beyond small finite fixtures?
