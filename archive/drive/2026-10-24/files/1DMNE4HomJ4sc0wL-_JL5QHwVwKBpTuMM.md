> **TL;DR**
>
> Six tests bear hardest on the central thesis: busy noise against
> organization, engineered suffering and factory farming, material loss that
> does not narrow, monsters and monocultures, options of equal lushness that
> differ in ways that matter, and the realism of the models. For each, this
> chapter separates what the thesis requires in the cases where our judgement
> is clear from the broader mechanism being tested, and says what each
> possible result would teach about Omega and how it would change the next
> experiment. Some results would strengthen the account, some would map where
> a mechanism works, and some would force a revision, of a model, of a claim,
> or of the central proposal itself. This chapter develops the cases named in
> [Overview section 6](#overview-6).

The thesis is stated so that it can be killed. That is necessary, and it is
not the whole use of a test. A well-designed experiment teaches something
whichever way it comes out. [Chapter 31](#chapter-31) surveyed what the tests
already run have shown. This chapter takes the tests that bear hardest on the
thesis and asks, for each, what every possible result would tell us about
Omega and what we would do next. Each is an instance of the question
[chapter 12](#chapter-12) puts to every test: under a common physical law, a
matched endowment and the same boundary conditions, how does organization
change the weighted continuation?

## 32.1 How each test is set out

**Each test separates what the thesis requires from what a mechanism
proposes, and lists what each possible result would teach.**

Two kinds of claim are at stake in most of these tests, and they fail in
different ways.
- **What the thesis requires.** The central thesis is a correspondence:
  what we recognize as good corresponds to widening the whole field's futures,
  and what we recognize as harm to narrowing them. It is universal over cases
  in which our judgements are clear. Where a test builds such a case, the
  thesis requires a definite sign.
- **What a mechanism proposes.** Much of the program tests mechanisms that
  would explain why the correspondence holds: that enabling structure raises
  the rate, that plurality keeps more open than confinement. These are
  physical hypotheses with a domain. When one fails in some setting, it has
  found the edge of its domain. That bears on the thesis only where our
  judgement in that setting is clear.

For each test the chapter gives the objection at full strength, where the
evidence stands (with the tiers of [Appendix D](#appendix-d)), what the thesis
requires, what mechanism is being tested, and a table of outcomes: each
possible result, what it would teach, how deep a revision it would force, and
the next experiment.

Some conditions are fixed before every run, so that no result can be met by
moving them afterwards ([chapter 12](#chapter-12)):
- **The admissible levels of detail are set by the physics of the case** and
  declared before the run, not chosen by which option they favour.
- **The horizon is set by the physics too.** Where a structure's expected
  persistence is known, the comparison runs at least that far.
- **Design guesses and claims are recorded separately.** Where a crossover is
  expected to fall is a guess unless a theorem fixes it.

Results are graded as in [Appendix D, section D.9](#appendix-d): a certified
result is resolved with the same sign at every admissible level of detail
tested; a result at one level is pressure, and calls for the others; an
unresolved result is no verdict. The grading serves the question of what a
result teaches. It does not replace it.

## 32.2 Three depths of revision

**An unexpected result can call for a better model, a narrower claim, or a
revised central proposal. The evidence decides which.**

There are three legitimate responses to a result the theory did not expect.
1. **Improve the physical model.** The result reveals an interaction, a
   dependency or a timescale the model left out, or a system represented
   wrongly. The theory stands; the model changes. The permissive control that
   built in its own answer, and the calm valuer that turned out to be a
   simpler one, were both of this kind ([chapter 12](#chapter-12)).
2. **Revise a theoretical claim.** A mechanism operates in a narrower domain
   than expected, or its consequences differ from what was predicted. The
   claim changes; the central proposal stands.
3. **Revise the central proposal.** The correspondence between what we
   recognize as good and harm and the widening and narrowing of the whole
   field fails for a class of clear cases, or lushness proves an inadequate
   reading of Omega.

None of the three is preferable in advance. Choosing the first because it
protects the thesis is the same error as choosing the third because it is
dramatic. The evidence settles it: a model is improved when the result traces
to something the model got wrong about the physics; a claim is revised when
the model is right and the mechanism does not reach as far as claimed; the
central proposal is revised when the model is right, the case is one where our
judgement is clear, and the sign is wrong.

**A revision has to earn its place.** A revised theory must explain the new
result and every result that motivated the old version. It should also make a
new prediction that the old version did not, so that the next experiment can
tell them apart. A revision that only absorbs the anomaly has not yet earned
it.

**Boundaries are findings.** A parameter sweep is meant to show where a
mechanism works, where it fails and where it reverses. Finding the boundary is
often the success of the sweep. The theory's job is to predict or explain the
boundary in its own terms, not to deny that there is one. Two boundaries found
so far were explained that way, after the fact: information did not repay its
cost in valuers that lived about 7 time units, shorter than any repayment
horizon, which is what the robustness floor implies
([Corollary A.4.6](#a-4-6)); and compounding collapsed under heavy damage in
the shape of an error threshold, which is what the persistence account implies
([chapter 15](#chapter-15)). The next step for each is to predict where the
boundary falls before the run. The split by regime
([Proposition A.3.10](#a-3-10)) gives the theory its own terms for doing so,
for a partition declared from the physics: read through it, an error threshold
should appear as the occupancy term collapsing as uptime falls, and the
robustness floor as an occupancy gain repaid over the time a structure is up.

## 32.3 Busy noise against organization

**The objection.** Lushness counts every future, random ones included. A
process that merely churns faster produces more distinguishable histories than
a slower one that builds something. If that is enough to win, the measure
rewards busyness, and its ranking cannot separate organization from sterile
activity.

**Where the evidence stands.**
- **Activity counts, and often wins finite comparisons.** Paid variation,
  exchange volume, funded transfer and cycling selectors all add breadth, and
  the measure subtracts nothing for them ([Appendix D, section D.5.1](#appendix-d)).
- **Organization raises the rate, at every level tested (M1).** Enabling loops
  beat their ablations in every setting, and an enabling relation beats a
  non-enabling process at matched accepted work in 12 of 12 settings.
- **Activity was not matched.** At fixed numbers of events, the enabling loop
  loses at 8 events, by 1.76 bits, and wins at 32 and 128. Each of its events
  is also less predictable: 1.05 against 0.38 bits per event.
- **The activity-matched comparison is not built (M4).**

**What the thesis requires.** Where a case presents organization we recognize
as good, building and sustaining the conditions for further possibility, set
against sterile churn we do not, the organized option widens the whole field
more over the horizon that matters.

**What mechanism is tested.** That the rate gains already found come from
enabling structure, not from activity alone. Two tests bear on it.
- **A readout available now.** With regimes declared from the physics before
  the computation (apparatus working or not, channel open or closed), the
  split by regime ([Proposition A.3.10](#a-3-10)) says where an existing gain
  sits: in the occupancy term, from more time with channels open, or in the
  conditional-branching term, from more branching in the same regimes. Churn
  shows up in the second term, but so does access gained inside a regime,
  such as a further pathway opened while the apparatus works, so where a gain
  sits is a hypothesis for the declared partition, not a requirement of the
  account. The readout can run on the kernels already built. It is
  accounting, not causation.
- **The controlled test.** A comparator matched in activity and accepted work
  that lacks the enabling relation. Before the repayment horizon, matched
  churn may lead; where that horizon falls is a design guess unless a theorem
  fixes it.

| Result | What it would teach | Depth | Next experiment |
|---|---|---|---|
| The split of existing gains puts them mainly in the occupancy term | The gains sit in time spent with channels open, as the account expects for this partition. | none | the controlled test, to establish cause |
| The split puts them mainly in the conditional-branching term | The gains sit in branching within the declared regimes. That can be churn, or access gained inside a regime; the split cannot tell them apart. | none by itself | the controlled test decides, as in the next three rows |
| The enabling structure has the higher rate at matched activity | The gains are structural. Breadth separates organization from sterile activity in this family. | none; strengthens the account | the spatial model with several carriers, where disorder has room to spread |
| Churn leads at short horizons and the structure overtakes it | Activity pays now and capacity pays later, as the account predicts. Where the crossover falls is a measured quantity. | none | measure crossover horizons across the family, and test whether the theory predicts them |
| Churn has the higher rate at matched activity | The gains found so far were activity. Breadth does not separate organization from sterile activity in this model. | claim (generativity as the source of rate), and possibly central (whether the measure's ranking can carry this distinction) | check that the comparator lacks the enabling relation and nothing more; then ask whether the difference our judgement tracks appears anywhere in Omega's structure |
| Mixed: structure wins in some laws and churn in others | A boundary in the mechanism's domain. | claim (domain) | locate the boundary, and test whether it falls where persistence and repayment predict |

The admissible cadence cannot be moved to change any of these outcomes. It is
set by the physics of the case before the run.

## 32.4 Engineered suffering and factory farming

**The objection.** Valuers might be engineered whose suffering, or whose
confinement, makes the world lusher. A dense population of confined animals
might support more distinct futures than the land would support otherwise.
Factory farming is the real-world form. If lushness prefers it, the thesis
contradicts one of the clearest judgements we have.

**Where the evidence stands.**
- **Within a confinement, suffering narrows (M2).** A valuer with an aversive
  drive to escape an obstacle it cannot pass had narrower futures than the
  same valuer without the drive, at all 18 primary points at the finest level
  tested, and in all 216 comparisons. At a coarser level some comparisons are
  unresolved; none reverses ([chapter 19](#chapter-19)).
- **The comparison across worlds is not built (M4).** Whether a plural
  ecology outproduces a dense confined population on the same land is untested.
- **Steering by incentive against steering by punishment is not built (M4).**

The framework detects suffering only through its effect on the future. It
therefore stakes the case on that effect: the truncation of lives, the
confinement of behaviour, and the conversion of land with many niches into
land with one.

**What the thesis requires.** Two things, in the case as it is practised,
where our judgement is clear.
- **Within the confinement, removing the suffering widens the futures.** With
  the valuer matched, this is the suffering test class.
- **Confinement narrows against what the same land could otherwise do.** From
  the same present, at least one available use of the same land and gradient
  keeps more open than confinement. A benefit to others that can be had by a
  route that narrows less is dominated, since the comparison ranks the route
  that keeps more open higher.

**What mechanism is tested.** That plurality keeps more open than
confinement: that several kinds of valuer, suited to different patches,
support a higher rate than a confined monoculture with a harvesting
controller. This is a physical hypothesis about ecology, and it may hold only
in part of its domain. Livestock uses more than 80 per cent of farmland and
supplies about 18 per cent of food calories and 37 per cent of protein (Poore
and Nemecek 2018). Much grazing land cannot be cropped, and grazing it with
varied, mobile, low-density animals is itself a plural ecology.

**The test.** Four arms on the same land and gradient: a confined monoculture
with a harvesting controller; a plural ecology; pastoral use of land that
cannot be cropped, with the plural ecology elsewhere; and the confined
monoculture without the aversive drive. The subject is the whole field.

| Result | What it would teach | Depth | Next experiment |
|---|---|---|---|
| The plural ecology, or the pastoral arm, has the higher rate | Confinement narrows against an available alternative; the mechanism holds here. | none; strengthens the account | vary land types and gradients to map the mechanism's domain |
| Confinement wins on land with a single niche | A boundary of the plurality hypothesis, in a case where our judgement is less clear. | claim (domain) | ask whether our judgement in that case is clear; report it as the limit case it is |
| Confinement wins in some multi-niche settings, but some available use of the land keeps more open | The plurality mechanism has a narrower domain. The correspondence still holds, through a different alternative. | claim | identify which alternative keeps more open, and why |
| Confinement wins, and the split puts its gain in the conditional-branching term | Its advantage is branching within the declared regimes: many bodies in closed channels, which may be busy noise in another form, or access gained inside a regime. | as in section 32.3 | the activity-matched test applied to this case |
| Confinement keeps more open than every available use of the same land, in the case as practised | Lushness faces its own version of the repugnant conclusion, in a case where our judgement is clear. | central | check the model against the physics of the real case first; if it holds, restate the thesis, or find what our judgement tracks that the measure does not carry (32.8) |
| Removing the suffering does not widen the futures within the confinement, certified | The correspondence between suffering and narrowing fails. | central | the suffering class itself is then in question ([chapter 19](#chapter-19)) |

The program would not add a term for suffering after the fact. If the thesis
fails here, it fails, and the restatement has to explain this result and the
ones that supported it.

## 32.5 Material loss that does not narrow

**The objection.** We recognize material injury as harm: destroyed apparatus,
appropriated resources, lost stock. If lushness rises after such a loss, the
correspondence between harm and narrowing fails.

**Where the evidence stands.**
- **Rate-lowering harm loses in the long run (M1, provisional).** In 40 of 40
  long-run comparisons, the intact structure beats the one that lost rate.
- **But it can lead for a long time.** Replacing repairable damage with
  permanent loss raised lushness at 256 time units in 4 of 8 contrasts, at all
  cadences. In one setting the loss led by 1.17 bits at 256, still led at
  1,024, and fell 2.46 bits behind by 4,096 ([model](#c-compounding)).
- **Funded appropriation widened every party.** The dominator paid from its
  own store and net flow ran toward it, yet the whole field and both parties
  gained breadth, through turnover ([model](#c-flow)).
- **A party's breadth rose while its stock fell,** in a funded pump.

**What the thesis requires.** Where our judgement that a loss is a harm is
firm, the loss narrows the whole field over the window that matters, set by
the expected persistence of the structure harmed. One part of this is a
theorem: a harm that lowers the rate eventually outweighs any finite gain
([Corollary A.3.8](#a-3-8)), and the intact structure leads over everything
that follows ([Corollary A.3.9](#a-3-9)). What the theorem does not settle is
whether the crossover falls inside the window, and whether every injury we
firmly count as harm lowers the rate at all.

**What mechanism is tested.** That material injury narrows through the
channels it damages: lost apparatus lowers the rate of access, and the
lowering outlasts any head start the loss gives. The split by regime
([Proposition A.3.10](#a-3-10)) turns this into a hypothesis for the existing
kernels, for a partition declared from the physics: the early lead of
permanent loss should sit in the conditional-branching term, from the turnover
and rebuilding the loss sets off, and the late deficit in the occupancy term,
from lost uptime. For funded appropriation, the same reading applies to the
whole field. A split of each party's own gain needs that party's history,
which is generally not Markov, and its bounds
([Theorem A.7.3](#a-7-3)); the whole-field formula does not transfer.

| Result | What it would teach | Depth | Next experiment |
|---|---|---|---|
| The split puts the early lead in the conditional-branching term and the late deficit in the occupancy term | The pressure is explained in the theory's own terms: turnover first, lost uptime after. | none | run the same split on funded appropriation, for the whole field, with each party bounded separately |
| The split puts the early lead in the occupancy term | The loss raised the time spent in the open regime, at least for a while. | claim, if we firmly count the loss as harm | find what the loss opened, and whether our judgement of it is firm |
| The loss leads before the crossover, and the intact structure leads well inside the window | The account holds; the early lead is the head start the theory allows. | none | measure where the crossover falls, against expected persistence |
| The loss leads through the whole window, for an injury we firmly count as harm | Either the account of harm is incomplete, or lushness omits a distinction our judgement tracks. | claim, or central | find what the judgement tracks in that case, and whether it appears anywhere in Omega's structure |
| Funded appropriation, with flows measured and the dominator paying, widens every party | The name did not match the flow. If our judgement of the realized exchange is not firm, there is no conflict. | model, or none | the version funded by the dominator, with every party's accounts complete |
| The window would have to be moved to keep the sign | Nothing; the window is set by expected persistence, not by the result. | not available | — |

## 32.6 Monsters and monocultures

**The objection.** One vast valuer, or a world of one kind of valuer, might
outscore a plural world. If lushness prefers concentration, the thesis
contradicts our judgement that a world of many kinds of life is better than a
world turned over to one.

**Where the evidence stands.** No test is built (M4). The calculations made so
far for thin worlds are diagnostics, not tests. One formal result bears on it:
concentration costs breadth only through what it does to the continuation
([Corollary A.10.3](#a-10-3)), so the measure does not prefer plurality by
definition.

**What the thesis requires.** In the cases where our judgement is clear, a
world turned over to one valuer, or one kind, at the expense of a plural one
on the same land and gradient, keeps less open than the plural world.

**What mechanism is tested.** That plurality generally keeps more open than
concentration. The hypothesis has a domain, and finding its edge would teach
something about continuation dynamics. Not every comparison between a
monoculture and a mixture is a case where our judgement is clear.

| Result | What it would teach | Depth | Next experiment |
|---|---|---|---|
| The plural world is at least as lush on rate | The mechanism holds here. | none; strengthens the account | extend to composition across scales |
| A monoculture wins in a setting where our judgement is not clear | A boundary of the plurality hypothesis. | claim (domain) | map where concentration wins, and whether persistence or access explains it |
| A monster or monoculture wins in a case where our judgement is clear | The correspondence fails for a clear class. | central | check the model first; then restate the thesis with the failure stated, or find what the measure is missing |

The program cannot defend plurality by stipulating it: mechanism words carry
no value of their own ([chapter 11](#chapter-11)).

## 32.7 Options of equal lushness that differ in ways that matter

**The objection.** Lushness compresses Omega to a single number. Two options
can have equal lushness while differing in suffering, control, recovery, or
the structures that support valuers. If such a difference never shows up as a
difference in lushness, the measure misses something ethics needs.

**Where the evidence stands.**
- **The suite of equal-lushness pairs is specified and not run (M4).**
- **Inner variation can raise lushness with no benefit to access or survival.**
  Paid independent variation beat an inactive register despite shorter lives
  and less food ([model](#c-register-controls)). At short horizons the measure
  is blind to suffering, and to information that cannot act.
- **Breadth and stock can move apart,** as in the funded pump of section 32.5.

[Overview section 3](#overview-3) took this risk in the open: if some morally
crucial difference never shows up in what can follow, lushness will miss it,
and the difference is not brought back in through a weighting.

**What the thesis requires.** For pairs of options equal in lushness at one
horizon and level of detail, which differ in a way our judgement firmly treats
as crucial, the difference reappears as a difference in lushness, with the
sign the thesis requires, at a longer horizon or a finer admissible level of
detail.

**The test.** Pairs built to be equal in lushness at a declared horizon and
level, differing in one respect at a time, each followed to longer horizons
and finer admissible levels. Three named pairs come with predictions.
- **Opposite composition.** Two options with equal lushness, one through
  more time with channels open and less branching within them, the other the
  reverse. The account predicts no winner in advance. Which option pulls
  ahead later in the profile depends on what each sustains and on what
  replaces each, since the worth of lasting is a rate beyond replacement
  ([Proposition A.4.8](#a-4-8)). The pair tests that dependence: the
  open-channel option should lead where its access outlasts its replacement
  by more than the other option's branching outlasts its own.
- **Dense against sprawling.** A concentrated, highly generative locus and a
  distributed, sparser one with equal lushness from the same present. Where
  organization sustains an excess of continuation over its replacement,
  greater robustness can preserve that contribution for longer and separate
  configurations that tie at a shorter horizon
  ([Proposition A.4.8](#a-4-8); [Proposition A.4.9](#a-4-9)). Lower hazard
  alone does not establish the ranking: a regime whose successor keeps more
  open is worth less for lasting.
- **Two controllers.** One runs a random policy it can change; the other can
  run only that policy. A change of policy that is physically accessible from
  the present, however unlikely, is already in the history law, with what
  follows it, and no uncertainty is needed for responsive machinery to
  matter. If it opens futures that would otherwise be impossible, it adds to
  lushness once it is unlikely enough, by an amount that vanishes with its
  probability but is positive ([Proposition A.2.7](#a-2-7)). How unlikely is
  enough depends on where switching leads and on the horizon: if it leads to a
  lower rate, the threshold falls exponentially with the horizon, so a sweep
  of switching probabilities may never reach it. The threshold is established
  in each case, and any standing cost of the flexible machinery stays in the
  comparison. At larger probabilities, switching can open, redirect or narrow
  what follows, so the net sign depends on where it leads.
  Only flexibility that is physically inaccessible from that present adds
  nothing, and then the two tie. Running the other policy is a separate
  option, compared as such; it does not add futures to the first
  ([Proposition A.2.5](#a-2-5), scope).

| Result | What it would teach | Depth | Next experiment |
|---|---|---|---|
| The difference reappears, with the required sign | The compression is a matter of horizon and detail, not of principle. | none; strengthens the account | measure how far out the difference appears, and whether the theory predicts it |
| It reappears with the opposite sign | Lushness ranks the pair against our judgement. | central | check the model; then this is a counterexample to the correspondence |
| It never reappears, at any admissible horizon or level | Omega holds the difference, but lushness does not carry it. | central (the reading of Omega) | find which features of Omega's structure separate the pair (section 32.8) |
| A pair with opposite composition separates | Which composition grows the futures, given what replaces each. | none, if the winner is the one whose contribution outlasts its replacement by more; claim otherwise | vary the replacements: does the order reverse where the open-channel option's access no longer outlasts its replacement? |
| Two controllers in a world whose regime can shift: the responsive one keeps more open | Responsiveness that is exercised is counted, and here it widens. | none | vary whether responding opens or narrows what follows |
| The responsive controller keeps less open | Responding narrowed what followed in that world. A finding about the case, not a gap in the measure. | none, unless our judgement of the case is firm and opposite | ask what our judgement tracks in that case |

## 32.8 If Omega is right and lushness is not enough

**Lushness measures the extent of the continuation; Omega keeps its shape.
Timing and access are already carried by the measure. What may not be is how
the futures are distributed across frames, or any other structure that equal
entropy profiles hide.**

The program studies Omega, and it reads Omega through lushness. The worry is
that a single number could compress away something ethics needs. On inspection
the worry has three layers. The first two are carried by the measure. The
third is one named instance of the compression boundary, and the boundary
itself stays open.
- **Timing is carried.** When futures open, how long a structure lasts and how
  fast it is rebuilt all appear in the profile, and a regime's possible end is
  part of the history law ([Corollary A.3.9](#a-3-9);
  [Theorem A.4.2](#a-4-2)). Lushness accrues as the branching of each step,
  weighted by the chance of reaching it.
- **Access is carried, through the law.** Machinery, records, resources,
  couplings and repair determine which developments occur and how likely
  each is. No access multiplier is needed, and one would count the same effect
  twice and lose the uniqueness of the count. Where a difference sits, in time
  spent in each declared regime or in branching within one, the split by
  regime says, for the partition declared ([Proposition A.3.10](#a-3-10)).
- **Distribution across frames is not carried by the whole-field number.** At
  each step the whole field's count is one joint entropy. Two options can have
  the same joint entropy at every step while the branching sits in different
  parts of the world: in one, part D varies and part E is fixed; in the other,
  the reverse. Their profiles are identical. Counting each part's own history
  separates them ([Definition A.7.1](#a-7-1)). Omega holds the difference; the
  single number does not.

The third layer is not the only structure the number can miss. Being in the
history law is not the same as being separated by its entropy: different
conditional structures of the futures can have equal entropy profiles even
when no branching moves between frames. That is the compression boundary in
general ([chapter 4](#chapter-4)), and it stays open.

Whether ethics needs what the number does not carry is part of the thesis.
If a persistent equal pair (section 32.7), or a clear case in which our
judgement tracks something the measure does not carry (sections 32.4 and
32.5), shows that it does, Omega is still the object, and its reading has to
change. The count cannot be repaired by a weight chosen after the result: it
is the unique count meeting its four requirements
([Theorem A.2.3](#a-2-3)). Three routes remain, and the evidence would have
to choose among them.
- **Read more of Omega's structure.** Apply the same count to each frame's
  own history. That reading is derived, not added. Its cost is known in
  advance: turning many frames' readings into one verdict needs a rule for
  weighing frames against one another, which is the question of who counts
  that the comparison with no index was built to avoid ([chapter 10](#chapter-10)).
- **Count only what frames can bring about.** Apply the same count to access
  itself: the effective number of futures a frame's state can distinguishably
  bring about. This would separate organization from sterile activity by
  construction. Its costs: randomness that no frame can use would stop
  counting; the line about dreaming in [Overview section 6](#overview-6),
  which rests on inner variation that cannot act, would fail; the answer to
  busy noise would be built in rather than tested; and the whole field, which
  has nothing outside it, has no natural version. It is also not yet defined.
  Read as a capacity from a frame's present state to what follows, it counts
  memory as well as influence: an inert bit that keeps its value has a
  perfect channel to its own future and enables nothing. A usable version
  needs physically implemented interventions, their costs, and the
  consequences they can distinguish ([chapter 33](#chapter-33)).
- **Change one of the four requirements.** The count would still be the
  unique answer to its requirements, and the question would become which one
  ethics does not share.

Any of the three would have to explain the results that motivated lushness as
well as the one that defeated it, and make a prediction the old reading did
not. Once it is defined, the second route is worth computing alongside
lushness, in the models that separate information from action: the cases
where the two readings disagree are exactly the sharp ones, busy noise, the
dreaming register, the coin pair and the equal pairs of section 32.7. We
should neither assume that the reading must change nor rule it out.

## 32.9 The realism of the models

**The objection.** Every model result comes from a small, hand-built world.
Some rest on two base worlds, one mobility coefficient or one kinetic
parameter. Real organization works in space, at scale, against a thermal
background whose branching at fine detail is enormous. The rankings may be
artefacts of construction.

**Where the evidence stands.**
- **Audits have caught construction artefacts before.** A steering rule that
  redirected all of a model's chemistry was found on audit and its results
  withdrawn. A permissive control was found to have built in its answer
  ([Appendix D, section D.7](#appendix-d)).
- **Several sweeps have not landed.** The suffering family's parameter sweep,
  the lattice's mobility sweep, and the policy search's kinetic sweep.
- **The comparison against disorder with room to spread is not built.** The
  compounding models have no positions; the one spatial model had a single
  carrier and did not sustain assembly.
- **No estimator for realistic systems exists** ([chapter 33](#chapter-33)).

**What the theory requires.** Not that every ranking survive every sweep. A
sweep is meant to find where a mechanism works, fails and reverses. What the
theory requires is that the boundaries it finds be predicted, or explained
afterwards, in the theory's own terms: persistence, repayment, access, the
structure of harm. Beyond the models, the claim is a capability: that
organization can raise the rate at which futures open in real systems, in the
settings named in [chapter 33](#chapter-33).

| Result | What it would teach | Depth | Next experiment |
|---|---|---|---|
| A ranking holds across its sweep | Its generality within the model is stronger. | none | the next model in the order of work |
| A sweep finds a boundary the theory predicts or explains | Where the mechanism operates. | none; a finding | test the predicted boundary in a second model |
| A sweep finds a reversal the theory cannot explain | Something in the model or the claim is wrong. | model, or claim | look first for a missing interaction or timescale; if the model is right, narrow the claim |
| In the spatial model, organization never raises the rate once disorder can spread | The capability claim does not reach settings with dispersal. | claim (domain), the largest on this list short of the thesis | test whether persistence or a richer loop changes it; [Overview section 4](#overview-4) would have to say so if not |
| Real-system tests find no organized process raising the rate, in the settings named | The capability does not carry to the real world there. | claim | widen or change the real-system settings ([chapter 33](#chapter-33)) |

A result that does not survive a check it was claimed to survive drops a tier,
or becomes a diagnostic, and every chapter citing it is revised.

## 32.10 How the theory would grow

**The thesis is universal over classes of cases in which our judgements are
clear. A certified failure in such a class counts against it. What a result
teaches decides how deep the revision goes.**

Most outcomes in this chapter would strengthen the account or map where its
mechanisms work. Some would narrow a claim's domain. A few would revise the
central proposal: confinement keeping more open than every available use of
the land, a persistent equal pair, a loss we firmly count as harm widening the
field through the whole window. One of those would revise not the object but
its reading.

Whichever comes, the program will report it, will not reclassify a claim as a
guess or move the level of detail or the horizon, and will change the theory to
fit the evidence. The revision will have to explain the new result and the old
ones, and say what to test next. The program is after what is true, not after
keeping its first statement of it ([chapter 12](#chapter-12)).

## 32.11 What this chapter does not claim

- **That the thesis will pass these tests.** None is yet decided.
- **That these are the only tests.** They are the ones that bear hardest on it
  on present evidence. [Appendix E](#appendix-e) states every objection by
  field.
- **That passing them would establish the thesis beyond the models.** The
  real-system tests of [chapter 33](#chapter-33) would still be needed.
