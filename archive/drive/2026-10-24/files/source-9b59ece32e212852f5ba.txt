CURRENT STATUS

The project is a research program aimed at explaining how meaningful possibility, situated perspective, persistence, agency, value, and eventually normative and phenomenal structure can arise within a physical universe. Its present technical center is the relation between universal quantum possibility and the local, situated structure available to a physical perspective within it.

The program does not begin by assuming a ready-made space of meaningful futures. It begins from the physical universe itself and asks what structure the physics supplies: what alternatives exist, which alternatives can remain coherent, how records stabilize, how locality and accessibility constrain a situated system, how past and future are organized relative to such a system, and which physical differences persist as genuine differences in continuation.

The current physical program can be summarized schematically as:

    universal quantum possibility/process structure
        -> intrinsic physical local/continuity structure
        -> situated Perspective
        -> perspectival present / record-conditioned access
        -> compatible pasts, trajectory structure, and conditional futures
        -> consequential continuation
        -> compression and effective geometry
        -> later questions of Lushness, agency, value, ethics, and consciousness

The first six documents in this checkpoint cover the physical and perspectival part of that sequence.


1. CENTRAL QUESTION

The broad question is:

    How does a physical universe containing quantum possibility give rise to local viewpoints from which some differences become persistent, consequential, actionable, and ultimately meaningful?

The problem has several parts that must fit together.

First, the universe is not treated as a classical catalogue of mutually exclusive trajectories with ordinary probabilities already attached. At fine resolution, quantum alternatives can interfere. Different ways of resolving a process can be incompatible. Quasiclassical histories emerge only under special physical conditions such as decoherence and stable record formation.

Second, a situated system does not have direct access to the universal structure as a whole. Its physical situation, boundary, records, couplings, and available interactions define a local support structure within the universal process. The program calls such a situated structure a Perspective. Perspective is a physical concept before it is a psychological one. A Perspective need not be conscious and need not correspond to an external observer choosing a coarse-graining.

Third, the ordinary distinctions among past, present, and future must be reconstructed from within such a Perspective. The present is not assumed to be a globally privileged cosmic instant. It is provisionally understood as the local frontier at which stable records, present physical support, and conditional continuation meet. Past structure is available through records and correlations. Future structure is available through conditional physical continuation. A Perspective can therefore have a real local present without requiring a universal now.

Fourth, the project asks what makes two physically distinct possibilities consequentially different. A microscopic difference that is immediately mixed away and can never influence any later continuation is not the same kind of structure as a difference that is amplified, recorded, inherited, or opens a distinct regime of continuation. This distinction is intended to be derived from the physical structure of continuation rather than imposed through semantic labels.

The later parts of the research program build on this physical account. Once consequential continuation is grounded, the project asks how to remove redundant description while preserving future-relevant structure, how to characterize the resulting geometry of effective possibility, whether that geometry supports a useful notion of Lushness, how persistent self-maintaining systems occupy and alter it, and how value and normative relations arise among such systems.


2. THE PHYSICAL STARTING POINT

The leading physical picture is a universal quantum possibility or process structure. Depending on formalism and regime, this may be represented using a universal quantum state, a histories object, a process-like object, a boundary formulation, a local-algebraic description, or another mathematically equivalent or more general carrier.

The important content is not commitment to one notation. It is that the universal physical structure is capable of representing:

- coherent alternatives;
- interference among alternatives;
- incompatible resolutions of the same process;
- entanglement and nonclassical correlation;
- compositional relations among regions or stages;
- decoherence and the emergence of approximately autonomous sectors;
- stable records;
- conditional continuation from a situated physical state or support;
- physical weighting, including Born weights where ordinary additive probabilities are licensed.

At fine resolution, the primitive structure is closer to amplitudes, density operators, process operators, or a decoherence functional than to an ordinary probability distribution over classical histories.

A representative histories expression is:

    C_alpha = P_{alpha_n}(t_n) ... P_{alpha_1}(t_1)

with decoherence functional

    D(alpha,beta) = Tr(C_alpha rho C_beta^dagger).

When the off-diagonal terms are negligible for a physically relevant coarse-graining,

    D(alpha,beta) approximately 0    for alpha != beta,

the corresponding alternatives can behave approximately like an ordinary probabilistic history family. This is one route by which familiar classical-looking alternatives emerge from the quantum structure.

The program therefore treats branch count, raw state count, and classical trajectory count with caution. The existence of many formal alternatives is not yet a physically meaningful measure of possibility. Support, amplitude, interference, decoherence, probability, and consequence are different structures.


3. INTRINSIC PHYSICAL LOCAL STRUCTURE

A major current target is the intrinsic local structure of physical possibility. For shorthand this is sometimes represented as a topology or local geometry, but ordinary point-set topology is only one possible mathematical realization.

The intended object is whatever fundamental physics uses to determine facts such as:

- which physical possibilities are locally connected;
- which transitions compose;
- which alternatives can interfere;
- which configurations or process fragments are continuously related;
- which regions are causally or operationally accessible from which others;
- which distinctions can be amplified or propagated;
- which structures are separated by genuine physical obstruction;
- which changes are merely coordinate, basis, gauge, or representational changes.

In quantum theory the appropriate structure may involve several mathematical layers at once: Hilbert or projective geometry, operator algebras and their topologies, trace or process norms, causal structure, tensor-factor or subsystem relations where physically justified, spacetime locality, process composition, boundary state spaces, or sheaf-like/local-algebraic organization.

The key research commitment is that this structure is supplied by physics. Observables and experiments can characterize it, reveal it, or reconstruct aspects of it, but they are not free primitives whose arbitrary selection manufactures the ontology.

This matters because any later notion of consequence must inherit its legitimacy from physical structure. A coarse description of possibility should respect the actual local and compositional organization of the physical world. Consequential equivalence should therefore be constrained by what the physics permits to remain connected, distinct, amplifiable, accessible, or composable.


4. PERSPECTIVE

A Perspective is a situated physical structure within the universal possibility structure. It is not defined as a conscious observer and is not simply a partition chosen by an analyst.

The current working idea combines several features:

- bounded physical access;
- a local or compositional boundary;
- stable records and correlations;
- environmental coupling and decoherence;
- a physically available support at the current boundary;
- conditional continuation from that support;
- a local distinction between what is recorded, what is currently accessible, and what remains unresolved.

A useful quantum scaffold is to imagine a record structure R_r and a universal state rho. Conditioning on that record gives a relative or conditional sector such as

    rho^(r) = R_r rho R_r / Tr(R_r rho),

when the mathematical assumptions needed for such an expression hold.

The conceptual point is broader than this formula. A Perspective is a physically situated organization of quantum support and uncertainty around stable records. Some distinctions have become robustly encoded. Others remain coherent or unresolved. Some incompatible quantities cannot be simultaneously sharpened. The Perspective is the structured local situation formed by those physical facts.

This makes Perspective compatible with nested scales. A cell, organism, detector, laboratory, or larger organized system may each support distinct but related local interfaces and records, provided the relevant physical individuation can be specified. The final theory need not privilege ordinary object boundaries if the fundamental physical structure provides a different notion of locality or composition.


5. THE PERSPECTIVAL PRESENT

The program treats the present as perspectivally special rather than globally privileged.

A situated system has direct physical access through its current support and couplings. Remote past structure is available through records and correlations that survive into that support. Remote future structure is available through conditional amplitudes, channels, processes, or continuation relations from that support.

This motivates the idea of a present boundary or record frontier:

    record-compatible past
        | present situated support |
    conditional future continuation

The vertical divider is not a universal metaphysical instant. It is the local physical frontier associated with a Perspective.

Decoherence and conditioning play different roles here. Decoherence suppresses usable interference between certain alternatives and stabilizes approximately autonomous, record-bearing structures. Conditioning on a record situates the Perspective relative to one such structure. In an Everettian reading, decoherence does not by itself select one uniquely real world; it explains why branch-like sectors can become dynamically autonomous and why records can support stable local viewpoints.

The present therefore acts as a local membrane of physical access: not necessarily one exact configuration, but a coherent support structure through which both past records and future conditional possibilities are represented.


6. PASTS, TRAJECTORIES, AND FOCUS

The project distinguishes three related but nonidentical notions.

Configuration is a possible physical arrangement or support on a cut or local boundary.

Trajectory direction is an orientation of continuation through successive local structures. In semiclassical mechanics this can become momentum-like or tangent-like.

Focus is a remote condition relative to which compatible histories or trajectory contributions are being resolved. A focus can lie away from the local present and need not represent an actually performed postselection.

A present Perspective generally does not determine one unique microscopic past. Many history contributions may be compatible with the same current records and support. In quantum theory these alternatives need not represent ignorance about a single hidden classical trajectory; they may remain genuinely coherent until decoherence or further conditioning makes a classical-like description appropriate.

In suitable semiclassical regimes, a wavefunction

    psi(q) = R(q) exp(i S(q)/hbar)

supports the local relation

    p(q) = grad S(q),

and a propagator

    K(q_f,t_f;q,t) approximately sum_a A_a exp(i S_a(q_f,q)/hbar)

can associate a present configuration and remote endpoint with a family of stationary-action trajectory directions. This gives concrete mathematical support to the distinction among configuration, trajectory direction, and remote focus without treating the semiclassical formulas as universal definitions.

A future condition represented by an effect E_F at time tau can be represented at an earlier boundary by backward propagation,

    E_F(t) = U(tau,t)^dagger E_F U(tau,t),

so that its compatibility with a present state can be evaluated locally. This is the sense in which a remote focus can be relevant to a present Perspective through the current boundary.


7. CONSEQUENCE AS ORIENTED CONTINUATION

The physical program ultimately needs to distinguish raw physical difference from consequential physical difference.

The working intuition is that a difference becomes consequential when it alters structured continuation in a way that survives within the physical local geometry. Such a difference may change later accessibility, records, control possibilities, composition, amplification, or the conditional future process available from a situated state.

This gives a natural role to several phenomena:

- amplification: a microscopic difference becomes a persistent macroscopic record;
- erasure or mixing: a distinction ceases to affect later continuation;
- recombination: different histories lead to effectively indistinguishable residual processes;
- persistence: a distinction remains consequential across increasing horizon;
- generativity: a realized structure enables new differentiated continuation that itself becomes a source of further continuation.

The project intends to formalize these structures without assuming that semantic importance, biological identity, moral status, or consciousness is already present.

Once a physical Perspective and its continuation structure are identified, the next technical problem is compression: removing distinctions that are redundant for continuation while preserving the structure that can still matter. That downstream problem leads to predictive sufficiency, process-level operational geometry, and eventually a candidate geometry of nonredundant possibility.


8. RELATION TO THE BROADER PROGRAM

The physical/perspectival work is the foundation for later parts of the project.

Persistence and agency ask how organized systems can maintain identity and exert control within the continuation structure.

Valuerhood asks when continuation differences become endogenous stakes for a system rather than merely external descriptions.

Multi-agent structure asks how different valuers' continuations are compatible, incompatible, recoverable, or mutually constraining.

Gradient Ethics asks how irreversible contraction, recovery, standing, compensation, and authority should be treated once value-bearing systems are in view.

Consciousness asks which, if any, physically situated structures are accompanied by phenomenal experience and how formal or physical perspective is related to first-person actuality.

Metaphysical and theological interpretation asks what, if anything, the completed physical and perspectival picture implies or suggests about broader questions of being, divinity, providence, and meaning.

Those later layers depend on the physical core but are not required to define it.


9. CURRENT RESEARCH FRONTIER

The immediate frontier is to turn the physical picture into a sufficiently canonical mathematical construction.

The main tasks are:

- identify the correct physical local structure for quantum possibility;
- formulate Perspective within that structure without importing an external observer;
- characterize the present record/access frontier in relativistically acceptable terms;
- express compatible histories, trajectory direction, and focus without depending on one semiclassical representation;
- derive consequential continuation from the physical structure;
- determine which equivalences and coarse descriptions are physically natural;
- establish invariance under changes that should not alter the physics;
- connect the resulting structure to process-level compression and geometry.

The target is not merely a descriptive vocabulary. It is a mathematical bridge from the structure of quantum possibility to the structured continuation available from within a situated physical system.
