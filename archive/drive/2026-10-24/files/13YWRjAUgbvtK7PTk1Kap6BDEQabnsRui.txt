THE PERSPECTIVAL PRESENT, RECORDS, AND DECOHERENCE
v2.5 checkpoint
Current through 21 September 2026

CURRENT STATUS

The program treats the present as a local physical feature of Perspective rather than a universal cosmic instant. The leading picture is a record-conditioned access frontier: past structure is available through stable records and correlations, future structure is available through conditional continuation, and the Perspective occupies the local boundary at which these meet.

Decoherence, record formation, and conditioning are central to this picture. They organize how a universal quantum process can support stable local viewpoints and quasiclassical temporal structure without assuming a fundamental classical world partition.


1. WHY THE PRESENT REQUIRES A PERSPECTIVAL ACCOUNT

Modern physics does not naturally supply one universal, absolute present.

Relativity denies a frame-independent global simultaneity relation across spacelike separated events. Quantum theory, particularly in cosmological or closed-system settings, does not require an external observer who advances a universal state one moment at a time.

Yet finite systems possess real temporal organization. They carry records, anticipate outcomes, act, update, and distinguish what is settled from what is unresolved.

The project therefore asks:

    What physical structure makes a local present real for a situated system without making it a universal property of the cosmos?

The current answer is that presentness is tied to the physical support and record structure of a Perspective.


2. THE RECORD FRONTIER

A compact picture is:

    record-compatible past
        | present support |
    conditional future continuation

The middle term is the record frontier or perspectival present.

This frontier is not required to be infinitesimally thin or globally synchronized. It can be distributed over a finite physical region and over the response times of the system that realizes it.

For a detector, the frontier may be associated with a current apparatus state and memory register.

For an organism, it may involve a temporally extended integration window, sensory buffers, working memory, ongoing motor control, and internal prediction.

For a relativistic local region, it may be associated with a causal boundary, local algebra, world tube, or compositional interface rather than a constant-time hypersurface.

The central role of the frontier is that it separates two physically different modes of relation:

- pastward information is carried by records that already constrain the present state;
- futureward information is carried by conditional physical continuation from the present state.


3. RECORDS AS PHYSICAL CORRELATIONS

A record is not merely a semantic statement about the past. It is a present physical correlation produced by earlier interaction.

A robust record can be encoded in:

- a memory device;
- a stable molecular or cellular configuration;
- a pointer state;
- environmental scattering;
- a neural state;
- a fossil or macroscopic trace;
- a distributed redundancy pattern in the environment.

Records matter because they allow past events to remain physically consequential at the present boundary.

If a past distinction leaves no surviving correlation and no effect on future continuation, then relative to the present Perspective it may be physically unrecoverable. If a distinction is redundantly recorded and can influence many future processes, it becomes part of the stable effective past.

This makes record persistence an important bridge between microscopic quantum dynamics and macroscopic temporal structure.


4. DECOHERENCE

Decoherence occurs when a system becomes entangled with environmental degrees of freedom in a way that suppresses the observable interference between certain alternatives.

A simple schematic state may take the form

    |Psi> = sum_i c_i |s_i> |E_i>,

where the environment states |E_i> become nearly orthogonal:

    <E_i|E_j> approximately 0

for i != j.

The reduced state of the system then becomes approximately diagonal in a dynamically selected basis:

    rho_S approximately sum_i |c_i|^2 |s_i><s_i|.

This does not turn the universal pure state into a literal classical mixture. It does make the interference between the alternatives inaccessible to local operations to extremely high precision.

The project uses this as a physical mechanism for the emergence of stable, approximately autonomous sectors.


5. DECOHERENCE IS NOT CONDITIONING

The program maintains a strict conceptual distinction.

Decoherence explains why alternatives become dynamically autonomous and why stable records can exist.

Conditioning identifies the local sector relative to which a particular Perspective is situated.

If R_r represents a record associated with outcome r, a relative state may be represented schematically by

    rho^(r) = R_r rho R_r / Tr(R_r rho).

The existence of the decohered alternatives does not by itself say which conditional sector describes a given situated Perspective. The conditioning relation is tied to the records physically present at that Perspective.

In an Everettian interpretation, all decohered sectors remain within the universal state while individual Perspectives are branch-relative or record-relative.

In another interpretation, the ontology may be described differently. The local mathematical distinction between decoherence and record-conditioned description remains useful.


6. THE EMERGENCE OF A QUASICLASSICAL PAST

A classical-looking past does not require one exact microscopic history.

Suppose a present macroscopic record is compatible with many fine-grained quantum histories. If those histories agree on a robust sequence of coarse records and if interference among the relevant alternatives is negligible, then the Perspective can possess one stable effective past even though the microscopic history remains nonunique.

Schematically:

    many compatible fine histories
        -> shared stable records
        -> one effective quasiclassical trajectory.

This is particularly important for a block or Everettian cosmology. The past experienced by a Perspective can be sharply structured at the macroscopic level without requiring that one exact fine-grained classical path was always the fundamental ontology.


7. QUANTUM DARWINISM AND REDUNDANT RECORDS

Quantum Darwinism studies how information about selected system observables can become redundantly encoded in many environmental fragments.

A macroscopic state may be indirectly observable by many systems because photons, air molecules, or other environmental carriers contain correlated information about it.

This provides a candidate physical basis for local objectivity:

- many Perspectives can access compatible records;
- they need not interact directly with the original system;
- repeated observations reveal the same stable coarse fact;
- the environment acts as a communication channel carrying pointer information.

For the project, redundant recording may help explain why multiple local Perspectives can occupy one apparently shared classical world despite the underlying universal quantum structure.


8. THE PRESENT AS A MEMBRANE OF ACCESS

The term "present membrane" is a useful geometric metaphor.

The Perspective does not necessarily occupy a single exact configuration q. Instead, its present support may be a quantum distribution or process boundary that coherently includes multiple compatible states or directions.

In a simple representation,

    |psi_t> = integral dq psi_t(q) |q>.

The Perspective's local support can therefore have finite spread and quantum uncertainty.

What makes it the present is not exact localization. It is that all currently available records and all accessible conditional continuations are represented through this boundary.

Remote past and future structure matter only insofar as they are physically encoded at or connected through the membrane.


9. TEMPORAL ASYMMETRY

The universal equations may be time-reversal symmetric or globally block-like while local Perspectives exhibit a strong past/future asymmetry.

Several physical structures contribute:

- low-entropy boundary conditions;
- thermodynamic irreversibility;
- record accumulation;
- decoherence;
- causal asymmetry;
- memory architecture;
- control and feedback;
- computational asymmetry between stored data and unresolved outcomes.

The project does not currently reduce all of these arrows to one primitive arrow. It treats their relationship as an important physical question.

The record frontier provides one local manifestation:

    past = structure already compressed into stable correlations;
    future = structure represented by conditional continuation from those correlations.


10. RECORD LOSS AND ERASURE

Records can be erased, corrupted, decohered into inaccessible degrees of freedom, or made causally irrelevant.

This introduces a distinction between:

- a fact having once left a consequence;
- that consequence remaining accessible now;
- the fact remaining capable of affecting future continuation.

For example, a transient bit may be recorded and later overwritten. It was consequential at an earlier horizon but may no longer distinguish the present residual process.

This distinction later becomes important for cumulative versus surviving consequence.

At the present-layer level, it means the effective past of a Perspective is defined by surviving record structure, not by perfect retention of every physical event.


11. LOCAL PRESENTS IN RELATIVITY

Different Perspectives can have different local present boundaries.

In a relativistic setting, one can use structures such as:

- a world tube of a physical system;
- a causal diamond;
- a local algebra associated with a spacetime region;
- a boundary hypersurface of a finite region;
- proper-time ordering along a local trajectory;
- causal networks of records and interactions.

No universal agreement on distant simultaneity is required.

Shared macroscopic reality can arise through overlapping causal histories and mutually consistent records. Two Perspectives can agree on an event once signals from that event lie within both of their causal pasts, even if they previously assigned different temporal coordinates to it.

A mature formalism should therefore define presentness through local causal and record structure rather than global time labels.


12. PRESENTNESS AND COMPUTATION

The present is also a computational boundary for bounded systems.

A system cannot generally compute the entire universal future. It acts using compressed internal states, local records, predictive models, and limited resources.

This creates a practical distinction between:

- the full conditional future process physically implied by the present;
- the subset represented in the system's internal model;
- the subset that can be evaluated before action is required.

The present therefore combines physical and computational boundedness.

This does not mean that Perspective is defined by ignorance. The physical conditional structure exists whether or not the local system can calculate it. Computation determines how much of that structure the system can exploit.


13. COHERENCE AT THE PRESENT BOUNDARY

Some alternatives represented at the present boundary can remain coherent.

Two alternatives alpha and beta remain physically coherent when their relative phase can still affect at least one admissible continuation. In a histories description, this is indicated by non-negligible cross terms

    D(alpha,beta).

A Perspective can therefore occupy a present support that includes coherent uncertainty rather than one classical outcome.

This is especially relevant at microscopic scales or for deliberately isolated quantum systems. The concept of present Perspective must be broad enough to include quantum laboratories and coherent devices, not only decohered macroscopic organisms.


14. CONDITIONING WITHOUT LITERAL POSTSELECTION

The project sometimes uses remote focus or future-effect mathematics to describe how a future condition can be represented at the present boundary.

This should not be confused with claiming that the universe literally postselects on a chosen future event.

If E_F represents a possible future condition at time tau, its effect at time t can be written

    E_F(t) = U(tau,t)^dagger E_F U(tau,t).

This tells us how the future condition constrains present amplitudes or intermediate probabilities.

The same formal move can be used diagnostically to study trajectory bundles and focus-relative coherence without asserting retrocausality or a special final boundary condition.


15. PRESENT AND ACTION

For an agentive system, the present is the point at which records become input to action.

The local system combines:

- memory of prior conditions;
- current sensory or internal state;
- predictive continuation;
- control policy;
- available interventions.

Action then changes which continuation becomes locally realized or accessible.

This makes the present frontier the natural interface between descriptive physics and later decision theory.

The physical program must first explain the frontier itself. Agency is added only once the system has control-relevant structure across it.


16. MAIN FORMAL TARGETS

A mature account of the perspectival present should provide:

1. A physically defined local boundary or support.
2. A mathematical representation of present records.
3. A conditioning rule for the local quantum state or process.
4. A criterion for record stability.
5. A relation between decoherence and effective classical trajectory structure.
6. A local definition of past compatibility.
7. A local definition of conditional future continuation.
8. A relativistically acceptable treatment without a preferred foliation.
9. A rule for overlapping or interacting Perspectives.
10. A description of how the frontier changes under measurement, memory update, action, and decoherence.


17. CURRENT RESEARCH QUESTIONS

The current questions include:

- What is the best quantum object for the present support of a Perspective: state, local algebra, boundary state, process slice, record-conditioned process, or another object?

- How are records defined in fully quantum field-theoretic settings?

- What physical condition distinguishes a stable record from a transient correlation?

- How does the record frontier relate to thermodynamic and causal arrows?

- How large can the temporal integration window of a Perspective be while still functioning as one present?

- How should quantum-coherent Perspectives be described when no classical record has yet formed?

- What transformations leave the same perspectival present physically unchanged?

- How can local presents be composed into shared macroscopic histories without introducing a preferred global now?

These questions form the technical core of the project's treatment of presentness.
