## Standing rulings applied

The originator's rulings govern every row below.

- **Subject.** Ethics and motivation use the same objective. Ethics applies it to
  the whole field; motivation applies it to the self. Futures are intertwined.
- **Diagnostics.** Results from models that do not capture the intended object
  are diagnostics. They stay in provenance and carry no weight as evidence for
  the theory.
- **Randomness and gas.** Randomness counts, and gas is counted. The claim to
  defend is the capability to generate a sustained rate increment, not a large
  margin over the thermal background.
- **Protection.** Keep the channel open; do not maximize protection.
- **Rule A** is parked.
- **Research stance.** We are still drafting, and empirical results guide the
  theory. Predictions are recorded where useful but are not held to strict
  preregistration: the aim is to seek truth, not to declare it and wait for
  confirmation.
- **Process.** No chapter is edited before discussion, and nothing is committed

## Evidence rubric

A result can support a claim only if its model instantiates the object the claim
is about. A model may still support claims about the objects it does instantiate,
such as a theorem about the measure, a local mechanism or a single-agent result.

Theory-bearing evidence for a claim requires all of the following:

1. **Matching.** Conserved quantities and resources are matched across the
   comparison.
2. **Presence.** The relevant coupling or subject is physically present. Claims
   about appropriation, domination, whole-field ethics or coupled futures need
   those relations in the model.
3. **A stable objective.** The primary objective was not changed after the
   results were seen.
4. **Accounting.** Resource accounting is sufficient for the claim.
5. **No built-in answer.** No analytical feature predetermines the ranking being
   sought.

If any of these is missing, the result teaches us about the diagnostic, not about
the target claim. Classification follows the structure of the model. Make it
before interpreting a result wherever practicable, and apply it equally to
favourable and unfavourable results. Demoting a failure after the fact requires
naming which criterion it fails.

**Structural account.** Every important scalar result is reported together with
answers to nine questions:

1. What changed in Omega?
2. Which channel or dependency caused the change?
3. How robust, repairable or substitutable is that channel?
4. Does using the channel deplete it or maintain it?
5. Do descendants reconstruct or expand it?
6. What remains after it is lost, and what would be available without it?
7. What does finite-history lushness report?
8. What does the stationary rate report?
9. Are those results stable across the declared resolution corridor?

The structural account explains and predicts. It does not overrule lushness. A
disagreement between them is evidence about the compression boundary (A14) and
the central wager.

**Two experimental layers.**

- **Oracle layer.** The model is known exactly. It shows what lushness itself
  ranks.
- **Bounded-agent layer.** The controller has partial observation, uncertainty
  or a mismatched model. It shows what an embedded controller can know and
  implement.

Every row's evidence is tagged by layer. Precaution, correction and deference in
v4 derive from uncertainty, so oracle results neither confirm nor refute them.

A controller that receives perfect state information and re-plans from each new
present (receding horizon) still has the exact model. It belongs to the oracle
layer, as a check that a result does not depend on the restriction to stationary
controllers.

In bounded-agent experiments, report how much of the value of inspection or
consultation comes from its effect on later choices, and how much from the
randomness of the inspection event itself. Randomness counts, but the two must
not be confused. CORR-1 and S5 both showed probing chosen partly for its own
entropy.

### A4. Protecting channels, not stock

- **Current wording.** 5.3, "Persistence: the metavalue", and 5.4, strict and
  precautionary protection. The current text does not distinguish stock from
  capacity.
- **Disposition.** Revise.
- **Proposed wording.** Distinguish five things:
  - stock preservation;
  - functional availability;
  - recovery;
  - a continued channel of access;
  - generative capacity.

  Protection targets the capacity for further continuation. Maximal protection
  of particular structures can amount to lock-in. Margin is precautionary, graded
  by uncertainty and irreversibility.
- **Type and class.** Normative, with theory-bearing experiments as illustration.
- **Evidence (test bed, whole-world objective):**
  - **Between lock-in and burnout.** The breadth winner sits between the
    protective controls (idle, always service) and the active ones (always
    export, half/half) on every protection readout.
  - **Margin.** It keeps 4.8–9.2% less working apparatus than always servicing,
    across capture factors 1–12. Under this ruling that is a margin readout, not
    a hit.
  - **Recoverability.** Protection rises with recoverability: with shallow wells,
    the stationary usable fraction is 0.66 at depth 0 and 0.03 at depth −4.
- **Diagnostic, not cited.** The legacy surviving-hardware deep grid (closure
  2.106% against 1.080%). Its ranking was decided by the tail after closure.
- **Pressure.** Closure risk remains a diagnostic pressure point. See C9.
- **Pressure (reserve batch).** Doubling the hazard, and making shocks fully
  correlated, changed none of the 15 registered winners. In that fixture every
  harm was repairable (working apparatus became worn) or reversible (trapping at
  depth −1). A reserve therefore bought nothing that repair did not. The result
  is read narrowly: it does not test whether precaution responds to the shape of
  harm (C14).
- **Evidence (harm sweep, oracle layer).** The structure of harm changes policy.
  Irreversible loss of the powered repair hardware led the winner to service
  *less* than under fast repair, at T64 and T256 with h = 0.02. Hardware was
  mostly used rather than preserved: an 87–91% chance of losing it. The channel
  never closed, because basal construction and thermal recovery remained as
  substitutes. This is consistent with protecting the channel rather than stock.
  When one capability is lost irreversibly but substitutes keep the channel open,
  that particular structure is not protected.
- **Dependencies.** A5.

### B1. The whole-field ethical subject

- **Current wording.** Absent. v4 never states the subject distinction.
- **Disposition.** Add.
- **Proposed wording.** Motivation evaluates continuation relative to the acting
  self. Ethics evaluates the encompassing continuation. Because futures are
  intertwined, the two are not separable worlds. Moving from the self to the
  whole field still requires an impartiality premise, which intertwining narrows
  but does not remove.
- **Type and class.** Normative architecture.
- **Evidence.** Round 4 Game 1: within one model, the self-directed objective
  excludes others and the whole-field objective does not. This is a small model,
  and the adjudicators should confirm that it captures the object.
- **Pending test.** C5's subject arms.

### B2. Rate-first as a decision rule

- **Current wording.** 4.5 (rate-then-offset is adopted, "not forced by the
  entropy formula"). 6: "Where a harm does permanently lower the rate, it
  eventually outweighs any finite offset." 9.4, with rates first.
- **Disposition.** Qualify.
- **Proposed wording.** Separate two uses:
  - **Scientific use:** rate as a diagnostic of sustained generativity, as in A3.
  - **Decision use:** must represent the generative window, irreplaceability and
    uncertainty explicitly. Whether these suffice to prevent pathological lexical
    trades is an open question.
- **Type and class.** Decision-theoretic; an analytic property of the rule.
- **Evidence (analytic, S4 growth regimes):**
  - **Fixed gains:** never accepted once the world is large.
  - **Growth-rate gains with replaceable losses:** accepted for any speed-up,
    with crossover near Δ/ε.
  - **Irreplaceable generators:** accepted only above ε* = 1/(1−φ) − 1.
- **Pressure.** Mutually exclusive trades under unbounded novelty leave the limit
  rule with no optimal policy.

### B3. The resolution corridor

- **Current wording.** 4.7: "The target is dominance across resolutions," with
  decoherence records as the candidate privileged resolution.
- **Disposition.** Revise the target.
- **Proposed wording.** The sign of a matched rate difference must be the same
  across admissible grains. A grain is admissible if it preserves relations named
  before the comparison: actuator dependence, resource conservation, control
  alternatives, recovery routes, and the continuation distinction under test.
  Cadence robustness alone is not grain robustness.
- **Type and class.** Measure; methodological.
- **Evidence.** The calibration showed cadence sensitivity: 5.5 to 28.9 bits for
  one system.
- **First sign flip in a primary comparison (harm sweep).** The reserve policy's
  advantage over constant servicing reverses with cadence:
  - fast repair, T64: −0.97, +0.21 and +1.29 bits at cadences 0.125, 0.25 and
    0.5;
  - slow repair, T256: −0.24, +0.55 and +1.39 bits.

  The advantage rises steadily as the cadence coarsens. Policy differences of
  about one bit lie within the measure's sensitivity to grain, so these rankings
  cannot be claimed.
- **Conceptual pressure.** Decoherence records of dispersed matter may favour
  dispersal. This is conceptual pressure, not evidence.

- **Disposition.** Add one sentence.
- **Proposed wording.** Gas and organized structure are assessed by the same

### B7. Separability as a modelling choice

- **Current wording.** Absent.
- **Disposition.** Add to 11.1.
- **Proposed wording.** A model may support only claims about objects it actually
  instantiates. Claims about interpersonal appropriation, domination, whole-field
  ethics or coupled futures require those relations to be physically present. For
  those claims, separable models are diagnostics. They can still support theorems
  about the measure, local mechanisms and single-agent claims. See the evidence
  rubric.

### B8. The generative window

- **Current wording.** 4.6: "the interval before forgetting".
- **Disposition.** Qualify.
- **Proposed wording.** Give a physically justified window T_G, or a horizon
  profile, beside the asymptotic rate. The complete continuation remains
  primary, and the rate is one summary of it.

### B9. Impartiality: where it is derived and where it is a premise

- **Current wording.** 5.4: "Impartiality is not an extra axiom. It is simply the
  starting point of an analysis that does not yet know who counts."
- **Disposition.** Qualify. This row reconciles chapter 5 with B1.
- **Proposed wording.** The derivation in chapter 5 holds for an agent committed
  to value wherever it lies. Its uncertainty about where value is realized makes
  the whole structure its object, without a further axiom. It does not hold for
  an agent whose ends are self-directed. For such an agent, persistence and
  dependence extend its concern only to what it depends on, and to whatever it
  cannot rule out depending on (precautionary protection). Moving that agent to
  the whole-field subject needs an impartiality or universalization premise.
  Intertwining narrows the gap but does not close it. Chapter 5 should state both
  cases and say which one the ethics addresses.
- **Type and class.** Normative architecture.
- **Dependencies.** B1.
