Omega Cosmology v3.1 — start here

A standalone, multipart primer for the research program.

The central question

Compare a nebula with a civilization. The civilization is lawful physics, and its futures may already lie within the nebula's possible continuations. Yet tools, records, repair, coordination, and construction have changed what can happen next, how reliably, at what cost, and what can happen together. Omega Cosmology studies that difference:

  Generativity concerns processes that establish, maintain, and transform the conditions for further processes. Lushness seeks to capture the resulting organization of possible continuation and its capacity to support value.

Both are research targets. This edition presents the construction that makes them concrete, from physical continuation through generative organization, lushness, identity, agency, Gradient Ethics, and Omega Decision Theory, with awareness and the Alpha–Omega horizon treated as interpretive extensions.

Reading paths

For the whole program, read the parts in order. For the central new material, read 00, 02, 03, and 04. For a technical collaborator designing experiments, read 02, 03, 11, 12, and 14. For ethics and alignment, read 00, 03, 04, 07, and 08. For awareness and metaphysics, read 00, 01, 09, and 10.

For a model or research assistant, ingest the complete Markdown edition or the complete plain-text edition, and keep the status markers intact: [established], [project result], [proposed], [conjecture].
