GLOSSARY, NOTATION, AND CLAIM STATUS
v2.5 checkpoint
Current through 21 September 2026

PURPOSE

This is the machine-readable lookup file for the v2.5 checkpoint.

Each entry gives:

    TERM / SYMBOL
    DOMAIN
    CURRENT MEANING
    STATUS
    COMMON CONFUSIONS

Status vocabulary is intentionally light:

    FORMAL
        precisely defined and supported in declared mathematical models;

    PHYSICAL TARGET
        current physical research object or architectural commitment;

    WORKING CONCEPT
        active concept with a reasonably stable intended meaning;

    CANDIDATE
        one proposed mathematical or metaphysical realization among several;

    OPEN
        required structure not yet uniquely formalized;

    NORMATIVE
        explicitly evaluative or reason-giving layer;

    METAPHYSICAL
        interpretive structure beyond the physical formalism.

COMMON TYPE ERRORS

1. Omega_phys = Gamma_P
    Incorrect.
    Universal possibility and consequence factorization are distinct.

2. Perspective = conscious observer
    Incorrect.
    Perspective is physical; consciousness is a downstream realization.

3. Decoherence = world selection
    Incorrect.
    Decoherence suppresses interference; conditioning situates the Perspective.

4. Branch count = Born measure
    Incorrect.
