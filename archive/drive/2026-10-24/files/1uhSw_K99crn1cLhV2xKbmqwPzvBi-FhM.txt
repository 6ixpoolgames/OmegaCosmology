Omega and Gradient Ethics

1. Orientation

Omega is a proposal about physical possibility, persistence, emergence, and the conditions under which value can continue to exist.

Its starting point is simple:

«Value requires valuers.»

Whatever value ultimately is, it cannot be realized without physical systems capable of valuing. Valuers are physical processes. Physical processes require continuation. And physical arrangements differ radically in the kinds of continuation they make possible.

This motivates the study of what will be called possibility extent: the physically grounded extent of lawful continuation available from a present physical state.

Possibility extent is not probability. It is not mere branch count, state count, energy, complexity, biodiversity, entropy, life, intelligence, or duration.

It concerns the structured space of what a physical present can continue into.

The organization of that extent across time and scale will be called lushness.

Lushness is not itself identified with moral value. It is a proposed description of the physical substrate through which value-capable organization can persist and emerge.

Gradient Ethics begins from the further observation that, under severe uncertainty about value, preserving and enlarging the physical conditions under which valuers and value can continue has broad instrumental importance.

The cosmology and the ethics therefore meet at the same question:

[
\boxed{
\text{What kinds of physical organization sustain and generate consequential continuation?}
}
]

---

2. Alpha

At the proposed basement of the formalism is Alpha.

Alpha is not a particular microscopic physics. It is a minimal structural schema from which physical theories may be regarded as realizations.

Alpha consists of three primitives:

[
\boxed{\text{Distinction}}
]

[
\boxed{\text{Relation}}
]

[
\boxed{\text{Asymmetry}}
]

Distinction

A distinction is primitive difference.

At the smallest Alpha resolution it is binary only in the minimal sense:

[
\text{same};/;\text{distinct}.
]

This should not be interpreted as a classical bit or quantum bit.

It says only that a structure capable of physical differentiation must admit distinctions.

Relation

A distinction alone has no structure beyond its difference.

Relation concerns how distinctions compose, constrain, enable, deform or exclude one another.

The important sense of relation is therefore not merely static adjacency.

A relation is physically consequential when the condition or deformation of one distinction changes what can lawfully happen through another.

Schematically:

[
A;R;B
]

means that the lawful continuation involving B depends consequentially on A.

Asymmetry

Relation alone does not provide becoming.

Asymmetry supplies orientation.

An event is a realized movement along an asymmetric direction of lawful deformation.

Thus:

[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\text{Distinction} &\rightarrow \text{what can differ},\
\text{Relation} &\rightarrow \text{how differences affect and compose},\
\text{Asymmetry} &\rightarrow \text{oriented deformation}.
\end{aligned}
}
]

Alpha need not specify a preferred clock, lattice, graph, spacetime, particle ontology or update rate.

Those belong to physical realization.

3. Physical frames

Consider an admissible physical present section

[
\Sigma.
]

A physical theory supplies a sufficient physical state on that section:

[
x_\Sigma,
]

together with the native law governing its continuation:

[
\mathcal L.
]

A frame is the physical present understood together with the local continuation structure induced by the law:

[
\boxed{
F_\Sigma=(x_\Sigma,\mathcal G_\Sigma).
}
]

Here

[
\mathcal G_\Sigma
]

is the frame's deformation structure or continuation germ.

It need not be an additional ontological variable. In many theories it can be derived from the combination

[
(x_\Sigma,\mathcal L).
]

Naming it separately emphasizes an important fact:

«A physical present is not merely a list of what exists. Its organization determines how it can lawfully deform.»

The present therefore contains the local orientation of its own possible continuation.

4. The relational structure of a frame

The frozen relational organization of a frame can be provisionally separated into three layers.

4.1 Configuration relations

These are facts about the presently instantiated arrangement.

Examples include:

- spatial incidence;
- bonds;
- contact;
- geometry;
- field configuration;
- conformation;
- component membership;
- resource location.

They describe what is related to what in the present configuration.

4.2 Deformation relations

These describe how one potential deformation changes another.

Suppose A and B are available distinctions or physical deformation channels.

Let

[
C_F(B)
]

denote the continuation available through B at frame F.

Then a local relational response can be written schematically as

[
\boxed{
R_F(A\rightarrow B)=\Delta_A C_F(B).
}
]

If

[
R_F(A\rightarrow B)=0,
]

then deformation along A leaves the relevant continuation through B unchanged.

If it is nonzero, the two are consequentially related.

The effect may involve:

- support;
- rate;
- energetic accessibility;
- timing;
- compatibility;
- resource availability;
- further downstream continuation.

This supplies an operational meaning for relation without introducing semantic primitives such as catalyst, repair, harm or cooperation.

Those descriptions may later be recognized as patterns in the underlying relational deformation.

4.3 Correlation relations

Statistical or quantum correlation is conceptually distinct from direct deformation.

Two systems may be correlated without currently interacting.

Two systems may interact before substantial correlation develops.

Entanglement, classical correlation and interaction therefore should not be collapsed into one generic edge relation.

5. Higher-order relation

Pairwise interaction is not generally sufficient.

It is possible that

[
R(A,B)=R(A,C)=R(B,C)=0
]

while

[
R(A,B,C)\neq0.
]

A transformation may require three components jointly even though no pair alone exhibits the relevant relation.

The relational structure of a frame should therefore be allowed to take the form of a hyperstructure:

[
\boxed{
\mathscr R_F

\left(
R^{(1)},R^{(2)},R^{(3)},\ldots
\right).
}
]

Possible interpretations are:

[
R^{(1)}

\text{available individual deformation},
]

[
R^{(2)}

\text{irreducible pairwise coupling},
]

[
R^{(3)}

\text{irreducible three-way coupling},
]

and so on.

No claim is made that the final mathematics must literally take this tensor hierarchy form.

The important point is that relation may be irreducibly compositional.

6. The local-derivative interpretation

There is a useful analogy between a physical frame and the local differential structure of a mathematical object.

Knowing only

[
f(x)
]

does not tell us everything locally relevant about a function.

Its derivatives,

[
f'(x),f''(x),\ldots,
]

describe how nearby change behaves.

Likewise, the physical configuration

[
x_\Sigma
]

can be distinguished from its hierarchy of lawful responses:

[
\boxed{
J(F_\Sigma)

\left(
x_\Sigma,
R^{(1)}\Sigma,
R^{(2)}\Sigma,
R^{(3)}_\Sigma,\ldots
\right).
}
]

This should be read as an analogy rather than a commitment to smooth jet-bundle mathematics.

Its conceptual lesson is stronger:

«The relational structure of a frozen frame is the local differential structure of its possible continuation.»

Relations are properties of the present even when they are detected through the deformations they produce across time.

A derivative is local despite being revealed by variation.

The same can be true of physical relation.

7. From frames to Omega

A frame does not stand alone.

Its relational structure and physical law generate lawful successor developments.

Schematically:

[
F
\longrightarrow
F_i.
]

Each development carries its native physical weighting.

But a successor list is insufficient.

Possible developments may themselves be:

- alternatives;
- compatible;
- independent;
- mutually exclusive;
- mutually enabling;
- resource competing;
- redundant;
- synergistic;
- reconvergent.

The continuation from F is therefore a structured object.

Write it schematically as

[
\boxed{
\Omega(F)

\left(
F,
\mathcal D_F,
W_F,
\mathcal C_F,
{\Omega(F_i)}
\right).
}
]

Here:

[
\mathcal D_F

\text{lawful immediate developments},
]

[
W_F

\text{their native physical weighting},
]

[
\mathcal C_F

\text{their compatibility and composition structure},
]

and

[
\Omega(F_i)
]

is the residual continuation from each resulting frame.

This recursive continuation object is Omega.

8. The futuresfield

Every future frame is itself another structured present.

Therefore:

[
\boxed{
\text{the future of every point in the futuresfield is itself another futuresfield}.
}
]

Omega is consequently recursive or, more precisely, naturally describable in coinductive terms.

A frame produces future frames.

Those future frames contain relational structure that produces further future frames.

Thus:

[
F
\rightarrow
F_i
\rightarrow
F_{ij}
\rightarrow
F_{ijk}
\rightarrow\cdots
]

while at every stage the same general kind of object recurs.

This is the physical basis of generativity.

Two presents can have identical immediate branching while having radically different further continuation because their successors differ in what they themselves are capable of becoming.

Continuation cannot therefore be characterized completely by one-step alternatives.

---

9. Omega is not a tree

A branching tree is often a useful visualization, but it is not the full object.

Suppose:

[
F\rightarrow A\rightarrow C
]

and

[
F\rightarrow B\rightarrow C.
]

The two prefixes remain distinct developments from the original root.

The universe went through different intermediate configurations.

But once both reach the same sufficient physical present C under the same law, their residual future is shared:

[
\Omega_C^{(A)}

\Omega_C^{(B)}.
]

The complete continuation object therefore includes reconvergence.

The correct distinction is:

[
\boxed{
\text{distinct histories can possess identical remaining futures}.
}
]

Re-rooting at C asks a different question from evaluating the future from the earlier root.

Historical distinction should not be erased retrospectively.

Residual continuation should not be duplicated merely because multiple histories arrive there.

Omega is therefore more naturally represented as a branching and reconverging domain than as a simple tree.

---

10. Composition

Omega contains two fundamentally different notions that should not be conflated.

Alternative continuation

Write:

[
A\oplus B.
]

This means that A and B represent distinct alternative developments.

This is the branching or multiway dimension.

Joint composition

Write:

[
A\otimes B.
]

This means that the developments or structures represented by A and B can occur jointly within one physical continuation.

This is the compositional dimension.

Branch count concerns primarily \oplus.

Emergence depends deeply on \otimes.

A world can contain few alternatives but rich composition.

It can contain huge stochastic branching but little persistent compositional organization.

The two axes are physically distinct.

11. Independence and interaction

A graph diamond does not by itself establish independence.

If two events A and B can occur in either order,

[
AB
]

and

[
BA,
]

they may still change one another's rates, resources or later continuation.

True independence requires a stronger factorization condition.

For genuinely independent systems A and B, the native continuation law should factor in the appropriate physical sense:

[
\mathcal L_{A\otimes B}

\mathcal L_A\otimes\mathcal L_B.
]

Failure of factorization reveals relational structure.

This makes nonfactorization one promising route toward a substrate-independent definition of physical relation.

12. Relativistic present sections

The word frame here does not imply a preferred universal simultaneity.

Let

[
\Sigma
]

be an admissible spacelike present section.

The relevant physical state is the state on that section:

[
F_\Sigma.
]

Different valid foliations may serialize independent spacelike events differently.

A fundamental possibility measure should not change merely because

[
A;B
]

is represented instead as

[
B;A
]

when the ordering difference is only foliation convention.

Thus Omega requires a refoliation-consistency principle.

The mathematics privileges present-rooted conditioning without necessarily committing to metaphysical presentism.

A block universe and a becoming universe may generate the same conditional continuation structure from a given section.

Omega can study that structure without first settling the metaphysics of whether the future already exists.

13. A Wolframian realization

One natural computational realization of this architecture is a multiway rewriting system.

In such a realization:

- a hypergraph or similar object represents the current relational configuration;
- rewrite events provide lawful deformation;
- the multiway structure represents alternative continuation;
- the causal graph represents event dependency;
- reconvergence represents shared resulting configurations;
- branchial slices provide section-dependent views of alternative states.

This is a useful laboratory because the machinery naturally combines:

[
\text{branching}
+
\text{causality}
+
\text{reconvergence}
+
\text{composition}.
]

The proposal does not require Wolfram's cosmological claims to be correct.

A multiway representation is one possible physical/computational realization of the deeper continuation structure.

Omega should ultimately be defined by invariants of that structure rather than by one particular graph encoding.

14. Everettian interpretation

In quantum physics, alternatives cannot generally be treated as classical branches with ordinary probabilities.

Fine-grained histories can interfere.

A quantum realization of Omega therefore cannot simply assign an independent probability to every microscopic path.

A more appropriate object may involve a decoherence functional or related quantum-history structure.

When environmental interactions suppress interference between sufficiently coarse histories, robust approximately classical sectors may emerge.

A persistent macroscopic structure need not correspond to one microscopic branch.

It may instead correspond to a stable relational pattern realized across a bundle of decohered microscopic developments.

Thus the physical unity of an organism, vortex or machine need not be the identity of one exact microhistory.

Its unity can lie in persistent relational organization across many compatible realizations.

15. Persistent structures

A persistent physical entity is not necessarily a collection of permanently identical constituents.

Let

[
V_\Sigma
]

be a relational substructure present on section \Sigma.

It persists when lawful deformation tends to produce later frames containing a related organization:

[
V_\Sigma
\leadsto
V_{\Sigma'}
\leadsto
V_{\Sigma''}.
]

Its microscopic constituents may change.

Its identity is carried by organization.

This provides a general physical interpretation of vortex-like entities.

A strong persistent structure may exhibit:

1. relational continuity;
2. internal regeneration;
3. robustness across multiple microdevelopments;
4. consequential deformation of its environment;
5. ability to participate in higher-order composition.

Stars, autocatalytic systems, organisms, minds and social structures may all instantiate different versions of this architecture without being physically or morally equivalent.

16. Emergence across scale

Microscopic relations can compose into persistent structures.

Those structures can themselves become components of larger relational organizations.

Schematically:

[
F^{(0)}
\rightarrow
F^{(1)}
\rightarrow
F^{(2)}
\rightarrow\cdots
]

where the superscript denotes an effective scale rather than chronological order.

The same universe can therefore possess continuation structure along two axes:

[
\boxed{\text{causal depth}}
]

and

[
\boxed{\text{compositional scale}}.
]

An emergent structure is physically meaningful when its organization preserves consequential information about continuation.

Its existence is not established merely because a human finds a description convenient.

The criterion is dynamical:

«Does the higher-order organization alter or predict lawful continuation in ways that survive the coarse description?»

This allows emergence without introducing new supernatural causal laws.

The higher-level effect is the organized expression of lower-level relations.

17. Probability is not possibility extent

The native physical law assigns weights to continuation.

Let:

[
P_\Sigma
]

denote the classical probability measure over lawful continuation from a frame.

Then:

[
P_\Sigma(\Omega_\Sigma)=1.
]

But this normalization says nothing about how much possibility structure exists.

A probability distribution can have total weight one over:

- one future;
- a billion futures;
- a continuum;
- a highly structured deterministic development.

Therefore:

[
\boxed{
\text{probability}
\neq
\text{possibility extent}.
}
]

Probability describes how physical continuation is weighted.

Possibility extent is intended to describe the size or extent of the continuation structure being weighted.

18. The first classical measurement hypothesis

For a finite discrete Markov realization, let x be the exact sufficient present and let

[
K(x,y)
]

be the native transition law.

For horizon n, a complete configuration sequence is

[
\gamma=(x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_n),
\qquad x_0=x.
]

Its native probability is

[
P_x^n(\gamma)

\prod_{t=0}^{n-1}
K(x_t,x_{t+1}).
]

The first explicit candidate for effective continuation breadth is:

[
\ell_x(n)=H(P_x^n),
]

[
\boxed{
L_x(n)=\exp[\ell_x(n)].
}
]

This is the Shannon effective number of complete future sequences.

For N equally weighted futures:

[
L=N.
]

Probability remains normalized to one while effective breadth can increase.

The rooted path-law formulation and its exact recurrence constitute the current minimal classical measurement hypothesis.

19. Recursive identity for continuation breadth

For a discrete Markov process:

[
\boxed{
\ell_x(n+1)

H(K(x,\cdot))
+
\sum_y K(x,y)\ell_y(n).
}
]

This has an important conceptual interpretation.

The continuation breadth from x consists of:

1. the branching generated at the present frame;
2. plus the expected continuation breadth contained in the resulting frames.

Thus even the simple path-entropy candidate already exhibits the recursive structure of Omega.

At an intermediate cut k:

[
\ell_x(n)

H(X_1,\ldots,X_k|x)
+
\sum_y
P_x(X_k=y)\ell_y(n-k).
]

Re-rooting at y returns the residual future from y.

Recovering the original-root question requires retaining the prefix distribution.

Reconvergence therefore does not create a contradiction between shared residual computation and distinct original-root histories.

20. What continuation breadth measures

The interpretation of L should remain precise.

It measures:

[
\boxed{
\text{effective weighted alternative breadth of complete continuation sequences}.
}
]

It does not automatically measure all structural organization.

A deterministic future has:

[
L=1,
]

whether it is frozen or contains an elaborate deterministic machine.

Likewise, two branching structures with the same complete-path probability spectrum can receive the same L despite different internal relational organization.

These are not mathematical errors.

They reveal what L is measuring.

Accordingly, effective continuation breadth is the first exact baseline hypothesis for possibility extent, not a declaration that all continuation structure reduces to entropy.

21. Structural possibility measure

A more general possibility remains open.

Suppose the continuation object admits a structural measure

[
\Lambda_\Sigma.
]

Then:

[
P_\Sigma
]

would represent native physical weighting while

[
\Lambda_\Sigma
]

would represent underlying continuation extent.

If a justified \Lambda can be constructed, one possible effective volume is

[
\boxed{
\mathcal V_\Sigma

\exp\left[
-\int
\log
\frac{dP_\Sigma}{d\Lambda_\Sigma}
,dP_\Sigma
\right].
}
]

For discrete structural volumes \lambda_\gamma:

[
\mathcal V_\Sigma

\exp\left[
-\sum_\gamma
p_\gamma
\log
\frac{p_\gamma}{\lambda_\gamma}
\right].
]

When

[
\lambda_\gamma=1,
]

this reduces to Shannon effective breadth.

Thus L can be viewed as the flat-measure case.

No unique physical \Lambda is presently established, and such a generalization should not be treated as necessary until the simpler breadth measure encounters a relevant failure that a structural measure can explain.

22. Requirements on any structural extent

Any candidate for possibility extent should survive strong invariance tests.

It should not increase merely because:

- objects are renamed;
- a computational implementation duplicates states;
- one physical event is represented by several bookkeeping steps;
- independent spacelike events are serialized in another order;
- a shared residual future is stored twice.

It should be able to respond to:

- alternative breadth;
- deterministic internal development;
- timing where timing is physically consequential;
- interaction and nonfactorization;
- resource exclusion;
- enabling;
- reconvergence;
- higher-order composition.

It must also distinguish physical structure from representation.

This is the central mathematical challenge.

23. Independent composition and excess

If two continuation systems A and B are genuinely independent, a natural candidate requirement is:

[
\mathcal V(A\otimes B)

\mathcal V(A)\mathcal V(B).
]

Equivalently:

[
\log\mathcal V(A\otimes B)

\log\mathcal V(A)+\log\mathcal V(B).
]

This has not been established as mandatory, but it provides a useful baseline.

For interacting systems one can then define a compositional residual:

[
\boxed{
\Delta_\otimes

\log\mathcal V(A\otimes_{\rm coupled}B)

\log\mathcal V(A)

\log\mathcal V(B).
}
]

Such a residual could reveal:

- redundancy;
- exclusion;
- enabling;
- synergy;
- new bottlenecks;
- newly opened continuation.

Its sign carries no automatic moral interpretation.

It is a structural property.

24. Generativity

Generativity is stronger than immediate branching.

A present is generative when its continuation tends to produce frames that themselves contain substantial or expanding continuation structure.

Schematically:

[
F
\rightarrow
F_i
\rightarrow
F_{ij}
\rightarrow\cdots
]

with the recursive continuation remaining rich or developing new levels of composition.

This distinguishes:

«many immediate alternatives»

from:

«alternatives that generate further structured possibility.»

The latter is closer to the intended meaning of lushness.

25. Lushness

Possibility extent asks:

«How much consequential continuation exists from this physical present?»

Lushness asks a richer question:

«How is that extent organized, maintained, transformed and generated across time and scale?»

Lushness should therefore initially be treated as a profile rather than forced into one scalar.

Schematically:

[
\boxed{
\mathcal L_\Sigma

\mathcal L
(
\text{extent},
\text{horizon},
\text{scale},
\text{composition},
\text{persistence}
).
}
]

Possible coordinates may ultimately include:

[
\mathcal V_\Sigma(s,T),
]

compositional excess,

[
\Delta_\otimes,
]

persistence and recovery properties, and the rate at which higher-order continuation structure is generated.

The exact profile remains open.

26. Gas as baseline

Thermal matter is not the negation of possibility.

Gas contains real physical continuation.

It branches, collides, transports energy, fluctuates and explores lawful configurations.

It therefore establishes a natural baseline possibility substrate.

This baseline should be measured under the same:

- physical law;
- conserved quantities;
- boundaries;
- resolution;
- clock;
- horizon

as the structured preparation being compared.

The correct question is not:

«Is organization more valuable than gas by definition?»

It is:

«Does a particular physical organization produce continuation structure exceeding the matched thermal baseline, and in what dimensions?»

Gas may win some comparisons.

That is an empirical result, not a failure.

The comparison should root each calculation at the actual physical configuration rather than giving thermal matter artificial ensemble uncertainty unavailable to the structured comparison.

27. Persistence

Possibility can be destroyed.

Two presents may differ strongly now while converging toward indistinguishable residual futures.

Other distinctions may propagate for extremely long periods.

Persistence therefore concerns how long a physical distinction remains consequential for continuation.

Persistence is not identical to possibility extent.

A system can have:

- large extent with little persistent organization;
- strong persistence with little alternative breadth.

But persistence matters because value-capable organization requires continuation rather than momentary occurrence alone.

28. Cosmology

The cosmological picture implied by Omega is not fundamentally one of isolated objects moving through a passive stage.

It is a universe of relational structures continuously deforming into further relational structures.

Schematically:

[
\boxed{
\text{relational present}
\rightarrow
\text{lawful deformation}
\rightarrow
\text{relational present}
\rightarrow\cdots
}
]

Persistent objects are stable modes within this relational flow.

Higher-order objects are compositions of lower-order modes.

The universe therefore possesses a recursive architecture of becoming.

Microscopic distinctions compose into relational structures.

Relational structures produce new continuation.

Some continuations generate persistent organizations.

Some persistent organizations compose into further scales.

At sufficiently high levels, systems may become capable of modeling, selecting and intentionally altering continuation itself.

Valuers then appear not as exceptions inserted into physics, but as one class of highly developed physical continuation structure.

29. Being and becoming

The distinction between a static present and its dynamics is useful analytically but incomplete metaphysically.

The present physical organization determines the local space of lawful deformation.

Therefore:

«present structure is frozen becoming.»

And:

«becoming is relational structure propagated through asymmetry.»

A frame contains the conditions of its own further deformation.

The resulting frame contains new conditions.

Reality can therefore be understood as a recursively deforming relational structure rather than as a sequence of featureless states.

---

30. Structural realism

Omega suggests a restrained form of structural realism.

Physical objects matter, but their identities are not exhausted by constituent labels.

What persists across turnover is frequently relational organization.

A cell survives molecular replacement.

A vortex survives fluid replacement.

A computation survives electrical carrier replacement.

The same principle may apply more broadly.

Identity is therefore often better associated with dynamically persistent relational pattern than with permanent microscopic substance.

This does not imply that all patterns are equally real.

A higher-order pattern earns physical significance when it carries genuine information about lawful continuation.

31. The arrow of time

Alpha asymmetry suggests that causal orientation may be deeper than the thermodynamic arrow.

The hierarchy would then be:

[
\boxed{
\text{asymmetry}
\rightarrow
\text{causal orientation}
\rightarrow
\text{physical temporal structure}
\rightarrow
\text{thermodynamic arrow}.

Entropy increase remains physically important, but it need not define the most primitive meaning of forward.

This is a cosmological hypothesis, not a derived result.

32. Valuers

A valuer must be a physical process capable of realizing value.

Omega does not presently define a necessary and sufficient condition for valuerhood.

A plausible structural basis is nevertheless visible.

A valuer is likely to require some combination of:

- persistent organization;
- internal differentiation;
- integration;
- memory;
- predictive structure;
- sensitivity to alternatives;
- capacity to act upon continuation;
- capacity to preserve or transform its own organization.

In Omega language, such a system is a persistent relational composition that participates actively in shaping its own futuresfield.

This is only a candidate structural basis.

It does not establish that every highly generative system is a valuer.

---

33. Gradient Ethics

Gradient Ethics begins from:

[
\boxed{\text{value requires valuers}.}
]

Valuers are physical.

Physical valuers require continuation.

Therefore the physical conditions enabling value-capable continuation have broad instrumental importance.

A cautious ladder is:

1. Value requires valuers.
2. Valuers are physical processes.
3. Physical processes require continuation.
4. Physical configurations differ in the value-capable continuation they support.
5. Such continuation can be damaged, foreclosed, preserved or expanded.
6. Preserving the substrate of value-capable continuation therefore has broad instrumental importance under uncertainty about ultimate value.

This motivates persistence as a metavalue.

Persistence is not identical to indefinite survival.

It does not imply that every existing system should be preserved forever.

It means that the continued existence of value-capable possibility is instrumentally important because without it no further value can be realized.

---

34. Value substrate

The value substrate is physically realized continuation structure capable of supporting value.

It is not itself value.

Thus:

[
\boxed{
\text{value}
\neq
\text{value substrate}
\neq
\text{lushness}.
}
]

Lushness describes features of the substrate.

A lush region may support many future valuers and rich possibilities.

That does not prove every realized state within it is good.

A universe containing vast suffering could possess substantial continuation structure while realizing severe negative value.

The ethical theory therefore cannot terminate at substrate measurement.

As understanding of value improves, action should increasingly steer not merely toward more substrate but toward regions of possibility that realize value more densely and avoid severe negative value.

---

35. Why use a substrate proxy?

Agents do not possess complete moral knowledge.

A practical ethical system therefore needs proxies.

But proxies are vulnerable to Goodhart effects.

Gradient Ethics treats lushness as corrigible rather than sacred.

The goal is not:

«maximize one number regardless of consequence.»

It is:

«use the best available structural proxy for preservation of value-capable continuation while remaining willing to revise that proxy as understanding improves.»

The substrate measure is therefore provisional by design.

36. No privileged biological category

The framework does not assign automatic value to:

- life;
- ecology;
- diversity;
- reproduction;
- intelligence;
- technology.

Any of these may correlate with value-capable continuation in particular contexts.

None receives a semantic bonus.

The comparison must occur through physical consequences.

A sterile machine could conceivably produce more value-capable continuation than an ecosystem.

An ecosystem could vastly outperform a swarm of simple replicators.

A thermal environment could outperform both on some dimensions.

The theory is intended to identify a corridor of value-supporting possibility rather than to crown a familiar category in advance.

Whole-configuration updates should be used rather than arbitrary serializations of simultaneous local events. Coarse projections should preserve unresolved conditional information rather than inventing a new Markov rule when the coarse process is non-Markovian.

38. Analytical screening

Before large simulations, define local row entropy:

[
h(x)=H(K(x,\cdot)).
]

If:

[
h(x)=h_0
]

for every reachable configuration, then:

[
\ell_x(n)=nh_0
]

for every root.

The continuation-breadth candidate is then analytically blind to organizational differences in that model.

No parameter sweep can repair that blindness.

If instead h(x) depends on configuration, then physical organization can influence continuation breadth by steering the process toward states with different future branching.

This screening should precede broad computation.

39. Comparison protocol

For two actual preparations x and b under the same native law, define:

[
\Delta_{x,b}(n)

\ell_x(n)-\ell_b(n)

\log
\frac{L_x(n)}{L_b(n)}.
]

The whole horizon profile should be reported.

A positive value means only:

«the first preparation possesses greater effective weighted future-sequence breadth at that horizon and resolution.»

It does not automatically mean:

- more value;
- more life;
- more intelligence;
- more organization.

For thermal comparison, use physically matched configurations under the same conserved resources, law and boundary conditions.

Preparation uncertainty should not be inserted into one root but not the other.

External resources required to create a preparation should be included when the claimed comparison depends on a matched resource budget.

40. Required adversarial tests

Any candidate possibility measure should face at least the following cases.

Deterministic organization

Can an elaborate deterministic development differ appropriately from a frozen state?

Selective catalysis

Can the measure distinguish structural changes in access from simple clock acceleration?

Same support, different kinetics

Does the measure respond when reachable states remain identical but transition structure changes?

Reconvergence

Does it retain distinct prefixes without duplicating the common suffix?

Independent composition

Does it satisfy a principled composition rule?

Coupled composition

Does interaction create a measurable deviation from the independent baseline?

Redundancy and complementarity

Can multiple interchangeable routes be distinguished from jointly necessary routes?

Resource competition

Can physically exclusive uses of the same resource be represented without counting impossible joint futures?

Thermal baseline

How do organized and thermal preparations compare under the same law?

Representation invariance

Do relabeling and empty computational subdivision leave the answer unchanged?

Resolution and clock

How much of the result is physical and how much is an artifact of chosen temporal or spatial discretization?

41. Quantum requirements

A mature Omega must eventually handle quantum continuation.

At minimum this requires addressing:

- interference;
- decoherence;
- entanglement;
- noncommutativity;
- history coarse-graining;
- the absence of ordinary additive probability for arbitrary fine histories.

A natural future generalization may replace the classical probability measure with a decoherence functional or another quantum process object.

The current classical measure should therefore be understood as an adapter-level realization rather than the final universal ontology.
