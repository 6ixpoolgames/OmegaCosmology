LITERATURE AND MATHEMATICAL INTERFACES
v2.5 checkpoint
Current through 21 September 2026

PURPOSE

This document maps the current research program to neighboring scientific and mathematical literatures.

The purpose is not to assign intellectual ancestry or to identify one adopted formalism.

For each area, the relevant question is:

    What mathematical capability does this literature provide
    for the current program?

The program currently borrows structures and theorem packages rather than importing one neighboring ontology wholesale.


PART I — QUANTUM POSSIBILITY, HISTORIES, AND PERSPECTIVE


1. DECOHERENT / CONSISTENT HISTORIES

Representative names:
    Robert Griffiths
    Roland Omnès
    Murray Gell-Mann
    James Hartle
    John Halliwell

Relevant mathematics:
    history class operators;
    decoherence functional;
    consistency/decoherence conditions;
    quasiclassical domains;
    records.

Standard structure:

    C_alpha
        =
    P_{alpha_n}(t_n) ... P_{alpha_1}(t_1)

    D(alpha,beta)
        =
    Tr(C_alpha rho C_beta^\dagger).

Project interface:
    compatible quantum pasts;
    coherent versus decoherent histories;
    emergence of approximately classical trajectories;
    record-conditioned Perspective;
    focus-relative history bundles.

What it contributes:
    A mature way to discuss histories without assuming fine-grained classical
    mutually exclusive trajectories.

Key caution:
    Different consistent families need not possess one common classical refinement.


2. QUANTUM MEASURE THEORY

Representative name:
    Rafael Sorkin

Relevant mathematics:

    decoherence functional;
    non-additive quantum measure.

Typical object:

        =
    D(A,A).

Project interface:
    prevents naive branch-counting or additive probability at the fully coherent
    fine-grained level.

What it contributes:
    A histories-first mathematical language in which interference remains primitive.

Key consequence:
    additive Born probabilities appear only on appropriate decoherent alternatives.

PART X — WHAT TO BORROW FIRST

The most immediately useful interfaces for the active physical research are:

1. Decoherent histories
    for coherent versus quasiclassical history structure.

2. Algebraic QFT / general-boundary approaches
    for local, covariant Perspective and compositional boundaries.

3. Process tensors / quantum combs
    for residual continuation with memory and intervention.

4. Quantum discrimination and information geometry
    for physically meaningful local separation.

5. Causal states / PSRs / rate-distortion
    for exact and approximate consequence-preserving compression.

6. Topology / quotient theory / metric geometry
    for representation-independent consequence structure.

7. Viability / robust control
    for downstream agency and feasible continuation.

8. Hypergraphs / simplicial complexes / covering theory
    for higher-order compatibility and policy atlases.


PART XI — CENTRAL INTERFACE QUESTION

The unifying mathematical research problem can be stated as:

    Starting from the intrinsic local structure of a universal quantum process,
    can one derive a situated Perspective and then construct a representation-
    invariant geometry of consequential continuation that admits principled
