# Current position

**Working status:** 16 September 2026. This document is the reading entry point
for the v2 chapters and their subsequent corrections.

Omega begins from a completed, relational reality encountered from within
perspectives. Gradient Ethics investigates the premise that value requires
valuers and the consequences of actions for the structured possibility of
further value. Valuerhood remains intentionally broad and incompletely defined.

The global futures object is the proposed substrate of value. Lushness names
an open conjecture about a structural signature in that object. A bounded
agent's representation may guide action but does not replace the referent.

## Current commitments and corrections

- Investigate structure before imposing an ordering. Scalars and optimization
  remain possible later outcomes, rather than required or prohibited forms.
- Persistence and generativity are proposed emergent relations. Do not make
  them admission criteria or bonuses in a candidate lushness score.
- The persistence filter reveals continuation-compatible regimes, without
  entailing a universal cooperative winner.
- Preserve probabilities, interference, joint relations, legal composition,
  actual history occurrences, and the physical limits of control.
- Generative integration can be compatible with plurality. Terminal solitude
  chiefly concerns excisive closure, not simply the number of controllers.
- Rights and virtues are hypotheses about recurring vulnerabilities in
  particular interagent regimes. Their authority is not derived by naming a
  continuation structure.
- Phenomenality, identity, universal habitation, and a final physical comparison
  resolution remain open. Theology is currently apophatic and suggestive.
- A commercial or alignment capability benefit is optional.

These corrections override stronger inherited wording in manuscript chapters
04, 05, 06 and 07. The full [inherited correction and claim ledger](../sources/handoff/02_CURRENT_POSITION_AND_CORRECTIONS.md)
retains the detailed provenance and qualifications. The manuscript is awaiting
an explicit reconciliation pass; it is not silently being presented as revised.

## What the mathematics currently establishes

The [finite quantum note](../sources/quantum_continuation/10_FINITE_QUANTUM_CONTINUATION_FORMALIZATION.md)
defines a declared finite continuation object and proves scope-relative
predictive equivalence, the pullback condition for quotient updates, finite
stationary closure, and scope transport. Its worked instances have 233 exact
checks. The general proofs are ordinary proofs, not Lean-checked results.

[P001](../research/probes/P001/report.md) gives an exact limited counterexample
to a weighted distance summary of a selected boundary family. Its later-stage
support counts differ. It establishes a loss of response information in that
summary and no value ranking.

## Live lead

Weighted, compositional geometry of physically reachable continuation remains
a lead to investigate. The missing bridge is whether its organization reveals
value-capable differentiation, rather than physical variety alone.

The next task will reuse the finite formal object and make its preservation
requirements explicit in the code and probe interfaces. It will not turn
the easiest implementation into the definition of the philosophical target.
