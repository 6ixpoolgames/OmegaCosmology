# Research roadmap

The immediate aim is a faithful account of continuation structure, from which
candidate properties can be investigated. The project has not identified a
lushness measure or a completed account of valuerhood.

## Starting evidence

- The finite quantum formal note supplies the declared process object and its
  scope-relative proofs; its worked baseline has 233 exact checks.
- P001 shows that weighted boundary distances can lose later response
  information. Its terminal support sizes are different, so it leaves richer
  descriptions retaining each stage untested.

## Recommended first task

[001 — Make the finite continuation object a shared research contract](tasks/001-continuation-contract.md).
Reuse the existing formal definition and make explicit how each implementation
retains states, histories, weights, legal controls, composition and observation
scope. Adapt P001 to that contract and report its losses faithfully. This makes
the next experiment interpretable across representations.

## Following work, conditional on that result

1. Design P002 to match stage-by-stage support and distance summaries while
   varying legal sequential connections, if that isolates a useful distinction.
2. Test candidate structural descriptions against repetition, noise, feedback
   and integration with comparable physical budgets. Record failures without
   tuning a score to favored examples.
3. Investigate the missing relation to valuation. Physical control variety
   alone supplies no ethical ranking.
4. Reconcile the manuscript with the current position and retained results,
   then generate a new reading edition with an explicit change log.

Phenomenality, terminal physical resolution and theological interpretation
remain live parallel questions. They are not prerequisites for the small
finite investigations. Rights, decision rules and capability claims should
follow a justified structural interpretation rather than determine it.

Stop a probe when its stated question is answered or its formulation needs a
material missing assumption. More examples of already understood saturation
are not a research milestone by themselves.
