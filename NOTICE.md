# Attribution and status

The Omega / Gradient Ethics research program was originated by S. Poole and
developed through sustained human-directed work with AI systems. The v2 and
formal source documents retain their original attribution. v4.1 is the canonical
starting reference selected on 24 October 2026; the imported historical Drive
archive preserves the older editions and deprecated documents with provenance.
Native Google Docs in that archive are explicitly identified DOCX exports.

On 26 October 2026 the project owner authorized replacing identifying names,
addresses, source links and editor metadata with anonymized representations.
Official attribution uses S. Poole. Current manifests verify those sanitized
bytes, not identity with the original exports. Scientific content and third-party
citations retain their meaning. See [repository privacy](docs/privacy.md).

Selected prior Omega reports preserve their stated scope and provenance.
They are evidence snapshots, not a fresh execution of the earlier repository's
full code or Lean suite. External works are referenced rather than relicensed.

The existing GitHub repository includes the MIT LICENSE created by its owner;
its terms are retained, with copyright attribution under the owner's pseudonym.
Imported historical material retains
any applicable original notices and attribution. An archive import does not
grant rights in third-party material beyond the rights its source permits.
