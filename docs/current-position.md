## Working commitments

Omega is the weighted physical continuation structure from admissible presents.
A frame specifies an admissible present section; the present is a sufficient
physical specification on it. Local observers can have incomplete records.

Lushness is the effective number of complete continuation histories under the
declared conditioning. In the first classical adapter its logarithm is sampled
history entropy. Access, organization and generativity affect the native law
and its further possibilities; none receives an independent multiplier.
All represented physical randomness counts, including thermal and control
activity. Rule A is not a primary filter.

The programme investigates prospective value through that physical substrate.
The central ethical correspondence remains a substantive thesis tested against
judgments and physically explained disagreements. A finite computation does not
settle consciousness, valuer identity or the cosmology.

Whole modeled field and a subsystem projection differ. So do conditioning on
the full present and a subsystem's own present. Preparations, mechanism
contrasts and implemented options require different comparison claims.

The continuation profile is the object. Finite horizons disclose partial
assessments; rate/offset describe its long-horizon behavior where their
assumptions hold. A stationary calculation does not replace a finite query.
